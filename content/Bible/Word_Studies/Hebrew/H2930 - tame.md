# H2930 — טָמֵא

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| טָמֵא | ṭâmêʼ | taw-may' | to be foul |

## Definition

to be foul, especially in a ceremial or moral sense (contaminated)

KJV renders: defile (self), pollute (self), be (make, make self, pronounce) unclean, utterly.
