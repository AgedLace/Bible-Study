# H6131 — עָקַר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| עָקַר | ʻâqar | aw-kar' | to pluck up (especially by the roots) |

## Definition

to pluck up (especially by the roots); specifically, to hamstring; figuratively, to exterminate

KJV renders: dig down, hough, pluck up, root up.
