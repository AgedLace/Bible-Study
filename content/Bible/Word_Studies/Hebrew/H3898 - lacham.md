# H3898 — לָחַם

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| לָחַם | lâcham | law-kham' | to feed on |

## Definition

to feed on; figuratively, to consume; by implication, to battle (as destruction)

KJV renders: devour, eat, ever, fight(-ing), overcome, prevail, (make) war(-ring).
