# H0887 — בָּאַשׁ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| בָּאַשׁ | bâʼash | baw-ash' | to smell bad |

## Definition

to smell bad; figuratively, to be offensive morally

KJV renders: (make to) be abhorred (had in abomination, loathsome, odious), (cause a, make to) stink(-ing savour), utterly.
