# H5375 — נָשָׂא

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| נָשָׂא | nâsâʼ | naw-saw' | to lift |

## Definition

to lift, in a great variety of applications, literal and figurative, absolute and relative

KJV renders: accept, advance, arise, (able to, (armor), suffer to) bear(-er, up), bring (forth), burn, carry (away), cast, contain, desire, ease, exact, exalt (self), extol, fetch, forgive, furnish, further, give, go on, help, high, hold up, honorable ( man), lade, lay, lift (self) up, lofty, marry, magnify, needs, obtain, pardon, raise (up), receive, regard, respect, set (up), spare, stir up, swear, take (away, up), utterly, wear, yield.
