# H3803 — כָּתַר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| כָּתַר | kâthar | kaw-thar' | to enclose |

## Definition

to enclose; hence (in a friendly sense) to crown, (in a hostile one) to besiege; also to wait (as restraining oneself)

KJV renders: beset round, compass about, be crowned inclose round, suffer.
