# H4676 — מַצֵּבָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| מַצֵּבָה | matstsêbâh | mats-tsay-baw' | something stationed |

## Definition

something stationed, i.e. a column or (memorial stone); by analogy, an idol

KJV renders: garrison, (standing) image, pillar.
