# H8478 — תַּחַת

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| תַּחַת | tachath | takh'-ath | the bottom (as depressed) |

## Definition

the bottom (as depressed); only adverbially, below (often with prepositional prefix underneath), in lieu of, etc.

KJV renders: as, beneath, flat, in(-stead), (same) place (where...is), room, for...sake, stead of, under, unto, when...was mine, whereas, (where-) fore, with.
