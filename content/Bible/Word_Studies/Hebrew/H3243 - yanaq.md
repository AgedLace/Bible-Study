# H3243 — יָנַק

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| יָנַק | yânaq | yaw-nak' | to suck |

## Definition

KJV renders: milch, nurse(-ing mother), (give, make to) suck(-ing child, -ling).
