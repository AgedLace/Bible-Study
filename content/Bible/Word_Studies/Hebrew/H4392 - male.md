# H4392 — מָלֵא

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| מָלֵא | mâlêʼ | maw-lay' | full (literally or figuratively) or filling (literally) |

## Definition

full (literally or figuratively) or filling (literally); also (concretely) fulness; adverbially, fully

KJV renders: she that was with child, fill(-ed, -ed with), full(-ly), multitude, as is worth.
