# H5102 — נָהַר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| נָהַר | nâhar | naw-har' | to sparkle |

## Definition

to sparkle, i.e. (figuratively) be cheerful; hence (from the sheen of a running stream) to flow, i.e. (figuratively) assemble

KJV renders: flow (together), be lightened.
