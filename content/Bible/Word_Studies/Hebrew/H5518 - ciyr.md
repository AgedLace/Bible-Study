| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| סִיר | çîyr | seer | a pot |

## Definition

a pot; also a thorn (as springing up rapidly); by implication, a hook

KJV renders: caldron, fishhook, pan, (wash-)pot, thorn.
