# H8159 — שָׁעָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| שָׁעָה | shâʻâh | shaw-aw' | to gaze at or about (properly |

## Definition

to gaze at or about (properly, for help); by implication, to inspect, consider, compassionate, be nonplussed (as looking around in amazement) or bewildered

KJV renders: depart, be dim, be dismayed, look (away), regard, have respect, spare, turn.
