# H5068 — נָדַב

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| נָדַב | nâdab | naw-dab' | to impel |

## Definition

to impel; hence, to volunteer (as a soldier), to present spontaneously

KJV renders: offer freely, be (give, make, offer self) willing(-ly).
