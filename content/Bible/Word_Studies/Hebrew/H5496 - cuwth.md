# H5496 — סוּת

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| סוּת | çûwth | sooth | properly |

## Definition

properly, to prick, i.e. (figuratively) stimulate; by implication, to seduce

KJV renders: entice, move, persuade, provoke, remove, set on, stir up, take away.
