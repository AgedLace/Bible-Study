# H2706 — חֹק

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| חֹק | chôq | khoke | an enactment |

## Definition

an enactment; hence, an appointment (of time, space, quantity, labor or usage)

KJV renders: appointed, bound, commandment, convenient, custom, decree(-d), due, law, measure, necessary, ordinance(-nary), portion, set time, statute, task.
