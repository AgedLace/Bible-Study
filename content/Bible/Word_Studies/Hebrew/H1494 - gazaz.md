# H1494 — גַּזָז

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| גַּזָז | gazâz | gaw-zaz' | to cut off |

## Definition

to cut off; specifically to shear aflock or shave the hair; figuratively to destroy an enemy

KJV renders: cut off (down), poll, shave, (sheep-) shear(-er).
