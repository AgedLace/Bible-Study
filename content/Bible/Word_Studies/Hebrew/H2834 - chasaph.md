# H2834 — חָשַׂף

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| חָשַׂף | châsaph | khaw-saf' | to strip off |

## Definition

to strip off, i.e. generally to make naked (for exertion or in disgrace), to drain away or bail up (a liquid)

KJV renders: make bare, clean, discover, draw out, take, uncover.
