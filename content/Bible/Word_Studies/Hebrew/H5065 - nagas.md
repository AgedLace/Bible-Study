# H5065 — נָגַשׂ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| נָגַשׂ | nâgas | naw-gas' | to drive (an animal |

## Definition

to drive (an animal, a workman, a debtor, an army); by implication, to tax, harass, tyrannize

KJV renders: distress, driver, exact(-or), oppress(-or), raiser of taxes, taskmaster.
