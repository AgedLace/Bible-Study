# H6664 — צֶדֶק

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| צֶדֶק | tsedeq | tseh'-dek | the right (natural |

## Definition

the right (natural, moral or legal); also (abstractly) equity or (figuratively) prosperity

KJV renders: even, ( that which is altogether) just(-ice), (un-)right(-eous) (cause, -ly, -ness).
