# H1431 — גָּדַל

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| גָּדַל | gâdal | gaw-dal' | to be (causatively make) large (in various senses |

## Definition

to be (causatively make) large (in various senses, as in body, mind, estate or honor, also in pride)

KJV renders: advance, boast, bring up, exceed, excellent, be(-come, do, give, make, wax), great(-er, come to... estate, things), grow(up), increase, lift up, magnify(-ifical), be much set by, nourish (up), pass, promote, proudly (spoken), tower.
