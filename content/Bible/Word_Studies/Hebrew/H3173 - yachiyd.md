# H3173 — יָחִיד

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| יָחִיד | yâchîyd | yaw-kheed' | properly |

## Definition

properly, united, i.e. sole; by implication, beloved; also lonely; (feminine) the life (as not to be replaced)

KJV renders: darling, desolate, only (child, son), solitary.
