# H7535 — רַק

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| רַק | raq | rak | properly |

## Definition

properly, leanness, i.e. (figuratively) limitation; only adverbial, merely, or conjunctional, although

KJV renders: but, even, except, howbeit howsoever, at the least, nevertheless, nothing but, notwithstanding, only, save, so (that), surely, yet (so), in any wise.
