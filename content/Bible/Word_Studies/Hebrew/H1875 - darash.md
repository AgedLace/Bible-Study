# H1875 — דָּרַשׁ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| דָּרַשׁ | dârash | daw-rash' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to tread or frequent; usually to follow (for pursuit or search); by implication, to seek or ask; specifically to worship

KJV renders: ask, at all, care for, diligently, inquire, make inquisition, (necro-) mancer, question, require, search, seek (for, out), surely.
