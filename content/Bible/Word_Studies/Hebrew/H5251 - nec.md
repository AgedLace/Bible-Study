# H5251 — נֵס

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| נֵס | nêç | nace | a flag |

## Definition

a flag; also a sail; by implication, a flagstaff; generally a signal; figuratively, a token

KJV renders: banner, pole, sail, (en-) sign, standard.
