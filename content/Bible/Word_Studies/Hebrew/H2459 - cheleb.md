# H2459 — חֶלֶב

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| חֶלֶב | cheleb | kheh'-leb | fat |

## Definition

fat, whether literally or figuratively; hence, the richest or choice part

KJV renders: best, fat(-ness), finest, grease, marrow.
