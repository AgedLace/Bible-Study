# H8585 — תְּעָלָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| תְּעָלָה | tᵉʻâlâh | teh-aw-law' | a channel (into which water is raised for irrigation) |

## Definition

a channel (into which water is raised for irrigation); also a bandage or plaster (as placed upon a wound)

KJV renders: conduit, cured, healing, little river, trench, watercourse.
