# H0481 — אָלַם

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| אָלַם | ʼâlam | aw-lam' | to tie fast |

## Definition

to tie fast; hence (of the mouth) to be tongue-tied

KJV renders: bind, be dumb, put to silence.
