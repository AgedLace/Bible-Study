# H4454 — מָלַק

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| מָלַק | mâlaq | maw-lak' | to crack a joint |

## Definition

to crack a joint; by implication, to wring the neck of afowl (without separating it)

KJV renders: wring off.
