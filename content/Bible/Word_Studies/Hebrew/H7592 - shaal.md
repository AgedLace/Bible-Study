# H7592 — שָׁאַל

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| שָׁאַל | shâʼal | shaw-al' | to inquire |

## Definition

to inquire; by implication, to request; by extension, to demand

KJV renders: ask (counsel, on), beg, borrow, lay to charge, consult, demand, desire, earnestly, enquire, greet, obtain leave, lend, pray, request, require, salute, straitly, surely, wish.
