# H4060 — מִדָּה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| מִדָּה | middâh | mid-daw' | properly |

## Definition

properly, extension, i.e. height or breadth; also a measure (including its standard); hence a portion (as measured) or a vestment; specifically, tribute (as measured)

KJV renders: garment, measure(-ing, meteyard, piece, size, (great) stature, tribute, wide.
