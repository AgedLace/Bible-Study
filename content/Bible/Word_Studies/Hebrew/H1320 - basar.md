# H1320 — בָּשָׂר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| בָּשָׂר | bâsâr | baw-sawr' | flesh (from its freshness) |

## Definition

flesh (from its freshness); by extension, body, person; also (by euphemistically) the pudenda of aman

KJV renders: body, (fat, lean) flesh(-ed), kin, (man-) kind, nakedness, self, skin.
