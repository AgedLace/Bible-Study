# H5594 — סָפַד

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| סָפַד | çâphad | saw-fad' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to tear the hair and beat the breasts (as Orientals do in grief); generally to lament; by implication, to wail

KJV renders: lament, mourn(-er), wail.
