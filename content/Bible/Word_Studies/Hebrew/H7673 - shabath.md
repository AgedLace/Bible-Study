# H7673 — שָׁבַת

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| שָׁבַת | shâbath | shaw-bath' | to repose |

## Definition

to repose, i.e. desist from exertion; used in many implied relations (causative, figurative or specific)

KJV renders: (cause to, let, make to) cease, celebrate, cause (make) to fail, keep (sabbath), suffer to be lacking, leave, put away (down), (make to) rest, rid, still, take away.
