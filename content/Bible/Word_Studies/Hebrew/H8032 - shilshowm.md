# H8032 — שִׁלְשׁוֹם

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|

## Definition

trebly, i.e. (in time) day before yesterday

KJV renders: before (that time, -time), excellent things (from the margin), heretofore, three days, time past.
