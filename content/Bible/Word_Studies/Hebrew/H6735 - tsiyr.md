# H6735 — צִיר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| צִיר | tsîyr | tseer | a hinge (as pressed in turning) |

## Definition

a hinge (as pressed in turning); also a throe (as a phys. or mental pressure); also a herald or erranddoer (as constrained by the principal)

KJV renders: ambassador, hinge, messenger, pain, pang, sorrow. Compare H6736 (צִיר).
