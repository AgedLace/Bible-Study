| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |

| אָבָה | ʼâbâh | aw-baw' | to breathe after |

## Definition

to breathe after, i.e. (figuratively) to be acquiescent

KJV renders: consent, rest content will, be willing.
