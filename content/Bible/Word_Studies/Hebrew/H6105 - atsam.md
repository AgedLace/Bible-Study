# H6105 — עָצַם

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| עָצַם | ʻâtsam | aw-tsam' | to bind fast |

## Definition

to bind fast, i.e. close (the eyes); intransitively, to be (causatively, make) powerful or numerous; to crunch the bones

KJV renders: break the bones, close, be great, be increased, be (wax) mighty(-ier), be more, shut, be(-come, make) strong(-er).
