# H0319 — אַחֲרִית

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| אַחֲרִית | ʼachărîyth | akh-ar-eeth' | the last or end |

## Definition

the last or end, hence, the future; also posterity

KJV renders: (last, latter) end (time), hinder (utter) -most, length, posterity, remnant, residue, reward.
