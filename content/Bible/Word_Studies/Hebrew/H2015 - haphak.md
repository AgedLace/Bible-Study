# H2015 — הָפַךְ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| הָפַךְ | hâphak | haw-fak' | to turn about or over |

## Definition

to turn about or over; by implication, to change, overturn, return, pervert

KJV renders: become, change, come, be converted, give, make (a bed), overthrow (-turn), perverse, retire, tumble, turn (again, aside, back, to the contrary, every way).
