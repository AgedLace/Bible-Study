# H3381 — יָרַד

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| יָרַד | yârad | yaw-rad' | to descend (literally |

## Definition

to descend (literally, to go downwards; or conventionally to a lower region, as the shore, a boundary, the enemy, etc.; or figuratively, to fall); causatively, to bring down (in all the above applications)

KJV renders: abundantly, bring down, carry down, cast down, (cause to) come(-ing) down, fall (down), get down, go(-ing) down(-ward), hang down, indeed, let down, light (down), put down (off), (cause to, let) run down, sink, subdue, take down.
