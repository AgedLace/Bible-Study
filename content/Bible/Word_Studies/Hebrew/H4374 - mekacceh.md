# H4374 — מְכַסֶּה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| מְכַסֶּה | mᵉkaççeh | mek-as-seh' | a covering |

## Definition

a covering, i.e. garment; specifically, a coverlet (for a bed), an awning (from the sun); also the omentum (as covering the intestines)

KJV renders: clothing, to cover, that which covereth.
