# H0314 — אַחֲרוֹן

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| אַחֲרוֹן | ʼachărôwn | akh-ar-one' | hinder |

## Definition

hinder; generally, late or last; specifically (as facing the east) western

KJV renders: after (-ward), to come, following, hind(-er, -ermost, -most), last, latter, rereward, ut(ter) most.
