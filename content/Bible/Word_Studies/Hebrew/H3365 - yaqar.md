# H3365 — יָקַר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| יָקַר | yâqar | yaw-kar' | properly |

## Definition

properly, apparently, to be heavy, i.e. (figuratively) valuable; causatively, to make rare (figuratively, to inhibit)

KJV renders: be (make) precious, be prized, be set by, withdraw.
