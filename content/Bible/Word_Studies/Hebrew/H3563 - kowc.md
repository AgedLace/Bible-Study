# H3563 — כּוֹס

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| כּוֹס | kôwç | koce | a cup (as a container) |

## Definition

a cup (as a container), often figuratively, a lot (as if a potion); also some unclean bird, probably an owl (perhaps from the cup-like cavity of its eye)

KJV renders: cup, (small) owl. Compare H3599 (כִּיס).
