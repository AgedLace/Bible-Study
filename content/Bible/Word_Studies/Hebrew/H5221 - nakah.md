# H5221 — נָכָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| נָכָה | nâkâh | naw-kaw' | to strike (lightly or severely |

## Definition

to strike (lightly or severely, literally or figuratively)

KJV renders: beat, cast forth, clap, give (wounds), go forward, indeed, kill, make (slaughter), murderer, punish, slaughter, slay(-er, -ing), smite(-r, -ing), strike, be stricken, (give) stripes, surely, wound.
