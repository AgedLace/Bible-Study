# H3051 — יָהַב

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| יָהַב | yâhab | yaw-hab' | to give (whether literal or figurative) |

## Definition

to give (whether literal or figurative); generally, to put; imperatively (reflexive) come

KJV renders: ascribe, bring, come on, give, go, set, take.
