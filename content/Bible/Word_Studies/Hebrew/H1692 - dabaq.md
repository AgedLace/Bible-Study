# H1692 — דָּבַק

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| דָּבַק | dâbaq | daw-bak' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to impinge, i.e. cling or adhere; figuratively, to catch by pursuit

KJV renders: abide fast, cleave (fast together), follow close (hard after), be joined (together), keep (fast), overtake, pursue hard, stick, take.
