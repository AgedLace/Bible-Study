# H2505 — חָלַק

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| חָלַק | châlaq | khaw-lak' | to be smooth (figuratively) |

## Definition

to be smooth (figuratively); by implication (as smooth stones were used for lots) to apportion or separate

KJV renders: deal, distribute, divide, flatter, give, (have, im-) part(-ner), take away a portion, receive, separate self, (be) smooth(-er).
