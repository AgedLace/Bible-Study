# H2734 — חָרָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| חָרָה | chârâh | khaw-raw' | to glow or grow warm |

## Definition

to glow or grow warm; figuratively (usually) to blaze up, of anger, zeal, jealousy

KJV renders: be angry, burn, be displeased, earnestly, fret self, grieve, be (wax) hot, be incensed, kindle, very, be wroth. See H8474 (תַּחָרָה).
