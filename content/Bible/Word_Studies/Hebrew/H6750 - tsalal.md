# H6750 — צָלַל

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| צָלַל | tsâlal | tsaw-lal' | to tinkle |

## Definition

to tinkle, i.e. rattle together (as the ears in reddening with shame, or the teeth in chattering with fear)

KJV renders: quiver, tingle.
