# H3644 — כְּמוֹ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| כְּמוֹ | kᵉmôw | kem-o' | a form of the prefix 'k-' |

## Definition

a form of the prefix 'k-', but used separately  as, thus, so

KJV renders: according to, (such) as (it were, well as), in comparison of, like (as, to, unto), thus, when, worth.
