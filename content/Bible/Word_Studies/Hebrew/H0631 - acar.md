# H0631 — אָסַר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| אָסַר | ʼâçar | aw-sar' | to yoke or hitch |

## Definition

to yoke or hitch; by analogy, to fasten in any sense, to join battle

KJV renders: bind, fast, gird, harness, hold, keep, make ready, order, prepare, prison(-er), put in bonds, set in array, tie.
