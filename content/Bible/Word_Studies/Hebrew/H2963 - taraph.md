# H2963 — טָרַף

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| טָרַף | ṭâraph | taw-raf' | to pluck off or pull to pieces |

## Definition

to pluck off or pull to pieces; causatively to supply with food (as in morsels)

KJV renders: catch, without doubt, feed, ravin, rend in pieces, surely, tear (in pieces).
