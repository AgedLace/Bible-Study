# H4033 — מָגוּר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |

| מָגוּר | mâgûwr | maw-goor' | a temporary abode |

## Definition

a temporary abode; by extension, a permanent residence

KJV renders: dwelling, pilgrimage, where sojourn, be a stranger. Compare H4032 (מָגוֹר).
