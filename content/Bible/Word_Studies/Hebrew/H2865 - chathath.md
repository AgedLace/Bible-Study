# H2865 — חָתַת

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| חָתַת | châthath | khaw-thath' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to prostrate; hence, to break down, either (literally) by violence, or (figuratively) by confusion and fear

KJV renders: abolish, affright, be (make) afraid, amaze, beat down, discourage, (cause to) dismay, go down, scare, terrify.
