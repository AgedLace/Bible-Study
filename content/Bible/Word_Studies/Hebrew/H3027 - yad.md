| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |

## Definition

a hand (the open one (indicating power, means, direction, etc.),

KJV renders: ( be) able, about, armholes, at, axletree, because of, beside, border, bounty, broad, (broken-) handed, by, charge, coast, consecrate, creditor, custody, debt, dominion, enough, fellowship, force, from, hand(-staves, -y work), he, himself, in, labour, large, ledge, (left-) handed, means, mine, ministry, near, of, order, ordinance, our, parts, pain, power, presumptuously, service, side, sore, state, stay, draw with strength, stroke, swear, terror, thee, by them, themselves, thine own, thou, through, throwing, thumb, times, to, under, us, wait on, (way-) side, where, wide, with (him, me, you), work, yield, yourselves.
