# H7799 — שׁוּשַׁן

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| שׁוּשַׁן | shûwshan | shoo-shan' | a lily (from its whiteness) |

## Definition

a lily (from its whiteness), as aflower of architectural ornament; also a (straight) trumpet (from the tubular shape)

KJV renders: lily, Shoshannim.
