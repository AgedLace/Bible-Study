| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |

| עֶצֶב | ʻetseb | eh'-tseb | an earthen vessel |

## Definition

an earthen vessel; usually (painful) toil; also a pang (whether of body or mind)

KJV renders: grievous, idol, labor, sorrow.
