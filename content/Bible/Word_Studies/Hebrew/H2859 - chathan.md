# H2859 — חָתַן

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| חָתַן | châthan | khaw-than' | to give (a daughter) away in marriage |

## Definition

to give (a daughter) away in marriage; hence (generally) to contract affinity by marriage

KJV renders: join in affinity, father in law, make marriages, mother in law, son in law.
