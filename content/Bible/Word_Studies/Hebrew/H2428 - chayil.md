# H2428 — חַיִל

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| חַיִל | chayil | khah'-yil | probably a force |

## Definition

probably a force, whether of men, means or other resources; an army, wealth, virtue, valor, strength

KJV renders: able, activity, () army, band of men (soldiers), company, (great) forces, goods, host, might, power, riches, strength, strong, substance, train, () valiant(-ly), valour, virtuous(-ly), war, worthy(-ily).
