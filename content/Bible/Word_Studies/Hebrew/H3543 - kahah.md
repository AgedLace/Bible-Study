# H3543 — כָּהָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| כָּהָה | kâhâh | kaw-haw' | to be weak |

## Definition

to be weak, i.e. (figuratively) to despond (causatively, rebuke), or (of light, the eye) to grow dull

KJV renders: darken, be dim, fail, faint, restrain, utterly.
