# H7321 — רוּעַ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| רוּעַ | rûwaʻ | roo-ah' | to mar (especially by breaking) |

## Definition

to mar (especially by breaking); figuratively, to split the ears (with sound), i.e. shout (for alarm or joy)

KJV renders: blow an alarm, cry (alarm, aloud, out), destroy, make a joyful noise, smart, shout (for joy), sound an alarm, triumph.
