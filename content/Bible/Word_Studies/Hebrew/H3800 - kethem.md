# H3800 — כֶּתֶם

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| כֶּתֶם | kethem | keh'-them | properly |

## Definition

properly, something carved out, i.e. ore; hence, gold (pure as originally mined)

KJV renders: ((most) fine, pure) gold(-en wedge).
