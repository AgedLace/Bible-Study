# H6828 — צָפוֹן

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| צָפוֹן | tsâphôwn | tsaw-fone' | properly |

## Definition

properly, hidden, i.e. dark; used only of the north as aquarter (gloomy and unknown)

KJV renders: north(-ern, side, -ward, wind).
