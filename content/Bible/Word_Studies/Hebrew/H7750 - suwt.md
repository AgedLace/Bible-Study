# H7750 — שׂוּט

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| שׂוּט | sûwṭ | soot | to detrude |

## Definition

to detrude, i.e. (intransitively and figuratively) become derelict (wrongly practise; namely, idolatry)

KJV renders: turn aside to.
