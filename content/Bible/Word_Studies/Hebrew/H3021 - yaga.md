# H3021 — יָגַע

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| יָגַע | yâgaʻ | yaw-gah' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to gasp; hence, to be exhausted, to tire, to toil

KJV renders: faint, (make to) labour, (be) weary.
