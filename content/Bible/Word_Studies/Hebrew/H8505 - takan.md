# H8505 — תָּכַן

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| תָּכַן | tâkan | taw-kan' | to balance |

## Definition

to balance, i.e. measure out (by weight or dimension); figuratively, arrange, equalize, through the idea of levelling (ment. estimate, test)

KJV renders: bear up, direct, be (un-)equal, mete, ponder, tell, weigh.
