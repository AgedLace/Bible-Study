# H7069 — קָנָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| קָנָה | qânâh | kaw-naw' | to erect |

## Definition

to erect, i.e. create; by extension, to procure, especially by purchase (causatively, sell); by implication to own

KJV renders: attain, buy(-er), teach to keep cattle, get, provoke to jealousy, possess(-or), purchase, recover, redeem, surely, verily.
