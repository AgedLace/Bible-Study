# H6567 — פָּרָשׁ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| פָּרָשׁ | pârâsh | paw-rash' | to separate |

## Definition

to separate, literally (to disperse) or figuratively (to specify); also (by implication) to wound

KJV renders: scatter, declare, distinctly, shew, sting.
