# H0582 — אֱנוֹשׁ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| אֱנוֹשׁ | ʼĕnôwsh | en-oshe' | a man in general (singly or collectively) |

## Definition

a man in general (singly or collectively)

KJV renders: another, (blood-) thirsty, certain, chap(-man); divers, fellow, in the flower of their age, husband, (certain, mortal) man, people, person, servant, some ( of them), stranger, those, their trade. It is often unexpressed in the English versions, especially when used in apposition with another word. Compare H376 (אִישׁ).
