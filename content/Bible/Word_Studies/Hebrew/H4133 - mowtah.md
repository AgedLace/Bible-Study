# H4133 — מוֹטָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| מוֹטָה | môwṭâh | mo-taw' | a pole |

## Definition

a pole; by implication, an ox-bow; hence, a yoke (either literal or figurative)

KJV renders: bands, heavy, staves, yoke.
