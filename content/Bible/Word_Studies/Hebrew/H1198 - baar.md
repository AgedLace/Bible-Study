# H1198 — בַּעַר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| בַּעַר | baʻar | bah'-ar | properly |

## Definition

properly, foot (as consumed); i.e. (by exten.) of cattle brutishness; (concretely) stupid

KJV renders: brutish (person), foolish.
