# H1497 — גָּזַל

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| גָּזַל | gâzal | gaw-zal' | to pluck off |

## Definition

to pluck off; specifically to flay, strip or rob

KJV renders: catch, consume, exercise (robbery), pluck (off), rob, spoil, take away (by force, violence), tear.
