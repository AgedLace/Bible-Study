# H4522 — מַס

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| מַס | maç | mas | properly |

## Definition

properly, a burden (as causing to faint), i.e. a tax in the form of forced labor

KJV renders: discomfited, levy, task(-master), tribute(-tary).
