# H6087 — עָצַב

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| עָצַב | ʻâtsab | aw-tsab' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to carve, i.e. fabricate or fashion; hence (in a bad sense) to worry, pain or anger

KJV renders: displease, grieve, hurt, make, be sorry, vex, worship, wrest.
