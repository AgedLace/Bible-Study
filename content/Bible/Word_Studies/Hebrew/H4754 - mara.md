# H4754 — מָרָא

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| מָרָא | mârâʼ | maw-raw' | to rebel |

## Definition

to rebel; hence (through the idea of maltreating) to whip, i.e. lash (self with wings, as the ostrich in running)

KJV renders: be filthy, lift up self.
