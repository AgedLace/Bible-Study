# H6999 — קָטַר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| קָטַר | qâṭar | kaw-tar' | to smoke |

## Definition

to smoke, i.e. turn into fragrance by fire (especially as an act of worship)

KJV renders: burn (incense, sacrifice) (upon), (altar for) incense, kindle, offer (incense, a sacrifice).
