# H7126 — קָרַב

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| קָרַב | qârab | kaw-rab' | to approach (causatively |

## Definition

to approach (causatively, bring near) for whatever purpose

KJV renders: (cause to) approach, (cause to) bring (forth, near), (cause to) come (near, nigh), (cause to) draw near (nigh), go (near), be at hand, join, be near, offer, present, produce, make ready, stand, take.
