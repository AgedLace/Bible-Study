# H7639 — שְׂבָכָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| שְׂבָכָה | sᵉbâkâh | seb-aw-kaw' | a net-work |

## Definition

a net-work, i.e. (in hunting) a snare, (in architecture) a ballustrade; also a reticulated ornament to apillar

KJV renders: checker, lattice, network, snare, wreath(-enwork).
