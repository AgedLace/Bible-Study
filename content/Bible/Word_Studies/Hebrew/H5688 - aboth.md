# H5688 — עֲבֹת

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |

| עֲבֹת | ʻăbôth | ab-oth' | something intwined |

## Definition

something intwined, i.e. a string, wreath or foliage

KJV renders: band, cord, rope, thick bough (branch), wreathen (chain).
