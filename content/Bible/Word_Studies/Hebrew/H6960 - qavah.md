# H6960 — קָוָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| קָוָה | qâvâh | kaw-vaw' | to bind together (perhaps by twisting) |

## Definition

to bind together (perhaps by twisting), i.e. collect; (figuratively) to expect

KJV renders: gather (together), look, patiently, tarry, wait (for, on, upon).
