# H4603 — מָעַל

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| מָעַל | mâʻal | maw-al' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to cover up; used only figuratively, to act covertly, i.e. treacherously

KJV renders: transgress, (commit, do a) trespass(-ing).
