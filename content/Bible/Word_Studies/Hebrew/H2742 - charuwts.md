# H2742 — חֲרוּץ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| חֲרוּץ | chărûwts | khaw-roots' | properly |

## Definition

properly, incised or (active) incisive; hence (as noun masculine or feminine) a trench (as dug), gold (as mined), a threshing-sledge (having sharp teeth); (figuratively) determination; also eager

KJV renders: decision, diligent, (fine) gold, pointed things, sharp, threshing instrument, wall.
