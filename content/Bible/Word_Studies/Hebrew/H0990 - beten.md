# H0990 — בֶּטֶן

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| בֶּטֶן | beṭen | beh'-ten | the belly |

## Definition

the belly, especially the womb; also the bosom or body of anything

KJV renders: belly, body, as they be born, within, womb.
