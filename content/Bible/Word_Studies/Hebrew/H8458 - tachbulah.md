# H8458 — תַּחְבֻּלָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| תַּחְבֻּלָה | tachbulâh | takh-boo-law' | (only in plural) properly |

## Definition

(only in plural) properly, steerage (as a management of ropes), i.e. (figuratively) guidance or (by implication) a plan

KJV renders: good advice, (wise) counsels.
