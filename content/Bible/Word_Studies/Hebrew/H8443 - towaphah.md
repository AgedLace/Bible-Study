# H8443 — תּוֹעָפָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| תּוֹעָפָה | tôwʻâphâh | to-aw-faw' | (only in plural collective) weariness |

## Definition

(only in plural collective) weariness, i.e. (by implication) toil (treasure so obtained) or speed

KJV renders: plenty, strength.
