# H0650 — אָפִיק

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| אָפִיק | ʼâphîyq | aw-feek' | properly |

## Definition

properly, containing, i.e. a tube; also a bed or valley of astream; also a strong thing or a hero

KJV renders: brook, channel, mighty, river, scale, stream, strong piece.
