# H3820 — לֵב

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| לֵב | lêb | labe | the heart |

## Definition

the heart; also used (figuratively) very widely for the feelings, the will and even the intellect; likewise for the centre of anything

KJV renders: care for, comfortably, consent, considered, courag(-eous), friend(-ly), ((broken-), (hard-), (merry-), (stiff-), (stout-), double) heart(-ed), heed, I, kindly, midst, mind(-ed), regard(-ed), themselves, unawares, understanding, well, willingly, wisdom.
