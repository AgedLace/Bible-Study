# H2287 — חָגַג

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| חָגַג | châgag | khaw-gag' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to move in acircle, i.e. (specifically) to march in asacred procession, to observe afestival; by implication, to be giddy

KJV renders: celebrate, dance, (keep, hold) a (solemn) feast (holiday), reel to and fro.
