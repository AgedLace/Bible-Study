# H3972 — מְאוּמָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |

## Definition

properly, a speck or point, i.e. (by implication) something; with negative, nothing

KJV renders: fault, no(-ught), ought, somewhat, any (no-)thing.
