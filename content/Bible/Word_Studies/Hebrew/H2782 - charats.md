# H2782 — חָרַץ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| חָרַץ | chârats | khaw-rats' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to point sharply, i.e. (literally) to wound; figuratively, to be alert, to decide

KJV renders: bestir self, decide, decree, determine, maim, move.
