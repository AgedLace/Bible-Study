# H1101 — בָּלַל

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| בָּלַל | bâlal | baw-lal' | to overflow (specifically with oil.) |

## Definition

to overflow (specifically with oil.); by implication, to mix; to fodder

KJV renders: anoint, confound, fade, mingle, mix (self), give provender, temper.
