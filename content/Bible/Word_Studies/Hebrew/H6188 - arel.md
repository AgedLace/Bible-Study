# H6188 — עָרֵל

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| עָרֵל | ʻârêl | aw-rale' | to expose or remove the prepuce |

## Definition

to expose or remove the prepuce, whether literal (to go naked) or figurative (to refrain from using)

KJV renders: count uncircumcised, foreskin to be uncovered.
