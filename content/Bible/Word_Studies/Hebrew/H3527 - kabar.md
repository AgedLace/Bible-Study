# H3527 — כָּבַר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| כָּבַר | kâbar | kaw-bar' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to plait together, i.e. (figuratively) to augment (especially in number or quantity, to accumulate)

KJV renders: in abundance, multiply.
