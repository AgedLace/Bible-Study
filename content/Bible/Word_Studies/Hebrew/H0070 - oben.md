# H0070 — אֹבֶן

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| אֹבֶן | ʼôben | o' ben | a pair of stones (only dual) |

## Definition

a pair of stones (only dual); a potter's wheel or a midwife's stool (consisting alike of two horizontal disks with a support between)

KJV renders: wheel, stool.
