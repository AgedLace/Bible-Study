# H2280 — חָבַשׁ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| חָבַשׁ | châbash | khaw-bash' | to wrap firmly (especially a turban |

## Definition

to wrap firmly (especially a turban, compress, or saddle); figuratively, to stop, to rule

KJV renders: bind (up), gird about, govern, healer, put, saddle, wrap about.
