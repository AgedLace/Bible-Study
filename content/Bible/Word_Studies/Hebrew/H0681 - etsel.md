# H0681 — אֵצֶל

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| אֵצֶל | ʼêtsel | ay'-tsel | a side |

## Definition

a side; (as a preposition) near

KJV renders: at, (hard) by, (from) (beside), near (unto), toward, with. See also H1018 (בֵּית הָאֵצֶל).
