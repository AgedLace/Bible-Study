# H6629 — צֹאן

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| צֹאן | tsôʼn | tsone | a collective name for a flock (of sheep or goats) |

## Definition

a collective name for a flock (of sheep or goats); also figuratively (of men)

KJV renders: (small) cattle, flock ( -s), lamb ( -s), sheep(-cote, -fold, -shearer, -herds).
