# H3863 — לוּא

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| לוּא | lûwʼ | loo | a conditional particle |

## Definition

a conditional particle; if; by implication (interj. as a wish) would that!

KJV renders: if (haply), peradventure, I pray thee, though, I would, would God (that).
