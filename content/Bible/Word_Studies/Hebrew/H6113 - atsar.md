# H6113 — עָצָר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| עָצָר | ʻâtsâr | aw-tsar' | to inclose |

## Definition

to inclose; by analogy, to hold back; also to maintain, rule, assemble

KJV renders: be able, close up, detain, fast, keep (self close, still), prevail, recover, refrain, reign, restrain, retain, shut (up), slack, stay, stop, withhold (self).
