# H6231 — עָשַׁק

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| עָשַׁק | ʻâshaq | aw-shak' | to press upon |

## Definition

to press upon, i.e. oppress, defraud, violate, overflow

KJV renders: get deceitfully, deceive, defraud, drink up, (use) oppress(-ion), -or), do violence (wrong).
