# H6419 — פָּלַל

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| פָּלַל | pâlal | paw-lal' | to judge (officially or mentally) |

## Definition

to judge (officially or mentally); by extension, to intercede, pray

KJV renders: intreat, judge(-ment), (make) pray(-er, -ing), make supplication.
