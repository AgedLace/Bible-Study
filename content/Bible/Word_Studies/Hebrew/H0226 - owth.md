# H0226 — אוֹת

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| אוֹת | ʼôwth | oth | a signal (literally or figuratively) |

## Definition

a signal (literally or figuratively), as aflag, beacon, monument, omen, prodigy, evidence, etc.

KJV renders: mark, miracle, (en-) sign, token.
