# H2757 — חָרִיץ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| חָרִיץ | chârîyts | khaw-reets' | properly |

## Definition

properly, incisure or (passively) incised; hence, a threshing-sledge (with sharp teeth); also a slice (as cut)

KJV renders: cheese, harrow.
