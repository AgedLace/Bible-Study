# H5445 — סָבַל

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| סָבַל | çâbal | saw-bal' | to carry (literally or figuratively) |

## Definition

to carry (literally or figuratively), or (reflexively) be burdensome; specifically, to be gravid

KJV renders: bear, be a burden, carry, strong to labour.
