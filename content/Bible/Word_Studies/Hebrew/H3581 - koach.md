# H3581 — כֹּחַ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| כֹּחַ | kôach | ko'-akh | vigor |

## Definition

vigor, literally (force, in a good or a bad sense) or figuratively (capacity, means, produce); also (from its hardiness) a large lizard

KJV renders: ability, able, chameleon, force, fruits, might, power(-ful), strength, substance, wealth.
