# H3238 — יָנָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| יָנָה | yânâh | yaw-naw' | to rage or be violent |

## Definition

to rage or be violent; by implication, to suppress, to maltreat

KJV renders: destroy, (thrust out by) oppress(-ing, -ion, -or), proud, vex, do violence.
