# H6186 — עָרַךְ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| עָרַךְ | ʻârak | aw-rak' | to set in a row |

## Definition

to set in a row, i.e. arrange, put in order (in a very wide variety of applications)

KJV renders: put (set) (the battle, self) in array, compare, direct, equal, esteem, estimate, expert (in war), furnish, handle, join (battle), ordain, (lay, put, reckon up, set) (in) order, prepare, tax, value.
