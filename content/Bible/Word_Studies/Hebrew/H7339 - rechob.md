# H7339 — רְחֹב

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| רְחֹב | rᵉchôb | rekh-obe' | a width |

## Definition

a width, i.e. (concretely) avenue or area

KJV renders: broad place (way), street. See also H1050 (בֵּית רְחוֹב).
