# H2314 — חָדַר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| חָדַר | châdar | khaw-dar' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to inclose (as a room), i.e. (by analogy,) to beset (as in a siege)

KJV renders: enter a privy chamber.
