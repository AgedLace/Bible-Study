# H4924 — מַשְׁמָן

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| מַשְׁמָן | mashmân | mash-mawn' | fat |

## Definition

fat, i.e. (literally and abstractly) fatness; but usually (figuratively and concretely) a rich dish, a fertile field, a robust man

KJV renders: fat (one, -ness, -test, -test place).
