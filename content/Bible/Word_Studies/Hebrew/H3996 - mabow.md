# H3996 — מָבוֹא

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| מָבוֹא | mâbôwʼ | maw-bo' | an entrance (the place or the act) |

## Definition

an entrance (the place or the act); specifically sunset or the west; also (adverb with preposition) towards

KJV renders: by which came, as cometh, in coming, as men enter into, entering, entrance into, entry, where goeth, going down, westward. Compare H4126 (מוֹבָא).
