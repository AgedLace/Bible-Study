# H4584 — מָעוֹן

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| מָעוֹן | Mâʻôwn | maw-ohn' | Maon |

## Definition

Maon, the name of an Israelite and of a place in Palestine

KJV renders: Maon, Maonites. Compare H1010 (בֵּית בַּעַל מְעוֹן), H4586 (מְעוּנִי)lemma מְעוּנָי third vowel, corrected to מְעוּנִי.
