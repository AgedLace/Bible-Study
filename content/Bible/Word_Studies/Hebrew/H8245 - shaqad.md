# H8245 — שָׁקַד

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| שָׁקַד | shâqad | shaw-kad' | to be alert |

## Definition

to be alert, i.e. sleepless; hence to be on the lookout (whether for good or ill)

KJV renders: hasten, remain, wake, watch (for).
