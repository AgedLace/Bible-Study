# H6979 — קוּר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| קוּר | qûwr | koor | to trench |

## Definition

to trench; by implication, to throw forth;  to wall up, whether literal (to build a wall) or figurative (to estop)

KJV renders: break down, cast out, destroy, dig.
