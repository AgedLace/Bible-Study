# H3885 — לוּן

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| לוּן | lûwn | loon | to stop (usually over night) |

## Definition

to stop (usually over night); by implication, to stay permanently; hence (in a bad sense) to be obstinate (especially in words, to complain)

KJV renders: abide (all night), continue, dwell, endure, grudge, be left, lie all night, (cause to) lodge (all night, in, -ing, this night), (make to) murmur, remain, tarry (all night, that night).
