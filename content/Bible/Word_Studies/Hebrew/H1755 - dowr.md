# H1755 — דּוֹר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| דּוֹר | dôwr | dore | properly |

## Definition

properly, a revolution of time, i.e. an age or generation; also a dwelling

KJV renders: age, evermore, generation, (n-) ever, posterity.
