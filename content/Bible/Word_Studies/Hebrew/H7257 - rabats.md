# H7257 — רָבַץ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| רָבַץ | râbats | raw-bats' | to crouch (on all four legs folded |

## Definition

to crouch (on all four legs folded, like a recumbent animal); be implication, to recline, repose, brood, lurk, imbed

KJV renders: crouch (down), fall down, make a fold, lay, (cause to, make to) lie (down), make to rest, sit.
