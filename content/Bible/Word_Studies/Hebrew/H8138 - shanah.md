# H8138 — שָׁנָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| שָׁנָה | shânâh | shaw-naw' | to fold |

## Definition

to fold, i.e. duplicate (literally or figuratively); by implication, to transmute (transitive or intransitive)

KJV renders: do (speak, strike) again, alter, double, (be given to) change, disguise, (be) diverse, pervert, prefer, repeat, return, do the second time.
