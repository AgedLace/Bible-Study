# H5077 — נָדָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| נָדָה | nâdâh | naw-daw' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to toss; figuratively, to exclude, i.e. banish, postpone, prohibit

KJV renders: cast out, drive, put far away.
