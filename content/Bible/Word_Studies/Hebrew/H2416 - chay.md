# H2416 — חַי

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| חַי | chay | khah'-ee | alive |

## Definition

alive; hence, raw (flesh); fresh (plant, water, year), strong; also (as noun, especially in the feminine singular and masculine plural) life (or living thing), whether literally or figuratively

KJV renders: age, alive, appetite, (wild) beast, company, congregation, life(-time), live(-ly), living (creature, thing), maintenance, merry, multitude, (be) old, quick, raw, running, springing, troop.
