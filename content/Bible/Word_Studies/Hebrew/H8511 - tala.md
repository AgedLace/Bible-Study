# H8511 — תָּלָא

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| תָּלָא | tâlâʼ | taw-law' | to suspend |

## Definition

to suspend; figuratively (through hesitation) to be uncertain; by implication (of mental dependence) to habituate

KJV renders: be bent, hang (in doubt).
