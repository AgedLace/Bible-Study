# H7971 — שָׁלַח

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| שָׁלַח | shâlach | shaw-lakh' | to send away |

## Definition

to send away, for, or out (in a great variety of applications)

KJV renders: any wise, appoint, bring (on the way), cast (away, out), conduct, earnestly, forsake, give (up), grow long, lay, leave, let depart (down, go, loose), push away, put (away, forth, in, out), reach forth, send (away, forth, out), set, shoot (forth, out), sow, spread, stretch forth (out).
