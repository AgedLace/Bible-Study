# H6275 — עָתַק

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| עָתַק | ʻâthaq | aw-thak' | to remove (intransitive or transitive) figuratively |

## Definition

to remove (intransitive or transitive) figuratively, to grow old; specifically, to transcribe

KJV renders: copy out, leave off, become (wax) old, remove.
