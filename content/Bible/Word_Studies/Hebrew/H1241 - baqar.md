# H1241 — בָּקָר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |

## Definition

beef cattle or an animal of the ox family of either gender (as used for plowing); collectively, a herd

KJV renders: beeve, bull ( -ock), calf, cow, great (cattle), heifer, herd, kine, ox.
