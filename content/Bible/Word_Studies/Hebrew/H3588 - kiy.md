# H3588 — כִּי

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| כִּי | kîy | kee | (by implication) very widely used as a relative conjunction or adverb (as below) |

## Definition

(by implication) very widely used as a relative conjunction or adverb (as below); often largely modified by other particles annexed

KJV renders: and, + (forasmuch, inasmuch, where-) as, assured(-ly), + but, certainly, doubtless, + else, even, + except, for, how, (because, in, so, than) that, + nevertheless, now, rightly, seeing, since, surely, then, therefore, + (al-) though, + till, truly, + until, when, whether, while, whom, yea, yet.
