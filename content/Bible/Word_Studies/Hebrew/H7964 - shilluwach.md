# H7964 — שִׁלּוּחַ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| שִׁלּוּחַ | shillûwach | shil-loo'-akh | (only in plural) a dismissal |

## Definition

(only in plural) a dismissal, i.e. (of a wife) divorce (especially the document); also (of a daughter) dower

KJV renders: presents, have sent back.
