# H1272 — בָּרַח

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| בָּרַח | bârach | baw-rakh' | to bolt |

## Definition

to bolt, i.e. figuratively, to flee suddenly

KJV renders: chase (away); drive away, fain, flee (away), put to flight, make haste, reach, run away, shoot.
