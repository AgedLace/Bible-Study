# H3001 — יָבֵשׁ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| יָבֵשׁ | yâbêsh | yaw-bashe' | to be ashamed |

## Definition

to be ashamed, confused or disappointed; also (as failing) to dry up (as water) or wither (as herbage)

KJV renders: be ashamed, clean, be confounded, (make) dry (up), (do) shame(-fully), utterly, wither (away).
