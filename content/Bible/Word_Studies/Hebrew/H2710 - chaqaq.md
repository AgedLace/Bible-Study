# H2710 — חָקַק

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| חָקַק | châqaq | khaw-kak' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to hack, i.e. engrave (Judges 5:14, to be a scribe simply); by implication, to enact (laws being cut in stone or metal tablets in primitive times) or (gen.) prescribe

KJV renders: appoint, decree, governor, grave, lawgiver, note, pourtray, print, set.
