# H4799 — מָרַח

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| מָרַח | mârach | maw-rakh' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to soften by rubbing or pressure; hence (medicinally) to apply as an emollient

KJV renders: lay for a plaister.
