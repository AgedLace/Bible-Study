# H4302 — מַטָּע

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| מַטָּע | maṭṭâʻ | mat-taw' | something planted |

## Definition

something planted, i.e. the place (a garden or vineyard), or the thing (a plant, figuratively or men); by implication, the act, planting

KJV renders: plant(-ation, -ing).
