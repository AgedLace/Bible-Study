# H6776 — צֶמֶד

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| צֶמֶד | tsemed | tseh'-med | hence |

## Definition

hence, an acre (i.e. day's task for a yoke of cattle to plough)

KJV renders: acre, couple, together, two (donkeys), yoke (of oxen).
