# H2266 — חָבַר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| חָבַר | châbar | khaw-bar' | to join (literally or figuratively) |

## Definition

to join (literally or figuratively); specifically (by means of spells) to fascinate

KJV renders: charm(-er), be compact, couple (together), have fellowship with, heap up, join (self, together), league.
