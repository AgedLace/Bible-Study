# H5172 — נָחַשׁ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| נָחַשׁ | nâchash | naw-khash' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to hiss, i.e. whisper a (magic) spell; generally, to prognosticate

KJV renders: certainly, divine, enchanter, (use) enchantment, learn by experience, indeed, diligently observe.
