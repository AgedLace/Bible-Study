# H3908 — לַחַשׁ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| לַחַשׁ | lachash | lakh'-ash | properly |

## Definition

properly, a whisper, i.e. by implication, (in a good sense) a private prayer, (in a bad one) an incantation; concretely, an amulet

KJV renders: charmed, earring, enchantment, orator, prayer.
