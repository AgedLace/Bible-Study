# H4800 — מֶרְחָב

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| מֶרְחָב | merchâb | mer-khawb' | enlargement |

## Definition

enlargement, either literally (an open space, usually in a good sense), or figuratively (liberty)

KJV renders: breadth, large place (room).
