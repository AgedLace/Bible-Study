# H1869 — דָּרַךְ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| דָּרַךְ | dârak | daw-rak' | to tread |

## Definition

to tread; by implication, to walk; also to string abow (by treading on it in bending)

KJV renders: archer, bend, come, draw, go (over), guide, lead (forth), thresh, tread (down), walk.
