# H2803 — חָשַׁב

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| חָשַׁב | châshab | khaw-shab' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to plait or interpenetrate, i.e. (literally) to weave or (generally) to fabricate; figuratively, to plot or contrive (usually in a malicious sense); hence (from the mental effort) to think, regard, value, compute

KJV renders: (make) account (of), conceive, consider, count, cunning (man, work, workman), devise, esteem, find out, forecast, hold, imagine, impute, invent, be like, mean, purpose, reckon(-ing be made), regard, think.
