# H3519 — כָּבוֹד

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| כָּבוֹד | kâbôwd | kaw-bode' | properly |

## Definition

properly, weight, but only figuratively in a good sense, splendor or copiousness

KJV renders: glorious(-ly), glory, honour(-able).
