# H2151 — זָלַל

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| זָלַל | zâlal | zaw-lal' | to shake (as in the wind) |

## Definition

to shake (as in the wind), i.e. to quake; figuratively, to be loose morally, worthless or prodigal

KJV renders: blow down, glutton, riotous (eater), vile.
