# H2686 — חָצַץ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| חָצַץ | châtsats | khaw-tsats' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to chop into, pierce or sever; hence, to curtail, to distribute (into ranks);  to shoot an arrow

KJV renders: archer, bands, cut off in the midst.
