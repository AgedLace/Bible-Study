# H3548 — כֹּהֵן

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |

## Definition

literally one officiating, a priest; also (by courtesy) an acting priest (although a layman)

KJV renders: chief ruler, own, priest, prince, principal officer.
