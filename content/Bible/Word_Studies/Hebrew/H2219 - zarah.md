# H2219 — זָרָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| זָרָה | zârâh | zaw-raw' | to toss about |

## Definition

KJV renders: cast away, compass, disperse, fan, scatter (away), spread, strew, winnow.
