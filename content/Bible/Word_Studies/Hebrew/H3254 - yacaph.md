# H3254 — יָסַף

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| יָסַף | yâçaph | yaw-saf' | to add or augment (often adverbial |

## Definition

to add or augment (often adverbial, to continue to do a thing)

KJV renders: add, again, any more, cease, come more, conceive again, continue, exceed, further, gather together, get more, give more-over, henceforth, increase (more and more), join, longer (bring, do, make, much, put), (the, much, yet) more (and more), proceed (further), prolong, put, be (strong-) er, yet, yield.
