# H8552 — תָּמַם

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| תָּמַם | tâmam | taw-mam' | to complete |

## Definition

to complete, in a good or a bad sense, literal, or figurative, transitive or intransitive

KJV renders: accomplish, cease, be clean (pass-) ed, consume, have done, (come to an, have an, make an) end, fail, come to the full, be all gone, be all here, be (make) perfect, be spent, sum, be (shew self) upright, be wasted, whole.
