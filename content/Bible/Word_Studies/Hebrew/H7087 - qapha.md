# H7087 — קָפָא

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| קָפָא | qâphâʼ | kaw-faw' | to shrink |

## Definition

to shrink, i.e. thicken (as unracked wine, curdled milk, clouded sky, frozen water)

KJV renders: congeal, curdle, dark, settle.
