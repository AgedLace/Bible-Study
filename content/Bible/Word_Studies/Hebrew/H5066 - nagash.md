# H5066 — נָגַשׁ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| נָגַשׁ | nâgash | naw-gash' | to be or come (causatively |

## Definition

to be or come (causatively, bring) near (for any purpose); euphemistically, to lie with a woman; as an enemy, to attack; religious to worship; causatively, to present; figuratively, to adduce an argument; by reversal, to stand back

KJV renders: (make to) approach (nigh), bring (forth, hither, near), (cause to) come (hither, near, nigh), give place, go hard (up), (be, draw, go) near (nigh), offer, overtake, present, put, stand.
