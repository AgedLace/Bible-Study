# H3320 — יָצַב

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| יָצַב | yâtsab | yaw-tsab' | to place (any thing so as to stay) |

## Definition

to place (any thing so as to stay); reflexively, to station, offer, continue

KJV renders: present selves, remaining, resort, set (selves), (be able to, can, with-) stand (fast, forth, -ing, still, up).
