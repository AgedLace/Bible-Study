# H7843 — שָׁחַת

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| שָׁחַת | shâchath | shaw-khath' | to decay |

## Definition

to decay, i.e. (causatively) ruin (literally or figuratively)

KJV renders: batter, cast off, corrupt(-er, thing), destroy(-er, -uction), lose, mar, perish, spill, spoiler, utterly, waste(-r).
