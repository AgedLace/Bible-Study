# H7921 — שָׁכֹל

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| שָׁכֹל | shâkôl | shaw-kole' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to miscarry, i.e. suffer abortion; by analogy, to bereave (literally or figuratively)

KJV renders: bereave (of children), barren, cast calf (fruit, young), be (make) childless, deprive, destroy, expect, lose children, miscarry, rob of children, spoil.
