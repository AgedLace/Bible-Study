# H4591 — מָעַט

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| מָעַט | mâʻaṭ | maw-at' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to pare off, i.e. lessen; intransitively, to be (or causatively, to make) small or few (or figuratively, ineffective)

KJV renders: suffer to decrease, diminish, (be, borrow a, give, make) few (in number, -ness), gather least (little), be (seem) little, ( give the) less, be minished, bring to nothing.
