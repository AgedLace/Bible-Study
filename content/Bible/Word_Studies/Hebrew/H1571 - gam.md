# H1571 — גַּם

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| גַּם | gam | gam | properly |

## Definition

properly, assemblage; used only adverbially also, even, yea, though; often repeated as correl. both...and

KJV renders: again, alike, also, (so much) as (soon), both (so)...and, but, either...or, even, for all, (in) likewise (manner), moreover, nay...neither, one, then(-refore), though, what, with, yea.
