# H3334 — יָצַר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| יָצַר | yâtsar | yaw-tsar' | to press (intransitive) |

## Definition

to press (intransitive), i.e. be narrow; figuratively, be in distress

KJV renders: be distressed, be narrow, be straitened (in straits), be vexed.
