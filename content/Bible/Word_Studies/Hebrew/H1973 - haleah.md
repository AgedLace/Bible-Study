# H1973 — הָלְאָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |

| הָלְאָה | hâlᵉʼâh | haw-leh-aw' | to the distance |

## Definition

to the distance, i.e. far away; also (of time) thus far

KJV renders: back, beyond, (hence,-) forward, hitherto, thence, forth, yonder.
