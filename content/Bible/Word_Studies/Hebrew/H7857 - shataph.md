# H7857 — שָׁטַף

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| שָׁטַף | shâṭaph | shaw-taf' | to gush |

## Definition

to gush; by implication, to inundate, cleanse; by analogy, to gallop, conquer

KJV renders: drown, (over-) flow(-whelm, rinse, run, rush, (throughly) wash (away).
