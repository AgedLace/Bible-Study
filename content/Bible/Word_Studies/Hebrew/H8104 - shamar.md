# H8104 — שָׁמַר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| שָׁמַר | shâmar | shaw-mar' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to hedge about (as with thorns), i.e. guard; generally, to protect, attend to, etc.

KJV renders: beward, be circumspect, take heed (to self), keep(-er, self), mark, look narrowly, observe, preserve, regard, reserve, save (self), sure, (that lay) wait (for), watch(-man).
