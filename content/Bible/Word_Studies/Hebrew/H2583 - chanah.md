# H2583 — חָנָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| חָנָה | chânâh | khaw-naw' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to incline; by implication, to decline (of the slanting rays of evening); specifically, to pitch atent; gen. to encamp (for abode or siege)

KJV renders: abide (in tents), camp, dwell, encamp, grow to an end, lie, pitch (tent), rest in tent.
