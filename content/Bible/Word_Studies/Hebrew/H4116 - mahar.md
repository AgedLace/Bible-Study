# H4116 — מָהַר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| מָהַר | mâhar | maw-har' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to be liquid or flow easily, i.e. (by implication); to hurry (in a good or a bad sense); often used (with another verb) adverbially, promptly

KJV renders: be carried headlong, fearful, (cause to make, in, make) haste(-n, -ily), (be) hasty, (fetch, make ready) quickly, rash, shortly, (be so) soon, make speed, speedily, straightway, suddenly, swift.
