# H6119 — עָקֵב

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| עָקֵב | ʻâqêb | aw-kabe' | a heel (as protuberant) |

## Definition

a heel (as protuberant); hence, a track; figuratively, the rear (of an army)

KJV renders: heel, (horse-) hoof, last, lier in wait (by mistake for H6120 (עָקֵב)), (foot-) step.
