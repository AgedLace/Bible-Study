# H1239 — בָּקַר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| בָּקַר | bâqar | baw-kar | properly |

## Definition

properly, to plough, or (generally) break forth, i.e. (figuratively) to inspect, admire, care for, consider

KJV renders: (make) inquire (-ry), (make) search, seek out.
