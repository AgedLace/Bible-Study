# H7167 — קָרַע

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| קָרַע | qâraʻ | kaw-rah' | to rend |

## Definition

to rend, literally or figuratively (revile, paint the eyes, as if enlarging them)

KJV renders: cut out, rend, surely, tear.
