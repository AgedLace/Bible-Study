# H7911 — שָׁכַח

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| שָׁכַח | shâkach | shaw-kakh' | to mislay |

## Definition

to mislay, i.e. to be oblivious of, from want of memory or attention

KJV renders: at all, (cause to) forget.
