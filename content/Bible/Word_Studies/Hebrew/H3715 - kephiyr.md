# H3715 — כְּפִיר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| כְּפִיר | kᵉphîyr | kef-eer' | a village (as covered in by walls) |

## Definition

a village (as covered in by walls); also a young lion (perhaps as covered with a mane)

KJV renders: (young) lion, village. Compare H3723 (כָּפָר).
