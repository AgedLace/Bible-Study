# H2764 — חֵרֶם

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| חֵרֶם | chêrem | khay'-rem | physical (as shutting in) a net (either literally or figuratively) |

## Definition

physical (as shutting in) a net (either literally or figuratively); usually a doomed object; abstractly extermination

KJV renders: (ac-) curse(-d, -d thing), dedicated thing, things which should have been utterly destroyed, (appointed to) utter destruction, devoted (thing), net.
