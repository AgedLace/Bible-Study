# H5930 — עֹלָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| עֹלָה | ʻôlâh | o-law' | a step or (collectively |

## Definition

a step or (collectively, stairs, as ascending); usually a holocaust (as going up in smoke)

KJV renders: ascent, burnt offering (sacrifice), go up to. See also H5766 (עֶוֶל).
