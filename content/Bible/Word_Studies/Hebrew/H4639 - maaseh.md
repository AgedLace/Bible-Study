# H4639 — מַעֲשֶׂה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| מַעֲשֶׂה | maʻăseh | mah-as-eh' | an action (good or bad) |

## Definition

an action (good or bad); generally, a transaction; abstractly, activity; by implication, a product (specifically, a poem) or (generally) property

KJV renders: act, art, bakemeat, business, deed, do(-ing), labor, thing made, ware of making, occupation, thing offered, operation, possession, well, (handy-, needle-, net-) work(ing, -manship), wrought.
