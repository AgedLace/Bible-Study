# H5549 — סָלַל

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| סָלַל | çâlal | saw-lal' | to mound up (especially a turnpike) |

## Definition

to mound up (especially a turnpike); figurative, to exalt; reflexively, to oppose (as by a dam)

KJV renders: cast up, exalt (self), extol, make plain, raise up.
