# H1350 — גָּאַל

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| גָּאַל | gâʼal | gaw-al' | to be the next of kin (and as such to buy back a relative's property |

## Definition

to be the next of kin (and as such to buy back a relative's property, marry his widow, etc.)

KJV renders: in any wise, at all, avenger, deliver, (do, perform the part of near, next) kinsfolk(-man), purchase, ransom, redeem(-er), revenger.
