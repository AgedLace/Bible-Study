# H5186 — נָטָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| נָטָה | nâṭâh | naw-taw' | to stretch or spread out |

## Definition

to stretch or spread out; by implication, to bend away (including moral deflection); used in a great variety of application

KJV renders: afternoon, apply, bow (down, -ing), carry aside, decline, deliver, extend, go down, be gone, incline, intend, lay, let down, offer, outstretched, overthrown, pervert, pitch, prolong, put away, shew, spread (out), stretch (forth, out), take (aside), turn (aside, away), wrest, cause to yield.
