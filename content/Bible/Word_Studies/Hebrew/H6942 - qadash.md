# H6942 — קָדַשׁ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| קָדַשׁ | qâdash | kaw-dash' | to be (causatively |

## Definition

to be (causatively, make, pronounce or observe as) clean (ceremonially or morally)

KJV renders: appoint, bid, consecrate, dedicate, defile, hallow, (be, keep) holy(-er, place), keep, prepare, proclaim, purify, sanctify(-ied one, self), wholly.
