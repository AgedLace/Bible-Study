# H1310 — בָּשַׁל

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| בָּשַׁל | bâshal | baw-shal' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to boil up; hence, to be done in cooking; figuratively to ripen

KJV renders: bake, boil, bring forth, roast, seethe, sod (be sodden).
