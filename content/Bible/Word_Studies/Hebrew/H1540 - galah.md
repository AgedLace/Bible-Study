# H1540 — גָּלָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| גָּלָה | gâlâh | gaw-law' | to denude (especially in a disgraceful sense) |

## Definition

to denude (especially in a disgraceful sense); by implication, to exile (captives being usually stripped); figuratively, to reveal

KJV renders: advertise, appear, bewray, bring, (carry, lead, go) captive (into captivity), depart, disclose, discover, exile, be gone, open, plainly, publish, remove, reveal, shamelessly, shew, surely, tell, uncover.
