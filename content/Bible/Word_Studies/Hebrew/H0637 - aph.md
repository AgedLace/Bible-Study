# H0637 — אַף

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| אַף | ʼaph | af | meaning accession (used as an adverb or conjunction) |

## Definition

meaning accession (used as an adverb or conjunction); also or yea; adversatively though

KJV renders: also, although, and (furthermore, yet), but, even, how much less (more, rather than), moreover, with, yea.
