# H6862 — צַר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| צַר | tsar | tsar | narrow |

## Definition

narrow; (as a noun) a tight place (usually figuratively, i.e. trouble); also a pebble ; (transitive) an opponent (as crowding)

KJV renders: adversary, afflicted(-tion), anguish, close, distress, enemy, flint, foe, narrow, small, sorrow, strait, tribulation, trouble.
