# H7219 — רֹאשׁ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| רֹאשׁ | rôʼsh | roshe | a poisonous plant |

## Definition

a poisonous plant, probably the poppy (from its conspicuous head); generally poison (even of serpents)

KJV renders: gall, hemlock, poison, venom.
