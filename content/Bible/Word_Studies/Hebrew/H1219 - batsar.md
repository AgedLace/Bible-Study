# H1219 — בָּצַר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| בָּצַר | bâtsar | baw-tsar' | to gather grapes |

## Definition

to gather grapes; also to be isolated (i.e. inaccessible by height or fortification)

KJV renders: cut off, (de-) fenced, fortify, (grape) gather(-er), mighty things, restrain, strong, wall (up), withhold.
