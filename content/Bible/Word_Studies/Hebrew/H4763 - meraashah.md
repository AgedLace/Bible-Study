# H4763 — מְרַאֲשָׁה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| מְרַאֲשָׁה | mᵉraʼăshâh | mer-ah-ash-aw' | properly |

## Definition

properly, a headpiece, i.e. (plural for adverbial) at (or as) the head-rest (or pillow)

KJV renders: bolster, head, pillow. Compare H4772 (מַרְגְלָה).
