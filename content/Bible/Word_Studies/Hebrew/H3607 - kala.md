# H3607 — כָּלָא

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| כָּלָא | kâlâʼ | kaw-law' | to restrict |

## Definition

to restrict, by act (hold back or in) or word (prohibit)

KJV renders: finish, forbid, keep (back), refrain, restrain, retain, shut up, be stayed, withhold.
