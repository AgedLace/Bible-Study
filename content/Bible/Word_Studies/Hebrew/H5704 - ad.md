# H5704 — עַד

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| עַד | ʻad | ad | as far (or long |

## Definition

as far (or long, or much) as, whether of space (even unto) or time (during, while, until) or degree (equally with)

KJV renders: against, and, as, at, before, by (that), even (to), for(-asmuch as), (hither-) to, how long, into, as long (much) as, (so) that, till, toward, until, when, while, ( as) yet.
