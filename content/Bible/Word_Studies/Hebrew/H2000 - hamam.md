# H2000 — הָמַם

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| הָמַם | hâmam | haw-mam' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to put in commotion; by implication, to disturb, drive, destroy

KJV renders: break, consume, crush, destroy, discomfit, trouble, vex.
