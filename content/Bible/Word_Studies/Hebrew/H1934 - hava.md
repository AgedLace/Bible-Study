# H1934 — הָוָא

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| הָוָא | hâvâʼ | hav-aw' | to exist |

## Definition

to exist; used in a great variety of applications (especially in connection with other words)

KJV renders: be, become, behold, came (to pass), cease, cleave, consider, do, give, have, judge, keep, labour, mingle (self), put, see, seek, set, slay, take heed, tremble, walk, would.
