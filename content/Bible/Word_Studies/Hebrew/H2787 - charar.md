# H2787 — חָרַר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| חָרַר | chârar | khaw-rar' | to glow |

## Definition

to glow, i.e. literally (to melt, burn, dry up) or figuratively (to show or incite passion)

KJV renders: be angry, burn, dry, kindle.
