# H8327 — שָׁרַשׁ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| שָׁרַשׁ | shârash | shaw-rash' | to root |

## Definition

to root, i.e. strike into the soil, or (by implication) to pluck from it

KJV renders: (take, cause to take) root (out).
