# H7561 — רָשַׁע

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |

## Definition

to be (causatively, do or declare) wrong; by implication, to disturb, violate

KJV renders: condemn, make trouble, vex, be (commit, deal, depart, do) wicked(-ly, -ness).
