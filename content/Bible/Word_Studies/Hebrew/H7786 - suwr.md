| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |

| שׂוּר | sûwr | soor | properly |

## Definition

properly, to vanquish; by implication, to rule (causatively, crown)

KJV renders: make princes, have power, reign. See H5493 (סוּר).
