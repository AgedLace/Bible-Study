# H3245 — יָסַד

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| יָסַד | yâçad | yaw-sad' | to set (literally or figuratively) |

## Definition

to set (literally or figuratively); intensively, to found; reflexively, to sit down together, i.e. settle, consult

KJV renders: appoint, take counsel, establish, (lay the, lay for a) found(-ation), instruct, lay, ordain, set, sure.
