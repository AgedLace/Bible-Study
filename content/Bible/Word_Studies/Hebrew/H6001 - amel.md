# H6001 — עָמֵל

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| עָמֵל | ʻâmêl | aw-male' | toiling |

## Definition

toiling; concretely, a laborer; figuratively, sorrowful

KJV renders: that laboureth, that is a misery, had taken (labour), wicked, workman.
