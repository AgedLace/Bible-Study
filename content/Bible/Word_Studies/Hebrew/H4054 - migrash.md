# H4054 — מִגְרָשׁ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| מִגְרָשׁ | migrâsh | mig-rawsh' | a suburb (i.e. open country whither flocks are driven from pasture) |

## Definition

a suburb (i.e. open country whither flocks are driven from pasture); hence, the area around abuilding, or the margin of the sea

KJV renders: cast out, suburb.
