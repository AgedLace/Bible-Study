# H3201 — יָכֹל

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| יָכֹל | yâkôl | yaw-kole' | to be able |

## Definition

to be able, literally (can, could) or morally (may, might)

KJV renders: be able, any at all (ways), attain, can (away with, (-not)), could, endure, might, overcome, have power, prevail, still, suffer.
