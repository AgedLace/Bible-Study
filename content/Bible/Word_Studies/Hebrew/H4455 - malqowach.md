# H4455 — מַלְקוֹחַ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| מַלְקוֹחַ | malqôwach | mal-ko'-akh | transitively (in dual) the jaws (as taking food) |

## Definition

transitively (in dual) the jaws (as taking food); intransitively, spoil (and captives) (as taken)

KJV renders: booty, jaws, prey.
