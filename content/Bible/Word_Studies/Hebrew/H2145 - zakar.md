# H2145 — זָכָר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| זָכָר | zâkâr | zaw-kawr' | properly |

## Definition

properly, remembered, i.e. a male (of man or animals, as being the most noteworthy sex)

KJV renders: him, male, man(child, -kind).
