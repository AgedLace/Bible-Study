# H6903 — קְבֵל

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| קְבֵל | qᵉbêl | keb-ale' | (adverbially) in front of |

## Definition

(adverbially) in front of; usually (with other particles) on account of, so as, since, hence

KJV renders: according to, as, because, before, for this cause, forasmuch as, by this means, over against, by reason of, that, therefore, though, wherefore.
