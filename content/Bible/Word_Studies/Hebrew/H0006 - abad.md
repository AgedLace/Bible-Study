# H0006 — אָבַד

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| אָבַד | ʼâbad | aw-bad' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to wander away, i.e. lose oneself; by implication to perish (causative, destroy)

KJV renders: break, destroy(-uction), not escape, fail, lose, (cause to, make) perish, spend, and surely, take, be undone, utterly, be void of, have no way to flee.
