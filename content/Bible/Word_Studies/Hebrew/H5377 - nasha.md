# H5377 — נָשָׁא

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| נָשָׁא | nâshâʼ | naw-shaw' | to lead astray |

## Definition

to lead astray, i.e. (mentally) to delude, or (morally) to seduce

KJV renders: beguile, deceive, greatly, utterly.
