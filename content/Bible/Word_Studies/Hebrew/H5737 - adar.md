# H5737 — עֲדַר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| עֲדַר | ʻădar | aw-dar' | to arrange as a battle |

## Definition

to arrange as a battle, a vineyard (to hoe); hence, to muster and so to miss (or find wanting)

KJV renders: dig, fail, keep (rank), lack.
