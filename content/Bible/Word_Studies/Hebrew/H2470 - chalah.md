# H2470 — חָלָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| חָלָה | châlâh | khaw-law' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to be rubbed or worn; hence (figuratively) to be weak, sick, afflicted; or (causatively) to grieve, make sick; also to stroke (in flattering), entreat

KJV renders: beseech, (be) diseased, (put to) grief, be grieved, (be) grievous, infirmity, intreat, lay to, put to pain, pray, make prayer, be (fall, make) sick, sore, be sorry, make suit ( supplication), woman in travail, be (become) weak, be wounded.
