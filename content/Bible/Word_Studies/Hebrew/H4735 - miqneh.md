# H4735 — מִקְנֶה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| מִקְנֶה | miqneh | mik-neh' | something bought |

## Definition

something bought, i.e. property, but only livestock; abstractly, acquisition

KJV renders: cattle, flock, herd, possession, purchase, substance.
