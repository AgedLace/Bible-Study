# H7650 — שָׁבַע

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| שָׁבַע | shâbaʻ | shaw-bah' | to seven oneself |

## Definition

to seven oneself, i.e. swear (as if by repeating a declaration seven times)

KJV renders: adjure, charge (by an oath, with an oath), feed to the full (by mistake for H7646 (שָׂבַע)), take an oath, straitly, (cause to, make to) swear.
