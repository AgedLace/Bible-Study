# H7292 — רָהַב

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| רָהַב | râhab | raw-hab' | to urge severely |

## Definition

to urge severely, i.e. (figuratively) importune, embolden, capture, act insolently

KJV renders: overcome, behave self proudly, make sure, strengthen.
