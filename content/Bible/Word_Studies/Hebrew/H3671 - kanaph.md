# H3671 — כָּנָף

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| כָּנָף | kânâph | kaw-nawf' | an edge or extremity |

## Definition

an edge or extremity; specifically (of a bird or army) a wing, (of a garment or bedclothing) a flap, (of the earth) a quarter, (of a building) a pinnacle

KJV renders: bird, border, corner, end, feather(-ed), flying, (one an-) other, overspreading, quarters, skirt, sort, uttermost part, wing(-ed).
