# H5115 — נָוָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| נָוָה | nâvâh | naw-vaw' | to rest (as at home) |

## Definition

to rest (as at home); causatively (through the implied idea of beauty ), to celebrate (with praises)

KJV renders: keept at home, prepare an habitation.
