# H4339 — מֵישָׁר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| מֵישָׁר | mêyshâr | may-shawr' | evenness |

## Definition

evenness, i.e. (figuratively) prosperity or concord; also straightness, i.e. (figuratively) rectitude (only in plural with singular sense; often adverbially)

KJV renders: agreement, aright, that are equal, equity, (things that are) right(-eously, things), sweetly, upright(-ly, -ness).
