# H2308 — חָדַל

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| חָדַל | châdal | khaw-dal' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to be flabby, i.e. (by implication) desist; (figuratively) be lacking or idle

KJV renders: cease, end, fall, forbear, forsake, leave (off), let alone, rest, be unoccupied, want.
