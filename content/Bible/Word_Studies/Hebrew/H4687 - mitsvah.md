# H4687 — מִצְוָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| מִצְוָה | mitsvâh | mits-vaw' | a command |

## Definition

a command, whether human or divine (collectively, the Law)

KJV renders: (which was) commanded(-ment), law, ordinance, precept.
