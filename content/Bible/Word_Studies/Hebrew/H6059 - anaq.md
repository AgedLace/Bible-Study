# H6059 — עָנַק

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| עָנַק | ʻânaq | aw-nak' | to collar |

## Definition

to collar, i.e. adorn with anecklace; figuratively, to fit out with supplies

KJV renders: compass about as a chain, furnish, liberally.
