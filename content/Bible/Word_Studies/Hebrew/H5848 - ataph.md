# H5848 — עָטַף

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| עָטַף | ʻâṭaph | aw-taf' | to shroud |

## Definition

to shroud, i.e. clothe (whether transitive or reflex.); hence (from the idea of darkness) to languish

KJV renders: cover (over), fail, faint, feebler, hide self, be overwhelmed, swoon.
