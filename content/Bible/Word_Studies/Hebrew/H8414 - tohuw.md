# H8414 — תֹּהוּ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| תֹּהוּ | tôhûw | to'-hoo | a desolation (of surface) |

## Definition

a desolation (of surface), i.e. desert; figuratively, a worthless thing; adverbially, in vain

KJV renders: confusion, empty place, without form, nothing, (thing of) nought, vain, vanity, waste, wilderness.
