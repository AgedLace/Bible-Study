# H5606 — סָפַק

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| סָפַק | çâphaq | saw-fak' | to clap the hands (in token of compact |

## Definition

to clap the hands (in token of compact, derision, grief, indignation, or punishment); by implication of satisfaction, to be enough; by implication of excess, to vomit

KJV renders: clap, smite, strike, suffice, wallow.
