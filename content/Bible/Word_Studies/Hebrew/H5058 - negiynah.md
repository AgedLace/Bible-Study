# H5058 — נְגִינָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| נְגִינָה | nᵉgîynâh | neg-ee-naw' | properly |

## Definition

properly, instrumental music; by implication, a stringed instrument; by extension, a poem set to music; specifically, an epigram

KJV renders: stringed instrument, musick, Neginoth (plural), song.
