# H4490 — מָנָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| מָנָה | mânâh | maw-naw' | properly |

## Definition

properly, something weighed out, i.e. (generally) a division; specifically (of food) a ration; also a lot

KJV renders: such things as belonged, part, portion.
