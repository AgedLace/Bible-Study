# H5975 — עָמַד

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| עָמַד | ʻâmad | aw-mad' | to stand |

## Definition

to stand, in various relations (literal and figurative, intransitive and transitive)

KJV renders: abide (behind), appoint, arise, cease, confirm, continue, dwell, be employed, endure, establish, leave, make, ordain, be (over), place, (be) present (self), raise up, remain, repair, serve, set (forth, over, -tle, up), (make to, make to be at a, with-) stand (by, fast, firm, still, up), (be at a) stay (up), tarry.
