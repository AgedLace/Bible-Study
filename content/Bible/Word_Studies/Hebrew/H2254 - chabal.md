# H2254 — חָבַל

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| חָבַל | châbal | khaw-bal' | to wind tightly (as a rope) |

## Definition

to wind tightly (as a rope), i.e. to bind; specifically, by a pledge; figuratively, to pervert, destroy; also to writhe in pain (especially of parturition)

KJV renders: at all, band, bring forth, (deal) corrupt(-ly), destroy, offend, lay to (take a) pledge, spoil, travail, very, withhold.
