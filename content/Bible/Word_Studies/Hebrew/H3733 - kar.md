# H3733 — כַּר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| כַּר | kar | kar | a ram (as full-grown and fat) |

## Definition

a ram (as full-grown and fat), including a battering-ram (as butting); hence, a meadow (as for sheep); also a pad or camel's saddle (as puffed out)

KJV renders: captain, furniture, lamb, (large) pasture, ram. See also H1033 (בֵּית כַּר), H3746 (כָּרִי).
