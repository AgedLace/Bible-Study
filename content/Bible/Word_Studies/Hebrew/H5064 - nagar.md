# H5064 — נָגַר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| נָגַר | nâgar | naw-gar' | to flow |

## Definition

to flow; figuratively, to stretch out; causatively, to pour out or down; figuratively, to deliver over

KJV renders: fall, flow away, pour down (out), run, shed, spilt, trickle down.
