# H7641 — שִׁבֹּל

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| שִׁבֹּל | shibbôl | shib-bole | a stream (as flowing) |

## Definition

a stream (as flowing); also an ear of grain (as growing out); by analogy, a branch

KJV renders: branch, channel, ear (of corn), (water-)flood, Shibboleth. Compare H5451 (סִבֹּלֶת).
