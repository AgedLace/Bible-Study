# H3092 — יְהוֹשָׁפָט

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| יְהוֹשָׁפָט | Yᵉhôwshâphâṭ | yeh-ho-shaw-fawt' | Jehoshaphat |

## Definition

Jehoshaphat, the name of six Israelites; also of a valley near Jerusalem

KJV renders: Jehoshaphat. Compare H3146 (יוֹשָׁפָט).
