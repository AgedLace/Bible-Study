# H3499 — יֶתֶר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| יֶתֶר | yether | yeh'-ther | properly |

## Definition

properly, an overhanging, i.e. (by implication) an excess, superiority, remainder; also a small rope (as hanging free)

KJV renders: abundant, cord, exceeding, excellancy(-ent), what they leave, that hath left, plentifully, remnant, residue, rest, string, with.
