# H8257 — שָׁקַע

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| שָׁקַע | shâqaʻ | shaw-kah' | to subside |

## Definition

to subside; by implication, to be overflowed, cease; causatively, to abate, subdue

KJV renders: make deep, let down, drown, quench, sink.
