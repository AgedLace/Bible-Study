# H3474 — יָשַׁר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| יָשַׁר | yâshar | yaw-shar' | to be straight or even |

## Definition

to be straight or even; figuratively, to be (causatively, to make) right, pleasant, prosperous

KJV renders: direct, fit, seem good (meet), please (will), be (esteem, go) right (on), bring (look, make, take the) straight (way), be upright(-ly).
