# H7965 — שָׁלוֹם

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| שָׁלוֹם | shâlôwm | shaw-lome' | safe |

## Definition

safe, i.e. (figuratively) well, happy, friendly; also (abstractly) welfare, i.e. health, prosperity, peace

KJV renders: do, familiar, fare, favour, friend, great, (good) health, ( perfect, such as be at) peace(-able, -ably), prosper(-ity, -ous), rest, safe(-ty), salute, welfare, ( all is, be) well, wholly.
