# H5257 — נְסִיךְ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| נְסִיךְ | nᵉçîyk | nes-eek' | properly |

## Definition

properly, something poured out, i.e. a libation; also a molten image; by implication, a prince (as anointed)

KJV renders: drink offering, duke, prince(-ipal).
