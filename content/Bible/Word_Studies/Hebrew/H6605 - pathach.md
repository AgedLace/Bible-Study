# H6605 — פָּתַח

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| פָּתַח | pâthach | paw-thakh' | to open wide (literally or figuratively) |

## Definition

to open wide (literally or figuratively); specifically, to loosen, begin, plough, carve

KJV renders: appear, break forth, draw (out), let go free, (en-) grave(-n), loose (self), (be, be set) open(-ing), put off, ungird, unstop, have vent.
