# H2904 — טוּל

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |

| טוּל | ṭûwl | tool | to pitch over or reel |

## Definition

to pitch over or reel; hence (transitively) to cast down or out

KJV renders: carry away, (utterly) cast (down, forth, out), send out.
