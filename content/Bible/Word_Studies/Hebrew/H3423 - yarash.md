# H3423 — יָרַשׁ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| יָרַשׁ | yârash | yaw-rash' | to occupy (by driving out previous tenants |

## Definition

to occupy (by driving out previous tenants, and possessing in their place); by implication, to seize, to rob, to inherit; also to expel, to impoverish, to ruin

KJV renders: cast out, consume, destroy, disinherit, dispossess, drive(-ing) out, enjoy, expel, without fail, (give to, leave for) inherit(-ance, -or) magistrate, be (make) poor, come to poverty, (give to, make to) possess, get (have) in (take) possession, seize upon, succeed, utterly.
