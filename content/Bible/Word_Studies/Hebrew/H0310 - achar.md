# H0310 — אַחַר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| אַחַר | ʼachar | akh-ar' | properly |

## Definition

properly, the hind part; generally used as an adverb or conjunction, after (in various senses)

KJV renders: after (that, -ward), again, at, away from, back (from, -side), behind, beside, by, follow (after, -ing), forasmuch, from, hereafter, hinder end, out (over) live, persecute, posterity, pursuing, remnant, seeing, since, thence(-forth), when, with.
