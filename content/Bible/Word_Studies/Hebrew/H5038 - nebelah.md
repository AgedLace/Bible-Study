# H5038 — נְבֵלָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| נְבֵלָה | nᵉbêlâh | neb-ay-law' | a flabby thing |

## Definition

a flabby thing, i.e. a carcase or carrion (human or bestial, often collectively); figuratively, an idol

KJV renders: (dead) body, (dead) carcase, dead of itself, which died, (beast) that (which) dieth of itself.
