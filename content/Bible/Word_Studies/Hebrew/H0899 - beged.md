# H0899 — בֶּגֶד

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| בֶּגֶד | beged | behg'-ed | a covering |

## Definition

a covering, i.e. clothing; also treachery or pillage

KJV renders: apparel, cloth(-es, ing), garment, lap, rag, raiment, robe, very (treacherously), vesture, wardrobe.
