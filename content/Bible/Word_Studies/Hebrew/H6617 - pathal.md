# H6617 — פָּתַל

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| פָּתַל | pâthal | paw-thal' | to twine |

## Definition

to twine, i.e. (literally) to struggle or (figuratively) be (morally) tortuous

KJV renders: (shew self) froward, shew self unsavoury, wrestle.
