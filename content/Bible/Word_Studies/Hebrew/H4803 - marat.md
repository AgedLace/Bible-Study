# H4803 — מָרַט

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| מָרַט | mâraṭ | maw-rat' | to polish |

## Definition

to polish; by implication, to make bald (the head), to gall (the shoulder); also, to sharpen

KJV renders: bright, furbish, (have his) hair (be) fallen off, peeled, pluck off (hair).
