# H4585 — מְעוֹנָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |

## Definition

{an abode, of God (the Tabernacle or the Temple), men (their home) or animals (their lair); hence, a retreat (asylum)}

KJV renders: den, habitation, (dwelling) place, refuge.
