# H1870 — דֶּרֶךְ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| דֶּרֶךְ | derek | deh'-rek | a road (as trodden) |

## Definition

a road (as trodden); figuratively, a course of life or mode of action, often adverb

KJV renders: along, away, because of, by, conversation, custom, (east-) ward, journey, manner, passenger, through, toward, (high-) (path-) way(-side), whither(-soever).
