# H5181 — נָחַת

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| נָחַת | nâchath | naw-khath' | to sink |

## Definition

to sink, i.e. descend; causatively, to press or lead down

KJV renders: be broken, (cause to) come down, enter, go down, press sore, settle, stick fast.
