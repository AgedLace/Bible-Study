# H1104 — בָּלַע

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| בָּלַע | bâlaʻ | baw-lah' | to make away with (specifically by swallowing) |

## Definition

to make away with (specifically by swallowing); generally, to destroy

KJV renders: cover, destroy, devour, eat up, be at end, spend up, swallow down (up).
