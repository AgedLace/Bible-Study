# H5091 — נָהָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| נָהָה | nâhâh | naw-haw' | to groan |

## Definition

to groan, i.e. bewail; hence (through the idea of crying aloud), to assemble (as if on proclamation)

KJV renders: lament, wail.
