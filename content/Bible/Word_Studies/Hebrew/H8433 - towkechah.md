# H8433 — תּוֹכֵחָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |

| תּוֹכֵחָה | tôwkêchâh | to-kay-khaw' | chastisement |

## Definition

chastisement; figuratively (by words) correction, refutation, proof (even in defence)

KJV renders: argument, chastened, correction, reasoning, rebuke, reproof, be (often) reproved.
