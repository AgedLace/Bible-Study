# H6504 — פָּרַד

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| פָּרַד | pârad | paw-rad' | to break through |

## Definition

to break through, i.e. spread or separate (oneself)

KJV renders: disperse, divide, be out of joint, part, scatter (abroad), separate (self), sever self, stretch, sunder.
