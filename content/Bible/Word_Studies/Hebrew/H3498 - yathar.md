# H3498 — יָתַר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| יָתַר | yâthar | yaw-thar' | to jut over or exceed |

## Definition

to jut over or exceed; by implication, to excel; (intransitively) to remain or be left; causatively, to leave, cause to abound, preserve

KJV renders: excel, leave (a remnant), left behind, too much, make plenteous, preserve, (be, let) remain(-der, -ing, -nant), reserve, residue, rest.
