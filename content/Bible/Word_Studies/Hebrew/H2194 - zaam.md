# H2194 — זָעַם

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| זָעַם | zâʻam | zaw-am' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to foam at the mouth, i.e. to be enraged

KJV renders: abhor, abominable, (be) angry, defy, (have) indignation.
