# H6485 — פָּקַד

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| פָּקַד | pâqad | paw-kad' | to visit (with friendly or hostile intent) |

## Definition

to visit (with friendly or hostile intent); by analogy, to oversee, muster, charge, care for, miss, deposit, etc.

KJV renders: appoint, at all, avenge, bestow, (appoint to have the, give a) charge, commit, count, deliver to keep, be empty, enjoin, go see, hurt, do judgment, lack, lay up, look, make, by any means, miss, number, officer, (make) overseer, have (the) oversight, punish, reckon, (call to) remember(-brance), set (over), sum, surely, visit, want.
