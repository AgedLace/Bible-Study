# H5128 — נוּעַ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| נוּעַ | nûwaʻ | noo'-ah | to waver |

## Definition

to waver, in a great variety of applications, literally and figuratively (as subjoined)

KJV renders: continually, fugitive, make, to (go) up and down, be gone away, (be) move(-able, -d), be promoted, reel, remove, scatter, set, shake, sift, stagger, to and fro, be vagabond, wag, (make) wander (up and down).
