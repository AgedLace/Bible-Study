# H1696 — דָבַר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| דָבַר | dâbar | daw-bar' | perhaps properly |

## Definition

perhaps properly, to arrange; but used figuratively (of words), to speak; rarely (in a destructive sense) to subdue

KJV renders: answer, appoint, bid, command, commune, declare, destroy, give, name, promise, pronounce, rehearse, say, speak, be spokesman, subdue, talk, teach, tell, think, use (entreaties), utter, well, work.
