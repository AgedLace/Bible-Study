# H1157 — בְּעַד

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| בְּעַד | bᵉʻad | beh-ad' | in up to or over against |

## Definition

in up to or over against; generally at, beside, among, behind, for, etc.

KJV renders: about, at by (means of), for, over, through, up (-on), within.
