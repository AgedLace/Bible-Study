# H5590 — סָעַר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| סָעַר | çâʻar | saw-ar' | to rush upon |

## Definition

to rush upon; by implication, to toss (transitive or intransitive, literal or figurative)

KJV renders: be (toss with) tempest(-uous), be sore, troubled, come out as a (drive with the, scatter with a) whirlwind.
