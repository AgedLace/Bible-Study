# H0123 — אֱדֹם

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| אֱדֹם | ʼĔdôm | ed-ome' | Edom |

## Definition

Edom, the elder twin-brother of Jacob; hence the region (Idumaea) occupied by him

KJV renders: Edom, Edomites, Idumea.
