# H3632 — כָּלִיל

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| כָּלִיל | kâlîyl | kaw-leel' | complete |

## Definition

complete; as noun, the whole (specifically, a sacrifice entirely consumed); as adverb, fully

KJV renders: all, every whit, flame, perfect(-ion), utterly, whole burnt offering (sacrifice), wholly.
