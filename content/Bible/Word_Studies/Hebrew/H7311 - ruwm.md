# H7311 — רוּם

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| רוּם | rûwm | room | to be high actively |

## Definition

to be high actively, to rise or raise (in various applications, literally or figuratively)

KJV renders: bring up, exalt (self), extol, give, go up, haughty, heave (up), (be, lift up on, make on, set up on, too) high(-er, one), hold up, levy, lift(-er) up, (be) lofty, ( a-) loud, mount up, offer (up), presumptuously, (be) promote(-ion), proud, set up, tall(-er), take (away, off, up), breed worms.
