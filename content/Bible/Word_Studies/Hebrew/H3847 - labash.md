# H3847 — לָבַשׁ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| לָבַשׁ | lâbash | law-bash' | properly |

## Definition

properly, wrap around, i.e. (by implication) to put on agarment or clothe (oneself, or another), literally or figuratively

KJV renders: (in) apparel, arm, array (self), clothe (self), come upon, put (on, upon), wear.
