# H6238 — עָשַׁר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| עָשַׁר | ʻâshar | aw-shar' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to accumulate; chiefly (specifically) to grow (causatively, make) rich

KJV renders: be(-come, en-, make, make self, wax) rich, make (1 Kings 22:48 marg). See H6240 (עָשָׂר).
