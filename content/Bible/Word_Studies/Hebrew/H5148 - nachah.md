# H5148 — נָחָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| נָחָה | nâchâh | naw-khaw' | to guide |

## Definition

to guide; by implication, to transport (into exile, or as colonists)

KJV renders: bestow, bring, govern, guide, lead (forth), put, straiten.
