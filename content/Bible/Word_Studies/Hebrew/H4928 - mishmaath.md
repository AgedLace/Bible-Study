# H4928 — מִשְׁמַעַת

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| מִשְׁמַעַת | mishmaʻath | mish-mah'-ath | audience |

## Definition

audience, i.e. the royal court; also obedience, i.e. (concretely) a subject

KJV renders: bidding, guard, obey.
