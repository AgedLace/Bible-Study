# H0098 — אֲגַם

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| אֲגַם | ʼăgam | ag-am' | a marsh |

## Definition

a marsh; hence a rush (as growing in swamps); hence a stockade of reeds

KJV renders: pond, pool, standing (water).
