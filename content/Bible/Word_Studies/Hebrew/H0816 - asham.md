# H0816 — אָשַׁם

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| אָשַׁם | ʼâsham | aw-sham' | to be guilty |

## Definition

to be guilty; by implication to be punished or perish

KJV renders: certainly, be(-come, made) desolate, destroy, greatly, be(-come, found, hold) guilty, offend (acknowledge offence), trespass.
