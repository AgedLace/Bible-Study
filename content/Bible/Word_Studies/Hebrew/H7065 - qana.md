# H7065 — קָנָא

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |

## Definition

to be (causatively, make) zealous, i.e. (in a bad sense) jealous or envious

KJV renders: (be) envy(-ious), be (move to, provoke to) jealous(-y), very, (be) zeal(-ous).
