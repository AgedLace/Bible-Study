# H7130 — קֶרֶב

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| קֶרֶב | qereb | keh'-reb | properly |

## Definition

properly, the nearest part, i.e. the center, whether literal, figurative or adverbial (especially with preposition)

KJV renders: among, before, bowels, unto charge, eat (up), heart, him, in, inward ( -ly, part, -s, thought), midst, out of, purtenance, therein, through, within self.
