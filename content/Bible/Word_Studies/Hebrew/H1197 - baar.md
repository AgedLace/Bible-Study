# H1197 — בָּעַר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| בָּעַר | bâʻar | baw-ar' | to kindle |

## Definition

to kindle, i.e. consume (by fire or by eating);  to be(-come) brutish

KJV renders: be brutish, bring (put, take) away, burn, (cause to) eat (up), feed, heat, kindle, set (on fire), waste.
