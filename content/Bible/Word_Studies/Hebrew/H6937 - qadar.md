# H6937 — קָדַר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| קָדַר | qâdar | kaw-dar' | to be ashy |

## Definition

to be ashy, i.e. darkcolored; by implication, to mourn (in sackcloth or sordid garments)

KJV renders: be black(-ish), be (make) dark(-en), heavily, (cause to) mourn.
