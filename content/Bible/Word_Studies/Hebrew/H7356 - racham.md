# H7356 — רַחַם

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| רַחַם | racham | rakh'-am | compassion (in the plural) |

## Definition

compassion (in the plural); by extension, the womb (as cherishing the fetus); by implication, a maiden

KJV renders: bowels, compassion, damsel, tender love, (great, tender) mercy, pity, womb.
