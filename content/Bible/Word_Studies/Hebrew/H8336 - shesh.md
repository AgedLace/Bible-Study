# H8336 — שֵׁשׁ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| שֵׁשׁ | shêsh | shaysh | bleached stuff |

## Definition

bleached stuff, i.e. white linen or (by analogy) marble

KJV renders: blue, fine (twined) linen, marble, silk.
