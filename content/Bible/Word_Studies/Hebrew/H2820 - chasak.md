# H2820 — חָשַׂךְ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |

| חָשַׂךְ | châsak | khaw-sak' | to restrain or (reflex.) refrain |

## Definition

to restrain or (reflex.) refrain; by implication, to refuse, spare, preserve; to observe

KJV renders: assuage, darken, forbear, hinder, hold back, keep (back), punish, refrain, reserve, spare, withhold.
