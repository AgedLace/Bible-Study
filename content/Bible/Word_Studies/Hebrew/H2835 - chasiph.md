# H2835 — חָשִׂף

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| חָשִׂף | châsiph | khaw-seef' | properly |

## Definition

properly, drawn off, i.e. separated; hence, a small company (as divided from the rest)

KJV renders: little flock.
