# H7812 — שָׁחָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| שָׁחָה | shâchâh | shaw-khaw' | to depress |

## Definition

to depress, i.e. prostrate (especially reflexive, in homage to royalty or God)

KJV renders: bow (self) down, crouch, fall down (flat), humbly beseech, do (make) obeisance, do reverence, make to stoop, worship.
