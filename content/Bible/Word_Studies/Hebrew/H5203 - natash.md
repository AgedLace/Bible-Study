# H5203 — נָטַשׁ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| נָטַשׁ | nâṭash | naw-tash' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to pound, i.e. smite; by implication (as if beating out, and thus expanding) to disperse; also, to thrust off, down, out or upon (inclusively, reject, let alone, permit, remit, etc.)

KJV renders: cast off, drawn, let fall, forsake, join (battle), leave (off), lie still, loose, spread (self) abroad, stretch out, suffer.
