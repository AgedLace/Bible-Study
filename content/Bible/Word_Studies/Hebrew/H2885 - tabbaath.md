# H2885 — טַבַּעַת

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| טַבַּעַת | ṭabbaʻath | tab-bah'-ath | properly |

## Definition

properly, a seal (as sunk into the wax), i.e. signet (for sealing); hence (generally) a ring of any kind

KJV renders: ring.
