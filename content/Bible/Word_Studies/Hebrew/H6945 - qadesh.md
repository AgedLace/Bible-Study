# H6945 — קָדֵשׁ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| קָדֵשׁ | qâdêsh | kaw-dashe' | a (quasi) sacred person |

## Definition

a (quasi) sacred person, i.e. (technically) a (male) devotee (by prostitution) to licentious idolatry

KJV renders: sodomite, unclean.
