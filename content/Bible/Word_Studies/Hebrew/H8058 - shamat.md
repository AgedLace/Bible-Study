# H8058 — שָׁמַט

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| שָׁמַט | shâmaṭ | shaw-mat' | to fling down |

## Definition

to fling down; incipiently to jostle; figuratively, to let alone, desist, remit

KJV renders: discontinue, overthrow, release, let rest, shake, stumble, throw down.
