# H4917 — מִשְׁלַחַת

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| מִשְׁלַחַת | mishlachath | mish-lakh'-ath | a mission |

## Definition

a mission, i.e. (abstractly and favorable) release, or (concretely and unfavorable) an army

KJV renders: discharge, sending.
