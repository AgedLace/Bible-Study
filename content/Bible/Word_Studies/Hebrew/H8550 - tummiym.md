# H8550 — תֻּמִּים

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| תֻּמִּים | Tummîym | toom-meem' | perfections |

## Definition

perfections, i.e. (techn.) one of the epithets of the objects in the high-priest's breastplate as an emblem of complete Truth

KJV renders: Thummim.
