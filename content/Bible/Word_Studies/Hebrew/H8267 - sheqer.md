# H8267 — שֶׁקֶר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| שֶׁקֶר | sheqer | sheh'-ker | an untruth |

## Definition

an untruth; by implication, a sham (often adverbial)

KJV renders: without a cause, deceit(-ful), false(-hood, -ly), feignedly, liar, lie, lying, vain (thing), wrongfully.
