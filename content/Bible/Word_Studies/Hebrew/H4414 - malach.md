# H4414 — מָלַח

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| מָלַח | mâlach | maw-lakh' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to rub to pieces or pulverize; intransitively, to disappear as dust; to salt whether internally (to season with salt) or externally (to rub with salt)

KJV renders: at all, salt, season, temper together, vanish away.
