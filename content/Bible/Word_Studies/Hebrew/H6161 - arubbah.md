# H6161 — עֲרֻבָּה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| עֲרֻבָּה | ʻărubbâh | ar-oob-baw' | something given as security |

## Definition

something given as security, i.e. (literally) a token (of safety) or (metaphorically) a bondsman

KJV renders: pledge, surety.
