# H5057 — נָגִיד

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| נָגִיד | nâgîyd | naw-gheed' | a commander (as occupying the front) |

## Definition

a commander (as occupying the front), civil, military or religious; generally (abstractly, plural), honorable themes

KJV renders: captain, chief, excellent thing, (chief) governor, leader, noble, prince, (chief) ruler.
