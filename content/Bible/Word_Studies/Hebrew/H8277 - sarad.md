# H8277 — שָׂרַד

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |

| שָׂרַד | sârad | saw-rad' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to puncture, i.e. (figuratively through the idea of slipping out) to escape or survive

KJV renders: remain.
