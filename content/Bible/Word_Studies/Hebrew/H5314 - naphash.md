# H5314 — נָפַשׁ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| נָפַשׁ | nâphash | naw-fash' | to breathe |

## Definition

to breathe; passively, to be breathed upon , i.e. (figuratively) refreshed (as if by a current of air)

KJV renders: (be) refresh selves (-ed).
