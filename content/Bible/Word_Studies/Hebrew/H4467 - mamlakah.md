# H4467 — מַמְלָכָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| מַמְלָכָה | mamlâkâh | mam-law-kaw' | dominion |

## Definition

dominion, i.e. (abstractly) the estate (rule) or (concretely) the country (realm)

KJV renders: kingdom, king's, reign, royal.
