# H6191 — עָרַם

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| עָרַם | ʻâram | aw-ram' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to be (or make) bare; but used only in the derivative sense (through the idea perhaps of smoothness) to be cunning (usually in a bad sense)

KJV renders: very, beware, take crafty (counsel), be prudent, deal subtilly.
