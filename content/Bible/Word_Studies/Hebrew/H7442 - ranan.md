# H7442 — רָנַן

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| רָנַן | rânan | raw-nan' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to creak (or emit a stridulous sound), i.e. to shout (usually for joy)

KJV renders: aloud for joy, cry out, be joyful (greatly, make to) rejoice, (cause to) shout (for joy), (cause to) sing (aloud, for joy, out), triumph.
