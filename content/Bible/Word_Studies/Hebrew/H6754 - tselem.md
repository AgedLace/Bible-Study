# H6754 — צֶלֶם

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| צֶלֶם | tselem | tseh'-lem | a phantom |

## Definition

a phantom, i.e. (figuratively) illusion, resemblance; hence, a representative figure, especially an idol

KJV renders: image, vain shew.
