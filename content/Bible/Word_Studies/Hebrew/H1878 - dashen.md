# H1878 — דָּשֵׁן

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| דָּשֵׁן | dâshên | daw-shane' | to be fat |

## Definition

to be fat; transitively, to fatten (or regard as fat); specifically to anoint; figuratively, to satisfy;  to remove (fat) ashes (of sacrifices)

KJV renders: accept, anoint, take away the (receive) ashes (from), make (wax) fat.
