# H4026 — מִגְדָּל

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| מִגְדָּל | migdâl | mig-dawl' | a tower (from its size or height) |

## Definition

a tower (from its size or height); by analogy, a rostrum; figuratively, a (pyramidal) bed of flowers

KJV renders: castle, flower, tower. Compare the names following.
