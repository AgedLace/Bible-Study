# H5012 — נָבָא

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| נָבָא | nâbâʼ | naw-baw' | to prophesy |

## Definition

to prophesy, i.e. speak (or sing) by inspiration (in prediction or simple discourse)

KJV renders: prophesy(-ing), make self a prophet.
