# H4165 — מוּצָק

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| מוּצָק | mûwtsâq | moo-tsawk' | properly |

## Definition

properly, fusion, i.e. literally, a casting (of metal); figuratively, a mass (of clay)

KJV renders: casting, hardness.
