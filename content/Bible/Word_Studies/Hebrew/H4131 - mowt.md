# H4131 — מוֹט

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| מוֹט | môwṭ | mote | to waver |

## Definition

to waver; by implication, to slip, shake, fall

KJV renders: be carried, cast, be out of course, be fallen in decay, exceedingly, fall(-ing down), be (re-) moved, be ready, shake, slide, slip.
