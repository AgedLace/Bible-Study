# H1322 — בֹּשֶׁת

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| בֹּשֶׁת | bôsheth | bo'-sheth | shame (the feeling and the condition |

## Definition

shame (the feeling and the condition, as well as its cause); by implication (specifically) an idol

KJV renders: ashamed, confusion, greatly, (put to) shame(-ful thing).
