# H3293 — יַעַר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| יַעַר | yaʻar | yah'-ar | a copse of bushes |

## Definition

a copse of bushes; hence, a forest; hence, honey in the comb (as hived in trees)

KJV renders: (honey-) comb, forest, wood.
