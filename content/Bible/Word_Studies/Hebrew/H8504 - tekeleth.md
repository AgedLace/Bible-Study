# H8504 — תְּכֵלֶת

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| תְּכֵלֶת | tᵉkêleth | tek-ay'-leth | the cerulean mussel |

## Definition

the cerulean mussel, i.e. the color (violet) obtained therefrom or stuff dyed therewith

KJV renders: blue.
