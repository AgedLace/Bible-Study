# H8251 — שִׁקּוּץ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| שִׁקּוּץ | shiqqûwts | shik-koots' | disgusting |

## Definition

disgusting, i.e. filthy; especially idolatrous or (concretely) an idol

KJV renders: abominable filth (idol, -ation), detestable (thing).
