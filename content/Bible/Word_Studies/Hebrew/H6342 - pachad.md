# H6342 — פָּחַד

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| פָּחַד | pâchad | paw-kkad' | to be startled (by a sudden alarm) |

## Definition

to be startled (by a sudden alarm); hence, to fear in general

KJV renders: be afraid, stand in awe, (be in) fear, make to shake.
