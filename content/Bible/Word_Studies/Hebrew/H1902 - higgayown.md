# H1902 — הִגָּיוֹן

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| הִגָּיוֹן | higgâyôwn | hig-gaw-yone' | a murmuring sound |

## Definition

a murmuring sound, i.e. a musical notation (probably similar to the modern affettuoso to indicate solemnity of movement); by implication, a machination

KJV renders: device, Higgaion, meditation, solemn sound.
