# H6424 — פָּלַס

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| פָּלַס | pâlaç | paw-las' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to roll flat, i.e. prepare (a road); also to revolve, i.e. weigh (mentally)

KJV renders: make, ponder, weigh.
