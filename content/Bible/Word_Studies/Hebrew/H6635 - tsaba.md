# H6635 — צָבָא

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| צָבָא | tsâbâʼ | tsaw-baw' | a mass of persons (or figuratively |

## Definition

a mass of persons (or figuratively, things), especially reg. organized forwar (an army); by implication, a campaign, literally or figuratively (specifically, hardship, worship)

KJV renders: appointed time, () army, () battle, company, host, service, soldiers, waiting upon, war(-fare).
