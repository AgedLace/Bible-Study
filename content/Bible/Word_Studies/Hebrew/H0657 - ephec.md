# H0657 — אֶפֶס

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| אֶפֶס | ʼepheç | eh'-fes | cessation |

## Definition

cessation, i.e. an end (especially of the earth); often used adverb, no further; also the ankle (in the dual), as being the extremity of the leg or foot

KJV renders: ankle, but (only), end, howbeit, less than nothing, nevertheless (where), no, none (beside), not (any, -withstanding), thing of nought, save(-ing), there, uttermost part, want, without (cause).
