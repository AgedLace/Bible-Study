# H3318 — יָצָא

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| יָצָא | yâtsâʼ | yaw-tsaw' | to go (causatively |

## Definition

to go (causatively, bring) out, in a great variety of applications, literally and figuratively, direct and proxim.

KJV renders: after, appear, assuredly, bear out, begotten, break out, bring forth (out, up), carry out, come (abroad, out, thereat, without), be condemned, depart(-ing, -ure), draw forth, in the end, escape, exact, fail, fall (out), fetch forth (out), get away (forth, hence, out), (able to, cause to, let) go abroad (forth, on, out), going out, grow, have forth (out), issue out, lay (lie) out, lead out, pluck out, proceed, pull out, put away, be risen, scarce, send with commandment, shoot forth, spread, spring out, stand out, still, surely, take forth (out), at any time, to (and fro), utter.
