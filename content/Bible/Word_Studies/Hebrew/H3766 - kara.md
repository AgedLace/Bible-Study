# H3766 — כָּרַע

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| כָּרַע | kâraʻ | kaw-rah' | to bend the knee |

## Definition

to bend the knee; by implication, to sink, to prostrate

KJV renders: bow (down, self), bring down (low), cast down, couch, fall, feeble, kneeling, sink, smite (stoop) down, subdue, very.
