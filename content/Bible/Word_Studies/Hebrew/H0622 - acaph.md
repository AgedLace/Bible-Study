# H0622 — אָסַף

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| אָסַף | ʼâçaph | aw-saf' | to gather for any purpose |

## Definition

to gather for any purpose; hence, to receive, take away, i.e. remove (destroy, leave behind, put up, restore, etc.)

KJV renders: assemble, bring, consume, destroy, felch, gather (in, together, up again), generally, get (him), lose, put all together, receive, recover (another from leprosy), (be) rereward, surely, take (away, into, up), utterly, withdraw.
