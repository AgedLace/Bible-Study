# H5315 — נֶפֶשׁ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| נֶפֶשׁ | nephesh | neh'-fesh | properly |

## Definition

properly, a breathing creature, i.e. animal of (abstractly) vitality; used very widely in a literal, accommodated or figurative sense (bodily or mental)

KJV renders: any, appetite, beast, body, breath, creature, dead(-ly), desire, (dis-) contented, fish, ghost, greedy, he, heart(-y), (hath, jeopardy of) life ( in jeopardy), lust, man, me, mind, mortally, one, own, person, pleasure, (her-, him-, my-, thy-) self, them (your) -selves, slay, soul, tablet, they, thing, ( she) will, would have it.
