# H4279 — מָחָר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| מָחָר | mâchâr | maw-khar' | properly |

## Definition

properly, deferred, i.e. the morrow; usually (adverbially) tomorrow; indefinitely, hereafter

KJV renders: time to come, tomorrow.
