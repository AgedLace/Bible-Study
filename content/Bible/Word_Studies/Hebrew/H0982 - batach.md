# H0982 — בָּטַח

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| בָּטַח | bâṭach | baw-takh' | figuratively |

## Definition

figuratively, to trust, be confident or sure

KJV renders: be bold (confident, secure, sure), careless (one, woman), put confidence, (make to) hope, (put, make to) trust.
