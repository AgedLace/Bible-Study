# H6670 — צָהַל

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| צָהַל | tsâhal | tsaw-hal' | to gleam |

## Definition

to gleam, i.e. (figuratively) be cheerful; by transference to sound clear (of various animal or human expressions)

KJV renders: bellow, cry aloud (out), lift up, neigh, rejoice, make to shine, shout.
