# H0389 — אַךְ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| אַךְ | ʼak | ak | a particle of affirmation |

## Definition

a particle of affirmation, surely; hence (by limitation) only

KJV renders: also, in any wise, at least, but, certainly, even, howbeit, nevertheless, notwithstanding, only, save, surely, of a surety, truly, verily, wherefore, yet (but).
