# H8378 — תַּאֲוָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| תַּאֲוָה | taʼăvâh | tah-av-aw' | a longing |

## Definition

a longing; by implication, a delight (subjectively, satisfaction, objectively, a charm)

KJV renders: dainty, desire, exceedingly, greedily, lust(ing), pleasant. See also H6914 (קִבְרוֹת הַתַּאֲוָה).
