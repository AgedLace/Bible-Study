# H7092 — קָפַץ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| קָפַץ | qâphats | kaw-fats' | to draw together |

## Definition

to draw together, i.e. close; by implication, to leap (by contracting the limbs); specifically, to die (from gathering up the feet)

KJV renders: shut (up), skip, stop, take out of the way.
