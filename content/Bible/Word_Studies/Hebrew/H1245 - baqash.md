# H1245 — בָּקַשׁ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| בָּקַשׁ | bâqash | baw-kash' | to search out (by any method |

## Definition

to search out (by any method, specifically in worship or prayer); by implication, to strive after

KJV renders: ask, beg, beseech, desire, enquire, get, make inquisition, procure, (make) request, require, seek (for).
