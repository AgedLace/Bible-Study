# H3669 — כְּנַעַנִי

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| כְּנַעַנִי | Kᵉnaʻanîy | ken-ah-an-ee' | a Kenaanite or inhabitant of Kenaan |

## Definition

a Kenaanite or inhabitant of Kenaan; by implication, a pedlar (the Canaanites standing for their neighbors the Ishmaelites, who conducted mercantile caravans)

KJV renders: Canaanite, merchant, trafficker.
