# H4932 — מִשְׁנֶה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| מִשְׁנֶה | mishneh | mish-neh' | properly |

## Definition

properly, a repetition, i.e. a duplicate (copy of a document), or a double (in amount); by implication, a second (in order, rank, age, quality or location)

KJV renders: college, copy, double, fatlings, next, second (order), twice as much.
