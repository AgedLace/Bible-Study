# H6172 — עֶרְוָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| עֶרְוָה | ʻervâh | er-vaw' | nudity |

## Definition

nudity, literally (especially the pudenda) or figuratively (disgrace, blemish)

KJV renders: nakedness, shame, unclean(-ness).
