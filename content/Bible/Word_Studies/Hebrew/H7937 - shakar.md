# H7937 — שָׁכַר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| שָׁכַר | shâkar | shaw-kar' | to become tipsy |

## Definition

to become tipsy; in a qualified sense, to satiate with astimulating drink or (figuratively) influence

KJV renders: (be filled with) drink (abundantly), (be, make) drunk(-en), be merry. (Superlative of H8248 (שָׁקָה).)
