# H6817 — צָעַק

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| צָעַק | tsâʻaq | tsaw-ak' | to shriek |

## Definition

to shriek; (by implication) to proclaim (an assembly)

KJV renders: at all, call together, cry (out), gather (selves) (together).
