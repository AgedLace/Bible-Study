# H1354 — גַב

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| גַב | gab | gab | the back (as rounded) |

## Definition

the back (as rounded); by analogy, the top or rim, a boss, a vault, arch of eye, bulwarks, etc.

KJV renders: back, body, boss, eminent (higher) place, (eye) brows, nave, ring.
