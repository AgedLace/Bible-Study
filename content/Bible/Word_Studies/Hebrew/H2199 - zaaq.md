# H2199 — זָעַק

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| זָעַק | zâʻaq | zaw-ak' | to shriek (from anguish or danger) |

## Definition

to shriek (from anguish or danger); by analogy, (as a herald) to announce or convene publicly

KJV renders: assemble, call (together), (make a) cry (out), come with such a company, gather (together), cause to be proclaimed.
