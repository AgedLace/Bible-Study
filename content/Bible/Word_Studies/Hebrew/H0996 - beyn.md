# H0996 — בֵּין

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| בֵּין | bêyn | bane | between (repeated before each noun |

## Definition

between (repeated before each noun, often with other particles); also as a conjunction, either...or

KJV renders: among, asunder, at, between (-twixt...and), from (the widest), in, out of, whether (it be...or), within.
