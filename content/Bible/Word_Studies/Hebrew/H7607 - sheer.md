# H7607 — שְׁאֵר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| שְׁאֵר | shᵉʼêr | sheh-ayr' | flesh (as swelling out) |

## Definition

flesh (as swelling out), as living or forfood; generally food of any kind; figuratively, kindred by blood

KJV renders: body, flesh, food, (near) kin(-sman, -swoman), near (nigh) (of kin).
