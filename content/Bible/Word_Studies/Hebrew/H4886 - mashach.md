# H4886 — מָשַׁח

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| מָשַׁח | mâshach | maw-shakh' | to rub with oil |

## Definition

to rub with oil, i.e. to anoint; by implication, to consecrate; also to paint

KJV renders: anoint, paint.
