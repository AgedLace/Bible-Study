# H5676 — עֵבֶר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| עֵבֶר | ʻêber | ay'-ber | properly |

## Definition

properly, a region across; but used only adverbially (with or without a preposition) on the opposite side (especially of the Jordan; ususally meaning the east)

KJV renders: against, beyond, by, from, over, passage, quarter, (other, this) side, straight.
