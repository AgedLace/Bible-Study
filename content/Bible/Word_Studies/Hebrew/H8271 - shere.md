# H8271 — שְׁרֵא

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| שְׁרֵא | shᵉrêʼ | sher-ay' | to free |

## Definition

to free, separate; figuratively, to unravel, commence; by implication (of unloading beasts) to reside

KJV renders: begin, dissolve, dwell, loose.
