# H7918 — שָׁכַךְ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| שָׁכַךְ | shâkak | shaw-kak' | to weave (i.e. lay) a trap |

## Definition

to weave (i.e. lay) a trap; figuratively, (through the idea of secreting) to allay (passions; physically, abate a flood)

KJV renders: appease, assuage, make to cease, pacify, set.
