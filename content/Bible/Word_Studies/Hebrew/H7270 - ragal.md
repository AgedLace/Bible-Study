# H7270 — רָגַל

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| רָגַל | râgal | raw-gal' | to walk along |

## Definition

to walk along; but only in specifically, applications, to reconnoiter, to be atale-bearer (i.e. slander); to lead about

KJV renders: backbite, search, slander, (e-) spy (out), teach to go, view.
