# H4736 — מִקְנָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| מִקְנָה | miqnâh | mik-naw' | properly |

## Definition

properly, a buying, i.e. acquisition; concretely, a piece of property (land or living); also the sum paid

KJV renders: (he that is) bought, possession, piece, purchase.
