# H4394 — מִלֻּא

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| מִלֻּא | milluʼ | mil-loo' | a fulfilling (only in plural) |

## Definition

a fulfilling (only in plural), i.e. (literally) a setting (of gems), or (technically) consecration (also concretely, a dedicatory sacrifice)

KJV renders: consecration, be set.
