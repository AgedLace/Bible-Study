# H2181 — זָנָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| זָנָה | zânâh | zaw-naw' | to commit adultery (usually of the female |

## Definition

to commit adultery (usually of the female, and less often of simple fornication, rarely of involuntary ravishment); figuratively, to commit idolatry (the Jewish people being regarded as the spouse of Jehovah)

KJV renders: (cause to) commit fornication, continually, great, (be an, play the) harlot, (cause to be, play the) whore, (commit, fall to) whoredom, (cause to) go a-whoring, whorish.
