# H1238 — בָּקַק

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| בָּקַק | bâqaq | baw-kah' | to pour out |

## Definition

to pour out, i.e. to empty, figuratively, to depopulate; by analogy, to spread out (as a fruitful vine)

KJV renders: (make) empty (out), fail, utterly, make void.
