# H4422 — מָלַט

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| מָלַט | mâlaṭ | maw-lat' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to be smooth, i.e. (by implication) to escape (as if by slipperiness); causatively, to release or rescue; specifically, to bring forth young, emit sparks

KJV renders: deliver (self), escape, lay, leap out, let alone, let go, preserve, save, speedily, surely.
