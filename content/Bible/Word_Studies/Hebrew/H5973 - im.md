# H5973 — עִם

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| עִם | ʻim | eem | adverb or preposition |

## Definition

adverb or preposition, with (i.e. in conjunction with), in varied applications; specifically, equally with; often with prepositional prefix (and then usually unrepresented in English)

KJV renders: accompanying, against, and, as ( long as), before, beside, by (reason of), for all, from (among, between), in, like, more than, of, (un-) to, with(-al).
