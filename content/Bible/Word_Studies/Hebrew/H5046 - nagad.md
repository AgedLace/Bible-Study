# H5046 — נָגַד

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| נָגַד | nâgad | naw-gad' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to front, i.e. stand boldly out opposite; by implication (causatively), to manifest; figuratively, to announce (always by word of mouth to one present); specifically, to expose, predict, explain, praise

KJV renders: bewray, certainly, certify, declare(-ing), denounce, expound, fully, messenger, plainly, profess, rehearse, report, shew (forth), speak, surely, tell, utter.
