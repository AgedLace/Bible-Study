# H3943 — לָפַת

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| לָפַת | lâphath | law-fath' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to bend, i.e. (by implication) to clasp; also (reflexively) to turn around or aside

KJV renders: take hold, turn aside (self).
