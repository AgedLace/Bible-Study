# H1516 — גַּיְא

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |

| גַּיְא | gayʼ | gah'-ee | a gorge (from its lofty sides |

## Definition

a gorge (from its lofty sides; hence, narrow, but not a gully or winter-torrent)

KJV renders: valley.
