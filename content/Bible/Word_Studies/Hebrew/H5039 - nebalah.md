# H5039 — נְבָלָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| נְבָלָה | nᵉbâlâh | neb-aw-law' | foolishness |

## Definition

foolishness, i.e. (morally) wickedness; concretely, a crime; by extension, punishment

KJV renders: folly, vile, villany.
