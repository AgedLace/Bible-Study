# H5869 — עַיִן

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| עַיִן | ʻayin | ah'-yin | an eye (literally or figuratively) |

## Definition

an eye (literally or figuratively); by analogy, a fountain (as the eye of the landscape)

KJV renders: affliction, outward appearance, before, think best, colour, conceit, be content, countenance, displease, eye((-brow), (-d), -sight), face, favour, fountain, furrow (from the margin), him, humble, knowledge, look, ( well), me, open(-ly), (not) please, presence, regard, resemblance, sight, thee, them, think, us, well, you(-rselves).
