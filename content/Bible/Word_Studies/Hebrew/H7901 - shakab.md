# H7901 — שָׁכַב

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| שָׁכַב | shâkab | shaw-kab' | to lie down (for rest |

## Definition

to lie down (for rest, sexual connection, decease or any other purpose)

KJV renders: at all, cast down, (lover-)lay (self) (down), (make to) lie (down, down to sleep, still with), lodge, ravish, take rest, sleep, stay.
