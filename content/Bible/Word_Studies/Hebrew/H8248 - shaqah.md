# H8248 — שָׁקָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| שָׁקָה | shâqâh | shaw-kaw' | to quaff |

## Definition

to quaff, i.e. (causatively) to irrigate or furnish apotion to

KJV renders: cause to (give, give to, let, make to) drink, drown, moisten, water. See H7937 (שָׁכַר), H8354 (שָׁתָה).
