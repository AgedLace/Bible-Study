# H5425 — נָתַר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| נָתַר | nâthar | naw-thar' | to jump |

## Definition

to jump, i.e. be violently agitated; causatively, to terrify, shake off, untie

KJV renders: drive asunder, leap, (let) loose, make, move, undo.
