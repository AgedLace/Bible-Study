# H2600 — חִנָּם

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| חִנָּם | chinnâm | khin-nawm' | gratis |

## Definition

gratis, i.e. devoid of cost, reason or advantage

KJV renders: without a cause (cost, wages), causeless, to cost nothing, free(-ly), innocent, for nothing (nought, in vain.
