# H2398 — חָטָא

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| חָטָא | châṭâʼ | khaw-taw' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to miss; hence (figuratively and generally) to sin; by inference, to forfeit, lack, expiate, repent, (causatively) lead astray, condemn

KJV renders: bear the blame, cleanse, commit (sin), by fault, harm he hath done, loss, miss, (make) offend(-er), offer for sin, purge, purify (self), make reconciliation, (cause, make) sin(-ful, -ness), trespass.
