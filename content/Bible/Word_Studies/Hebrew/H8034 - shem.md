| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |

| שֵׁם | shêm | shame | an appellation |

## Definition

an appellation, as amark or memorial of individuality; by implication honor, authority, character

KJV renders: base, (in-) fame(-ous), named(-d), renown, report.
