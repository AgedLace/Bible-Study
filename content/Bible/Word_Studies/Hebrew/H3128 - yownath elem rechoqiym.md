# H3128 — יוֹנַת אֵלֶם רְחֹקִים

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| יוֹנַת אֵלֶם רְחֹקִים | yôwnath ʼêlem rᵉchôqîym | yo-nath' ay'-lem rekh-o-keem' | dove of (the) silence (i.e. dumb Israel) of (i.e. among) distances (i.e. strangers) |

## Definition

dove of (the) silence (i.e. dumb Israel) of (i.e. among) distances (i.e. strangers); the title of a ditty (used for a name of its melody)

KJV renders: Jonath-elem-rechokim.
