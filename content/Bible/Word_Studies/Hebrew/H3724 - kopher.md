# H3724 — כֹּפֶר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| כֹּפֶר | kôpher | ko'-fer | properly |

## Definition

properly, a cover, i.e. (literally) a village (as covered in); (specifically) bitumen (as used for coating), and the henna plant (as used for dyeing); figuratively, a redemption-price

KJV renders: bribe, camphire, pitch, ransom, satisfaction, sum of money, village.
