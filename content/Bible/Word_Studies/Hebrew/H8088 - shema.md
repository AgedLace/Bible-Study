# H8088 — שֵׁמַע

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| שֵׁמַע | shêmaʻ | shay'-mah | something heard |

## Definition

something heard, i.e. a sound, rumor, announcement; abstractly, audience

KJV renders: bruit, fame, hear(-ing), loud, report, speech, tidings.
