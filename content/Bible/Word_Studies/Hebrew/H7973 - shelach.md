# H7973 — שֶׁלַח

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| שֶׁלַח | shelach | sheh'-lakh | a missile of attack |

## Definition

a missile of attack, i.e. spear; also (figuratively) a shoot of growth; i.e. branch

KJV renders: dart, plant, put off, sword, weapon.
