# H3615 — כָּלָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| כָּלָה | kâlâh | kaw-law' | to end |

## Definition

to end, whether intransitive (to cease, be finished, perish) or transitived (to complete, prepare, consume)

KJV renders: accomplish, cease, consume (away), determine, destroy (utterly), be (when... were) done, (be an) end (of), expire, (cause to) fail, faint, finish, fulfil, fully, have, leave (off), long, bring to pass, wholly reap, make clean riddance, spend, quite take away, waste.
