# H5274 — נָעַל

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| נָעַל | nâʻal | naw-al' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to fasten up, i.e. with abar or cord; hence, to sandal, i.e. furnish with slippers

KJV renders: bolt, inclose, lock, shoe, shut up.
