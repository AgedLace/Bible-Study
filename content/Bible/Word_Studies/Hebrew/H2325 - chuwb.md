# H2325 — חוּב

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| חוּב | chûwb | khoob | properly |

## Definition

properly, perhaps to tie, i.e. (figuratively and reflexively) to owe, or (by implication) to forfeit

KJV renders: make endanger.
