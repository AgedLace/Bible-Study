# H2616 — חָסַד

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| חָסַד | châçad | khaw-sad' | properly |

## Definition

properly, perhaps to bow (the neck only  in courtesy to an equal), i.e. to be kind; also (by euphemistically, but rarely) to reprove

KJV renders: shew self merciful, put to shame.
