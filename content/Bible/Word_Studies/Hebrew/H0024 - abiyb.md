# H0024 — אָבִיב

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| אָבִיב | ʼâbîyb | aw-beeb' | green |

## Definition

green, i.e. a young ear of grain; hence, the name of the month Abib or Nisan

KJV renders: Abib, ear, green ears of corn (not maize).
