# H2729 — חָרַד

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| חָרַד | chârad | khaw-rad' | to shudder with terror |

## Definition

to shudder with terror; hence, to fear; also to hasten (with anxiety)

KJV renders: be (make) afraid, be careful, discomfit, fray (away), quake, tremble.
