# H5265 — נָסַע

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| נָסַע | nâçaʻ | naw-sah' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to pull up, especially the tent-pins, i.e. start on ajourney

KJV renders: cause to blow, bring, get, (make to) go (away, forth, forward, onward, out), (take) journey, march, remove, set aside (forward), still, be on his (go their) way.
