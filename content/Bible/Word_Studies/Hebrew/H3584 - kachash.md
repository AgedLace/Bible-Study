# H3584 — כָּחַשׁ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| כָּחַשׁ | kâchash | kaw-khash' | to be untrue |

## Definition

to be untrue, in word (to lie, feign, disown) or deed (to disappoint, fail, cringe)

KJV renders: deceive, deny, dissemble, fail, deal falsely, be found liars, (be-) lie, lying, submit selves.
