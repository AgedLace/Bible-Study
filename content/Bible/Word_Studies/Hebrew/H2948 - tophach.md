# H2948 — טֹפַח

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| טֹפַח | ṭôphach | to'-fakh | {a spread of the hand |

## Definition

{a spread of the hand, i.e. a palm-breadth (not 'span' of the fingers); architecturally, a corbel (as a supporting palm)}

KJV renders: hand-breadth (broad).
