# H7623 — שָׁבַח

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| שָׁבַח | shâbach | shaw-bakh' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to address in aloud tone, i.e. (specifically) loud; figuratively, to pacify (as if by words)

KJV renders: commend, glory, keep in, praise, still, triumph.
