# H7646 — שָׂבַע

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| שָׂבַע | sâbaʻ | saw-bah' | to sate |

## Definition

to sate, i.e. fill to satisfaction (literally or figuratively)

KJV renders: have enough, fill (full, self, with), be (to the) full (of), have plenty of, be satiate, satisfy (with), suffice, be weary of.
