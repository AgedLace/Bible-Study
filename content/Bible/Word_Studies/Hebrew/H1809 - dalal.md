# H1809 — דָּלַל

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| דָּלַל | dâlal | daw-lal' | to slacken or be feeble |

## Definition

to slacken or be feeble; figuratively, to be oppressed

KJV renders: bring low, dry up, be emptied, be not equal, fail, be impoverished, be made thin.
