# H1288 — בָרַךְ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| בָרַךְ | bârak | baw-rak' | to kneel |

## Definition

to kneel; by implication to bless God (as an act of adoration), and (vice-versa) man (as a benefit); also (by euphemism) to curse (God or the king, as treason)

KJV renders: abundantly, altogether, at all, blaspheme, bless, congratulate, curse, greatly, indeed, kneel (down), praise, salute, still, thank.
