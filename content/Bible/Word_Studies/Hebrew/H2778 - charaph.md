# H2778 — חָרַף

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| חָרַף | châraph | khaw-raf' | to pull off |

## Definition

to pull off, i.e. (by implication) to expose (as by stripping); specifically, to betroth (as if a surrender); figuratively, to carp at, i.e. defame; to spend the winter

KJV renders: betroth, blaspheme, defy, jeopard, rail, reproach, upbraid.
