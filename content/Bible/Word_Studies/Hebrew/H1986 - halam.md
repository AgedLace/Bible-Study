# H1986 — הָלַם

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| הָלַם | hâlam | haw-lam' | to strike down |

## Definition

to strike down; by implication, to hammer, stamp, conquer, disband

KJV renders: beat (down), break (down), overcome, smite (with the hammer).
