# H0672 — אֶפְרָת

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |

| אֶפְרָת | ʼEphrâth | ef-rawth' | Ephrath |

## Definition

Ephrath, another name for Bethlehem;

KJV renders: once (Psalm 132:6) perhaps for Ephraim; also of an Israelitish woman; Ephrath, Ephratah.
