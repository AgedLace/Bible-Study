# H2654 — חָפֵץ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| חָפֵץ | châphêts | khaw-fates' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to incline to; by implication (literally but rarely) to bend; figuratively, to be pleased with, desire

KJV renders: any at all, (have, take) delight, desire, favour, like, move, be (well) pleased, have pleasure, will, would.
