# H7604 — שָׁאַר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| שָׁאַר | shâʼar | shaw-ar' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to swell up, i.e. be (causatively, make) redundant

KJV renders: leave, (be) left, let, remain, remnant, reserve, the rest.
