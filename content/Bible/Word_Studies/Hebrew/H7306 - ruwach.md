# H7306 — רוּחַ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| רוּחַ | rûwach | roo'-akh | properly |

## Definition

properly, to blow, i.e. breathe; only (literally) to smell or (by implication, perceive (figuratively, to anticipate, enjoy)

KJV renders: accept, smell, touch, make of quick understanding.
