# H4889 — מַשְׁחִית

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| מַשְׁחִית | mashchîyth | mash-kheeth' | destructive |

## Definition

destructive, i.e. (as noun) destruction, literally (specifically a snare) or figuratively (corruption)

KJV renders: corruption, (to) destroy(-ing), destruction, trap, utterly.
