# H2111 — זוּעַ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| זוּעַ | zûwaʻ | zoo'-ah | properly |

## Definition

properly, to shake off, i.e. (figuratively) to agitate (as with fear)

KJV renders: move, tremble, vex.
