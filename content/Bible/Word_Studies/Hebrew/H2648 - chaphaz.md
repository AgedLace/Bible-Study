# H2648 — חָפַז

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |

| חָפַז | châphaz | khaw-faz' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to start up suddenly, i.e. (by implication) to hasten away, to fear

KJV renders: (make) haste (away), tremble.
