# H6252 — עַשְׁתָּרוֹת

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| עַשְׁתָּרוֹת | ʻAshtârôwth | ash-taw-roth' | Ashtaroth |

## Definition

Ashtaroth, the name of a Sidonian deity, and of a place East of the Jordan

KJV renders: Asharoth, Astaroth. See also H1045 (בֵּית עַשְׁתָּרוֹת), H6253 (עַשְׁתֹּרֶת), H6255 (עַשְׁתְּרֹת קַרְנַיִם).
