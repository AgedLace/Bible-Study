# H8175 — שָׂעַר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| שָׂעַר | sâʻar | saw-ar' | to storm |

## Definition

to storm; by implication, to shiver, i.e. fear

KJV renders: be (horribly) afraid, fear, hurl as a storm, be tempestuous, come like (take away as with) a whirlwind.
