# H4694 — מְצוּרָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| מְצוּרָה | mᵉtsûwrâh | mets-oo-raw' | a hemming in |

## Definition

a hemming in, i.e. (objectively) a mound (of siege), or (subjectively) a rampart (of protection), (abstractly) fortification

KJV renders: fenced (city, fort, munition, strong hold.
