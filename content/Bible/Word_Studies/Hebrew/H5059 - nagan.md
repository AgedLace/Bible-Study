# H5059 — נָגַן

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| נָגַן | nâgan | naw-gan' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to thrum, i.e. beat atune with the fingers; expectation. to play on astringed instrument; hence (generally), to make music

KJV renders: player on instruments, sing to the stringed instruments, melody, ministrel, play(-er, -ing).
