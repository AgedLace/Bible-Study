| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |

| סָכַן | çâkan | saw-kan' | to be familiar with |

## Definition

to be familiar with; by implication, to minister to, be serviceable to, be customary

KJV renders: acquaint (self), be advantage, ever, (be, (un-)) profit(-able), treasurer, be wont.
