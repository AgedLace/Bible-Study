# H3444 — יְשׁוּעָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| יְשׁוּעָה | yᵉshûwʻâh | yesh-oo'-aw | something saved |

## Definition

something saved, i.e. (abstractly) deliverance; hence, aid, victory, prosperity

KJV renders: deliverance, health, help(-ing), salvation, save, saving (health), welfare.
