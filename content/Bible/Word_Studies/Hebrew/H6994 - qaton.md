# H6994 — קָטֹן

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| קָטֹן | qâṭôn | kaw-tone' | to diminish |

## Definition

to diminish, i.e. be (causatively, make) diminutive or (figuratively) of no account

KJV renders: be a (make) small (thing), be not worthy.
