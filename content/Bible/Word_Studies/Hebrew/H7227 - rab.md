| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |

| רַב | rab | rab | abundant (in quantity |

## Definition

abundant (in quantity, size, age, number, rank, quality)

KJV renders: (in) abound(-undance, -ant, -antly), captain, elder, enough, exceedingly, full, great(-ly, man, one), increase, long (enough, (time)), (do, have) many(-ifold, things, a time), (ship-)master, mighty, more, (too, very) much, multiply(-tude), officer, often(-times), plenteous, populous, prince, process (of time), suffice(-lent).
