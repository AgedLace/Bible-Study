# H6031 — עָנָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| עָנָה | ʻânâh | aw-naw' | to depress literally or figuratively |

## Definition

to depress literally or figuratively, transitive or intransitive (in various applications, as follows)

KJV renders: abase self, afflict(-ion, self), answer (by mistake for H6030 (עָנָה)), chasten self, deal hardly with, defile, exercise, force, gentleness, humble (self), hurt, ravish, sing (by mistake for H6030 (עָנָה)), speak (by mistake for H6030 (עָנָה)), submit self, weaken, in any wise.
