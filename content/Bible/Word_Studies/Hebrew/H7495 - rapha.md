# H7495 — רָפָא

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| רָפָא | râphâʼ | raw-faw' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to mend (by stitching), i.e. (figuratively) to cure

KJV renders: cure, (cause to) heal, physician, repair, thoroughly, make whole. See H7503 (רָפָה).
