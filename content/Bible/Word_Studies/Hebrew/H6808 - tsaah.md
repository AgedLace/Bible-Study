# H6808 — צָעָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| צָעָה | tsâʻâh | tsaw-aw' | to tip over (for the purpose of spilling or pouring out) |

## Definition

to tip over (for the purpose of spilling or pouring out), i.e. (figuratively) depopulate; by implication, to imprison or conquer; (reflexive) to lie down (for coitus)

KJV renders: captive exile, travelling, (cause to) wander(-er).
