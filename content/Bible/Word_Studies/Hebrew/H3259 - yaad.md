# H3259 — יָעַד

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| יָעַד | yâʻad | yaw-ad' | to fix upon (by agreement or appointment) |

## Definition

to fix upon (by agreement or appointment); by implication, to meet (at a stated time), to summon (to trial), to direct (in a certain quarter or position), to engage (for marriage)

KJV renders: agree,(maxke an) appoint(-ment, a time), assemble (selves), betroth, gather (selves, together), meet (together), set (a time).
