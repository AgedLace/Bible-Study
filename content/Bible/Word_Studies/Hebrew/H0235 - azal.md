# H0235 — אָזַל

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| אָזַל | ʼâzal | aw-zal' | to go away |

## Definition

to go away, hence, to disappear

KJV renders: fail, gad about, go to and fro (but in Ezekiel 27:19 the word is rendered by many 'from Uzal,' by others 'yarn'), be gone (spent).
