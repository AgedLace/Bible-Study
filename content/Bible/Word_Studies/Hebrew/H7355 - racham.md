# H7355 — רָחַם

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| רָחַם | râcham | raw-kham' | to fondle |

## Definition

to fondle; by implication, to love, especially to compassionate

KJV renders: have compassion (on, upon), love, (find, have, obtain, shew) mercy(-iful, on, upon), (have) pity, Ruhamah, surely.
