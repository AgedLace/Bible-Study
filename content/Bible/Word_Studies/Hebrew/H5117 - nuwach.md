# H5117 — נוּחַ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| נוּחַ | nûwach | noo'-akh | to rest |

## Definition

to rest, i.e. settle down; used in a great variety of applications, literal and figurative, intransitive, transitive and causative (to dwell, stay, let fall, place, let alone, withdraw, give comfort, etc.)

KJV renders: cease, be confederate, lay, let down, (be) quiet, remain, (cause to, be at, give, have, make to) rest, set down. Compare H3241 (יָנִים).
