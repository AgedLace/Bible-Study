# H8068 — שָׁמִיר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| שָׁמִיר | shâmîyr | shaw-meer' | a thorn |

## Definition

a thorn; also (from its keenness for scratching) a gem, probably the diamond

KJV renders: adamant (stone), brier, diamond.
