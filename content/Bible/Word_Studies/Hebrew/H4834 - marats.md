# H4834 — מָרַץ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| מָרַץ | mârats | maw-rats' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to press, i.e. (figuratively) to be pungent or vehement; to irritate

KJV renders: embolden, be forcible, grievous, sore.
