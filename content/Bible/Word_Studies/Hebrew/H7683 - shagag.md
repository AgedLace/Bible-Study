# H7683 — שָׁגַג

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| שָׁגַג | shâgag | shaw-gag' | to stray |

## Definition

to stray, i.e. (figuratively) sin (with more or less apology)

KJV renders: also for that, deceived, err, go astray, sin ignorantly.
