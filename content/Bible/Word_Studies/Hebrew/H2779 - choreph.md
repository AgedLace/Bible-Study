# H2779 — חֹרֶף

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| חֹרֶף | chôreph | kho'-ref | properly |

## Definition

properly, the crop gathered, i.e. (by implication) the autumn (and winter) season; figuratively, ripeness of age

KJV renders: cold, winter (-house), youth.
