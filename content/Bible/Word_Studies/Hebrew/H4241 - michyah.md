# H4241 — מִחְיָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| מִחְיָה | michyâh | mikh-yaw' | preservation of life |

## Definition

preservation of life; hence, sustenance; also the live flesh, i.e. the quick

KJV renders: preserve life, quick, recover selves, reviving, sustenance, victuals.
