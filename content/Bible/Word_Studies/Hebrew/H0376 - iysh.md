# H0376 — אִישׁ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| אִישׁ | ʼîysh | eesh | a man as an individual or a male person |

## Definition

a man as an individual or a male person; often used as an adjunct to a more definite term (and in such cases frequently not expressed in translation)

KJV renders: also, another, any (man), a certain, champion, consent, each, every (one), fellow, (foot-, husband-) man, (good-, great, mighty) man, he, high (degree), him (that is), husband, man(-kind), none, one, people, person, steward, what (man) soever, whoso(-ever), worthy. Compare H802 (אִשָּׁה).
