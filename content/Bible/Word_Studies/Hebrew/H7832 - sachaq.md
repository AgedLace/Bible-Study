# H7832 — שָׂחַק

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| שָׂחַק | sâchaq | saw-khak' | to laugh (in pleasure or detraction) |

## Definition

to laugh (in pleasure or detraction); by implication, to play

KJV renders: deride, have in derision, laugh, make merry, mock(-er), play, rejoice, (laugh to) scorn, be in (make) sport.
