# H2223 — זַרְזִיר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| זַרְזִיר | zarzîyr | zar-zeer' | properly |

## Definition

properly, tightly girt, i.e. probably a racer, or some fleet animal (as being slender in the waist)

KJV renders: greyhound.
