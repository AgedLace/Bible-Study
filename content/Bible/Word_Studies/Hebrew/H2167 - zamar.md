# H2167 — זָמַר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| זָמַר | zâmar | zaw-mar' | play upon it |

## Definition

play upon it; to make music, accompanied by the voice; hence to celebrate in song and music

KJV renders: give praise, sing forth praises, psalms.
