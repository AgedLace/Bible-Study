# H7860 — שֹׁטֵר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| שֹׁטֵר | shôṭêr | sho-tare' | properly |

## Definition

properly, a scribe, i.e. (by analogy or implication) an official superintendent or magistrate

KJV renders: officer, overseer, ruler.
