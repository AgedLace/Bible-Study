# H5749 — עוּד

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| עוּד | ʻûwd | ood | to duplicate or repeat |

## Definition

to duplicate or repeat; by implication, to protest, testify (as by reiteration); intensively, to encompass, restore (as a sort of reduplication)

KJV renders: admonish, charge, earnestly, lift up, protest, call (take) to record, relieve, rob, solemnly, stand upright, testify, give warning, (bear, call to, give, take to) witness.
