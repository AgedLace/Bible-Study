# H7114 — קָצַר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| קָצַר | qâtsar | kaw-tsar' | to dock off |

## Definition

to dock off, i.e. curtail (transitive or intransitive, literal or figurative); especially to harvest (grass or grain)

KJV renders: at all, cut down, much discouraged, grieve, harvestman, lothe, mourn, reap(-er), (be, wax) short(-en, -er), straiten, trouble, vex.
