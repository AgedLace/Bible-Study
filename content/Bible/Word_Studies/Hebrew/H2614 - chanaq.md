# H2614 — חָנַק

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| חָנַק | chânaq | khaw-nak' | to be narrow |

## Definition

to be narrow; by implication, to throttle, or (reflex.) to choke oneself to death (by a rope)

KJV renders: hang self, strangle.
