# H8055 — שָׂמַח

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| שָׂמַח | sâmach | saw-makh' | probably to brighten up |

## Definition

probably to brighten up, i.e. (figuratively) be (causatively, make) blithe or gleesome

KJV renders: cheer up, be (make) glad, (have, make) joy(-ful), be (make) merry, (cause to, make to) rejoice, very.
