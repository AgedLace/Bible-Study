# H5193 — נָטַע

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| נָטַע | nâṭaʻ | naw-tah' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to strike in, i.e. fix; specifically, to plant (literally or figuratively)

KJV renders: fastened, plant(-er).
