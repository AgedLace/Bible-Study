# H6299 — פָּדָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| פָּדָה | pâdâh | paw-daw' | to sever |

## Definition

to sever, i.e. ransom; gener. to release, preserve

KJV renders: at all, deliver, by any means, ransom, (that are to be, let be) redeem(-ed), rescue, surely.
