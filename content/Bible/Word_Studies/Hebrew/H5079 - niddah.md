# H5079 — נִדָּה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| נִדָּה | niddâh | nid-daw' | properly |

## Definition

properly, rejection; by implication, impurity, especially personal (menstruation) or moral (idolatry, incest)

KJV renders: far, filthiness, flowers, menstruous (woman), put apart, removed (woman), separation, set apart, unclean(-ness, thing, with filthiness).
