# H3034 — יָדָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| יָדָה | yâdâh | yaw-daw' | physically |

## Definition

physically, to throw (a stone, an arrow) at or away; especially to revere or worship (with extended hands); intensively, to bemoan (by wringing the hands)

KJV renders: cast (out), (make) confess(-ion), praise, shoot, (give) thank(-ful, -s, -sgiving).
