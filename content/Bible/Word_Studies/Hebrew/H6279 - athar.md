# H6279 — עָתַר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| עָתַר | ʻâthar | aw-thar' | to burn incense in worship |

## Definition

to burn incense in worship, i.e. intercede (reciprocally, listen to prayer)

KJV renders: intreat, (make) pray(-er).
