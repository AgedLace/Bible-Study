# H6437 — פָּנָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| פָּנָה | pânâh | paw-naw' | to turn |

## Definition

to turn; by implication, to face, i.e. appear, look, etc.

KJV renders: appear, at (even-) tide, behold, cast out, come on, corner, dawning, empty, go away, lie, look, mark, pass away, prepare, regard, (have) respect (to), (re-) turn (aside, away, back, face, self), right (early).
