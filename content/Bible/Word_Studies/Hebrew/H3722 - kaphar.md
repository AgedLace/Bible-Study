# H3722 — כָּפַר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| כָּפַר | kâphar | kaw-far' | to cover (specifically with bitumen) |

## Definition

to cover (specifically with bitumen); figuratively, to expiate or condone, to placate or cancel

KJV renders: appease, make (an atonement, cleanse, disannul, forgive, be merciful, pacify, pardon, purge (away), put off, (make) reconcile(-liation).
