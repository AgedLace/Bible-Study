# H2220 — זְרוֹעַ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| זְרוֹעַ | zᵉrôwaʻ | zer-o'-ah | the arm (as stretched out) |

## Definition

the arm (as stretched out), or (of animals) the foreleg; figuratively, force

KJV renders: arm, help, mighty, power, shoulder, strength.
