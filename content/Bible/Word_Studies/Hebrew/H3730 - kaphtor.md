# H3730 — כַּפְתֹּר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| כַּפְתֹּר | kaphtôr | kaf-tore' | a chaplet |

## Definition

a chaplet; but used only in an architectonic sense, i.e. the capital of acolumn, or a wreath-like button or disk on the candelabrum

KJV renders: knop, (upper) lintel.
