# H3384 — יָרָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| יָרָה | yârâh | yaw-raw' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to flow as water (i.e. to rain); transitively, to lay or throw (especially an arrow, i.e. to shoot); figuratively, to point out (as if by aiming the finger), to teach

KJV renders: () archer, cast, direct, inform, instruct, lay, shew, shoot, teach(-er,-ing), through.
