# H3975 — מְאוּרָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| מְאוּרָה | mᵉʼûwrâh | meh-oo-raw' | something lighted |

## Definition

something lighted, i.e. an aperture; by implication, a crevice or hole (of a serpent)

KJV renders: den.
