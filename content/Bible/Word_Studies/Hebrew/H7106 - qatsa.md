# H7106 — קָצַע

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| קָצַע | qâtsaʻ | kaw-tsah' | to strip off |

## Definition

to strip off, i.e. (partially) scrape; by implication, to segregate (as an angle)

KJV renders: cause to scrape, corner.
