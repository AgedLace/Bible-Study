# H3925 — לָמַד

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| לָמַד | lâmad | law-mad' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to goad, i.e. (by implication) to teach (the rod being an Oriental incentive)

KJV renders: (un-) accustomed, diligently, expert, instruct, learn, skilful, teach(-er, -ing).
