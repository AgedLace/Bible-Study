# H8454 — תּוּשִׁיָּה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| תּוּשִׁיָּה | tûwshîyâh | too-shee-yaw' | support or (by implication) ability |

## Definition

support or (by implication) ability, i.e. (direct) help, (in purpose) an undertaking, (intellectual) understanding

KJV renders: enterprise, that which (thing as it) is, substance, (sound) wisdom, working.
