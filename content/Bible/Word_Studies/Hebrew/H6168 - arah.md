# H6168 — עָרָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |

## Definition

to be (causatively, make) bare; hence, to empty, pour out, demolish

KJV renders: leave destitute, discover, empty, make naked, pour (out), rase, spread self, uncover.
