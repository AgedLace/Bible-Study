# H1396 — גָּבַר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |

| גָּבַר | gâbar | gaw-bar' | to be strong |

## Definition

to be strong; by implication, to prevail, act insolently

KJV renders: exceed, confirm, be great, be mighty, prevail, put to more (strength), strengthen, be stronger, be valiant.
