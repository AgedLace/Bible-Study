# H5526 — סָכַךְ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| סָכַךְ | çâkak | saw-kak' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to entwine as ascreen; by implication, to fence in, cover over, (figuratively) protect

KJV renders: cover, defence, defend, hedge in, join together, set, shut up.
