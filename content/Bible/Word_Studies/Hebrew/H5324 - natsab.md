# H5324 — נָצַב

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| נָצַב | nâtsab | naw-tsab' | to station |

## Definition

to station, in various applications (literally or figuratively)

KJV renders: appointed, deputy, erect, establish, Huzzah (by mistake for a proper name), lay, officer, pillar, present, rear up, set (over, up), settle, sharpen, establish, (make to) stand(-ing, still, up, upright), best state.
