# H3433 — יָשֻׁבִי לֶחֶם

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| יָשֻׁבִי לֶחֶם | Yâshubîy Lechem | yaw-shoo-bee' leh'-khem | Jashubi-Lechem |

## Definition

Jashubi-Lechem, an Israelite

KJV renders: Jashubi-lehem. (Prob. the text should be pointed יֹשְׁבֵי לֶחֶם and rendered '(they were) inhabitants of Lechem,' i.e. of Bethlehem (by contraction). Compare H3902 (לַחְמִי)).
