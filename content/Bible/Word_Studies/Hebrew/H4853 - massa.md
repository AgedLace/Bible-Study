# H4853 — מַשָּׂא

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| מַשָּׂא | massâʼ | mas-saw' | a burden |

## Definition

a burden; specifically, tribute, or (abstractly) porterage; figuratively, an utterance, chiefly adoom, especially singing; mental, desire

KJV renders: burden, carry away, prophecy, they set, song, tribute.
