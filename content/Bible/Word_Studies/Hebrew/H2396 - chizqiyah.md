# H2396 — חִזְקִיָּה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |

| חִזְקִיָּה | Chizqîyâh | khiz-kee-yaw' | Chizkijah |

## Definition

Chizkijah, a king of Judah, also the name of two other Israelites

KJV renders: Hezekiah, Hizkiah, Hizkijah. Compare H3169 (יְחִזְקִיָּה).
