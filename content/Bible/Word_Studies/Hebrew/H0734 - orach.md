| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |

| אֹרַח | ʼôrach | o'-rakh | a well-trodden road (literally or figuratively) |

## Definition

KJV renders: manner, path, race, rank, traveller, troop, (by-, high-) way.
