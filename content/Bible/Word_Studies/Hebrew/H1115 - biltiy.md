# H1115 — בִּלְתִּי

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| בִּלְתִּי | biltîy | bil-tee' | properly |

## Definition

properly, a failure of, i.e. (used only as a negative particle, usually with a prepositional prefix) not, except, without, unless, besides, because not, until, etc.

KJV renders: because un(satiable), beside, but, continual, except, from, lest, neither, no more, none, not, nothing, save, that no, without.
