# H3510 — כָּאַב

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| כָּאַב | kâʼab | kaw-ab' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to feel pain; by implication, to grieve; figuratively, to spoil

KJV renders: grieving, mar, have pain, make sad (sore), (be) sorrowful.
