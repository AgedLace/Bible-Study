| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |

| עָבַט | ʻâbaṭ | aw-bat' | to pawn |

## Definition

to pawn; causatively, to lend (on security); figuratively, to entangle

KJV renders: borrow, break (ranks), fetch (a pledge), lend, surely.
