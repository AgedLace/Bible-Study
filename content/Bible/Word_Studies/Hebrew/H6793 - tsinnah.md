# H6793 — צִנָּה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |

| צִנָּה | tsinnâh | tsin-naw' | a hook (as pointed) |

## Definition

a hook (as pointed); also a (large) shield (as if guarding by prickliness); also cold (as piercing)

KJV renders: buckler, cold, hook, shield, target.
