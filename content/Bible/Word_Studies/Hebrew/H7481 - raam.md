# H7481 — רָעַם

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| רָעַם | râʻam | raw-am' | to tumble |

## Definition

to tumble, i.e. be violently agitated; specifically, to crash (of thunder); figuratively, to irritate (with anger)

KJV renders: make to fret, roar, thunder, trouble.
