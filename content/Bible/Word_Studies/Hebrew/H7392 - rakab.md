# H7392 — רָכַב

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| רָכַב | râkab | raw-kab' | to ride (on an animal or in a vehicle) |

## Definition

to ride (on an animal or in a vehicle); causatively, to place upon (for riding or generally), to despatch

KJV renders: bring (on (horse-) back), carry, get (oneself) up, on (horse-) back, put, (cause to, make to) ride (in a chariot, on, -r), set.
