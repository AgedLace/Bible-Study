# H0441 — אַלּוּף

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| אַלּוּף | ʼallûwph | al-loof' | familiar |

## Definition

familiar; a friend, also gentle; hence, a bullock (as being tame; applied, although masculine, to a cow); and so, a chieftain (as notable, like neat cattle)

KJV renders: captain, duke, (chief) friend, governor, guide, ox.
