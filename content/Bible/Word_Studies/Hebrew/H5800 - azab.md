# H5800 — עָזַב

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| עָזַב | ʻâzab | aw-zab' | to loosen |

## Definition

to loosen, i.e. relinquish, permit, etc.

KJV renders: commit self, fail, forsake, fortify, help, leave (destitute, off), refuse, surely.
