# H5034 — נָבֵל

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| נָבֵל | nâbêl | naw-bale' | to wilt |

## Definition

to wilt; generally, to fall away, fail, faint; figuratively, to be foolish or (morally) wicked; causatively, to despise, disgrace

KJV renders: disgrace, dishounour, lightly esteem, fade (away, -ing), fall (down, -ling, off), do foolishly, come to nought, surely, make vile, wither.
