# H1644 — גָּרַשׁ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| גָּרַשׁ | gârash | gaw-rash' | to drive out from a possession |

## Definition

to drive out from a possession; especially to expatriate or divorce

KJV renders: cast up (out), divorced (woman), drive away (forth, out), expel, surely put away, trouble, thrust out.
