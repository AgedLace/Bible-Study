# H7105 — קָצִיר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| קָצִיר | qâtsîyr | kaw-tseer' | severed |

## Definition

severed, i.e. harvest (as reaped), the crop, the time, the reaper, or figuratively; also a limb (of a tree, or simply foliage)

KJV renders: bough, branch, harvest (man).
