# H6743 — צָלַח

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| צָלַח | tsâlach | tsaw-lakh' | to push forward |

## Definition

to push forward, in various senses (literal or figurative, transitive or intransitive)

KJV renders: break out, come (mightily), go over, be good, be meet, be profitable, (cause to, effect, make to, send) prosper(-ity, -ous, -ously).
