# H5795 — עֵז

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| עֵז | ʻêz | aze | a she-goat (as strong) |

## Definition

a she-goat (as strong), but masculine in plural (which also is used elliptically for goat's hair)

KJV renders: (she) goat, kid.
