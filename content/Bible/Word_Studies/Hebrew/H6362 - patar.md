# H6362 — פָּטַר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| פָּטַר | pâṭar | paw-tar' | to cleave or burst through |

## Definition

to cleave or burst through, i.e. (causatively) to emit, whether literal or figurative (gape)

KJV renders: dismiss, free, let (shoot) out, slip away.
