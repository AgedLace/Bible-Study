# H1826 — דָּמַם

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| דָּמַם | dâmam | daw-man' | to be dumb |

## Definition

to be dumb; by implication, to be astonished, to stop; also to perish

KJV renders: cease, be cut down (off), forbear, hold peace, quiet self, rest, be silent, keep (put to) silence, be (stand) still, tarry, wait.
