# H7462 — רָעָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| רָעָה | râʻâh | raw-aw' | to tend a flock |

## Definition

to tend a flock; i.e. pasture it; intransitively, to graze (literally or figuratively); generally to rule; by extension, to associate with (as a friend)

KJV renders: break, companion, keep company with, devour, eat up, evil entreat, feed, use as a friend, make friendship with, herdman, keep (sheep) (-er), pastor, shearing house, shepherd, wander, waste.
