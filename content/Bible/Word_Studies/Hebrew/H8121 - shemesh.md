# H8121 — שֶׁמֶשׁ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| שֶׁמֶשׁ | shemesh | sheh'-mesh | the sun |

## Definition

the sun; by implication, the east; figuratively, a ray, i.e. (architectural) a notched battlement

KJV renders: east side(-ward), sun (rising), west(-ward), window. See also H1053 (בֵּית שֶׁמֶשׁ).
