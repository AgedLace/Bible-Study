# H2498 — חָלַף

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| חָלַף | châlaph | khaw-laf' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to slide by, i.e. (by implication) to hasten away, pass on, spring up, pierce or change

KJV renders: abolish, alter, change, cut off, go on forward, grow up, be over, pass (away, on, through), renew, sprout, strike through.
