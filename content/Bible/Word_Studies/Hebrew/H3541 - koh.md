# H3541 — כֹּה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| כֹּה | kôh | ko | properly |

## Definition

properly, like this, i.e. by implication, (of manner) thus (or so); also (of place) here (or hither); or (of time) now

KJV renders: also, here, + hitherto, like, on the other side, so (and much), such, on that manner, (on) this (manner, side, way, way and that way), + mean while, yonder.
