# H4541 — מַסֵּכָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| מַסֵּכָה | maççêkâh | mas-say-kaw' | properly |

## Definition

properly, a pouring over, i.e. fusion of metal (especially a cast image); by implication, a libation, i.e. league; concretely a coverlet (as if poured out)

KJV renders: covering, molten (image), vail.
