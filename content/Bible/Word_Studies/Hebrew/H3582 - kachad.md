# H3582 — כָּחַד

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| כָּחַד | kâchad | kaw-khad' | to secrete |

## Definition

to secrete, by act or word; hence (intensively) to destroy

KJV renders: conceal, cut down (off), desolate, hide.
