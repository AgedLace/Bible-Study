# H7146 — קָרַחַת

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| קָרַחַת | qârachath | kaw-rakh'-ath | a bald spot (on the back of the head) |

## Definition

a bald spot (on the back of the head); figuratively, a threadbare spot (on the back side of the cloth)

KJV renders: bald head, bare within.
