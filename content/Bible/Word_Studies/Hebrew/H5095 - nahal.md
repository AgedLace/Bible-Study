# H5095 — נָהַל

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| נָהַל | nâhal | naw-hal' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to run with asparkle, i.e. flow; hence (transitively), to conduct, and (by inference) to protect, sustain

KJV renders: carry, feed, guide, lead (gently, on).
