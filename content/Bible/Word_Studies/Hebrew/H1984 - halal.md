# H1984 — הָלַל

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| הָלַל | hâlal | haw-lal' | to be clear (orig. of sound |

## Definition

to be clear (orig. of sound, but usually of color); to shine; hence, to make ashow, to boast; and thus to be (clamorously) foolish; to rave; causatively, to celebrate; also to stultify

KJV renders: (make) boast (self), celebrate, commend, (deal, make), fool(-ish, -ly), glory, give (light), be (make, feign self) mad (against), give in marriage, (sing, be worthy of) praise, rage, renowned, shine.
