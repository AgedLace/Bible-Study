| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |

## Definition

three; occasionally (ordinal) third, or (multiple) thrice

KJV renders: fork, often(-times), third, thir(-teen, -teenth), three, thrice. Compare H7991 (שָׁלִישׁ).
