# H7919 — שָׂכַל

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |

## Definition

to be (causatively, make or act) circumspect and hence, intelligent

KJV renders: consider, expert, instruct, prosper, (deal) prudent(-ly), (give) skill(-ful), have good success, teach, (have, make to) understand(-ing), wisdom, (be, behave self, consider, make) wise(-ly), guide wittingly.
