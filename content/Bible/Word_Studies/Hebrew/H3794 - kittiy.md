# H3794 — כִּתִּי

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| כִּתִּי | Kittîy | kit-tee' | a Kittite or Cypriote |

## Definition

a Kittite or Cypriote; hence, an islander in general, i.e. the Greeks or Romans on the shores opposite Palestine

KJV renders: Chittim, Kittim.
