# H0206 — אָוֶן

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| אָוֶן | ʼÂven | aw'-ven | Aven |

## Definition

Aven, the contemptuous synonym of three places, one in Coele-Syria, one in Egypt (On), and one in Palestine (Bethel)

KJV renders: Aven. See also H204 (אוֹן), H1007 (בֵּית אָוֶן).
