# H6286 — פָּאַר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| פָּאַר | pâʼar | paw-ar' | to gleam |

## Definition

to gleam, i.e. (causatively) embellish; figuratively, to boast; also to explain (i.e. make clear) oneself; to shake a tree

KJV renders: beautify, boast self, go over the boughs, glorify (self), glory, vaunt self.
