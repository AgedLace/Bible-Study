# H6544 — פָּרַע

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| פָּרַע | pâraʻ | paw-rah' | to loosen |

## Definition

to loosen; by implication, to expose, dismiss; figuratively, absolve, begin

KJV renders: avenge, avoid, bare, go back, let, (make) naked, set at nought, perish, refuse, uncover.
