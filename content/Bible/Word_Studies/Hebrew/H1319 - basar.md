# H1319 — בָּשַׂר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| בָּשַׂר | bâsar | baw-sar' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to be fresh, i.e. full (rosy, (figuratively) cheerful); to announce (glad news)

KJV renders: messenger, preach, publish, shew forth, (bear, bring, carry, preach, good, tell good) tidings.
