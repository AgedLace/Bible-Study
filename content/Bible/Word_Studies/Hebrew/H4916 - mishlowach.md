# H4916 — מִשְׁלוֹחַ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| מִשְׁלוֹחַ | mishlôwach | mish-lo'-akh | a sending out |

## Definition

a sending out, i.e. (abstractly) presentation (favorable), or seizure (unfavorable); also (concretely) a place of dismissal, or a business to be discharged

KJV renders: to lay, to put, sending (forth), to set.
