# H2421 — חָיָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| חָיָה | châyâh | khaw-yaw' | to live |

## Definition

to live, whether literally or figuratively; causatively, to revive

KJV renders: keep (leave, make) alive, certainly, give (promise) life, (let, suffer to) live, nourish up, preserve (alive), quicken, recover, repair, restore (to life), revive, ( God) save (alive, life, lives), surely, be whole.
