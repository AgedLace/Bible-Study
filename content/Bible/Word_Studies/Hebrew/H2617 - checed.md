# H2617 — חֵסֵד

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| חֵסֵד | chêçêd | kheh'-sed | kindness |

## Definition

kindness; by implication (towards God) piety; rarely (by opposition) reproof, or (subject.) beauty

KJV renders: favour, good deed(-liness, -ness), kindly, (loving-) kindness, merciful (kindness), mercy, pity, reproach, wicked thing.
