# H3528 — כְּבָר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| כְּבָר | kᵉbâr | keb-awr' | properly |

## Definition

properly, extent of time, i.e. a great while; hence, long ago, formerly, hitherto

KJV renders: already, (seeing that which), now.
