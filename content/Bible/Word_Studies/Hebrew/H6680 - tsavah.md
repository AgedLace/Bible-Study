# H6680 — צָוָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| צָוָה | tsâvâh | tsaw-vaw' | (intensively) to constitute |

## Definition

(intensively) to constitute, enjoin

KJV renders: appoint, (for-) bid, (give a) charge, (give a, give in, send with) command(-er, -ment), send a messenger, put, (set) in order.
