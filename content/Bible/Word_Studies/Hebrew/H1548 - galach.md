# H1548 — גָּלַח

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| גָּלַח | gâlach | gaw-lakh' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to be bald, i.e. (causatively) to shave; figuratively to lay waste

KJV renders: poll, shave (off).
