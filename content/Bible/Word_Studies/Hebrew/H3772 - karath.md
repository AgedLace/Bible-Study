# H3772 — כָּרַת

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| כָּרַת | kârath | kaw-rath' | to cut (off |

## Definition

to cut (off, down or asunder); by implication, to destroy or consume; specifically, to covenant (i.e. make an alliance or bargain, originally by cutting flesh and passing between the pieces)

KJV renders: be chewed, be con-(feder-) ate, covenant, cut (down, off), destroy, fail, feller, be freed, hew (down), make a league (covenant), lose, perish, utterly, want.
