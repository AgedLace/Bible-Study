# H8598 — תַּפּוּחַ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| תַּפּוּחַ | tappûwach | tap-poo'-akh | an apple (from its fragrance) |

## Definition

an apple (from its fragrance), i.e. the fruit or the tree (probably includ. others of the pome order, as the quince, the orange, etc.)

KJV renders: apple (tree). See also H1054 (בֵּית תַּפּוּחַ).
