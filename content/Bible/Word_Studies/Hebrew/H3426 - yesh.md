# H3426 — יֵשׁ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| יֵשׁ | yêsh | yaysh | there is or are (or any other form of the verb to be |

## Definition

there is or are (or any other form of the verb to be, as may suit the connection)

KJV renders: (there) are, (he, it, shall, there, there may, there shall, there should) be, thou do, had, hast, (which) hath, (I, shalt, that) have, (he, it, there) is, substance, it (there) was, (there) were, ye will, thou wilt, wouldest.
