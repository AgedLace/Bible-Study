# H5341 — נָצַר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| נָצַר | nâtsar | naw-tsar' | to guard |

## Definition

to guard, in a good sense (to protect, maintain, obey, etc.) or a bad one (to conceal, etc.)

KJV renders: besieged, hidden thing, keep(-er, -ing), monument, observe, preserve(-r), subtil, watcher(-man).
