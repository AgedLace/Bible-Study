# H5337 — נָצַל

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| נָצַל | nâtsal | naw-tsal' | to snatch away |

## Definition

to snatch away, whether in a good or a bad sense

KJV renders: at all, defend, deliver (self), escape, without fail, part, pluck, preserve, recover, rescue, rid, save, spoil, strip, surely, take (out).
