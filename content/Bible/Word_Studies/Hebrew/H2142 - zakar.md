# H2142 — זָכַר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| זָכַר | zâkar | zaw-kar' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to mark (so as to be recognized), i.e. to remember; by implication, to mention;  to be male

KJV renders: burn (incense), earnestly, be male, (make) mention (of), be mindful, recount, record(-er), remember, make to be remembered, bring (call, come, keep, put) to (in) remembrance, still, think on, well.
