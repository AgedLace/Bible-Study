# H1817 — דֶּלֶת

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| דֶּלֶת | deleth | deh'-leth | something swinging |

## Definition

something swinging, i.e. the valve of adoor

KJV renders: door (two-leaved), gate, leaf, lid. (Psalm 141:3).
