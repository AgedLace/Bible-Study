# H6452 — פָּסַח

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| פָּסַח | pâçach | paw-sakh' | to hop |

## Definition

to hop, i.e. (figuratively) skip over (or spare); by implication, to hesitate; also (literally) to limp, to dance

KJV renders: halt, become lame, leap, pass over.
