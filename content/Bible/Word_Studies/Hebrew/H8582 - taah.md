# H8582 — תָּעָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| תָּעָה | tâʻâh | taw-aw' | to vacillate |

## Definition

to vacillate, i.e. reel or stray (literally or figuratively); also causative of both

KJV renders: (cause to) go astray, deceive, dissemble, (cause to, make to) err, pant, seduce, (make to) stagger, (cause to) wander, be out of the way.
