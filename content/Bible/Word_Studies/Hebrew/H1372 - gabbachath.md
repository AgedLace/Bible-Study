# H1372 — גַּבַּחַת

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| גַּבַּחַת | gabbachath | gab-bakh'-ath | baldness in the forehead |

## Definition

baldness in the forehead; by analogy, a bare spot on the right side of cloth

KJV renders: bald forehead, without.
