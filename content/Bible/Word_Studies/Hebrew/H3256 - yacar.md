# H3256 — יָסַר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| יָסַר | yâçar | yaw-sar' | to chastise |

## Definition

to chastise, literally (with blows) or figuratively (with words); hence, to instruct

KJV renders: bind, chasten, chastise, correct, instruct, punish, reform, reprove, sore, teach.
