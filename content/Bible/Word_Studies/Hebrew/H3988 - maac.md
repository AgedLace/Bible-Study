# H3988 — מָאַס

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| מָאַס | mâʼaç | maw-as' | to spurn |

## Definition

to spurn; also (intransitively) to disappear

KJV renders: abhor, cast away (off), contemn, despise, disdain, (become) loathe(some), melt away, refuse, reject, reprobate, utterly, vile person.
