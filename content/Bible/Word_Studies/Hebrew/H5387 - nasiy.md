# H5387 — נָשִׂיא

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| נָשִׂיא | nâsîyʼ | naw-see' | properly |

## Definition

properly, an exalted one, i.e. a king or sheik; also a rising mist

KJV renders: captain, chief, cloud, governor, prince, ruler, vapour.
