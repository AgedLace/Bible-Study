# H5352 — נָקָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| נָקָה | nâqâh | naw-kaw' | to be (or make) clean (literally or figuratively) |

## Definition

to be (or make) clean (literally or figuratively); by implication (in an adverse sense) to be bare, i.e. extirpated

KJV renders: acquit at all, altogether, be blameless, cleanse, (be) clear(-ing), cut off, be desolate, be free, be (hold) guiltless, be (hold) innocent, by no means, be quit, be (leave) unpunished, utterly, wholly.
