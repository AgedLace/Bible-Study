# H0898 — בָּגַד

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| בָּגַד | bâgad | baw-gad' | to cover (with a garment) |

## Definition

to cover (with a garment); figuratively, to act covertly; by implication, to pillage

KJV renders: deal deceitfully (treacherously, unfaithfully), offend, transgress(-or), (depart), treacherous (dealer, -ly, man), unfaithful(-ly, man), very.
