# H3665 — כָּנַע

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| כָּנַע | kânaʻ | kaw-nah' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to bend the knee; hence, to humiliate, vanquish

KJV renders: bring down (low), into subjection, under, humble (self), subdue.
