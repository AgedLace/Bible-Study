# H3289 — יָעַץ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| יָעַץ | yâʻats | yaw-ats' | to advise |

## Definition

to advise; reflexively, to deliberate or resolve

KJV renders: advertise, take advise, advise (well), consult, (give, take) counsel(-lor), determine, devise, guide, purpose.
