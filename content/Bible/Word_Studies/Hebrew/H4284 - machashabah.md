# H4284 — מַחֲשָׁבָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| מַחֲשָׁבָה | machăshâbâh | makh-ash-aw-baw' | a contrivance |

## Definition

a contrivance, i.e. (concretely) a texture, machine, or (abstractly) intention, plan (whether bad, a plot; or good, advice)

KJV renders: cunning (work), curious work, device(-sed), imagination, invented, means, purpose, thought.
