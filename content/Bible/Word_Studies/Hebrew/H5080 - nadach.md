# H5080 — נָדַח

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| נָדַח | nâdach | naw-dakh' | to push off |

## Definition

to push off; used in a great variety of applications, literally and figuratively (to expel, mislead, strike, inflict, etc.)

KJV renders: banish, bring, cast down (out), chase, compel, draw away, drive (away, out, quite), fetch a stroke, force, go away, outcast, thrust away (out), withdraw.
