# H8537 — תֹּם

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| תֹּם | tôm | tome | completeness |

## Definition

completeness; figuratively, prosperity; usually (morally) innocence

KJV renders: full, integrity, perfect(-ion), simplicity, upright(-ly, -ness), at a venture. See H8550 (תֻּמִּים).
