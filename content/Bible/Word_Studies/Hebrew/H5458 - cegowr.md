# H5458 — סְגוֹר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| סְגוֹר | çᵉgôwr | seg-ore' | properly |

## Definition

properly, shut up, i.e. the breast (as enclosing the heart); also gold (as generally shut up safely)

KJV renders: caul, gold.
