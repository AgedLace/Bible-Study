# H5414 — נָתַן

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| נָתַן | nâthan | naw-than' | to give |

## Definition

to give, used with greatest latitude of application (put, make, etc.)

KJV renders: add, apply, appoint, ascribe, assign, avenge, be (healed), bestow, bring (forth, hither), cast, cause, charge, come, commit, consider, count, cry, deliver (up), direct, distribute, do, doubtless, without fail, fasten, frame, get, give (forth, over, up), grant, hang (up), have, indeed, lay (unto charge, up), (give) leave, lend, let (out), lie, lift up, make, O that, occupy, offer, ordain, pay, perform, place, pour, print, pull, put (forth), recompense, render, requite, restore, send (out), set (forth), shew, shoot forth (up), sing, slander, strike, (sub-) mit, suffer, surely, take, thrust, trade, turn, utter, weep, willingly, withdraw, would (to) God, yield.
