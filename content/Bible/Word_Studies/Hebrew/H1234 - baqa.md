# H1234 — בָּקַע

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| בָּקַע | bâqaʻ | baw-kah' | to cleave |

## Definition

to cleave; generally, to rend, break, rip or open

KJV renders: make a breach, break forth (into, out, in pieces, through, up), be ready to burst, cleave (asunder), cut out, divide, hatch, rend (asunder), rip up, tear, win.
