# H5329 — נָצַח

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| נָצַח | nâtsach | naw-tsakh' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to glitter from afar, i.e. to be eminent (as a superintendent, especially of the Temple services and its music); to be permanent

KJV renders: excel, chief musician (singer), oversee(-r), set forward.
