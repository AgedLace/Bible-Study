# H6536 — פָּרַס

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| פָּרַס | pâraç | paw-ras' | to break in pieces |

## Definition

to break in pieces, i.e. (usually without violence) to split, distribute

KJV renders: deal, divide, have hoofs, part, tear.
