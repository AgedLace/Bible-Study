# H8311 — שָׂרַע

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| שָׂרַע | sâraʻ | saw-rah' | to prolong |

## Definition

to prolong, i.e. (reflex) be deformed by excess of members

KJV renders: stretch out self, (have any) superfluous thing.
