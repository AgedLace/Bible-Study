# H6584 — פָּשַׁט

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| פָּשַׁט | pâshaṭ | paw-shat' | to spread out (i.e. deploy in hostile array) |

## Definition

to spread out (i.e. deploy in hostile array); by analogy, to strip (i.e. unclothe, plunder, flay, etc.)

KJV renders: fall upon, flay, invade, make an invasion, pull off, put off, make a road, run upon, rush, set, spoil, spread selves (abroad), strip (off, self).
