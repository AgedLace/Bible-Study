# H0238 — אָזַן

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| אָזַן | ʼâzan | aw-zan' | to broaden out the ear (with the hand) |

## Definition

to broaden out the ear (with the hand), i.e. (by implication) to listen

KJV renders: give (perceive by the) ear, hear(-ken). See H239 (אָזַן).
