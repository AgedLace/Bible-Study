# H0176 — אוֹ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |

| אוֹ | ʼôw | o | desire (and so probably in Proverbs 31:4) |

## Definition

desire (and so probably in Proverbs 31:4); hence (by way of alternative) or, also if

KJV renders: also, and, either, if, at the least, nor, or, otherwise, then, whether.
