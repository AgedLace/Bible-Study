# H0095 — אֲגוֹרָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| אֲגוֹרָה | ʼăgôwrâh | ag-o-raw' | properly |

## Definition

properly, something gathered, i.e. perhaps a grain or berry; used only of a small (silver) coin

KJV renders: piece (of) silver.
