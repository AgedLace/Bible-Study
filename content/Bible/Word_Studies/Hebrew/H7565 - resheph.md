# H7565 — רֶשֶׁף

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| רֶשֶׁף | resheph | reh'-shef | a live coal |

## Definition

a live coal; by analogy lightning; figuratively, an arrow, (as flashing through the air); specifically, fever

KJV renders: arrow, (burning) coal, burning heat, spark, hot thunderbolt.
