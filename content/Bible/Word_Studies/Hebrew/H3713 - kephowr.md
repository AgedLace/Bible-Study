# H3713 — כְּפוֹר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| כְּפוֹר | kᵉphôwr | kef-ore' | properly |

## Definition

properly, a cover, i.e. (by implication) a tankard (or covered goblet); also white frost (as covering the ground)

KJV renders: bason, hoar(-y) frost.
