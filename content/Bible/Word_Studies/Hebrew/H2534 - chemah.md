# H2534 — חֵמָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| חֵמָה | chêmâh | khay-maw' | heat |

## Definition

heat; figuratively, anger, poison (from its fever)

KJV renders: anger, bottles, hot displeasure, furious(-ly, -ry), heat, indignation, poison, rage, wrath(-ful). See H2529 (חֶמְאָה).
