# H3415 — יָרַע

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| יָרַע | yâraʻ | yaw-rah' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to be broken up (with any violent action) i.e. (figuratively) to fear

KJV renders: be grevious (only Isaiah 15:4; the rest belong to H7489 (רָעַע)).
