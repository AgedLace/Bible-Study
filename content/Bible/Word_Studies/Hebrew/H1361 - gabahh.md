# H1361 — גָּבַהּ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| גָּבַהּ | gâbahh | gaw-bah' | to soar |

## Definition

to soar, i.e. be lofty; figuratively, to be haughty

KJV renders: exalt, be haughty, be (make) high(-er), lift up, mount up, be proud, raise up great height, upward.
