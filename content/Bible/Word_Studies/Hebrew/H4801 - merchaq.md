# H4801 — מֶרְחָק

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| מֶרְחָק | merchâq | mer-khawk' | remoteness |

## Definition

remoteness, i.e. (concretely) a distant place; often (adverbially) from afar

KJV renders: (a-, dwell in, very) far (country, off). See also H1023 (בֵּית הַמֶּרְחָק).
