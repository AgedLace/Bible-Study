# H2187 — זָנַק

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| זָנַק | zânaq | zaw-nak' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to draw together the feet (as an animal about to dart upon its prey), i.e. to spring forward

KJV renders: leap.
