# H0833 — אָשַׁר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| אָשַׁר | ʼâshar | aw-shar' | to be straight (used in the widest sense |

## Definition

to be straight (used in the widest sense, especially to be level, right, happy); figuratively, to go forward, be honest, proper

KJV renders: (call, be) bless(-ed, happy), go, guide, lead, relieve.
