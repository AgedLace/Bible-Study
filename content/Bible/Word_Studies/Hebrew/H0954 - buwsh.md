# H0954 — בּוּשׁ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| בּוּשׁ | bûwsh | boosh | properly |

## Definition

properly, to pale, i.e. by implication to be ashamed; also (by implication) to be disappointed or delayed

KJV renders: (be, make, bring to, cause, put to, with, a-) shamed(-d), be (put to) confounded(-fusion), become dry, delay, be long.
