# H4301 — מַטְמוֹן

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| מַטְמוֹן | maṭmôwn | mat-mone' | a secret storehouse |

## Definition

a secret storehouse; hence, a secreted valuable (buried); generally money

KJV renders: hidden riches, (hid) treasure(-s).
