# H2088 — זֶה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| זֶה | zeh | zeh | the masculine demonstrative pronoun |

## Definition

the masculine demonstrative pronoun, this or that

KJV renders: he, hence, here, it(-self), now, of him, the one...the other, than the other, ( out of) the (self) same, such (a one) that, these, this (hath, man), on this side...on that side, thus, very, which. Compare H2063 (זֹאת), H2090 (זֹה), H2097 (זוֹ), H2098 (זוּ).
