# H3557 — כּוּל

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| כּוּל | kûwl | kool | properly |

## Definition

properly, to keep in; hence, to measure; figuratively, to maintain (in various senses)

KJV renders: (be able to, can) abide, bear, comprehend, contain, feed, forbearing, guide, hold(-ing in), nourish(-er), be present, make provision, receive, sustain, provide sustenance (victuals).
