# H7602 — שָׁאַף

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| שָׁאַף | shâʼaph | shaw-af' | to inhale eagerly |

## Definition

to inhale eagerly; figuratively, to cover; by implication, to be angry; also to hasten

KJV renders: desire (earnestly), devour, haste, pant, snuff up, swallow up.
