# H0669 — אֶפְרַיִם

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |

| אֶפְרַיִם | ʼEphrayim | ef-rah'-yim | Ephrajim |

## Definition

Ephrajim, a son of Joseph; also the tribe descended from him, and its territory

KJV renders: Ephraim, Ephraimites.
