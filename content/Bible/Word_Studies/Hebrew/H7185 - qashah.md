# H7185 — קָשָׁה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| קָשָׁה | qâshâh | kaw-shaw' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to be dense, i.e. tough or severe (in various applications)

KJV renders: be cruel, be fiercer, make grievous, be ((ask a), be in, have, seem, would) hard(-en, (labour), -ly, thing), be sore, (be, make) stiff(-en, (-necked)).
