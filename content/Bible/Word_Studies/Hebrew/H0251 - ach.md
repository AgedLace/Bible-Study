| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |

## Definition

a brother (used in the widest sense of literal relationship and metaphorical affinity or resemblance (like father))

KJV renders: another, brother(-ly); kindred, like, other. Compare also the proper names beginning with 'Ah-' or 'Ahi-'.
