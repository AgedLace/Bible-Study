# H8173 — שָׁעַע

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| שָׁעַע | shâʻaʻ | shaw-ah' | (in a good acceptation) to look upon (with complacency) |

## Definition

(in a good acceptation) to look upon (with complacency), i.e. fondle, please or amuse (self); (in a bad one) to look about (in dismay), i.e. stare

KJV renders: cry (out) (by confusion with H7768 (שָׁוַע)), dandle, delight (self), play, shut.
