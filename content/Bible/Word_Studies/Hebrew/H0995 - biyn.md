# H0995 — בִּין

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| בִּין | bîyn | bene | to separate mentally (or distinguish) |

## Definition

to separate mentally (or distinguish), i.e.(generally) understand

KJV renders: attend, consider, be cunning, diligently, direct, discern, eloquent, feel, inform, instruct, have intelligence, know, look well to, mark, perceive, be prudent, regard, (can) skill(-full), teach, think, (cause, make to, get, give, have) understand(-ing), view, (deal) wise(-ly, man).
