# H3707 — כַּעַס

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| כַּעַס | kaʻaç | kaw-as' | to trouble |

## Definition

to trouble; by implication, to grieve, rage, be indignant

KJV renders: be angry, be grieved, take indignation, provoke (to anger, unto wrath), have sorrow, vex, be wroth.
