# H8643 — תְּרוּעָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| תְּרוּעָה | tᵉrûwʻâh | ter-oo-aw' | clamor |

## Definition

clamor, i.e. acclamation of joy or a battle-cry; especially clangorof trumpets, as an alarum

KJV renders: alarm, blow(-ing) (of, the) (trumpets), joy, jubile, loud noise, rejoicing, shout(-ing), (high, joyful) sound(-ing).
