# H6256 — עֵת

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| עֵת | ʻêth | ayth | time |

## Definition

time, especially (adverb with preposition) now, when, etc.

KJV renders: after, (al-) ways, certain, continually, evening, long, (due) season, so (long) as, (even-, evening-, noon-) tide, (meal-), what) time, when.
