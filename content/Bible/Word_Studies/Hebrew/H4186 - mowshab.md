# H4186 — מוֹשָׁב

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| מוֹשָׁב | môwshâb | mo-shawb' | a seat |

## Definition

a seat; figuratively, a site; abstractly, a session; by extension an abode (the place or the time); by implication, population

KJV renders: assembly, dwell in, dwelling(-place), wherein (that) dwelt (in), inhabited place, seat, sitting, situation, sojourning.
