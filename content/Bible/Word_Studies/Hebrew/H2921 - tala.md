# H2921 — טָלָא

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| טָלָא | ṭâlâʼ | taw-law' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to cover with pieces; i.e. (by implication) to spot or variegate (as tapestry)

KJV renders: clouted, with divers colours, spotted.
