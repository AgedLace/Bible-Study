# H4941 — מִשְׁפָּט

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| מִשְׁפָּט | mishpâṭ | mish-pawt' | properly |

## Definition

properly, a verdict (favorable or unfavorable) pronounced judicially, especially a sentence or formal decree (human or (participant's) divine law, individual or collective), including the act, the place, the suit, the crime, and the penalty; abstractly, justice, including a participant's right or privilege (statutory or customary), or even a style

KJV renders: adversary, ceremony, charge, crime, custom, desert, determination, discretion, disposing, due, fashion, form, to be judged, judgment, just(-ice, -ly), (manner of) law(-ful), manner, measure, (due) order, ordinance, right, sentence, usest, worthy, wrong.
