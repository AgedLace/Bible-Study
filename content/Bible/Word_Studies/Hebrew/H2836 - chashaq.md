# H2836 — חָשַׁק

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| חָשַׁק | châshaq | khaw-shak' | to cling |

## Definition

to cling, i.e. join, (figuratively) to love, delight in; elliptically; to deliver

KJV renders: have a delight, (have a) desire, fillet, long, set (in) love.
