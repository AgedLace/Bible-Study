# H1641 — גָּרַר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| גָּרַר | gârar | gaw-rar' | to drag off roughly |

## Definition

to drag off roughly; by implication, to bring up the cud (i.e. ruminate); by analogy, to saw

KJV renders: catch, chew, continuing, destroy, saw.
