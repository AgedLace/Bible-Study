# H3533 — כָּבַשׁ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| כָּבַשׁ | kâbash | kaw-bash' | to tread down |

## Definition

to tread down; hence, negatively, to disregard; positively, to conquer, subjugate, violate

KJV renders: bring into bondage, force, keep under, subdue, bring into subjection.
