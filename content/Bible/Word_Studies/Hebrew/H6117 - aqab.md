# H6117 — עָקַב

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| עָקַב | ʻâqab | aw-kab' | to seize by the heel |

## Definition

to seize by the heel; figuratively, to circumvent (as if tripping up the heels); also to restrain (as if holding by the heel)

KJV renders: take by the heel, stay, supplant, utterly.
