# H5183 — נַחַת

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| נַחַת | Nachath | nakh'-ath | a descent |

## Definition

a descent, i.e. imposition, unfavorable (punishment) or favorable (food); restfulness

KJV renders: lighting down, quiet(-ness), to rest, be set on.
