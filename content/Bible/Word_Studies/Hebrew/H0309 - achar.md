# H0309 — אָחַר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| אָחַר | ʼâchar | aw-khar' | to loiter (i.e. be behind) |

## Definition

to loiter (i.e. be behind); by implication to procrastinate

KJV renders: continue, defer, delay, hinder, be late (slack), stay (there), tarry (longer).
