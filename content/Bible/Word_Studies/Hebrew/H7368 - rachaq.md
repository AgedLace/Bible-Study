# H7368 — רָחַק

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| רָחַק | râchaq | raw-khak' | to widen (in any direction) |

## Definition

to widen (in any direction), i.e. (intransitively) recede or (transitively) remove (literally or figuratively, of place or relation)

KJV renders: (a-, be, cast, drive, get, go, keep (self), put, remove, be too, (wander), withdraw) far (away, off), loose, refrain, very, (be) a good way (off).
