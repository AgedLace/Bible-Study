# H2196 — זָעַף

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| זָעַף | zâʻaph | zaw-af' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to boil up, i.e. (figuratively) to be peevish or angry

KJV renders: fret, sad, worse liking, be wroth.
