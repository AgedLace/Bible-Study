# H7121 — קָרָא

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| קָרָא | qârâʼ | kaw-raw' | to call out to (i.e. properly |

## Definition

to call out to (i.e. properly, address by name, but used in a wide variety of applications)

KJV renders: bewray (self), that are bidden, call (for, forth, self, upon), cry (unto), (be) famous, guest, invite, mention, (give) name, preach, (make) proclaim(-ation), pronounce, publish, read, renowned, say.
