# H6470 — פָּעַם

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| פָּעַם | pâʻam | paw-am' | to tap |

## Definition

to tap, i.e. beat regularly; hence (generally) to impel or agitate

KJV renders: move, trouble.
