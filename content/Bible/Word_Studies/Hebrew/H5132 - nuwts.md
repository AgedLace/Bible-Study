# H5132 — נוּץ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| נוּץ | nûwts | noots | properly |

## Definition

properly, to flash; hence, to blossom (from the brilliancy of color); also, to fly away (from the quickness of motion)

KJV renders: flee away, bud (forth).
