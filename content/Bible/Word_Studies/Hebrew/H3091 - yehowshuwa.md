# H3091 — יְהוֹשׁוּעַ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| יְהוֹשׁוּעַ | Yᵉhôwshûwaʻ | yeh-ho-shoo'-ah | Jehoshua (i.e. Joshua) |

## Definition

Jehoshua (i.e. Joshua), the Jewish leader

KJV renders: Jehoshua, Jehoshuah, Joshua. Compare H1954 (הוֹשֵׁעַ), H3442 (יֵשׁוּעַ).
