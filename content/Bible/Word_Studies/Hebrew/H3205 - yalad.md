# H3205 — יָלַד

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| יָלַד | yâlad | yaw-lad' | to bear young |

## Definition

to bear young; causatively, to beget; medically, to act as midwife; specifically, to show lineage

KJV renders: bear, beget, birth(-day), born, (make to) bring forth (children, young), bring up, calve, child, come, be delivered (of a child), time of delivery, gender, hatch, labour, (do the office of a) midwife, declare pedigrees, be the son of, (woman in, woman that) travail(-eth, -ing woman).
