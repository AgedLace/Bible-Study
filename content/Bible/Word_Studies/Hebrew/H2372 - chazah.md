# H2372 — חָזָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| חָזָה | châzâh | khaw-zaw' | to gaze at |

## Definition

to gaze at; mentally to perceive, contemplate (with pleasure); specifically, to have avision of

KJV renders: behold, look, prophesy, provide, see.
