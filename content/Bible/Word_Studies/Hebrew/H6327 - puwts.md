# H6327 — פּוּץ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| פּוּץ | pûwts | poots | to dash in pieces |

## Definition

to dash in pieces, literally or figuratively (especially to disperse)

KJV renders: break (dash, shake) in (to) pieces, cast (abroad), disperse (selves), drive, retire, scatter (abroad), spread abroad.
