# H1802 — דָּלָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| דָּלָה | dâlâh | daw-law' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to dangle, i.e. to let down abucket (for drawing out water); figuratively, to deliver

KJV renders: draw (out), enough, lift up.
