# H4166 — מוּצָקָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| מוּצָקָה | mûwtsâqâh | moo-tsaw-kaw' | properly |

## Definition

properly, something poured out, i.e. a casting (of metal); by implication, a tube (as cast)

KJV renders: when it was cast, pipe.
