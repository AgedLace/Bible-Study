# H3637 — כָּלַם

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| כָּלַם | kâlam | kaw-lawm' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to wound; but only figuratively, to taunt or insult

KJV renders: be (make) ashamed, blush, be confounded, be put to confusion, hurt, reproach, (do, put to) shame.
