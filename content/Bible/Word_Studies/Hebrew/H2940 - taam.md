# H2940 — טַעַם

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| טַעַם | ṭaʻam | tah'-am | properly |

## Definition

properly, a taste, i.e. (figuratively) perception; by implication, intelligence; transitively, a mandate

KJV renders: advice, behaviour, decree, discretion, judgment, reason, taste, understanding.
