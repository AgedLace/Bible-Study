# H3605 — כֹּל

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| כֹּל | kôl | kole | properly |

## Definition

properly, the whole; hence, all, any or every (in the singular only, but often in a plural sense)

KJV renders: (in) all (manner, (ye)), altogether, any (manner), enough, every (one, place, thing), howsoever, as many as, (no-) thing, ought, whatsoever, (the) whole, whoso(-ever).
