| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |

| קָצַף | qâtsaph | kaw-tsaf' | to crack off |

## Definition

to crack off, i.e. (figuratively) burst out in rage

KJV renders: (be) anger(-ry), displease, fret self, (provoke to) wrath (come), be wroth.
