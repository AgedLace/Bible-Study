# H2370 — חֲזָא

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |

| חֲזָא | chăzâʼ | khaz-aw' | to gaze upon |

## Definition

to gaze upon; mentally to dream, be usual (i.e. seem)

KJV renders: behold, have (a dream), see, be wont.
