# H8548 — תָּמִיד

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| תָּמִיד | tâmîyd | taw-meed' | properly |

## Definition

properly, continuance (as indefinite extension); but used only (attributively as adjective) constant (or adverbially, constantly); ellipt. the regular (daily) sacrifice

KJV renders: alway(-s), continual (employment, -ly), daily, (n-)ever(-more), perpetual.
