# H3418 — יֶרֶק

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| יֶרֶק | yereq | yeh'-rek | properly |

## Definition

properly, pallor, i.e. hence, the yellowish green of young and sickly vegetation; concretely, verdure, i.e. grass or vegetation

KJV renders: grass, green (thing).
