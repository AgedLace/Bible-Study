# H1158 — בָּעָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| בָּעָה | bâʻâh | baw-aw' | to gush over |

## Definition

to gush over, i.e. to swell; (figuratively) to desire earnestly; by implication to ask

KJV renders: cause, inquire, seek up, swell out.
