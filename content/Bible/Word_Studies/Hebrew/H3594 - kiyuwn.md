# H3594 — כִּיּוּן

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| כִּיּוּן | Kîyûwn | kee-yoon' | properly |

## Definition

properly, a statue, i.e. idol; but used (by euphemism) forsome heathen deity (perhaps corresponding to Priapus or Baal-peor)

KJV renders: Chiun.
