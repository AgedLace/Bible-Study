# H7523 — רָצַח

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| רָצַח | râtsach | raw-tsakh' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to dash in pieces, i.e. kill (a human being), especially to murder

KJV renders: put to death, kill, (man-) slay(-er), murder(-er).
