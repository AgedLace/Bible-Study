# H7198 — קֶשֶׁת

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| קֶשֶׁת | qesheth | keh'-sheth | a bow |

## Definition

a bow, forshooting (hence, figuratively, strength) or the iris

KJV renders: arch(-er), arrow, bow(-man, -shot).
