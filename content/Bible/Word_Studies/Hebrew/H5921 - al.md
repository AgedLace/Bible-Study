# H5921 — עַל

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| עַל | ʻal | al | above |

## Definition

above, over, upon, or against (yet always in this last relation with a downward aspect) in a great variety of applications

KJV renders: above, according to(-ly), after, (as) against, among, and, as, at, because of, beside (the rest of), between, beyond the time, both and, by (reason of), had the charge of, concerning for, in (that), (forth, out) of, (from) (off), (up-) on, over, than, through(-out), to, touching, with.
