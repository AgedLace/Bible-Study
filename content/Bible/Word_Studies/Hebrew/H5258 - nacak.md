# H5258 — נָסַךְ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| נָסַךְ | nâçak | naw-sak' | to pour out |

## Definition

to pour out, especially a libation, or to cast (metal); by analogy, to anoint aking

KJV renders: cover, melt, offer, (cause to) pour (out), set (up).
