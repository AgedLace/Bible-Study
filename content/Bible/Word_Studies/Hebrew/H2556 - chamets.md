# H2556 — חָמֵץ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| חָמֵץ | châmêts | khaw-mates' | to be pungent |

## Definition

to be pungent; i.e. in taste (sour, i.e. literally fermented, or figuratively, harsh), in color (dazzling)

KJV renders: cruel (man), dyed, be grieved, leavened.
