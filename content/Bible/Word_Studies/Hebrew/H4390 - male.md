# H4390 — מָלֵא

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| מָלֵא | mâlêʼ | maw-lay' | to fill or (intransitively) be full of |

## Definition

to fill or (intransitively) be full of, in a wide application (literally and figuratively)

KJV renders: accomplish, confirm, consecrate, be at an end, be expired, be fenced, fill, fulfil, (be, become, draw, give in, go) full(-ly, -ly set, tale), (over-) flow, fulness, furnish, gather (selves, together), presume, replenish, satisfy, set, space, take a (hand-) full, have wholly.
