# H2560 — חָמַר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| חָמַר | châmar | khaw-mar' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to boil up; hence, to ferment (with scum); to glow (with redness); to smear with pitch

KJV renders: daub, befoul, be red, trouble.
