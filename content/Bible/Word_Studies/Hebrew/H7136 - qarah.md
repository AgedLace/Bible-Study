# H7136 — קָרָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| קָרָה | qârâh | kaw-raw' | to light upon (chiefly by accident) |

## Definition

to light upon (chiefly by accident); causatively, to bring about; specifically, to impose timbers (for roof or floor)

KJV renders: appoint, lay (make) beams, befall, bring, come (to pass unto), floor, (hap) was, happen (unto), meet, send good speed.
