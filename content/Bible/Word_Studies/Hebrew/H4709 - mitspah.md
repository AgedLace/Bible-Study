# H4709 — מִצְפָּה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| מִצְפָּה | Mitspâh | mits-paw' | Mitspah |

## Definition

Mitspah, the name of two places in Palestine

KJV renders: Mitspah. (This seems rather to be only an orthographic variation of H4708 (מִצְפֶּה) when 'in pause'.)
