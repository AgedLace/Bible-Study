# H8193 — שָׂפָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| שָׂפָה | sâphâh | saw-faw' | the lip (as a natural boundary) |

## Definition

the lip (as a natural boundary); by implication, language; by analogy, a margin (of a vessel, water, cloth, etc.)

KJV renders: band, bank, binding, border, brim, brink, edge, language, lip, prating, (sea-)shore, side, speech, talk, (vain) words.
