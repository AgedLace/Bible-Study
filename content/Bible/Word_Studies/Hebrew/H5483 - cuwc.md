# H5483 — סוּס

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| סוּס | çûwç | soos | a horse (as leaping) |

## Definition

a horse (as leaping); also a swallow (from its rapid flight)

KJV renders: crane, horse (-back, -hoof). Compare H6571 (פָּרָשׁ).
