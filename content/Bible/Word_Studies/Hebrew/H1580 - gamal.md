# H1580 — גָּמַל

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| גָּמַל | gâmal | gaw-mal' | to treat a person (well or ill) |

## Definition

to treat a person (well or ill), i.e. benefit or requite; by implication (of toil), to ripen, i.e. (specifically) to wean

KJV renders: bestow on, deal bountifully, do (good), recompense, requite, reward, ripen, serve, mean, yield.
