# H5062 — נָגַף

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| נָגַף | nâgaph | naw-gaf' | to push |

## Definition

to push, gore, defeat, stub (the toe), inflict (a disease)

KJV renders: beat, dash, hurt, plague, slay, smite (down), strike, stumble, surely, put to the worse.
