# H5301 — נָפַח

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| נָפַח | nâphach | naw-fakh' | to puff |

## Definition

to puff, in various applications (literally, to inflate, blow hard, scatter, kindle, expire; figuratively, to disesteem)

KJV renders: blow, breath, give up, cause to lose (life), seething, snuff.
