# H0369 — אַיִן

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| אַיִן | ʼayin | ah'-yin | a non-entity |

## Definition

a non-entity; generally used as a negative particle

KJV renders: else, except, fail, (father-) less, be gone, in(-curable), neither, never, no (where), none, nor, (any, thing), not, nothing, to nought, past, un(-searchable), well-nigh, without. Compare H370 (אַיִן).
