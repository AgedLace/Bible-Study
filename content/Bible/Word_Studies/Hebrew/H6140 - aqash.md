# H6140 — עָקַשׁ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| עָקַשׁ | ʻâqash | aw-kash' | to knot or distort |

## Definition

to knot or distort; figuratively, to pervert (act or declare perverse)

KJV renders: make crooked, (prove, that is) perverse(-rt).
