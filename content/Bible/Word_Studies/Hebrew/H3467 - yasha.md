# H3467 — יָשַׁע

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| יָשַׁע | yâshaʻ | yaw-shah' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to be open, wide or free, i.e. (by implication) to be safe; causatively, to free or succor

KJV renders: at all, avenging, defend, deliver(-er), help, preserve, rescue, be safe, bring (having) salvation, save(-iour), get victory.
