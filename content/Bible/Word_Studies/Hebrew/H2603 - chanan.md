# H2603 — חָנַן

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| חָנַן | chânan | khaw-nan' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to bend or stoop in kindness to an inferior; to favor, bestow; causatively to implore (i.e. move to favor by petition)

KJV renders: beseech, fair, (be, find, shew) favour(-able), be (deal, give, grant (gracious(-ly), intreat, (be) merciful, have (shew) mercy (on, upon), have pity upon, pray, make supplication, very.
