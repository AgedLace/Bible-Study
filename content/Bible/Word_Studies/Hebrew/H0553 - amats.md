# H0553 — אָמַץ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| אָמַץ | ʼâmats | aw-mats' | to be alert |

## Definition

to be alert, physically (on foot) or mentally (in courage)

KJV renders: confirm, be courageous (of good courage, stedfastly minded, strong, stronger), establish, fortify, harden, increase, prevail, strengthen (self), make strong (obstinate, speed).
