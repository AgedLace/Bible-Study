# H7291 — רָדַף

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| רָדַף | râdaph | raw-daf' | to run after (usually with hostile intent |

## Definition

to run after (usually with hostile intent; figuratively (of time) gone by)

KJV renders: chase, put to flight, follow (after, on), hunt, (be under) persecute(-ion, -or), pursue(-r).
