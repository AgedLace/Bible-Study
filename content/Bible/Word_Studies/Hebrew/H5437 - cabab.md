# H5437 — סָבַב

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| סָבַב | çâbab | saw-bab' | to revolve |

## Definition

to revolve, surround, or border; used in various applications, literally and figuratively

KJV renders: bring, cast, fetch, lead, make, walk, whirl, round about, be about on every side, apply, avoid, beset (about), besiege, bring again, carry (about), change, cause to come about, circuit, (fetch a) compass (about, round), drive, environ, on every side, beset (close, come, compass, go, stand) round about, inclose, remove, return, set, sit down, turn (self) (about, aside, away, back).
