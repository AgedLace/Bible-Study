# H5327 — נָצָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| נָצָה | nâtsâh | naw-tsaw' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to go forth, i.e. (by implication) to be expelled, and (consequently) desolate; causatively, to lay waste; also (specifically), to quarrel

KJV renders: be laid waste, runinous, strive (together).
