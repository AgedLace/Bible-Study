# H1086 — בָּלָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| בָּלָה | bâlâh | baw-law' | to fail |

## Definition

to fail; by implication to wear out, decay (causatively, consume, spend)

KJV renders: consume, enjoy long, become (make, wax) old, spend, waste.
