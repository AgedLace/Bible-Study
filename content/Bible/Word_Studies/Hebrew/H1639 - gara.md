# H1639 — גָּרַע

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| גָּרַע | gâraʻ | gaw-rah' | to scrape off |

## Definition

to scrape off; by implication, to shave, remove, lessen, withhold

KJV renders: abate, clip, (di-) minish, do (take) away, keep back, restrain, make small, withdraw.
