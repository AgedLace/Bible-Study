# H4945 — מַשְׁקֶה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| מַשְׁקֶה | mashqeh | mash-keh' | properly |

## Definition

properly, causing to drink, i.e. a butler; by implication (intransitively), drink (itself); figuratively, a well-watered region

KJV renders: butler(-ship), cupbearer, drink(-ing), fat pasture, watered.
