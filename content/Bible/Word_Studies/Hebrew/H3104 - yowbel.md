# H3104 — יוֹבֵל

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| יוֹבֵל | yôwbêl | yo-bale' | the blast of a horn (from its continuous sound) |

## Definition

the blast of a horn (from its continuous sound); specifically, the signal of the silver trumpets; hence, the instrument itself and the festival thus introduced

KJV renders: jubile, ram's horn, trumpet.
