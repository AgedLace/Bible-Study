# H5402 — נֶשֶׁק

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| נֶשֶׁק | nesheq | neh'-shek | military equipment |

## Definition

military equipment, i.e. (collectively) arms (offensive or defensive), or (concretely) an arsenal

KJV renders: armed men, armour(-y), battle, harness, weapon.
