# H1792 — דָּכָא

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| דָּכָא | dâkâʼ | daw-kaw' | to crumble |

## Definition

to crumble; transitively, to bruise (literally or figuratively)

KJV renders: beat to pieces, break (in pieces), bruise, contrite, crush, destroy, humble, oppress, smite.
