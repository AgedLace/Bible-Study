# H4229 — מָחָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| מָחָה | mâchâh | maw-khaw' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to stroke or rub; by implication, to erase; also to smooth (as if with oil), i.e. grease or make fat; also to touch, i.e. reach to

KJV renders: abolish, blot out, destroy, full of marrow, put out, reach unto, utterly, wipe (away, out).
