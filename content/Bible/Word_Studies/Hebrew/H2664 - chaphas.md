# H2664 — חָפַשׂ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| חָפַשׂ | châphas | khaw-fas' | to seek |

## Definition

to seek; causatively, to conceal oneself (i.e. let be sought), or mask

KJV renders: change, (make) diligent (search), disquise self, hide, search (for, out).
