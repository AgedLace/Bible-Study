# H6206 — עָרַץ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| עָרַץ | ʻârats | aw-rats' | to awe or (intransitive) to dread |

## Definition

to awe or (intransitive) to dread; hence, to harass

KJV renders: be affrighted (afraid, dread, feared, terrified), break, dread, fear, oppress, prevail, shake terribly.
