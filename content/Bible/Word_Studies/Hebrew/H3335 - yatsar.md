# H3335 — יָצַר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| יָצַר | yâtsar | yaw-tsar' | to mould into a form |

## Definition

to mould into a form; especially as apotter; figuratively, to determine (i.e. form a resolution)

KJV renders: earthen, fashion, form, frame, make(-r), potter, purpose.
