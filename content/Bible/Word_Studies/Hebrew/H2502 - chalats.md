# H2502 — חָלַץ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| חָלַץ | châlats | khaw-lats' | to pull off |

## Definition

to pull off; hence (intensively) to strip, (reflex.) to depart; by implication, to deliver, equip (for fight); present, strengthen

KJV renders: arm (self), (go, ready) armed ( man, soldier), deliver, draw out, make fat, loose, (ready) prepared, put off, take away, withdraw self.
