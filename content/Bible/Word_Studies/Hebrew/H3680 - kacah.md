# H3680 — כָּסָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| כָּסָה | kâçâh | kaw-saw' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to plump, i.e. fill up hollows; by implication, to cover (for clothing or secrecy)

KJV renders: clad self, close, clothe, conceal, cover (self), (flee to) hide, overwhelm. Compare H3780 (כָּשָׂה).
