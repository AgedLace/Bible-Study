# H2492 — חָלַם

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| חָלַם | châlam | khaw-lam' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to bind firmly, i.e. (by implication) to be (causatively to make) plump; also (through the figurative sense of dumbness) to dream

KJV renders: (cause to) dream(-er), be in good liking, recover.
