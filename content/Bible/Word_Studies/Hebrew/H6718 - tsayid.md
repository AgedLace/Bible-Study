# H6718 — צַיִד

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| צַיִד | tsayid | tsah'-yid | the chase |

## Definition

the chase; also game (thus taken); (generally) lunch (especially for a journey)

KJV renders: catcheth, food, hunter, (that which he took in) hunting, venison, victuals.
