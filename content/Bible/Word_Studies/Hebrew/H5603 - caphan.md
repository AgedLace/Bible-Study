# H5603 — סָפַן

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| סָפַן | çâphan | saw-fan' | to hide by covering |

## Definition

to hide by covering; specifically, to roof (passive participle as noun, a roof) or wainscot; figuratively, to reserve

KJV renders: cieled, cover, seated.
