# H5999 — עָמָל

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| עָמָל | ʻâmâl | aw-mawl' | toil |

## Definition

toil, i.e. wearing effort; hence, worry, wheth. of body or mind

KJV renders: grievance(-vousness), iniquity, labour, mischief, miserable(-sery), pain(-ful), perverseness, sorrow, toil, travail, trouble, wearisome, wickedness.
