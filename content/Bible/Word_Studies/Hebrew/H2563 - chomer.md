# H2563 — חֹמֶר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| חֹמֶר | chômer | kho'mer | properly |

## Definition

properly, a bubbling up, i.e. of water, a wave; of earth, mire or clay (cement); also a heap; hence, a chomer or dry measure

KJV renders: clay, heap, homer, mire, motion.
