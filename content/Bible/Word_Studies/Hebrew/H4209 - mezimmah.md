# H4209 — מְזִמָּה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |

| מְזִמָּה | mᵉzimmâh | mez-im-maw' | a plan |

## Definition

a plan, usually evil (machination), sometimes good (sagacity)

KJV renders: (wicked) device, discretion, intent, witty invention, lewdness, mischievous (device), thought, wickedly.
