# H6767 — צְלָצַל

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| צְלָצַל | tsᵉlâtsal | tsel-aw-tsal' | a clatter |

## Definition

a clatter, i.e. (abstractly) whirring (of wings); (concretely) a cricket; also a harpoon (as rattling), a cymbal (as clanging)

KJV renders: cymbal, locust, shadowing, spear.
