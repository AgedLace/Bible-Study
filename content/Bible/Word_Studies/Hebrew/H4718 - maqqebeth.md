# H4718 — מַקֶּבֶת

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| מַקֶּבֶת | maqqebeth | mak-keh'-beth | properly |

## Definition

properly, a perforator, i.e. a hammer (as piercing); also (intransitively) a perforation, i.e. a quarry

KJV renders: hammer, hole.
