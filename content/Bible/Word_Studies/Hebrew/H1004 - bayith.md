## Definition

a house (in the greatest variation of applications, especially family, etc.)

KJV renders: court, daughter, door, dungeon, family, forth of, great as would contain, hangings, home(born), (winter) house(-hold), inside(-ward), palace, place, prison, steward, tablet, temple, web, within(-out).
