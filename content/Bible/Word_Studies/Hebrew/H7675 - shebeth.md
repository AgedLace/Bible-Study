# H7675 — שֶׁבֶת

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| שֶׁבֶת | shebeth | sheh'-beth | properly |

## Definition

properly, session; but used also concretely, an abode or locality

KJV renders: place, seat. Compare H3429 (יֹשֵׁב בַּשֶּׁבֶת).
