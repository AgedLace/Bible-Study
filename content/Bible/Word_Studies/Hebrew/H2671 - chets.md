# H2671 — חֵץ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| חֵץ | chêts | khayts | properly |

## Definition

properly, a piercer, i.e. an arrow; by implication, a wound; figuratively, (of God) thunderbolt; the shaft of aspear

KJV renders: archer, arrow, dart, shaft, staff, wound.
