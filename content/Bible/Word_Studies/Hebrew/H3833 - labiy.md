# H3833 — לָבִיא

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| לָבִיא | lâbîyʼ | law-bee' | to roar |

## Definition

to roar; a lion (properly, a lioness as the fiercer (although not a roarer;))

KJV renders: (great, old, stout) lion, lioness, young (lion).
