# H5127 — נוּס

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| נוּס | nûwç | noos | to flit |

## Definition

to flit, i.e. vanish away (subside, escape; causatively, chase, impel, deliver)

KJV renders: abate, away, be displayed, (make to) flee (away, -ing), put to flight, hide, lift up a standard.
