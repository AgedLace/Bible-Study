# H1880 — דֶּשֶׁן

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| דֶּשֶׁן | deshen | deh'-shen | the fat |

## Definition

the fat; abstractly fatness, i.e. (figuratively) abundance; specifically the (fatty) ashes of sacrifices

KJV renders: ashes, fatness.
