# H8398 — תֵּבֵל

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| תֵּבֵל | têbêl | tay-bale' | the earth (as moist and therefore inhabited) |

## Definition

the earth (as moist and therefore inhabited); by extension, the globe; by implication, its inhabitants; specifically, a particular land, as Babylonia, Palestine

KJV renders: habitable part, world.
