# H2490 — חָלַל

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| חָלַל | châlal | khaw-lal' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to bore, i.e. (by implication) to wound, to dissolve; figuratively, to profane (a person, place or thing), to break (one's word), to begin (as if by an 'opening wedge'); to play (the flute)

KJV renders: begin ( men began), defile, break, defile, eat (as common things), first, gather the grape thereof, take inheritance, pipe, player on instruments, pollute, (cast as) profane (self), prostitute, slay (slain), sorrow, stain, wound.
