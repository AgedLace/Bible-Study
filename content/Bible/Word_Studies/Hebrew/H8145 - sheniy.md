# H8145 — שֵׁנִי

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| שֵׁנִי | shênîy | shay-nee' | properly |

## Definition

properly, double, i.e. second; also adverbially, again

KJV renders: again, either (of them), (an-) other, second (time).
