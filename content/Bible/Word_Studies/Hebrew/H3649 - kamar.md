# H3649 — כָּמָר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| כָּמָר | kâmâr | kaw-mawr' | properly |

## Definition

properly, an ascetic (as if shrunk with self-maceration), i.e. an idolatrous priest (only in plural)

KJV renders: Chemarims (idolatrous) priests.
