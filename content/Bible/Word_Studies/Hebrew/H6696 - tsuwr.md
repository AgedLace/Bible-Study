# H6696 — צוּר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| צוּר | tsûwr | tsoor | to cramp |

## Definition

to cramp, i.e. confine (in many applications, literally and figuratively, formative or hostile)

KJV renders: adversary, assault, beset, besiege, bind (up), cast, distress, fashion, fortify, inclose, lay siege, put up in bags.
