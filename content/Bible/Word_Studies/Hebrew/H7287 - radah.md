# H7287 — רָדָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| רָדָה | râdâh | raw-daw' | to tread down |

## Definition

to tread down, i.e. subjugate; specifically, to crumble off

KJV renders: (come to, make to) have dominion, prevail against, reign, (bear, make to) rule,(-r, over), take.
