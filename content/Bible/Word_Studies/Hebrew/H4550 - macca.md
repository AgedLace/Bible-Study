# H4550 — מַסַּע

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| מַסַּע | maççaʻ | mas-sah' | a departure (from striking the tents) |

## Definition

a departure (from striking the tents), i.e. march (not necessarily a single day's travel); by implication, a station (or point of departure)

KJV renders: journey(-ing).
