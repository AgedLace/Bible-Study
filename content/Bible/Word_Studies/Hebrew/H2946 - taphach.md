# H2946 — טָפַח

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |

| טָפַח | ṭâphach | taw-fakh' | to flatten out or extend (as a tent) |

## Definition

to flatten out or extend (as a tent); figuratively, to nurse achild (as promotive of growth);

KJV renders: span, swaddle.
