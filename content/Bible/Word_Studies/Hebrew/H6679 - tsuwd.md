# H6679 — צוּד

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| צוּד | tsûwd | tsood | to lie alongside (i.e. in wait) |

## Definition

to lie alongside (i.e. in wait); by implication, to catch an animal (figuratively, men); to victual (for a journey)

KJV renders: chase, hunt, sore, take (provision).
