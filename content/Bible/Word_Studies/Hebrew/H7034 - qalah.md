# H7034 — קָלָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| קָלָה | qâlâh | kaw-law' | to be light (as implied in rapid motion) |

## Definition

to be light (as implied in rapid motion), but figuratively, only (be (causatively, hold) in contempt)

KJV renders: base, contemn, despise, lightly esteem, set light, seem vile.
