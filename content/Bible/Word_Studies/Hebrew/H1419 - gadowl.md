| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |

## Definition

great (in any sense); hence, older; also insolent

KJV renders: aloud, elder(-est), exceeding(-ly), far, (man of) great (man, matter, thing,-er,-ness), high, long, loud, mighty, more, much, noble, proud thing, sore, () very.
