# H6805 — צָעַד

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| צָעַד | tsâʻad | tsaw-ad' | to pace |

## Definition

to pace, i.e. step regularly; (upward) to mount; (along) to march; (down and causatively) to hurl

KJV renders: bring, go, march (through), run over.
