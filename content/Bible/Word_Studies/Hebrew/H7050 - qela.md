# H7050 — קֶלַע

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| קֶלַע | qelaʻ | keh'-lah | a sling |

## Definition

a sling; also a (door) screen (as if slung across), or the valve (of the door) itself

KJV renders: hanging, leaf, sling.
