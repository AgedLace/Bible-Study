# H2250 — חַבּוּרָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| חַבּוּרָה | chabbûwrâh | khab-boo-raw' | properly |

## Definition

properly, bound (with stripes), i.e. a weal (or black-and-blue mark itself)

KJV renders: blueness, bruise, hurt, stripe, wound.
