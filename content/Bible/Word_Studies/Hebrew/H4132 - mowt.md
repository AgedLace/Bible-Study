# H4132 — מוֹט

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| מוֹט | môwṭ | mote | a wavering |

## Definition

a wavering, i.e. fall; by implication, a pole (as shaking); hence, a yoke (as essentially a bent pole)

KJV renders: bar, be moved, staff, yoke.
