# H6565 — פָּרַר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| פָּרַר | pârar | paw-rar' | to break up (usually figuratively) |

## Definition

to break up (usually figuratively), i.e. to violate, frustrate

KJV renders: any ways, break (asunder), cast off, cause to cease, clean, defeat, disannul, disappoint, dissolve, divide, make of none effect, fail, frustrate, bring (come) to nought, utterly, make void.
