# H5599 — סָפִיחַ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| סָפִיחַ | çâphîyach | saw-fee'-akh | something (spontaneously) falling off |

## Definition

something (spontaneously) falling off, i.e. a self-sown crop; figuratively, a freshet

KJV renders: (such) things as (which) grow (of themselves), which groweth of its own accord (itself).
