# H4349 — מָכוֹן

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| מָכוֹן | mâkôwn | maw-kone' | properly |

## Definition

properly, a fixture, i.e. a basis; generally a place, especially as an abode

KJV renders: foundation, habitation, (dwelling-, settled) place.
