# H6245 — עָשַׁת

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| עָשַׁת | ʻâshath | aw-shath' | probably to be sleek |

## Definition

probably to be sleek, i.e. glossy; hence (through the idea of polishing) to excogitate (as if forming in the mind)

KJV renders: shine, think.
