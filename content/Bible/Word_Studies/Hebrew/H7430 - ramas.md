# H7430 — רָמַשׂ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| רָמַשׂ | râmas | raw-mas' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to glide swiftly, i.e. to crawl or move with short steps; by analogy to swarm

KJV renders: creep, move.
