| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |

## Definition

KJV renders: a, alike, alone, altogether, and, any(-thing), apiece, a certain, (dai-) ly, each (one), eleven, every, few, first, highway, a man, once, one, only, other, some, together,
