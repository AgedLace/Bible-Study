# H5953 — עָלַל

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| עָלַל | ʻâlal | aw-lal' | to effect thoroughly |

## Definition

to effect thoroughly; specifically, to glean (also figuratively); by implication (in a bad sense) to overdo, i.e. maltreat, be saucy to, pain, impose (also literal)

KJV renders: abuse, affect, child, defile, do, glean, mock, practise, thoroughly, work (wonderfully).
