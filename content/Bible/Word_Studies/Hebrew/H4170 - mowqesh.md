# H4170 — מוֹקֵשׁ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| מוֹקֵשׁ | môwqêsh | mo-kashe' | a noose (for catching animals) (literally or figuratively) |

## Definition

a noose (for catching animals) (literally or figuratively); by implication, a hook (for the nose)

KJV renders: be ensnared, gin, (is) snare(-d), trap.
