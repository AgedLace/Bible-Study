# H2232 — זָרַע

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| זָרַע | zâraʻ | zaw-rah' | to sow |

## Definition

to sow; figuratively, to disseminate, plant, fructify

KJV renders: bear, conceive seed, set with sow(-er), yield.
