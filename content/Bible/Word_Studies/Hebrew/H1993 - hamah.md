# H1993 — הָמָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| הָמָה | hâmâh | haw-maw' | to make a loud sound (like English 'hum') |

## Definition

to make a loud sound (like English 'hum'); by implication, to be in great commotion or tumult, to rage, war, moan, clamor

KJV renders: clamorous, concourse, cry aloud, be disquieted, loud, mourn, be moved, make a noise, rage, roar, sound, be troubled, make in tumult, tumultuous, be in an uproar.
