# H1082 — בָּלַג

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| בָּלַג | bâlag | baw-lag' | to break off or loose (in a favorable or unfavorable sense) |

## Definition

to break off or loose (in a favorable or unfavorable sense), i.e. desist (from grief) or invade (with destruction)

KJV renders: comfort, (recover) strength(-en).
