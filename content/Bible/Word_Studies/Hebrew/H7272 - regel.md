# H7272 — רֶגֶל

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| רֶגֶל | regel | reh'-gel | a foot (as used in walking) |

## Definition

a foot (as used in walking); by implication, a step; by euphemistically the pudenda

KJV renders: be able to endure, according as, after, coming, follow, (broken-)foot(-ed, -stool), great toe, haunt, journey, leg, piss, possession, time.
