# H6586 — פָּשַׁע

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| פָּשַׁע | pâshaʻ | paw-shah' | to break away (from just authority) |

## Definition

KJV renders: offend, rebel, revolt, transgress(-ion, -or).
