# H2947 — טֵפַח

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| טֵפַח | ṭêphach | tay'-fakh | a spread of the hand |

## Definition

a spread of the hand, i.e. a palm-breadth (not 'span' of the fingers); architecturally, a corbel (as a supporting palm)

KJV renders: coping, hand-breadth.
