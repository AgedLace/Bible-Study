# H7440 — רִנָּה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|

## Definition

properly, a creaking (or shrill sound), i.e. shout (of joy or grief)

KJV renders: cry, gladness, joy, proclamation, rejoicing, shouting, sing(-ing), triumph.
