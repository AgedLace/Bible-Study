# H0268 — אָחוֹר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| אָחוֹר | ʼâchôwr | aw-khore' | the hinder part |

## Definition

the hinder part; hence (adverb) behind, backward; also (as facing north) the West

KJV renders: after(-ward), back (part, -side, -ward), hereafter, (be-) hind(-er part), time to come, without.
