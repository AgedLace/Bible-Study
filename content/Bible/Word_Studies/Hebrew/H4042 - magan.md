# H4042 — מָגַן

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| מָגַן | mâgan | maw-gan' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to shield; encompass with; figuratively, to rescue, to hand safely over (i.e. surrender)

KJV renders: deliver.
