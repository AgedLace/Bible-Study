# H2114 — זוּר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| זוּר | zûwr | zoor | to turn aside (especially for lodging) |

## Definition

to turn aside (especially for lodging); hence to be aforeigner, strange, profane; specifically (active participle) to commit adultery

KJV renders: (come from) another (man, place), fanner, go away, (e-) strange(-r, thing, woman).
