# H4998 — נָאָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| נָאָה | nâʼâh | naw-aw' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to be at home, i.e. (by implication) to be pleasant (or suitable), i.e. beautiful

KJV renders: be beautiful, become, be comely.
