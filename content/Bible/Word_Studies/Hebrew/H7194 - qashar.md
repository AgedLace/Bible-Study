# H7194 — קָשַׁר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| קָשַׁר | qâshar | kaw-shar' | to tie |

## Definition

to tie, physically (gird, confine, compact) or mentally (in love, league)

KJV renders: bind (up), (make a) conspire(-acy, -ator), join together, knit, stronger, work (treason).
