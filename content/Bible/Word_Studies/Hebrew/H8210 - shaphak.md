# H8210 — שָׁפַךְ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| שָׁפַךְ | shâphak | shaw-fak' | to spill forth (blood |

## Definition

to spill forth (blood, a libation, liquid metal; or even a solid, i.e. to mound up); also (figuratively) to expend (life, soul, complaint, money, etc.); intensively, to sprawl out

KJV renders: cast (up), gush out, pour (out), shed(-der, out), slip.
