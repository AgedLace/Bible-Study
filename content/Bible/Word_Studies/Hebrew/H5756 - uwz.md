# H5756 — עוּז

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| עוּז | ʻûwz | ooz | to be strong |

## Definition

to be strong; causatively, to stregthen, i.e. (figuratively) to save (by flight)

KJV renders: gather (self, self to flee), retire.
