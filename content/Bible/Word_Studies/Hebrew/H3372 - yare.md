# H3372 — יָרֵא

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| יָרֵא | yârêʼ | yaw-ray' | to fear |

## Definition

to fear; morally, to revere; caus. to frighten

KJV renders: affright, be (make) afraid, dread(-ful), (put in) fear(-ful, -fully, -ing), (be had in) reverence(-end), see, terrible (act, -ness, thing).
