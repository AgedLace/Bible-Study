# H6731 — צִיץ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| צִיץ | tsîyts | tseets | properly |

## Definition

properly, glistening, i.e. a burnished plate; also a flower (as bright-colored); a wing (as gleaming in the air)

KJV renders: blossom, flower, plate, wing.
