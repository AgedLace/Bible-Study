# H6148 — עָרַב

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| עָרַב | ʻârab | aw-rab' | to braid |

## Definition

to braid, i.e. intermix; technically, to traffic (as if by barter); also or give to be security (as a kind of exchange)

KJV renders: engage, (inter-) meddle (with), mingle (self), mortgage, occupy, give pledges, be(-come, put in) surety, undertake.
