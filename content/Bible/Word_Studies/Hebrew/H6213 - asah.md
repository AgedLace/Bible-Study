# H6213 — עָשָׂה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| עָשָׂה | ʻâsâh | aw-saw' | to do or make |

## Definition

to do or make, in the broadest sense and widest application

KJV renders: accomplish, advance, appoint, apt, be at, become, bear, bestow, bring forth, bruise, be busy, certainly, have the charge of, commit, deal (with), deck, displease, do, (ready) dress(-ed), (put in) execute(-ion), exercise, fashion, feast, (fight-) ing man, finish, fit, fly, follow, fulfill, furnish, gather, get, go about, govern, grant, great, hinder, hold (a feast), indeed, be industrious, journey, keep, labour, maintain, make, be meet, observe, be occupied, offer, officer, pare, bring (come) to pass, perform, pracise, prepare, procure, provide, put, requite, sacrifice, serve, set, shew, sin, spend, surely, take, thoroughly, trim, very, vex, be (warr-) ior, work(-man), yield, use.
