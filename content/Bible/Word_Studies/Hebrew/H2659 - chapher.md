# H2659 — חָפֵר

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| חָפֵר | châphêr | khaw-fare' | to blush |

## Definition

to blush; figuratively, to be ashamed, disappointed; causatively, to shame, reproach

KJV renders: be ashamed, be confounded, be brought to confusion (unto shame), come (be put to) shame, bring reproach.
