# H2403 — חַטָּאָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| חַטָּאָה | chaṭṭâʼâh | khat-taw-aw' | an offence (sometimes habitual sinfulness) |

## Definition

an offence (sometimes habitual sinfulness), and its penalty, occasion, sacrifice, or expiation; also (concretely) an offender

KJV renders: punishment (of sin), purifying(-fication for sin), sin(-ner, offering).
