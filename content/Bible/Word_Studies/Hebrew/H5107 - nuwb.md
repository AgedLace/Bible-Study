# H5107 — נוּב

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| נוּב | nûwb | noob | to germinate |

## Definition

to germinate, i.e. (figuratively) to (causatively, make) flourish; also (of words), to utter

KJV renders: bring forth (fruit), make cheerful, increase.
