# H1803 — דַּלָּה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| דַּלָּה | dallâh | dal-law' | properly |

## Definition

properly, something dangling, i.e. a loose thread or hair; figuratively, indigent

KJV renders: hair, pining sickness, poor(-est sort).
