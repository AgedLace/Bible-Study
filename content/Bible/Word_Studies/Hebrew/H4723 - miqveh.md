# H4723 — מִקְוֶה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| מִקְוֶה | miqveh | mik-veh' | something waited for |

## Definition

something waited for, i.e. confidence (objective or subjective); also a collection, i.e. (of water) a pond, or (of men and horses) a caravan or drove

KJV renders: abiding, gathering together, hope, linen yarn, plenty (of water), pool.
