# H7737 — שָׁוָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| שָׁוָה | shâvâh | shaw-vaw' | properly |

## Definition

properly, to level, i.e. equalize; figuratively, to resemble; by implication, to adjust (i.e. counterbalance, be suitable, compose, place, yield, etc.)

KJV renders: avail, behave, bring forth, compare, countervail, (be, make) equal, lay, be (make, a-) like, make plain, profit, reckon.
