# H4962 — מַת

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| מַת | math | math | properly |

## Definition

properly, an adult (as of full length); by implication, a man (only in the plural)

KJV renders: few, friends, men, persons, small.
