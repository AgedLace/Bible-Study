# H1820 — דָּמָה

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| דָּמָה | dâmâh | daw-mam' | to be dumb or silent |

## Definition

to be dumb or silent; hence, to fail or perish; trans. to destroy

KJV renders: cease, be cut down (off), destroy, be brought to silence, be undone, utterly.
