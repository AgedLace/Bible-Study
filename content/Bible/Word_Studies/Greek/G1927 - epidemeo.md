# G1927 — ἐπιδημέω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| ἐπιδημέω | epidēméō |  | to make oneself at home |

## Definition

 to make oneself at home, i.e. (by extension) to reside (in a foreign country)

KJV renders: (be) dwelling (which were) there, stranger
