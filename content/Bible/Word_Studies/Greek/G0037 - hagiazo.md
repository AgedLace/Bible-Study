# G0037 — ἁγιάζω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| ἁγιάζω | hagiázō |  | to make holy |

## Definition

 to make holy, i.e. (ceremonially) purify or consecrate; (mentally) to venerate

KJV renders: hallow, be holy, sanctify
