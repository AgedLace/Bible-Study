# G0568 — ἀπέχω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| ἀπέχω | apéchō |  | (actively) to have out |

## Definition

 (actively) to have out, i.e. receive in full; (intransitively) to keep (oneself) away, i.e. be distant (literally or figuratively)

KJV renders: be, have, receive
