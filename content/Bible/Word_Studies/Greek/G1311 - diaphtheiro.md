# G1311 — διαφθείρω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| διαφθείρω | diaphtheírō |  | to rot thoroughly |

## Definition

 to rot thoroughly, i.e. (by implication) to ruin (passively, decay utterly, figuratively, pervert)

KJV renders: corrupt, destroy, perish
