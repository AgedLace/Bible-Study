# G2433 — ἱλάσκομαι

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| ἱλάσκομαι | hiláskomai |  | to conciliate |

## Definition

 to conciliate, i.e. (transitively) to atone for (sin), or (intransitively) be propitious

KJV renders: be merciful, make reconciliation for
