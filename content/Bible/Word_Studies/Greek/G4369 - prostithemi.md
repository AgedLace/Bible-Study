# G4369 — προστίθημι

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| προστίθημι | prostíthēmi |  | to place additionally |

## Definition

 to place additionally, i.e. lay beside, annex, repeat

KJV renders: add, again, give more, increase, lay unto, proceed further, speak to any more
