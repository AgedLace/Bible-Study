# G5299 — ὑπωπιάζω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| ὑπωπιάζω | hypōpiázō |  | to hit under the eye (buffet or disable an antagonist as a pugilist) |

## Definition

 to hit under the eye (buffet or disable an antagonist as a pugilist), i.e. (figuratively) to tease or annoy (into compliance), subdue (one's passions)

KJV renders: keep under, weary
