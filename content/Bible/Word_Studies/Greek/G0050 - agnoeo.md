# G0050 — ἀγνοέω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| ἀγνοέω | agnoéō |  | not to know (through lack of information or intelligence) |

## Definition

 not to know (through lack of information or intelligence); by implication, to ignore (through disinclination)

KJV renders: (be) ignorant(-ly), not know, not understand, unknown
