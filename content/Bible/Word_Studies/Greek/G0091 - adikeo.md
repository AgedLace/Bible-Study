# G0091 — ἀδικέω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| ἀδικέω | adikéō |  | to be unjust |

## Definition

 to be unjust, i.e. (actively) do wrong (morally, socially or physically)

KJV renders: hurt, injure, be an offender, be unjust, (do, suffer, take) wrong
