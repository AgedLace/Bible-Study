# G2106 — εὐδοκέω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| εὐδοκέω | eudokéō |  | to think well of |

## Definition

 to think well of, i.e. approve (an act); specially, to approbate (a person or thing)

KJV renders: think good, (be well) please(-d), be the good (have, take) pleasure, be willing
