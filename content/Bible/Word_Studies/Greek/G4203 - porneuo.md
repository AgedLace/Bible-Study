# G4203 — πορνεύω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| πορνεύω | porneúō |  | to act the harlot |

## Definition

 to act the harlot, i.e. (literally) indulge unlawful lust (of either sex), or (figuratively) practise idolatry

KJV renders: commit (fornication)
