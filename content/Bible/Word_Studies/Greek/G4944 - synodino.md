# G4944 — συνωδίνω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |

| συνωδίνω | synōdínō |  | to have (parturition) pangs in company (concert |

## Definition

 to have (parturition) pangs in company (concert, simultaneously) with, i.e. (figuratively) to sympathize (in expectation of relief from suffering)

KJV renders: travail in pain together
