# G4797 — συγχέω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| συγχέω | synchéō |  | to commingle promiscuously |

## Definition

 to commingle promiscuously, i.e. (figuratively) to throw (an assembly) into disorder, to perplex (the mind)

KJV renders: confound, confuse, stir up, be in an uproar
