# G0235 — ἀλλά

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| ἀλλά | allá |  | properly |

## Definition

 properly, other things, i.e. (adverbially) contrariwise (in many relations)

KJV renders: and, but (even), howbeit, indeed, nay, nevertheless, no, notwithstanding, save, therefore, yea, yet
