# G1758 — ἐνέχω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| ἐνέχω | enéchō |  | to hold in or upon |

## Definition

 to hold in or upon, i.e. ensnare; by implication, to keep a grudge

KJV renders: entangle with, have a quarrel against, urge
