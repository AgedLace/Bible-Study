# G4221 — ποτήριον

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| ποτήριον | potḗrion |  | a drinking-vessel |

## Definition

 a drinking-vessel; by extension, the contents thereof, i.e. a cupful (draught); figuratively, a lot or fate

KJV renders: cup
