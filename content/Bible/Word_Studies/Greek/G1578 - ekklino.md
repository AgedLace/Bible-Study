# G1578 — ἐκκλίνω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| ἐκκλίνω | ekklínō |  | to deviate |

## Definition

 to deviate, i.e. (absolutely) to shun (literally or figuratively), or (relatively) to decline (from piety)

KJV renders: avoid, eschew, go out of the way
