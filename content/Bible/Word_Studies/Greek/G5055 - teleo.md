| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| τελέω | teléō |  | to end |

## Definition

 to end, i.e. complete, execute, conclude, discharge (a debt)

KJV renders: accomplish, make an end, expire, fill up, finish, go over, pay, perform
