# G5228 — ὑπέρ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| ὑπέρ | hypér |  | "over" |

## Definition

 "over", i.e. (with the genitive case) of place, above, beyond, across, or causal, for the sake of, instead, regarding; with the accusative case superior to, more than

KJV renders: (+ exceeding, abundantly) above, in (on) behalf of, beyond, by, + very chiefest, concerning, exceeding (above, -ly), for, + very highly, more (than), of, over, on the part of, for sake of, in stead, than, to(-ward), very
