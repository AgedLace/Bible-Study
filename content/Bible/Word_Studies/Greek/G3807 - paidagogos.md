# G3807 — παιδαγωγός

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| παιδαγωγός | paidagōgós |  | a boy-leader |

## Definition

 a boy-leader, i.e. a servant whose office it was to take the children to school; (by implication, (figuratively) a tutor ("pædagogue"))

KJV renders: instructor, schoolmaster
