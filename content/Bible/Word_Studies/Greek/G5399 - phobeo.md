# G5399 — φοβέω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| φοβέω | phobéō |  | to frighten |

## Definition

 to frighten, i.e. (passively) to be alarmed; by analogy, to be in awe of, i.e. revere

KJV renders: be (+ sore) afraid, fear (exceedingly), reverence
