# G1223 — διά

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| διά | diá |  | through (in very wide applications |

## Definition

 through (in very wide applications, local, causal, or occasional)

KJV renders: after, always, among, at, to avoid, because of (that), briefly, by, for (cause) … fore, from, in, by occasion of, of, by reason of, for sake, that, thereby, therefore, X though, through(-out), to, wherefore, with (-in)
