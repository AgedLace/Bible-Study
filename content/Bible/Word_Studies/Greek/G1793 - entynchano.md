# G1793 — ἐντυγχάνω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| ἐντυγχάνω | entynchánō |  | to chance upon |

## Definition

 to chance upon, i.e. (by implication) confer with; by extension to entreat (in favor or against)

KJV renders: deal with, make intercession
