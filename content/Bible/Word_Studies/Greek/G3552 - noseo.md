# G3552 — νοσέω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| νοσέω | noséō |  | to be sick |

## Definition

 to be sick, i.e. (by implication, of a diseased appetite) to hanker after (figuratively, to harp upon)

KJV renders: dote
