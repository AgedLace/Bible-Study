# G4820 — συμβάλλω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| συμβάλλω | symbállō |  | to combine |

## Definition

 to combine, i.e. (in speaking) to converse, consult, dispute, (mentally) to consider, (by implication) to aid, (personally) to join, attack

KJV renders: confer, encounter, help, make, meet with, ponder
