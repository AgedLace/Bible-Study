# G5274 — ὑπολαμβάνω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| ὑπολαμβάνω | hypolambánō |  | to take from below |

## Definition

 to take from below, i.e. carry upward; figuratively, to take up, i.e. continue a discourse or topic; mentally, to assume (presume)

KJV renders: answer, receive, suppose
