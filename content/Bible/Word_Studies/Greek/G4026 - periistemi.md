# G4026 — περιΐστημι

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| περιΐστημι | periḯstēmi |  | to stand all around |

## Definition

 to stand all around, i.e. (near) to be a bystander, or (aloof) to keep away from

KJV renders: avoid, shun, stand by (round about)
