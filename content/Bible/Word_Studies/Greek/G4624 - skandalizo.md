# G4624 — σκανδαλίζω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| σκανδαλίζω | skandalízō |  | to entrap |

## Definition

 to entrap, i.e. trip up (figuratively, stumble (transitively) or entice to sin, apostasy or displeasure)

KJV renders: (make to) offend
