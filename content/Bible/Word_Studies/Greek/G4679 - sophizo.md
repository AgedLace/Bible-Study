# G4679 — σοφίζω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| σοφίζω | sophízō |  | to render wise |

## Definition

 to render wise; in a sinister acceptation, to form "sophisms", i.e. continue plausible error

KJV renders: cunningly devised, make wise
