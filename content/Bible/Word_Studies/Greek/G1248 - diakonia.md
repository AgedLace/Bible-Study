# G1248 — διακονία

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| διακονία | diakonía |  | attendance (as a servant |

## Definition

 attendance (as a servant, etc.); figuratively (eleemosynary) aid, (official) service (especially of the Christian teacher, or technically of the diaconate)

KJV renders: (ad-)minister(-ing, -tration, -try), office, relief, service(-ing)
