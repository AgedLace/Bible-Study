# G0508 — ἀνώγεον

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| ἀνώγεον | anṓgeon |  | above the ground |

## Definition

 above the ground, i.e. (properly) the second floor of a building; used for a dome or a balcony on the upper story

KJV renders: upper room
