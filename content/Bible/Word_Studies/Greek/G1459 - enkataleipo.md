# G1459 — ἐγκαταλείπω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| ἐγκαταλείπω | enkataleípō |  | to leave behind in some place |

## Definition

 to leave behind in some place, i.e. (in a good sense) let remain over, or (in a bad sense) to desert

KJV renders: forsake, leave
