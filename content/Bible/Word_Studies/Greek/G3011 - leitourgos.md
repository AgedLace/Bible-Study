# G3011 — λειτουργός

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| λειτουργός | leitourgós |  | a public servant |

## Definition

 a public servant, i.e. a functionary in the Temple or Gospel, or (genitive case) a worshipper (of God) or benefactor (of man)

KJV renders: minister(-ed)
