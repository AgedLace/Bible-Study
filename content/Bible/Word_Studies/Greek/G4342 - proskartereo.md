# G4342 — προσκαρτερέω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| προσκαρτερέω | proskarteréō |  | to be earnest towards |

## Definition

 to be earnest towards, i.e. (to a thing) to persevere, be constantly diligent, or (in a place) to attend assiduously all the exercises, or (to a person) to adhere closely to (as a servitor)

KJV renders: attend (give self) continually (upon), continue (in, instant in, with), wait on (continually)
