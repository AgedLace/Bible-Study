# G2233 — ἡγέομαι

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| ἡγέομαι | hēgéomai |  | to lead |

## Definition

 to lead, i.e. command (with official authority); figuratively, to deem, i.e. consider

KJV renders: account, (be) chief, count, esteem, governor, judge, have the rule over, suppose, think
