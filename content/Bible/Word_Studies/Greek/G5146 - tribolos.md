| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |

| τρίβολος | tríbolos |  | properly |

## Definition

 properly, a crow-foot (three-pronged obstruction in war), i.e. (by analogy) a thorny plant (caltrop)

KJV renders: brier, thistle
