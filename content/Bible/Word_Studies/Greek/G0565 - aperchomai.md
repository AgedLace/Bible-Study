# G0565 — ἀπέρχομαι

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| ἀπέρχομαι | apérchomai |  | to go off (i.e. depart) |

## Definition

 to go off (i.e. depart), aside (i.e. apart) or behind (i.e. follow), literally or figuratively

KJV renders: come, depart, go (aside, away, back, out, … ways), pass away, be past
