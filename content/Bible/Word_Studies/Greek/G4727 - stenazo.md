# G4727 — στενάζω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |

## Definition

 to make (intransitively, be) in straits, i.e. (by implication) to sigh, murmur, pray inaudibly

KJV renders: with grief, groan, grudge, sigh
