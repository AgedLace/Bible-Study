# G5235 — ὑπερβάλλω

| ὑπερβάλλω | hyperbállō |  | to throw beyod the usual mark |

## Definition

 to throw beyod the usual mark, i.e. (figuratively) to surpass (only active participle supereminent)

KJV renders: exceeding, excel, pass
