# G0645 — ἀποσπάω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| ἀποσπάω | apospáō |  | to drag forth |

## Definition

 to drag forth, i.e. (literally) unsheathe (a sword), or relatively (with a degree of force implied) retire (personally or factiously)

KJV renders: (with-)draw (away), after we were gotten from
