# G1658 — ἐλεύθερος

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| ἐλεύθερος | eleútheros |  | unrestrained (to go at pleasure) |

## Definition

 unrestrained (to go at pleasure), i.e. (as a citizen) not a slave (whether freeborn or manumitted), or (genitive case) exempt (from obligation or liability)

KJV renders: free (man, woman), at liberty
