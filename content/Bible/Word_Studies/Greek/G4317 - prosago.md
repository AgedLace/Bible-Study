# G4317 — προσάγω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| προσάγω | proságō |  | to lead towards |

## Definition

 to lead towards, i.e. (transitively) to conduct near (summon, present), or (intransitively) to approach

KJV renders: bring, draw near
