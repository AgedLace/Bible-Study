# G5136 — τραχηλίζω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| τραχηλίζω | trachēlízō |  | to seize by the throat or neck |

## Definition

 to seize by the throat or neck, i.e. to expose the gullet of a victim for killing (generally, to lay bare)

KJV renders: opened
