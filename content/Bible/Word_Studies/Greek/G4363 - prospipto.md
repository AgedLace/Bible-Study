# G4363 — προσπίπτω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| προσπίπτω | prospíptō |  | to fall towards |

## Definition

 to fall towards, i.e. (gently) prostrate oneself (in supplication or homage), or (violently) to rush upon (in storm)

KJV renders: beat upon, fall (down) at (before)
