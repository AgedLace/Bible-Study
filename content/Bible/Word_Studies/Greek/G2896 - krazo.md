# G2896 — κράζω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| κράζω | krázō |  | properly |

## Definition

 properly, to "croak" (as a raven) or scream, i.e. (genitive case) to call aloud (shriek, exclaim, intreat)

KJV renders: cry (out)
