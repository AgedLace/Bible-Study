# G0968 — βῆμα

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| βῆμα | bēma |  | a step |

## Definition

 a step, i.e. foot-breath; by implication, a rostrum, i.e. a tribunal

KJV renders: judgment-seat, set (foot) on, throne
