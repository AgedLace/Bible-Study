# G4691 — σπερμολόγος

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| σπερμολόγος | spermológos |  | a seed-picker (as the crow) |

## Definition

 a seed-picker (as the crow), i.e. (figuratively) a sponger, loafer (specially, a gossip or trifler in talk)

KJV renders: babbler
