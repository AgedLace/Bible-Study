# G1411 — δύναμις

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| δύναμις | dýnamis |  | force (literally or figuratively) |

## Definition

 force (literally or figuratively); specially, miraculous power (usually by implication, a miracle itself)

KJV renders: ability, abundance, meaning, might(-ily, -y, -y deed), (worker of) miracle(-s), power, strength, violence, mighty (wonderful) work
