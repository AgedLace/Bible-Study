# G5288 — ὑποστέλλω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| ὑποστέλλω | hypostéllō |  | to withhold under (out of sight) |

## Definition

 to withhold under (out of sight), i.e. (reflexively) to cower or shrink, (figuratively) to conceal (reserve)

KJV renders: draw (keep) back, shun, withdraw
