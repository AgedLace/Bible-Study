# G2436 — ἵλεως

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| ἵλεως | híleōs |  | cheerful (as attractive) |

## Definition

 cheerful (as attractive), i.e. propitious; adverbially (by Hebraism) God be gracious!, i.e. (in averting some calamity) far be it

KJV renders: be it far, merciful
