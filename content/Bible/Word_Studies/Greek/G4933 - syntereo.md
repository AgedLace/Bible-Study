# G4933 — συντηρέω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| συντηρέω | syntēréō |  | to keep closely together |

## Definition

 to keep closely together, i.e. (by implication) to conserve (from ruin); mentally, to remember (and obey)

KJV renders: keep, observe, preserve
