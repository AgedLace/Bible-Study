# G4298 — προκόπτω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| προκόπτω | prokóptō |  | to drive forward (as if by beating) |

## Definition

 to drive forward (as if by beating), i.e. (figuratively and intransitively) to advance (in amount, to grow; in time, to be well along)

KJV renders: increase, proceed, profit, be far spent, wax
