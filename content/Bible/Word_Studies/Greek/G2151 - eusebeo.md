# G2151 — εὐσεβέω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| εὐσεβέω | eusebéō |  | to be pious |

## Definition

 to be pious, i.e. (towards God) to worship, or (towards parents) to respect (support)

KJV renders: show piety, worship
