# G1980 — ἐπισκέπτομαι

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| ἐπισκέπτομαι | episképtomai |  | to inspect |

## Definition

 to inspect, i.e. (by implication) to select; by extension, to go to see, relieve

KJV renders: look out, visit
