| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |

| στυγνάζω | stygnázō |  | to render gloomy |

## Definition

 to render gloomy, i.e. (by implication) glower (be overcast with clouds, or sombreness of speech)

KJV renders: lower, be sad
