# G5101 — τίς

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| τίς | tís |  | an interrogative pronoun |

## Definition

 an interrogative pronoun, who, which or what (in direct or indirect questions)

KJV renders: every man, how (much), + no(-ne, thing), what (manner, thing), where (-by, -fore, -of, -unto, - with, -withal), whether, which, who(-m, -se), why
