# G1287 — διασκορπίζω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| διασκορπίζω | diaskorpízō |  | to dissipate |

## Definition

 to dissipate, i.e. (genitive case) to rout or separate; specially, to winnow; figuratively, to squander

KJV renders: disperse, scatter (abroad), strew, waste
