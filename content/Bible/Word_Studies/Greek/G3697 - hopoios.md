# G3697 — ὁποῖος

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| ὁποῖος | hopoîos |  | of what kind that |

## Definition

 of what kind that, i.e. how (as) great (excellent) (specially, as an indefinite correlative to the definite antecedent G5108 (τοιοῦτος) of quality)

KJV renders: what manner (sort) of, such as whatsoever
