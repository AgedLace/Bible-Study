# G2398 — ἴδιος

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| ἴδιος | ídios |  | pertaining to self |

## Definition

 pertaining to self, i.e. one's own; by implication, private or separate

KJV renders: X his acquaintance, when they were alone, apart, aside, due, his (own, proper, several), home, (her, our, thine, your) own (business), private(-ly), proper, severally, their (own)
