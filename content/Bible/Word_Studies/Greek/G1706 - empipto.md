# G1706 — ἐμπίπτω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| ἐμπίπτω | empíptō |  | to fall on |

## Definition

 to fall on, i.e. (literally) to be entrapped by, or (figuratively) be overwhelmed with

KJV renders: fall among (into)
