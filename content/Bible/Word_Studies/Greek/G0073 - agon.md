# G0073 — ἀγών

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| ἀγών | agṓn |  | properly |

## Definition

 properly, a place of assembly (as if led), i.e. (by implication) a contest (held there); figuratively, an effort or anxiety

KJV renders: conflict, contention, fight, race
