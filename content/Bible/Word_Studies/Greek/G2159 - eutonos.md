# G2159 — εὐτόνως

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| εὐτόνως | eutónōs |  | in a well-strung manner |

## Definition

 in a well-strung manner, i.e. (figuratively) intensely (in a good sense, cogently; in a bad one, fiercely)

KJV renders: mightily, vehemently
