# G4084 — πιάζω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| πιάζω | piázō |  | to squeeze |

## Definition

 to squeeze, i.e. seize (gently by the hand (press), or officially (arrest), or in hunting (capture))

KJV renders: apprehend, catch, lay hand on, take
