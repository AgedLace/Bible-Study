# G2897 — κραιπάλη

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| κραιπάλη | kraipálē |  | properly |

## Definition

 properly, a headache (as a seizure of pain) from drunkenness, i.e. (by implication) a debauch (by analogy, a glut)

KJV renders: surfeiting
