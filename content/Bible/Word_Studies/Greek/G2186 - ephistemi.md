# G2186 — ἐφίστημι

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| ἐφίστημι | ephístēmi |  | to stand upon |

## Definition

 to stand upon, i.e. be present (in various applications, friendly or otherwise, usually literal); 

KJV renders: --assault, come (in, to, unto, upon), be at hand (instant), present, stand (before, by, over)
