# G0386 — ἀνάστασις

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| ἀνάστασις | anástasis |  | a standing up again |

## Definition

 a standing up again, i.e. (literally) a resurrection from death (individual, genitive case or by implication, (its author)), or (figuratively) a (moral) recovery (of spiritual truth)

KJV renders: raised to life again, resurrection, rise from the dead, that should rise, rising again
