# G4920 — συνίημι

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| συνίημι | syníēmi |  | to put together |

## Definition

 to put together, i.e. (mentally) to comprehend; by implication, to act piously

KJV renders: consider, understand, be wise
