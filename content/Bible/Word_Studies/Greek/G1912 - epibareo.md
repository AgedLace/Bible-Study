# G1912 — ἐπιβαρέω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| ἐπιβαρέω | epibaréō |  | to be heavy upon |

## Definition

 to be heavy upon, i.e. (pecuniarily) to be expensive to; figuratively, to be severe towards

KJV renders: be chargeable to, overcharge
