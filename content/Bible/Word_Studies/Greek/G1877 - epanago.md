# G1877 — ἐπανάγω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |

| ἐπανάγω | epanágō |  | to lead up on |

## Definition

 to lead up on, i.e. (technical) to put out (to sea); (intransitively) to return

KJV renders: launch (thrust) out, return
