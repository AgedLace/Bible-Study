# G5432 — φρουρέω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| φρουρέω | phrouréō |  | to be a watcher in advance |

## Definition

 to be a watcher in advance, i.e. to mount guard as a sentinel (post spies at gates); figuratively, to hem in, protect

KJV renders: keep (with a garrison)
