# G0746 — ἀρχή

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| ἀρχή | archḗ |  | (properly abstract) a commencement |

## Definition

 (properly abstract) a commencement, or (concretely) chief (in various applications of order, time, place, or rank)

KJV renders: beginning, corner, (at the, the) first (estate), magistrate, power, principality, principle, rule
