# G4121 — πλεονάζω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| πλεονάζω | pleonázō |  | to do |

## Definition

 to do, make or be more, i.e. increase (transitively or intransitively); by extension, to superabound

KJV renders: abound, abundant, make to increase, have over
