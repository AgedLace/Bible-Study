# G5562 — χωρέω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| χωρέω | chōréō |  | to be in (give) space |

## Definition

 to be in (give) space, i.e. (intransitively) to pass, enter, or (transitively) to hold, admit (literally or figuratively)

KJV renders: come, contain, go, have place, (can, be room to) receive
