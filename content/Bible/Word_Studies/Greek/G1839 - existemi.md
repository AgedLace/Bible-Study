# G1839 — ἐξίστημι

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| ἐξίστημι | exístēmi |  | to put (stand) out of wits |

## Definition

 to put (stand) out of wits, i.e. astound, or (reflexively) become astounded, insane

KJV renders: amaze, be (make) astonished, be beside self (selves), bewitch, wonder
