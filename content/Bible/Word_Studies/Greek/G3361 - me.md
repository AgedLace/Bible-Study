# G3361 — μή

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| μή | mḗ |  | (adverb) not |

## Definition

 (adverb) not, (conjunction) lest; also (as an interrogative implying a negative answer (whereas G3756 (οὐ) expects an affirmative one)) whether

KJV renders: any but (that), X forbear, + God forbid, + lack, lest, neither, never, no (X wise in), none, nor, (can-)not, nothing, that not, un(-taken), without
