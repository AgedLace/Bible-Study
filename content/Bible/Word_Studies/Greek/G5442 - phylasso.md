# G5442 — φυλάσσω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| φυλάσσω | phylássō |  | to watch |

## Definition

 to watch, i.e. be on guard (literally of figuratively); by implication, to preserve, obey, avoid

KJV renders: beward, keep (self), observe, save
