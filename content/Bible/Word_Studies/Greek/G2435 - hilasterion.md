# G2435 — ἱλαστήριον

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| ἱλαστήριον | hilastḗrion |  | an expiatory (place or thing) |

## Definition

 an expiatory (place or thing), i.e. (concretely) an atoning victim, or (specially) the lid of the Ark (in the Temple)

KJV renders: mercyseat, propitiation
