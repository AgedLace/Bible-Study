# G2702 — καταφέρω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| καταφέρω | kataphérō |  | to bear down |

## Definition

 to bear down, i.e. (figuratively) overcome (with drowsiness); specially, to cast a vote

KJV renders: fall, give, sink down
