# G2518 — καθεύδω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |

| καθεύδω | katheúdō |  | to lie down to rest |

## Definition

 to lie down to rest, i.e. (by implication) to fall asleep (literally or figuratively)

KJV renders: (be a-)sleep
