# G2647 — καταλύω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| καταλύω | katalýō |  | to loosen down (disintegrate) |

## Definition

 to loosen down (disintegrate), i.e. (by implication) to demolish (literally or figuratively); specially (compare G2646 (κατάλυμα)) to halt for the night

KJV renders: destroy, dissolve, be guest, lodge, come to nought, overthrow, throw down
