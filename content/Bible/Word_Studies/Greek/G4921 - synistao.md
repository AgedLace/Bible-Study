# G4921 — συνιστάω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| συνιστάω | synistáō |  | to set together |

## Definition

 to set together, i.e. (by implication) to introduce (favorably), or (figuratively) to exhibit; intransitively, to stand near, or (figuratively) to constitute

KJV renders: approve, commend, consist, make, stand (with)
