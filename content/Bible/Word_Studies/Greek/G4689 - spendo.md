# G4689 — σπένδω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| σπένδω | spéndō |  | to pour out as a libation |

## Definition

 to pour out as a libation, i.e. (figuratively) to devote (one's life or blood, as a sacrifice) ("spend")

KJV renders: (be ready to) be offered
