# G1986 — ἐπισπάομαι

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| ἐπισπάομαι | epispáomai |  | to draw over |

## Definition

 to draw over, i.e. (with G203 (ἀκροβυστία) implied) efface the mark of circumcision (by recovering with the foreskin)

KJV renders: become uncircumcised
