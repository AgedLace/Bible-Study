# G3870 — παρακαλέω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| παρακαλέω | parakaléō |  | to call near |

## Definition

 to call near, i.e. invite, invoke (by imploration, hortation or consolation)

KJV renders: beseech, call for, (be of good) comfort, desire, (give) exhort(-ation), intreat, pray
