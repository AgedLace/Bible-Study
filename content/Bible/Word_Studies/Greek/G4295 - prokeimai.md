# G4295 — πρόκειμαι

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| πρόκειμαι | prókeimai |  | to lie before the view |

## Definition

 to lie before the view, i.e. (figuratively) to be present (to the mind), to stand forth (as an example or reward)

KJV renders: be first, set before (forth)
