# G4053 — περισσός

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| περισσός | perissós |  | superabundant (in quantity) or superior (in quality) |

## Definition

 superabundant (in quantity) or superior (in quality); by implication, excessive; adverbially (with G1537 (ἐκ)) violently; neuter (as noun) preeminence

KJV renders: exceeding abundantly above, more abundantly, advantage, exceedingly, very highly, beyond measure, more, superfluous, vehement(-ly)
