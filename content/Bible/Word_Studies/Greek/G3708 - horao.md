# G3708 — ὁράω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| ὁράω | horáō |  | by extension |

## Definition

 by extension, to attend to; by Hebraism, to experience; passively, to appear

KJV renders: behold, perceive, see, take heed
