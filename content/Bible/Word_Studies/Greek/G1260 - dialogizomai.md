# G1260 — διαλογίζομαι

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| διαλογίζομαι | dialogízomai |  | to reckon thoroughly |

## Definition

 to reckon thoroughly, i.e. (genitive case) to deliberate (by reflection or discussion)

KJV renders: cast in mind, consider, dispute, muse, reason, think
