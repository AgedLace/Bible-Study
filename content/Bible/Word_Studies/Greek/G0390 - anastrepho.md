# G0390 — ἀναστρέφω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| ἀναστρέφω | anastréphō |  | to overturn |

## Definition

 to overturn; also to return; by implication, to busy oneself, i.e. remain, live

KJV renders: abide, behave self, have conversation, live, overthrow, pass, return, be used
