# G3544 — νομικός

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |

| νομικός | nomikós |  | according (or pertaining) to law |

## Definition

 according (or pertaining) to law, i.e. legal (ceremonially); as noun, an expert in the (Mosaic) law

KJV renders: about the law, lawyer
