# G4755 — στρατηγός

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| στρατηγός | stratēgós |  | a general |

## Definition

 a general, i.e. (by implication or analogy) a (military) governor (prætor), the chief (præfect) of the (Levitical) temple-wardens

KJV renders: captain, magistrate
