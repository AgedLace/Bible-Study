# G0878 — ἄφρων

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| ἄφρων | áphrōn |  | properly |

## Definition

 properly, mindless, i.e. stupid, (by implication) ignorant, (specially) egotistic, (practically) rash, or (morally) unbelieving

KJV renders: fool(-ish), unwise
