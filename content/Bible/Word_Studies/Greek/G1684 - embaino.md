# G1684 — ἐμβαίνω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| ἐμβαίνω | embaínō |  | to walk on |

## Definition

 to walk on, i.e. embark (aboard a vessel), reach (a pool)

KJV renders: come (get) into, enter (into), go (up) into, step in, take ship
