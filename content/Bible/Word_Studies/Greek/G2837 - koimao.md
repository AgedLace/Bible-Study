# G2837 — κοιμάω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| κοιμάω | koimáō |  | to put to sleep |

## Definition

 to put to sleep, i.e. (passively or reflexively) to slumber; figuratively, to decease

KJV renders: (be a-, fall a-, fall on) sleep, be dead
