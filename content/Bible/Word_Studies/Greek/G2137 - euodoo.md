# G2137 — εὐοδόω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |

| εὐοδόω | euodóō |  | to help on the road |

## Definition

 to help on the road, i.e. (passively) succeed in reaching; figuratively, to succeed in business affairs

KJV renders: (have a) prosper(-ous journey)
