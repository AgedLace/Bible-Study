# G3143 — μαρτύρομαι

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| μαρτύρομαι | martýromai |  | to be adduced as a witness |

## Definition

 to be adduced as a witness, i.e. (figuratively) to obtest (in affirmation or exhortation)

KJV renders: take to record, testify
