# G3954 — παῤῥησία

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| παῤῥησία | parrhēsía |  | all out-spokenness |

## Definition

 all out-spokenness, i.e. frankness, bluntness, publicity; by implication, assurance

KJV renders: bold (X -ly, -ness, -ness of speech), confidence, X freely, X openly, X plainly(-ness)
