# G4352 — προσκυνέω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| προσκυνέω | proskynéō |  | to fawn or crouch to |

## Definition

 to fawn or crouch to, i.e. (literally or figuratively) prostrate oneself in homage (do reverence to, adore)

KJV renders: worship
