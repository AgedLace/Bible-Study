| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| μαθητεύω | mathēteúō |  | intransitively |

## Definition

 intransitively, to become a pupil; transitively, to disciple, i.e. enrol as scholar

KJV renders: be disciple, instruct, teach
