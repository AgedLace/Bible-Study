# G3784 — ὀφείλω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| ὀφείλω | opheílō |  | to owe (pecuniarily) |

## Definition

 to owe (pecuniarily); figuratively, to be under obligation (ought, must, should); morally, to fail in duty

KJV renders: behove, be bound, (be) debt(-or), (be) due(-ty), be guilty (indebted), (must) need(-s), ought, owe, should
