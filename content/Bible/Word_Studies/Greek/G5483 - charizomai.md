# G5483 — χαρίζομαι

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| χαρίζομαι | charízomai |  | to grant as a favor |

## Definition

 to grant as a favor, i.e. gratuitously, in kindness, pardon or rescue

KJV renders: deliver, (frankly) forgive, (freely) give, grant
