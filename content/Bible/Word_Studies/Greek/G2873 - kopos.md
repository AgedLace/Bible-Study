# G2873 — κόπος

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| κόπος | kópos |  | a cut |

## Definition

 a cut, i.e. (by analogy) toil (as reducing the strength), literally or figuratively; by implication, pains

KJV renders: labour, + trouble, weariness
