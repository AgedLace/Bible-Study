# G0572 — ἁπλότης

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| ἁπλότης | haplótēs |  | singleness |

## Definition

 singleness, i.e. (subjectively) sincerity (without dissimulation or self-seeking), or (objectively) generosity (copious bestowal)

KJV renders: bountifulness, liberal(-ity), simplicity, singleness
