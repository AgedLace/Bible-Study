# G4155 — πνίγω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| πνίγω | pnígō |  | to wheeze |

## Definition

 to wheeze, i.e. (causative, by implication) to throttle or strangle (drown)

KJV renders: choke, take by the throat
