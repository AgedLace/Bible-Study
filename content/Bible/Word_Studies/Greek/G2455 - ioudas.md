# G2455 — Ἰούδας

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ἰούδας | Ioúdas |  | Judas (i.e. Jehudah) |

## Definition

 Judas (i.e. Jehudah), the name of ten Israelites; also of the posterity of one of them and its region

KJV renders: Juda(-h, -s); Jude
