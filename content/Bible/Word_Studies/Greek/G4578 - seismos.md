# G4578 — σεισμός

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| σεισμός | seismós |  | a commotion |

## Definition

 a commotion, i.e. (of the air) a gale, (of the ground) an earthquake

KJV renders: earthquake, tempest
