# G2621 — κατάκειμαι

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| κατάκειμαι | katákeimai |  | to lie down |

## Definition

 to lie down, i.e. (by implication) be sick; specially, to recline at a meal

KJV renders: keep, lie, sit at meat (down)
