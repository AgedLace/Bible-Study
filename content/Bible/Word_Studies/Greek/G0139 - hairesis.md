# G0139 — αἵρεσις

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| αἵρεσις | haíresis |  | properly |

## Definition

 properly, a choice, i.e. (specially) a party or (abstractly) disunion

KJV renders: heresy (which is the Greek word itself), sect
