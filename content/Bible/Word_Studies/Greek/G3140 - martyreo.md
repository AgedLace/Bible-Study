# G3140 — μαρτυρέω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| μαρτυρέω | martyréō |  | to be a witness |

## Definition

 to be a witness, i.e. testify (literally or figuratively)

KJV renders: charge, give (evidence), bear record, have (obtain, of) good (honest) report, be well reported of, testify, give (have) testimony, (be, bear, give, obtain) witness
