# G3114 — μακροθυμέω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| μακροθυμέω | makrothyméō |  | to be long-spirited |

## Definition

 to be long-spirited, i.e. (objectively) forbearing or (subjectively) patient

KJV renders: bear (suffer) long, be longsuffering, have (long) patience, be patient, patiently endure
