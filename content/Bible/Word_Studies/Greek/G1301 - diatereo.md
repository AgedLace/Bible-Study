# G1301 — διατηρέω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| διατηρέω | diatēréō |  | to watch thoroughly |

## Definition

 to watch thoroughly, i.e. (positively and transitively) to observe strictly, or (negatively and reflexively) to avoid wholly

KJV renders: keep
