# G3549 — νομοθετέω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| νομοθετέω | nomothetéō |  | to legislate |

## Definition

 to legislate, i.e. (passively) to have (the Mosaic) enactments injoined, be sanctioned (by them)

KJV renders: establish, receive the law
