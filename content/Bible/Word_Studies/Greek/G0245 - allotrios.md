| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| ἀλλότριος | allótrios |  | another's |

## Definition

 another's, i.e. not one's own; by extension foreign, not akin, hostile

KJV renders: alien, (an-)other (man's, men's), strange(-r)
