# G2168 — εὐχαριστέω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| εὐχαριστέω | eucharistéō |  | to be grateful |

## Definition

 to be grateful, i.e. (actively) to express gratitude (towards); specially, to say grace at a meal

KJV renders: (give) thank(-ful, -s)
