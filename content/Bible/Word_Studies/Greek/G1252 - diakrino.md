# G1252 — διακρίνω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| διακρίνω | diakrínō |  | to separate thoroughly |

## Definition

 to separate thoroughly, i.e. (literally and reflexively) to withdraw from, or (by implication) oppose; figuratively, to discriminate (by implication, decide), or (reflexively) hesitate

KJV renders: contend, make (to) differ(-ence), discern, doubt, judge, be partial, stagger, waver
