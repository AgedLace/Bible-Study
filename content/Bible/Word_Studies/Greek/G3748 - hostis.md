# G3748 — ὅστις

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| ὅστις | hóstis |  | which some |

## Definition

 which some, i.e. any that; also (definite) which same

KJV renders: X and (they), (such) as, (they) that, in that they, what(-soever), whereas ye, (they) which, who(-soever)
