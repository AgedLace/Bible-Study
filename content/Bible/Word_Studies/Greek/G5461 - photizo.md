# G5461 — φωτίζω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| φωτίζω | phōtízō |  | to shed rays |

## Definition

 to shed rays, i.e. to shine or (transitively) to brighten up (literally or figuratively)

KJV renders: enlighten, illuminate, (bring to, give) light, make to see
