# G1159 — δαπανάω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| δαπανάω | dapanáō |  | to expend |

## Definition

 to expend, i.e. (in a good sense) to incur cost, or (in a bad one) to waste

KJV renders: be at charges, consume, spend
