# G0281 — ἀμήν

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| ἀμήν | amḗn |  | properly |

## Definition

 properly, firm, i.e. (figuratively) trustworthy; adverbially, surely (often as interjection, so be it)

KJV renders: amen, verily
