# G5236 — ὑπερβολή

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| ὑπερβολή | hyperbolḗ |  | a throwing beyond others |

## Definition

 a throwing beyond others, i.e. (figuratively) supereminence; adverbially (with G1519 (εἰς) or G2596 (κατά)) pre- eminently

KJV renders: abundance, (far more) exceeding, excellency, more excellent, beyond (out of) measure
