# G4811 — συκοφαντέω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| συκοφαντέω | sykophantéō |  | to be a fig-informer (reporter of the law forbidding the exportation of figs from Greece) |

## Definition

 to be a fig-informer (reporter of the law forbidding the exportation of figs from Greece), "sycophant", i.e. (genitive and by extension) to defraud (exact unlawfully, extort)

KJV renders: accuse falsely, take by false accusation
