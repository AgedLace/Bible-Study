# G5302 — ὑστερέω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| ὑστερέω | hysteréō |  | to be later |

## Definition

 to be later, i.e. (by implication) to be inferior; generally, to fall short (be deficient)

KJV renders: come behind (short), be destitute, fail, lack, suffer need, (be in) want, be the worse
