# G2836 — κοιλία

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| κοιλία | koilía |  | a cavity |

## Definition

 a cavity, i.e. (especially) the abdomen; by implication, the matrix; figuratively, the heart

KJV renders: belly, womb
