# G4845 — συμπληρόω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |

| συμπληρόω | symplēróō |  | to implenish completely |

## Definition

 to implenish completely, i.e. (of space) to swamp (a boat), or (of time) to accomplish (passive, be complete)

KJV renders: (fully) come, fill up
