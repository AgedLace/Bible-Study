# G2588 — καρδία

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| καρδία | kardía |  | the heart |

## Definition

 the heart, i.e. (figuratively) the thoughts or feelings (mind); also (by analogy) the middle

KJV renders: (+ broken-)heart(-ed)
