# G0459 — ἄνομος

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| ἄνομος | ánomos |  | lawless |

## Definition

 lawless, i.e. (negatively) not subject to (the Jewish) law; (by implication, a Gentile), or (positively) wicked

KJV renders: without law, lawless, transgressor, unlawful, wicked
