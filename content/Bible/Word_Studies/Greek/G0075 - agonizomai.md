# G0075 — ἀγωνίζομαι

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| ἀγωνίζομαι | agōnízomai |  | to struggle |

## Definition

 to struggle, literally (to compete for a prize), figuratively (to contend with an adversary), or genitive case (to endeavor to accomplish something)

KJV renders: fight, labor fervently, strive
