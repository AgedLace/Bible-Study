# G3936 — παρίστημι

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| παρίστημι | parístēmi |  | to stand beside |

## Definition

 to stand beside, i.e. (transitively) to exhibit, proffer, (specially), recommend, (figuratively) substantiate; or (intransitively) to be at hand (or ready), aid

KJV renders: assist, bring before, command, commend, give presently, present, prove, provide, shew, stand (before, by, here, up, with), yield
