# G4355 — προσλαμβάνω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |

| προσλαμβάνω | proslambánō |  | to take to oneself |

## Definition

 to take to oneself, i.e. use (food), lead (aside), admit (to friendship or hospitality)

KJV renders: receive, take (unto)
