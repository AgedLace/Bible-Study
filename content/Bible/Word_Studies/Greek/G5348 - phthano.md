# G5348 — φθάνω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| φθάνω | phthánō |  | to be beforehand |

## Definition

 to be beforehand, i.e. anticipate or precede; by extension, to have arrived at

KJV renders: (already) attain, come, prevent
