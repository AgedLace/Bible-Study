# G0114 — ἀθετέω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| ἀθετέω | athetéō |  | to set aside |

## Definition

 to set aside, i.e. (by implication) to disesteem, neutralize or violate

KJV renders: cast off, despise, disannul, frustrate, bring to nought, reject
