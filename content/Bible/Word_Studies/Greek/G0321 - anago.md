# G0321 — ἀνάγω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| ἀνάγω | anágō |  | to lead up |

## Definition

 to lead up; by extension to bring out; specially, to sail away

KJV renders: bring (again, forth, up again), depart, launch (forth), lead (up), loose, offer, sail, set forth, take up
