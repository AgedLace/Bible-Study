# G2139 — εὐπερίστατος

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| εὐπερίστατος | euperístatos |  | well standing around |

## Definition

 well standing around, i.e. (a competitor) thwarting (a racer) in every direction (figuratively, of sin in genitive case)

KJV renders: which doth so easily beset
