# G1821 — ἐξαποστέλλω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |

| ἐξαποστέλλω | exapostéllō |  | to send away forth |

## Definition

 to send away forth, i.e. (on a mission) to despatch, or (peremptorily) to dismiss

KJV renders: send (away, forth, out)
