# G1438 — ἑαυτοῦ

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| ἑαυτοῦ | heautoû |  | him- (her- |

## Definition

 him- (her-, it-, them-, also (in conjunction with the personal pronoun of the other persons) my-, thy-, our-, your-) self (selves), etc.

KJV renders: alone, her (own, -self), (he) himself, his (own), itself, one (to) another, our (thine) own(-selves), + that she had, their (own, own selves), (of) them(-selves), they, thyself, you, your (own, own conceits, own selves, -selves)
