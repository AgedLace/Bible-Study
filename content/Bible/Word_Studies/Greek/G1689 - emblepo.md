# G1689 — ἐμβλέπω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| ἐμβλέπω | emblépō |  | to look on |

## Definition

 to look on, i.e. (relatively) to observe fixedly, or (absolutely) to discern clearly

KJV renders: behold, gaze up, look upon, (could) see
