| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| σχολή | scholḗ |  | properly |

## Definition

 properly, loitering (as a withholding of oneself from work) or leisure, i.e. (by implication) a "school" (as vacation from physical employment)

KJV renders: school
