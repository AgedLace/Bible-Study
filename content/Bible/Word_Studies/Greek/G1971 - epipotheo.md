# G1971 — ἐπιποθέω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| ἐπιποθέω | epipothéō |  | to dote upon |

## Definition

 to dote upon, i.e. intensely crave possession (lawfully or wrongfully)

KJV renders: (earnestly) desire (greatly), (greatly) long (after), lust
