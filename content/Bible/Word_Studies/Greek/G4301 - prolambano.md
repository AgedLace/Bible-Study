# G4301 — προλαμβάνω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| προλαμβάνω | prolambánō |  | to take in advance |

## Definition

 to take in advance, i.e. (literally) eat before others have an opportunity; (figuratively) to anticipate, surprise

KJV renders: come aforehand, overtake, take before
