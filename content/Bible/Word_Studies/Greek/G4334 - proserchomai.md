# G4334 — προσέρχομαι

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| προσέρχομαι | prosérchomai |  | to approach |

## Definition

 to approach, i.e. (literally) come near, visit, or (figuratively) worship, assent to

KJV renders: (as soon as he) come (unto), come thereunto, consent, draw near, go (near, to, unto)
