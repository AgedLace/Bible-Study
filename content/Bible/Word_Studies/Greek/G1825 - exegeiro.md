# G1825 — ἐξεγείρω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |

| ἐξεγείρω | exegeírō |  | to rouse fully |

## Definition

 to rouse fully, i.e. (figuratively) to resuscitate (from death), release (from infliction)

KJV renders: raise up
