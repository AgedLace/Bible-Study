# G0694 — ἀργύριον

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| ἀργύριον | argýrion |  | silvery |

## Definition

 silvery, i.e. (by implication) cash; specially, a silverling (i.e. drachma or shekel)

KJV renders: money, (piece of) silver (piece)
