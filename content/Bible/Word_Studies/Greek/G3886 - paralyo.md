| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |

| παραλύω | paralýō |  | to loosen beside |

## Definition

 to loosen beside, i.e. relax (perfect passive participle, paralyzed or enfeebled)

KJV renders: feeble, sick of the (taken with) palsy
