# G2865 — κομίζω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| κομίζω | komízō |  | properly |

## Definition

 properly, to provide for, i.e. (by implication) to carry off (as if from harm; genitive case obtain)

KJV renders: bring, receive
