# G2521 — κάθημαι

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| κάθημαι | káthēmai |  | and (to sit |

## Definition

 and (to sit; akin to the base of G1476 (ἑδραῖος)); to sit down; figuratively, to remain, reside

KJV renders: dwell, sit (by, down)
