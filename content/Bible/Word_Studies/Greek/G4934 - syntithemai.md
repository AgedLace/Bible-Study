# G4934 — συντίθεμαι

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| συντίθεμαι | syntíthemai |  | to place jointly |

## Definition

 to place jointly, i.e. (figuratively) to consent (bargain, stipulate), concur

KJV renders: agree, assent, covenant
