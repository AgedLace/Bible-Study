# G4374 — προσφέρω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| προσφέρω | prosphérō |  | to bear towards |

## Definition

 to bear towards, i.e. lead to, tender (especially to God), treat

KJV renders: bring (to, unto), deal with, do, offer (unto, up), present unto, put to
