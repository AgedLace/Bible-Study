# G5242 — ὑπερέχω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| ὑπερέχω | hyperéchō |  | to hold oneself above |

## Definition

 to hold oneself above, i.e. (figuratively) to excel; participle (as adjective, or neuter as noun) superior, superiority

KJV renders: better, excellency, higher, pass, supreme
