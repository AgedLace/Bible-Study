# G4916 — συνθάπτω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| συνθάπτω | syntháptō |  | to inter in company with |

## Definition

 to inter in company with, i.e. (figuratively) to assimilate spiritually (to Christ by a sepulture as to sin)

KJV renders: bury with
