| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |

| διαδίδωμι | diadídōmi |  | to give throughout a crowd |

## Definition

 to give throughout a crowd, i.e. deal out; also to deliver over (as to a successor)

KJV renders: (make) distribute(-ion), divide, give
