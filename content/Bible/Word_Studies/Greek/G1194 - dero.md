# G1194 — δέρω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| δέρω | dérō |  | properly |

## Definition

 properly, to flay, i.e. (by implication) to scourge, or (by analogy) to thrash

KJV renders: beat, smite
