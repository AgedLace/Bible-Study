# G2205 — ζῆλος

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| ζῆλος | zēlos |  | properly |

## Definition

 properly, heat, i.e. (figuratively) "zeal" (in a favorable sense, ardor; in an unfavorable one, jealousy, as of a husband (figuratively, of God), or an enemy, malice)

KJV renders: emulation, envy(-ing), fervent mind, indignation, jealousy, zeal
