# G0626 — ἀπολογέομαι

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| ἀπολογέομαι | apologéomai |  | to give an account (legal plea) of oneself |

## Definition

 to give an account (legal plea) of oneself, i.e. exculpate (self)

KJV renders: answer (for self), make defence, excuse (self), speak for self
