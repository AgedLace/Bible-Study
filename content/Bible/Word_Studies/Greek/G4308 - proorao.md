# G4308 — προοράω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| προοράω | prooráō |  | to behold in advance |

## Definition

 to behold in advance, i.e. (actively) to notice (another) previously, or (middle voice) to keep in (one's own) view

KJV renders: foresee, see before
