# G0611 — ἀποκρίνομαι

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| ἀποκρίνομαι | apokrínomai |  | to conclude for oneself |

## Definition

 to conclude for oneself, i.e. (by implication) to respond; by Hebraism (compare H06030) to begin to speak (where an address is expected)

KJV renders: answer
