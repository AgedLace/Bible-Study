# G1690 — ἐμβριμάομαι

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| ἐμβριμάομαι | embrimáomai |  | to have indignation on |

## Definition

 to have indignation on, i.e. (transitively) to blame, (intransitively) to sigh with chagrin, (specially) to sternly enjoin

KJV renders: straitly charge, groan, murmur against
