# G1080 — γεννάω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| γεννάω | gennáō |  | to procreate (properly |

## Definition

 to procreate (properly, of the father, but by extension of the mother); figuratively, to regenerate

KJV renders: bear, beget, be born, bring forth, conceive, be delivered of, gender, make, spring
