# G4138 — πλήρωμα

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| πλήρωμα | plḗrōma |  | repletion or completion |

## Definition

 repletion or completion, i.e. (subjectively) what fills (as contents, supplement, copiousness, multitude), or (objectively) what is filled (as container, performance, period)

KJV renders: which is put in to fill up, piece that filled up, fulfilling, full, fulness
