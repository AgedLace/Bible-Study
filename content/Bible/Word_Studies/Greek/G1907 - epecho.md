# G1907 — ἐπέχω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| ἐπέχω | epéchō |  | to hold upon |

## Definition

 to hold upon, i.e. (by implication) to retain; (by extension) to detain; (with implication, of G3563 (νοῦς)) to pay attention to

KJV renders: give (take) heed unto, hold forth, mark, stay
