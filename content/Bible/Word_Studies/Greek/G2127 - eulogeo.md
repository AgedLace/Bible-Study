# G2127 — εὐλογέω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| εὐλογέω | eulogéō |  | to speak well of |

## Definition

 to speak well of, i.e. (religiously) to bless (thank or invoke a benediction upon, prosper)

KJV renders: bless, praise
