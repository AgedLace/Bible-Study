# G0106 — ἄζυμος

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| ἄζυμος | ázymos |  | unleavened |

## Definition

 unleavened, i.e. (figuratively) uncorrupted; (in the neutral plural) specially (by implication) the Passover week

KJV renders: unleavened (bread)
