# G0937 — βασιλικός

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| βασιλικός | basilikós |  | regal (in relation) |

## Definition

 regal (in relation), i.e. (literally) belonging to (or befitting) the sovereign (as land, dress, or a courtier), or (figuratively) preeminent

KJV renders: king's, nobleman, royal
