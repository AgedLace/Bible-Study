# G3623 — οἰκονόμος

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| οἰκονόμος | oikonómos |  | a house-distributor (i.e. manager) |

## Definition

 a house-distributor (i.e. manager), or overseer, i.e. an employee in that capacity; by extension, a fiscal agent (treasurer); figuratively, a preacher (of the Gospel)

KJV renders: chamberlain, governor, steward
