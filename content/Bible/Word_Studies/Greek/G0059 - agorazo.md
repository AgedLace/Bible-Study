# G0059 — ἀγοράζω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| ἀγοράζω | agorázō |  | properly |

## Definition

 properly, to go to market, i.e. (by implication) to purchase; specially, to redeem

KJV renders: buy, redeem
