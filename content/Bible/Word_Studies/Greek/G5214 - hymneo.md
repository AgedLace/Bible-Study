# G5214 — ὑμνέω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| ὑμνέω | hymnéō |  | to hymn |

## Definition

 to hymn, i.e. sing a religious ode; by implication, to celebrate (God) in song

KJV renders: sing a hymn (praise unto)
