# G3854 — παραγίνομαι

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| παραγίνομαι | paragínomai |  | to become near |

## Definition

 to become near, i.e. approach (have arrived); by implication, to appear publicly

KJV renders: come, go, be present
