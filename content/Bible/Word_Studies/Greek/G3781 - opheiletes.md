# G3781 — ὀφειλέτης

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| ὀφειλέτης | opheilétēs |  | an ower |

## Definition

 an ower, i.e. person indebted; figuratively, a delinquent; morally, a transgressor (against God)

KJV renders: debtor, which owed, sinner
