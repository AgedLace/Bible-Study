# G4863 — συνάγω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| συνάγω | synágō |  | to lead together |

## Definition

 to lead together, i.e. collect or convene; specially, to entertain (hospitably)

KJV renders: + accompany, assemble (selves, together), bestow, come together, gather (selves together, up, together), lead into, resort, take in
