| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| θυμίαμα | thymíama |  | an aroma |

## Definition

 an aroma, i.e. fragrant powder burnt in religious service; by implication, the burning itself

KJV renders: incense, odour
