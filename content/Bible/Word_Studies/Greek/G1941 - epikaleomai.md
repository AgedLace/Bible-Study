# G1941 — ἐπικαλέομαι

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| ἐπικαλέομαι | epikaléomai |  | to entitle |

## Definition

 to entitle; by implication, to invoke (for aid, worship, testimony, decision, etc.)

KJV renders: appeal (unto), call (on, upon), surname
