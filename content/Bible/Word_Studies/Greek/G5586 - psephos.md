# G5586 — ψῆφος

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| ψῆφος | psēphos |  | a pebble (as worn smooth by handling) |

## Definition

 a pebble (as worn smooth by handling), i.e. (by implication, of use as a counter or ballot) a verdict (of acquittal) or ticket (of admission); a vote

KJV renders: stone, voice
