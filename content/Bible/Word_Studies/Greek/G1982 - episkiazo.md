# G1982 — ἐπισκιάζω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| ἐπισκιάζω | episkiázō |  | to cast a shade upon |

## Definition

 to cast a shade upon, i.e. (by analogy) to envelop in a haze of brilliancy; figuratively, to invest with preternatural influence

KJV renders: overshadow
