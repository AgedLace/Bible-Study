# G4350 — προσκόπτω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| προσκόπτω | proskóptō |  | to strike at |

## Definition

 to strike at, i.e. surge against (as water); specially, to stub on, i.e. trip up (literally or figuratively)

KJV renders: beat upon, dash, stumble (at)
