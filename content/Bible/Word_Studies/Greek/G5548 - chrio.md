# G5548 — χρίω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| χρίω | chríō |  | to smear or rub with oil |

## Definition

 to smear or rub with oil, i.e. (by implication) to consecrate to an office or religious service

KJV renders: anoint
