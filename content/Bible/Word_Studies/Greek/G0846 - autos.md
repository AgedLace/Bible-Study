# G0846 — αὐτός

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| αὐτός | autós |  | the reflexive pronoun self |

## Definition

 the reflexive pronoun self, used (alone or in the comparative G1438 (ἑαυτοῦ)) of the third person , and (with the proper personal pronoun) of the other persons

KJV renders: her, it(-self), one, the other, (mine) own, said, (self-), the) same, ((him-, my-, thy- )self, (your-)selves, she, that, their(-s), them(-selves), there(-at, - by, -in, -into, -of, -on, -with), they, (these) things, this (man), those, together, very, which
