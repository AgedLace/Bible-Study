# G0472 — ἀντέχομαι

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| ἀντέχομαι | antéchomai |  | to hold oneself opposite to |

## Definition

 to hold oneself opposite to, i.e. (by implication) adhere to; by extension to care for

KJV renders: hold fast, hold to, support
