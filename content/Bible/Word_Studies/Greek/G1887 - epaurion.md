# G1887 — ἐπαύριον

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| ἐπαύριον | epaúrion |  | occurring on the succeeding day |

## Definition

KJV renders: day following, morrow, next day (after)
