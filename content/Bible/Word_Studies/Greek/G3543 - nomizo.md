# G3543 — νομίζω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| νομίζω | nomízō |  | properly |

## Definition

 properly, to do by law (usage), i.e. to accustom (passively, be usual); by extension, to deem or regard

KJV renders: suppose, thing, be wont
