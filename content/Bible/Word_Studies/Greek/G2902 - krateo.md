# G2902 — κρατέω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| κρατέω | kratéō |  | to use strength |

## Definition

 to use strength, i.e. seize or retain (literally or figuratively)

KJV renders: hold (by, fast), keep, lay hand (hold) on, obtain, retain, take (by)
