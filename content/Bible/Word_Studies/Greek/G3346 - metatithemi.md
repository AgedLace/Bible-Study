# G3346 — μετατίθημι

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| μετατίθημι | metatíthēmi |  | to transfer |

## Definition

 to transfer, i.e. (literally) transport, (by implication) exchange, (reflexively) change sides, or (figuratively) pervert

KJV renders: carry over, change, remove, translate, turn
