# G2799 — κλαίω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| κλαίω | klaíō |  | to sob |

## Definition

 to sob, i.e. wail aloud (whereas 1145 is rather to cry silently)

KJV renders: bewail, weep
