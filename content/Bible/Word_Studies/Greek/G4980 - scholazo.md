# G4980 — σχολάζω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| σχολάζω | scholázō |  | to take a holiday |

## Definition

 to take a holiday, i.e. be at leisure for (by implication, devote oneself wholly to); figuratively, to be vacant (of a house)

KJV renders: empty, give self
