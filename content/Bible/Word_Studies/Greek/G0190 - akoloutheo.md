# G0190 — ἀκολουθέω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| ἀκολουθέω | akolouthéō |  | properly |

## Definition

 properly, to be in the same way with, i.e. to accompany (specially, as a disciple)

KJV renders: follow, reach
