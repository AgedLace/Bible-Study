# G0932 — βασιλεία

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| βασιλεία | basileía |  | properly |

## Definition

 properly, royalty, i.e. (abstractly) rule, or (concretely) a realm (literally or figuratively)

KJV renders: kingdom, + reign
