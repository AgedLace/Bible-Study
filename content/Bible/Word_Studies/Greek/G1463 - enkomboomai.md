# G1463 — ἐγκομβόομαι

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| ἐγκομβόομαι | enkombóomai |  | to engirdle oneself (for labor) |

## Definition

 to engirdle oneself (for labor), i.e. figuratively (the apron as being a badge of servitude) to wear (in token of mutual deference)

KJV renders: be clothed with
