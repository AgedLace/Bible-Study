# G4286 — πρόθεσις

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| πρόθεσις | próthesis |  | a setting forth |

## Definition

 a setting forth, i.e. (figuratively) proposal (intention); specially, the show-bread (in the Temple) as exposed before God

KJV renders: purpose, shew(-bread)
