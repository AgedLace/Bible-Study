# G2165 — εὐφραίνω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| εὐφραίνω | euphraínō |  | to put (middle voice or passively |

## Definition

 to put (middle voice or passively, be) in a good frame of mind, i.e. rejoice

KJV renders: fare, make glad, be (make) merry, rejoice
