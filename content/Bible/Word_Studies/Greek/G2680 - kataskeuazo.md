# G2680 — κατασκευάζω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| κατασκευάζω | kataskeuázō |  | to prepare thoroughly (properly |

## Definition

 to prepare thoroughly (properly, by external equipment; whereas G2090 (ἑτοιμάζω) refers rather to internal fitness); by implication, to construct, create

KJV renders: build, make, ordain, prepare
