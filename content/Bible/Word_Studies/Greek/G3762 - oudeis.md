| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |

## Definition

 not even one (man, woman or thing), i.e. none, nobody, nothing

KJV renders: any (man), aught, man, neither any (thing), never (man), no (man), none (+ of these things), not (any, at all, -thing), nought
