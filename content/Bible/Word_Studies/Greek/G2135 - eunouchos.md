# G2135 — εὐνοῦχος

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| εὐνοῦχος | eunoûchos |  | a castrated person (such being employed in Oriental bed-chambers) |

## Definition

 a castrated person (such being employed in Oriental bed-chambers); by extension an impotent or unmarried man; by implication, a chamberlain (state-officer)

KJV renders: eunuch
