# G4237 — πρασιά

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| πρασιά | prasiá |  | a garden plot |

## Definition

 a garden plot, i.e. (by implication, of regular beds) a row (repeated in plural by Hebraism, to indicate an arrangement)

KJV renders: in ranks
