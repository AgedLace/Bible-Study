# G1788 — ἐντρέπω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| ἐντρέπω | entrépō |  | to invert |

## Definition

 to invert, i.e. (figuratively and reflexively) in a good sense, to respect; or in a bad one, to confound

KJV renders: regard, (give) reference, shame
