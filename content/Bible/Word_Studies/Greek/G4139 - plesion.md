| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |

| πλησίον | plēsíon |  | (adverbially) close by |

## Definition

 (adverbially) close by; as noun, a neighbor, i.e. fellow (as man, countryman, Christian or friend)

KJV renders: near, neighbour
