# G3825 — πάλιν

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| πάλιν | pálin |  | (adverbially) anew |

## Definition

 (adverbially) anew, i.e. (of place) back, (of time) once more, or (conjunctionally) furthermore or on the other hand

KJV renders: again
