# G5613 — ὡς

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| ὡς | hōs |  | which how |

## Definition

 which how, i.e. in that manner (very variously used, as follows)

KJV renders: about, after (that), (according) as (it had been, it were), as soon (as), even as (like), for, how (greatly), like (as, unto), since, so (that), that, to wit, unto, when(-soever), while, X with all speed
