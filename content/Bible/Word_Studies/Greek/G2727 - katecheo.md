# G2727 — κατηχέω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| κατηχέω | katēchéō |  | to sound down into the ears |

## Definition

 to sound down into the ears, i.e. (by implication) to indoctrinate ("catechize") or (genitive case) to apprise of

KJV renders: inform, instruct, teach
