# G4676 — σουδάριον

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| σουδάριον | soudárion |  | a sudarium (sweat-cloth) |

## Definition

 a sudarium (sweat-cloth), i.e. towel (for wiping the perspiration from the face, or binding the face of a corpse)

KJV renders: handkerchief, napkin
