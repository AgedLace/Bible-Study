# G1199 — δεσμόν

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| δεσμόν | desmón |  | a band |

## Definition

 a band, i.e. ligament (of the body) or shackle (of a prisoner); figuratively, an impediment or disability

KJV renders: band, bond, chain, string
