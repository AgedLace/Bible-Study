# G4357 — προσμένω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| προσμένω | prosménō |  | to stay further |

## Definition

 to stay further, i.e. remain in a place, with a person; figuratively, to adhere to, persevere in

KJV renders: abide still, be with, cleave unto, continue in (with)
