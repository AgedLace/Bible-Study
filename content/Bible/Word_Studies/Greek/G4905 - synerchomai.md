# G4905 — συνέρχομαι

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| συνέρχομαι | synérchomai |  | to convene |

## Definition

 to convene, depart in company with, associate with, or (specially), cohabit (conjugally)

KJV renders: accompany, assemble (with), come (together), come (company, go) with, resort
