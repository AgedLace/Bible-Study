# G3195 — μέλλω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| μέλλω | méllō |  | to intend |

## Definition

 to intend, i.e. be about to be, do, or suffer something (of persons or things, especially events; in the sense of purpose, duty, necessity, probability, possibility, or hesitation)

KJV renders: about, after that, be (almost), (that which is, things, + which was for) to come, intend, was to (be), mean, mind, be at the point, (be) ready, + return, shall (begin), (which, that) should (after, afterwards, hereafter) tarry, which was for, will, would, be yet
