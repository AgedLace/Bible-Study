# G0853 — ἀφανίζω

| Lemma | Translit | Pronunciation | Short definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| ἀφανίζω | aphanízō |  | to render unapparent |

## Definition

 to render unapparent, i.e. (actively) consume (becloud), or (passively) disappear (be destroyed)

KJV renders: corrupt, disfigure, perish, vanish away
