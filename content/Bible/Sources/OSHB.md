# OSHB v2.2 — Open Scriptures Hebrew Bible

Aramaic word surface text for the gap verses only (Daniel 2:4-7:28, Ezra 4:8-6:18 and 7:12-26, Jeremiah 10:11), reproduced under CC BY 4.0 (https://opendocs.bible/). OSHB is never loaded across the Hebrew OT: same base text as the BSB TSVs but not byte-identical.

Verse text elsewhere in this vault, and all Strong's numbers, glosses, transliterations and parsing, are BSB (public domain). Qere readings are used where OSHB marks a ketiv/qere variant.
