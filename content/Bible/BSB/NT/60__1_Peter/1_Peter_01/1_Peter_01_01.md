# 1 Peter 1:1

## A Greeting from Peter

> [!quote] BSB
> "Peter, an apostle of Jesus Christ, To the elect who are exiles of the Dispersion throughout Pontus, Galatia, Cappadocia, Asia, and Bithynia, chosen" ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Literally *To the elect sojourners of the Diaspora of Pontus, Galatia, Cappadocia, Asia, and Bithynia*. These provinces were located in what is now Turkey.
%%FN-END%%
