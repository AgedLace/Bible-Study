# Luke 19:13

> [!quote] BSB
> "Beforehand, he called ten of his servants and gave them ten minas. ‘Conduct business with this until I return,’ he said." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- That is, he gave each servant one mina. A mina was most likely a silver coin worth a hundred drachmas, that is, about a hundred days’ wages.
%%FN-END%%
