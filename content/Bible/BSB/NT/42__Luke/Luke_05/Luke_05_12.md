# Luke 5:12

## The Leper’s Prayer

> [!quote] BSB
> "While Jesus was in one of the towns, a man came along who was covered with leprosy. When he saw Jesus, he fell facedown and begged Him, “Lord, if You are willing, You can make me clean.”" ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Leprosy was a term used for various skin diseases. See Leviticus 13.
%%FN-END%%
