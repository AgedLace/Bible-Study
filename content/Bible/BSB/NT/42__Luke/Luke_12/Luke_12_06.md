# Luke 12:6

> [!quote] BSB
> "Are not five sparrows sold for two pennies? Yet not one of them is forgotten by God." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Greek *two assaria*; an assarion was a Roman copper coin worth about 1/16 of a denarius.
