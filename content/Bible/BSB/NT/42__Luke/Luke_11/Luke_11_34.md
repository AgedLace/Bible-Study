# Luke 11:34

> [!quote] BSB
> "Your eye is the lamp of your body. When your eyes are good, your whole body also is full of light. But when they are bad, your body is full of darkness." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Literally *When your eye is sound*; see Proverbs 22:9.
- Literally *when it is evil*; see Proverbs 23:6 and Proverbs 28:22.
%%FN-END%%
