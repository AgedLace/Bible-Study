# Luke 16:7

> [!quote] BSB
> "Then he asked another, ‘And how much do you owe?’ ‘A hundred measures of wheat,’ he replied. ‘Take your bill and write eighty,’ he told him." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Greek *‘A hundred cors of wheat’*; that is, approximately 1,000 bushels or 35,000 liters (probably about 30 tons or 27 metric tons of wheat)
%%FN-END%%
