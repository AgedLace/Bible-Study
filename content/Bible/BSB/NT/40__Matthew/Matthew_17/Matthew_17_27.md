# Matthew 17:27

> [!quote] BSB
> "“But so that we may not offend them, go to the sea, cast a hook, and take the first fish you catch. When you open its mouth, you will find a four-drachma coin. Take it and give it to them for My tax and yours.”" ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Greek *a stater*; that is, a silver coin worth approximately one shekel
