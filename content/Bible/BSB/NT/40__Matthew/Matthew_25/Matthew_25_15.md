# Matthew 25:15

> [!quote] BSB
> "To one he gave five talents, to another two talents, and to another one talent—each according to his own ability. And he went on his journey." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- A talent was worth about twenty years’ wages for a laborer.
- Omitted-verse note (see previous verse): Or * **[v.15:]** ... And he went on his journey at once.  **[v.16:]** ... went and put them to work*. Translators vary as to the placement of the Greek adverb *eutheōs* (at once) at the end of verse 15 or at the beginning of verse 16.
%%FN-END%%
