# Matthew 25:16

> [!quote] BSB
> "The servant who had received the five talents went at once and put them to work and gained five more." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Or * **[v.15:]** ... And he went on his journey at once.  **[v.16:]** ... went and put them to work*. Translators vary as to the placement of the Greek adverb *eutheōs* (at once) at the end of verse 15 or at the beginning of verse 16.
