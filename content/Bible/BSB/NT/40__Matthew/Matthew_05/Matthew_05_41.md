# Matthew 5:41

> [!quote] BSB
> "and if someone forces you to go one mile, go with him two miles." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Greek *one milion*; that is, a Roman mile, approximately 4,855 feet or 1,480 meters
- Literally *go with him two.*
%%FN-END%%
