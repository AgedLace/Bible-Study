# Matthew 5:22

> [!quote] BSB
> "But I tell you that anyone who is angry with his brother will be subject to judgment. Again, anyone who says to his brother, ‘Raca,’ will be subject to the Sanhedrin. But anyone who says, ‘You fool!’ will be subject to the fire of hell." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- BYZ and TR include *without cause*.
- *Raca* is an Aramaic expression of contempt.
- Or *the Council*
- Or *the hell of fire*; Greek *the Gehenna of fire*
%%FN-END%%
