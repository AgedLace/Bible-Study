# John 5:3

> [!quote] BSB
> "On these walkways lay a great number of the sick, the blind, the lame, and the paralyzed." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Literally *In these*
- NE, BYZ, and TR include *awaiting the moving of the waters.  **[v.4:]** For from time to time an angel descended into the pool and stirred the water. As soon as it was stirred, the first to enter the pool would be healed of his disease.*
