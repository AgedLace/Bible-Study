# John 12:13

> [!quote] BSB
> "They took palm branches and went out to meet Him, shouting: “Hosanna!” “Blessed is He who comes in the name of the Lord!” “Blessed is the King of Israel!”" ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *Hosanna* is a transliteration of the Hebrew *Hosia-na*, meaning *Save, we pray* or *Save now*, which became a shout of praise; see Psalm 118:25.
- Psalm 118:26
%%FN-END%%
