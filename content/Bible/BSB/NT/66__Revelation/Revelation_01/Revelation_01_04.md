# Revelation 1:4

## John Greets the Seven Churches

> [!quote] BSB
> "John, To the seven churches in the province of Asia: Grace and peace to you from Him who is and was and is to come, and from the seven spirits before His throne," ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Literally *in Asia*; Asia was a Roman province in what is now western Turkey.
- Or *the sevenfold Spirit*
%%FN-END%%
