# Revelation 21:17

> [!quote] BSB
> "And he measured its wall to be 144 cubits, by the human measure the angel was using." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *144 cubits* is approximately 216 feet or 65.8 meters. The measure could indicate either height or thickness.
%%FN-END%%
