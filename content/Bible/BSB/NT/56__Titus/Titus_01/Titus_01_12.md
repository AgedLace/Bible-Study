# Titus 1:12

> [!quote] BSB
> "As one of their own prophets has said, “Cretans are always liars, evil beasts, lazy gluttons.”" ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- This quote, also known as the Epimenides paradox, has been attributed to the Cretan philosopher Epimenides of Knossos.
