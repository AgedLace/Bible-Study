# 1 Corinthians 16:19

## Signature and Final Greetings

> [!quote] BSB
> "The churches in the province of Asia send you greetings. Aquila and Prisca greet you warmly in the Lord, and so does the church that meets at their house." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Literally *in Asia*; Asia was a Roman province in what is now western Turkey.
- *Prisca* is a variant of *Priscilla*; see Acts 18:2.
%%FN-END%%
