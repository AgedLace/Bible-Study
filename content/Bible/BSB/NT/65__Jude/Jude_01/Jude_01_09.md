# Jude 1:9

> [!quote] BSB
> "But even the archangel Michael, when he disputed with the devil over the body of Moses, did not presume to bring a slanderous charge against him, but said, “The Lord rebuke you!”" ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- This account is attributed by Origen to the Testament of Moses, also called the Assumption of Moses.
