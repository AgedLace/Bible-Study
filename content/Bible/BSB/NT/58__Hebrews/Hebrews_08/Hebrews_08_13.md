# Hebrews 8:13

> [!quote] BSB
> "By speaking of a new covenant, He has made the first one obsolete; and what is obsolete and aging will soon disappear." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Literally *In saying new*; here and in Hebrews 9:1 and 18, *covenant* is included for clarity but is not contained in the Greek. A broader interpretation could also include *priesthood* or *tabernacle*.
%%FN-END%%
