# Acts 17:28

> [!quote] BSB
> "‘For in Him we live and move and have our being.’ As some of your own poets have said, ‘We are His offspring.’" ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- This is probably a quote from the Cretan philosopher Epimenides of Knossos.
- This is probably a quote from the poem “Phainomena” by the Cilician philosopher Aratus.
%%FN-END%%
