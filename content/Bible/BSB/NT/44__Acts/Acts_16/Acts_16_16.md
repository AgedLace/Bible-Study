# Acts 16:16

## Paul and Silas Imprisoned

> [!quote] BSB
> "One day as we were going to the place of prayer, we were met by a slave girl with a spirit of divination, who earned a large income for her masters by fortune-telling." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Greek *a spirit of Python*; that is, a spirit of divination named after the mythical serpent slain by Apollo
%%FN-END%%
