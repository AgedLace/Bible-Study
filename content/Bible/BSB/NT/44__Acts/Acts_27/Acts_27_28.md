# Acts 27:28

> [!quote] BSB
> "They took soundings and found that the water was twenty fathoms deep. Going a little farther, they took another set of soundings that read fifteen fathoms." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *20 fathoms* is approximately 120 feet or 36.6 meters.
- *15 fathoms* is approximately 90 feet or 27.4 meters.
%%FN-END%%
