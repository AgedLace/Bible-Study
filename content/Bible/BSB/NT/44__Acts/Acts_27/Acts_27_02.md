# Acts 27:2

> [!quote] BSB
> "We boarded an Adramyttian ship about to sail for ports along the coast of Asia, and we put out to sea. Aristarchus, a Macedonian from Thessalonica, was with us." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Literally *sail to the places along Asia*; Asia was a Roman province in what is now western Turkey.
%%FN-END%%
