# Acts 9:36

## The Raising of Tabitha

> [!quote] BSB
> "In Joppa there was a disciple named Tabitha (which is translated as Dorcas), who was always occupied with works of kindness and charity." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *Tabitha* in Aramaic and *Dorcas* in Greek both mean *gazelle*.
