# Acts 24:18

> [!quote] BSB
> "At the time they found me in the temple, I was ceremonially clean and was not inciting a crowd or an uproar. But there are some Jews from the province of Asia" ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Literally *from Asia*; Asia was a Roman province in what is now western Turkey.
