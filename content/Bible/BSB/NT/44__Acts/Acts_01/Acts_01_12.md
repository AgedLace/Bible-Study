# Acts 1:12

## Matthias Replaces Judas

> [!quote] BSB
> "Then they returned to Jerusalem from the Mount of Olives, which is near the city, a Sabbath day’s journey away." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *A Sabbath day’s journey* is 2,000 cubits (approximately 3,000 feet or 914.4 meters)
%%FN-END%%
