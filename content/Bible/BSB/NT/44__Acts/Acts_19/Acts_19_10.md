# Acts 19:10

> [!quote] BSB
> "This continued for two years, so that everyone who lived in the province of Asia, Jews and Greeks alike, heard the word of the Lord." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Literally *in Asia*; Asia was a Roman province in what is now western Turkey; similarly in verses 22, 26, 27, and 31.
