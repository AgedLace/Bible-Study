# Acts 19:19

> [!quote] BSB
> "And a number of those who had practiced magic arts brought their books and burned them in front of everyone. When the value of the books was calculated, the total came to fifty thousand drachmas." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Or *fifty thousand pieces of silver.* A drachma was a silver coin worth about one day’s wages.
%%FN-END%%
