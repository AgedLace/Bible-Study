# Mark 12:42

> [!quote] BSB
> "Then one poor widow came and put in two small copper coins, which amounted to a small fraction of a denarius." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Greek *put in two lepta, which is a kodrantēs*; a lepton was a Jewish coin of bronze or copper worth about 1/128 of a denarius.
%%FN-END%%
