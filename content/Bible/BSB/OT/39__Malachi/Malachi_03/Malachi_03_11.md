# Malachi 3:11

> [!quote] BSB
> "I will rebuke the devourer for you, so that it will not destroy the fruits of your land, and the vine in your field will not fail to produce fruit,” says the LORD of Hosts." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *The devourer* is probably a name for a crop-destroying pest.
