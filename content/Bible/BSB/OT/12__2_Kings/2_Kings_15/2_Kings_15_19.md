# 2 Kings 15:19

> [!quote] BSB
> "Then Pul king of Assyria invaded the land, and Menahem gave Pul a thousand talents of silver in order to gain his support and strengthen his own grip on the kingdom." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *Pul* is another name for *Tiglath-pileser*; see verse 29.
- *1,000 talents* is approximately 37.7 tons or 34.2 metric tons of silver.
%%FN-END%%
