# 2 Kings 11:2

> [!quote] BSB
> "But Jehosheba daughter of King Joram, the sister of Ahaziah, took Joash son of Ahaziah and stole him away from among the sons of the king who were being murdered. She put him and his nurse in a bedroom to hide him from Athaliah, and he was not killed." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *Jehosheba* is a variant of *Jehoshabeath*; see 2 Chronicles 22:11.
- *Joram* is a variant spelling of *Jehoram*.
%%FN-END%%
