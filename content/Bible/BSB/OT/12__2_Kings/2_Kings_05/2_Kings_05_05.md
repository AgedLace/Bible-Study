# 2 Kings 5:5

> [!quote] BSB
> "“Go now,” said the king of Aram, “and I will send you with a letter to the king of Israel.” So Naaman departed, taking with him ten talents of silver, six thousand shekels of gold, and ten sets of clothing." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *10 talents* is approximately 754 pounds or 342 kilograms of silver.
- *6,000 shekels* is approximately 150.8 pounds or 68.4 kilograms of gold.
%%FN-END%%
