# 2 Kings 6:25

> [!quote] BSB
> "So there was a great famine in Samaria. Indeed, they besieged the city so long that a donkey’s head sold for eighty shekels of silver, and a quarter cab of dove’s dung sold for five shekels of silver." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *80 shekels* is approximately 2 pounds or 907.2 grams of silver.
- Or *a quarter cab of seed pods*; that is, approximately 0.28 dry quarts or 0.31 liters
- *5 shekels* is approximately 2 ounces or 57 grams of silver.
%%FN-END%%
