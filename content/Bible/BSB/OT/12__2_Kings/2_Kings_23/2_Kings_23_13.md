# 2 Kings 23:13

> [!quote] BSB
> "The king also desecrated the high places east of Jerusalem, to the south of the Mount of Corruption, which King Solomon of Israel had built for Ashtoreth the abomination of the Sidonians, for Chemosh the abomination of the Moabites, and for Milcom the abomination of the Ammonites." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *Milcom* is a variant of *Molech*; see Leviticus 18:21 and 1 Kings 11:7.
%%FN-END%%
