# 2 Kings 18:4

> [!quote] BSB
> "He removed the high places, shattered the sacred pillars, and cut down the Asherah poles. He also demolished the bronze snake called Nehushtan that Moses had made, for up to that time the Israelites had burned incense to it." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *Nehushtan* sounds like the Hebrew for *bronze* and also for *snake*.
