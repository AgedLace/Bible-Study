# 2 Kings 18:14

> [!quote] BSB
> "So Hezekiah king of Judah sent word to the king of Assyria at Lachish, saying, “I have done wrong; withdraw from me, and I will pay whatever you demand from me.” And the king of Assyria exacted from Hezekiah king of Judah three hundred talents of silver and thirty talents of gold." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *300 talents* is approximately 11.3 tons or 10.3 metric tons of silver.
- *30 talents* is approximately 1.13 tons or 1.03 metric tons of gold.
%%FN-END%%
