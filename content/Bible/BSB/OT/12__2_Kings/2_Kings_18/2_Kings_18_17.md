# 2 Kings 18:17

## Sennacherib Threatens Jerusalem

> [!quote] BSB
> "Nevertheless, the king of Assyria sent the Tartan, the Rabsaris, and the Rabshakeh, along with a great army, from Lachish to King Hezekiah at Jerusalem. They advanced up to Jerusalem and stationed themselves by the aqueduct of the upper pool, on the road to the Launderer’s Field." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Hebrew *Tartan* is the title of a field marshal, general, or commander in the Assyrian military.
- Hebrew *Rabsaris* is the title of the chief eunuch in the Assyrian military.
- Hebrew *Rabshakeh* is the title of a high-ranking Assyrian military officer; here and throughout chapters 18 and 19, as well as Isaiah 36 and 37.
%%FN-END%%
