# 2 Kings 14:13

> [!quote] BSB
> "There at Beth-shemesh, Jehoash king of Israel captured Amaziah king of Judah, the son of Joash, the son of Ahaziah. Then Jehoash went to Jerusalem and broke down the wall of Jerusalem from the Ephraim Gate to the Corner Gate—a section of four hundred cubits." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *400 cubits* is approximately 600 feet or 182.9 meters.
%%FN-END%%
