# 2 Kings 25:17

> [!quote] BSB
> "Each pillar was eighteen cubits tall. The bronze capital atop one pillar was three cubits high, with a network of bronze pomegranates all around. The second pillar, with its network, was similar." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *18 cubits* is approximately 27 feet or 8.2 meters.
- *3 cubits* is approximately 4.5 feet or 1.4 meters.
%%FN-END%%
