# 2 Kings 25:23

> [!quote] BSB
> "When all the commanders of the armies and their men heard that the king of Babylon had appointed Gedaliah as governor, they came to Gedaliah at Mizpah—Ishmael son of Nethaniah, Johanan son of Kareah, Seraiah son of Tanhumeth the Netophathite, and Jaazaniah son of the Maacathite, as well as their men." ^text

> [!note] Footnotes
- *Jaazaniah* is a variant of *Jezaniah*; see Jeremiah 40:8.
