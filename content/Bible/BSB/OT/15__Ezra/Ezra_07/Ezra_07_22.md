# Ezra 7:22

> [!quote] BSB
> "up to a hundred talents of silver, a hundred cors of wheat, a hundred baths of wine, a hundred baths of olive oil, and salt without limit." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *100 talents* is approximately 3.77 tons or 3.42 metric tons of silver.
- *100 cors* is approximately 624 bushels or 22,000 liters (probably about 19.2 tons or 17.4 metric tons of wheat).
- *100 baths* is approximately 580 gallons or 2,200 liters of wine.
- Or *100 baths of oil*; that is, approximately 580 gallons or 2,200 liters
%%FN-END%%
