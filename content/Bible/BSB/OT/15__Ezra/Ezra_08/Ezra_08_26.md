# Ezra 8:26

> [!quote] BSB
> "I weighed out into their hands 650 talents of silver, articles of silver weighing 100 talents, 100 talents of gold," ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *650 talents* is approximately 24.5 tons or 22.2 metric tons of silver.
- *100 talents* is approximately 3.77 tons or 3.42 metric tons of silver articles.
- *100 talents* is approximately 3.77 tons or 3.42 metric tons of gold.
%%FN-END%%
