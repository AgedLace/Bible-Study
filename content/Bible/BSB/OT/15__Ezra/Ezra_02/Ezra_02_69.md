# Ezra 2:69

> [!quote] BSB
> "According to their ability, they gave to the treasury for this work 61,000 darics of gold, 5,000 minas of silver, and 100 priestly garments." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Or *61,000 gold drachmas*; that is, approximately 1,129.7 pounds or 512.4 kilograms of gold coins
- *5,000 minas* is approximately 3.14 tons or 2.85 metric tons of silver.
%%FN-END%%
