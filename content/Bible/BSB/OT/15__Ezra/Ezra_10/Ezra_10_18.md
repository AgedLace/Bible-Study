# Ezra 10:18

## Those Guilty of Intermarriage

> [!quote] BSB
> "Among the descendants of the priests who had married foreign women were found these descendants of Jeshua son of Jozadak and his brothers: Maaseiah, Eliezer, Jarib, and Gedaliah." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *Jozadak* is a variant of *Jehozadak*; see 1 Chronicles 6:14.
