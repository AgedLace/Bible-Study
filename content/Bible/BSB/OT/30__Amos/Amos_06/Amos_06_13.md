# Amos 6:13

> [!quote] BSB
> "you who rejoice in Lo-debar and say, ‘Did we not take Karnaim by our own strength?’" ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *Lo-debar* means *nothing*.
- *Karnaim* means *horn*, a symbol of strength.
%%FN-END%%
