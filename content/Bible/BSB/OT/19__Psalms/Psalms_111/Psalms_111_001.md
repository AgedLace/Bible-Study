# Psalms 111:1

## Majestic Is His Work

> [!quote] BSB
> "Hallelujah! I will give thanks to the LORD with all my heart in the council of the upright and in the assembly." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Or *Hallelu YAH*, meaning *Praise the LORD*. This psalm is an acrostic poem, each line beginning with the successive letters of the Hebrew alphabet.
