# Psalms 59:1

## Deliver Me from My Enemies

> [!quote] BSB
> "For the choirmaster. To the tune of “Do Not Destroy.” A Miktam of David, when Saul sent men to watch David’s house in order to kill him. Deliver me from my enemies, O my God; protect me from those who rise against me." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *Miktam* is probably a musical or liturgical term; used for Psalms 16 and 56–60.
%%FN-END%%
