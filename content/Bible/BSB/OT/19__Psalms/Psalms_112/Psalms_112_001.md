# Psalms 112:1

## The Blessed Fear of the LORD

> [!quote] BSB
> "Hallelujah! Blessed is the man who fears the LORD, who greatly delights in His commandments." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Or *Hallelu YAH*, meaning *Praise the LORD*. This psalm is an acrostic poem, each line beginning with the successive letters of the Hebrew alphabet.
