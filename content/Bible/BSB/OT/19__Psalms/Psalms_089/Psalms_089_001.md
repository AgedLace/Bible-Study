# Psalms 89:1

## I Will Sing of His Love Forever

> [!quote] BSB
> "A Maskil of Ethan the Ezrahite. I will sing of the loving devotion of the LORD forever; with my mouth I will proclaim Your faithfulness to all generations." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *Maskil* is probably a musical or liturgical term; used for Psalms 32, 42, 44–45, 52–55, 74, 78, 88–89, and 142.
