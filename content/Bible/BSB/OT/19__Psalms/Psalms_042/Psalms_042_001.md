# Psalms 42:1

## BOOK II Psalms 42–72 As the Deer Pants for the Water

> [!quote] BSB
> "For the choirmaster. A Maskil of the sons of Korah. As the deer pants for streams of water, so my soul longs after You, O God." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- In many Hebrew manuscripts Psalms 42 and 43 constitute one psalm. *Maskil* is probably a musical or liturgical term; used for Psalms 32, 42, 44–45, 52–55, 74, 78, 88–89, and 142.
%%FN-END%%
