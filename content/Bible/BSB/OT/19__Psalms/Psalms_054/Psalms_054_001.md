# Psalms 54:1

## Save Me by Your Name

> [!quote] BSB
> "For the choirmaster. With stringed instruments. A Maskil of David. When the Ziphites went to Saul and said, “Is David not hiding among us?” Save me, O God, by Your name, and vindicate me by Your might!" ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *Maskil* is probably a musical or liturgical term; used for Psalms 32, 42, 44–45, 52–55, 74, 78, 88–89, and 142.
%%FN-END%%
