# Psalms 46:1

## God Is Our Refuge and Strength

> [!quote] BSB
> "For the choirmaster. Of the sons of Korah. According to Alamoth. A song. God is our refuge and strength, an ever-present help in times of trouble." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *Alamoth* is probably a musical or liturgical term; here and in 1 Chronicles 15:20.
