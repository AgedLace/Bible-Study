# Psalms 44:1

## Redeem Us, O God

> [!quote] BSB
> "For the choirmaster. A Maskil of the sons of Korah. We have heard with our ears, O God; our fathers have told us the work You did in their days, in the days of old." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *Maskil* is probably a musical or liturgical term; used for Psalms 32, 42, 44–45, 52–55, 74, 78, 88–89, and 142.
%%FN-END%%
