# Psalms 56:1

## Be Merciful to Me, O God

> [!quote] BSB
> "For the choirmaster. To the tune of “A Dove on Distant Oaks.” A Miktam of David, when the Philistines seized him in Gath. Be merciful to me, O God, for men are hounding me; all day they press their attack." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *Miktam* is probably a musical or liturgical term; used for Psalms 16 and 56–60.
%%FN-END%%
