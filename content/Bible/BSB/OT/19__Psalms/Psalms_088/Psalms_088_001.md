# Psalms 88:1

## I Cry Out before You

> [!quote] BSB
> "A song. A Psalm of the sons of Korah. For the choirmaster. According to Mahalath Leannoth. A Maskil of Heman the Ezrahite. O LORD, the God of my salvation, day and night I cry out before You." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *Mahalath Leannoth* is probably a musical or liturgical term; see also Psalm 53:1.
- *Maskil* is probably a musical or liturgical term; used for Psalms 32, 42, 44–45, 52–55, 74, 78, 88–89, and 142.
%%FN-END%%
