# Psalms 32:1

## The Joy of Forgiveness

> [!quote] BSB
> "Of David. A Maskil. Blessed is he whose transgressions are forgiven, whose sins are covered." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *Maskil* is probably a musical or liturgical term; used for Psalms 32, 42, 44–45, 52–55, 74, 78, 88–89, and 142.
- LXX *Blessed is he whose lawless acts are forgiven, whose sins are covered*; cited in Romans 4:7
%%FN-END%%
