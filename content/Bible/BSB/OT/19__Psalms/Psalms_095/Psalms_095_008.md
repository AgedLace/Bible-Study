# Psalms 95:8

> [!quote] BSB
> "do not harden your hearts as you did at Meribah, in the day at Massah in the wilderness," ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- LXX *as you did in the rebellion*; *Meribah* means *quarreling*; see Exodus 17:7; cited in Hebrews 3:15 and Hebrews 4:7.
- LXX *in the day of testing in the wilderness*; *Massah* means *testing*; see Exodus 17:7.
%%FN-END%%
