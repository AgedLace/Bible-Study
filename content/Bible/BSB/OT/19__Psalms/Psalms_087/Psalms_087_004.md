# Psalms 87:4

> [!quote] BSB
> "“I will mention Rahab and Babylon among those who know Me—along with Philistia, Tyre, and Cush—when I say, ‘This one was born in Zion.’”" ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *Rahab* is a poetic name for Egypt.
- That is, the upper Nile region
