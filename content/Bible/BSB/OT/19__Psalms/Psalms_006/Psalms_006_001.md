# Psalms 6:1

## Do Not Rebuke Me in Your Anger

> [!quote] BSB
> "For the choirmaster. With stringed instruments, according to Sheminith. A Psalm of David. O LORD, do not rebuke me in Your anger or discipline me in Your wrath." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *Sheminith* is probably a musical term; here and in 1 Chronicles 15:21 and Psalm 12:1.
