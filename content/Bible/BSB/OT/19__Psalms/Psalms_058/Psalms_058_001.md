# Psalms 58:1

## God Judges the Earth

> [!quote] BSB
> "For the choirmaster. To the tune of “Do Not Destroy.” A Miktam of David. Do you indeed speak justly, O rulers? Do you judge uprightly, O sons of men?" ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *Miktam* is probably a musical or liturgical term; used for Psalms 16 and 56–60.
