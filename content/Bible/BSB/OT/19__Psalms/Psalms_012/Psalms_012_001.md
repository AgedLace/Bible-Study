# Psalms 12:1

## The Godly Are No More

> [!quote] BSB
> "For the choirmaster. According to Sheminith. A Psalm of David. Help, O LORD, for the godly are no more; the faithful have vanished from among men." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *Sheminith* is probably a musical term; here and in 1 Chronicles 15:21 and Psalm 6:1.
