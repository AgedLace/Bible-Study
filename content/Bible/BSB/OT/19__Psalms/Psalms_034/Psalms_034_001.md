# Psalms 34:1

## Taste and See That the LORD Is Good

> [!quote] BSB
> "Of David, when he pretended to be insane before Abimelech, so that the king drove him away. I will bless the LORD at all times; His praise will always be on my lips." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *Abimelech* is another name for *Achish*; see 1 Samuel 21–29 and 1 Kings 2:39.
- This psalm is an acrostic poem, each verse beginning with the successive letters of the Hebrew alphabet.
%%FN-END%%
