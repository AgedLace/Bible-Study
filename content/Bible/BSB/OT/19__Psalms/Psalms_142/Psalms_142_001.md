# Psalms 142:1

## I Lift My Voice to the LORD

> [!quote] BSB
> "A Maskil of David, when he was in the cave. A prayer. I cry aloud to the LORD; I lift my voice to the LORD for mercy." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *Maskil* is probably a musical or liturgical term; used for Psalms 32, 42, 44–45, 52–55, 74, 78, 88–89, and 142.
