# Psalms 8:1

## How Majestic Is Your Name!

> [!quote] BSB
> "For the choirmaster. According to Gittith. A Psalm of David. O LORD, our Lord, how majestic is Your name in all the earth! You have set Your glory above the heavens." ^text
