# Psalms 14:1

## The Fool Says There Is No God

> [!quote] BSB
> "For the choirmaster. Of David. The fool says in his heart, “There is no God.” They are corrupt; their acts are vile. There is no one who does good." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- The Hebrew words rendered *fool* throughout the Psalms denote one who is morally deficient.
%%FN-END%%
