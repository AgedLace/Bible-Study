# Psalms 57:1

## In You My Soul Takes Refuge

> [!quote] BSB
> "For the choirmaster. To the tune of “Do Not Destroy.” A Miktam of David, when he fled from Saul into the cave. Have mercy on me, O God, have mercy, for in You my soul takes refuge. In the shadow of Your wings I will take shelter until the danger has passed." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *Miktam* is probably a musical or liturgical term; used for Psalms 16 and 56–60.
%%FN-END%%
