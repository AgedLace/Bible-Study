# Psalms 9:1

## I Will Give Thanks to the LORD

> [!quote] BSB
> "For the choirmaster. To the tune of “The Death of the Son.” A Psalm of David. I will give thanks to the LORD with all my heart; I will recount all Your wonders." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Psalms 9 and 10 together follow an acrostic pattern, each stanza beginning with the successive letters of the Hebrew alphabet. In the LXX they form one psalm.
%%FN-END%%
