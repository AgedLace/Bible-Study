# 2 Samuel 12:31

> [!quote] BSB
> "David brought out the people who were there and put them to work with saws, iron picks, and axes, and he made them work at the brick kilns. He did the same to all the Ammonite cities. Then David and all his troops returned to Jerusalem." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Literally *put them under saws*
- Literally *and he made them pass through the brick kilns.*
%%FN-END%%
