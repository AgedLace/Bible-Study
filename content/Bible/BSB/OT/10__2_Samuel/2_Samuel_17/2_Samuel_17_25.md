# 2 Samuel 17:25

> [!quote] BSB
> "Absalom had appointed Amasa over the army in place of Joab. Amasa was the son of a man named Ithra, the Ishmaelite who had married Abigail, the daughter of Nahash and sister of Zeruiah the mother of Joab." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *Ithra* is a variant of *Jether*; see 1 Kings 2:5.
- Some LXX manuscripts (see also 1 Chronicles 2:17); MT and other LXX manuscripts *Israelite*
- Or *Abigal*, a variant of *Abigail*
- Or *of Jesse*; see DSS and 1 Chronicles 2:13–16
%%FN-END%%
