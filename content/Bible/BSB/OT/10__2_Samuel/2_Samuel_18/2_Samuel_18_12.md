# 2 Samuel 18:12

> [!quote] BSB
> "The man replied, “Even if a thousand shekels of silver were weighed out into my hands, I would not raise my hand against the son of the king. For we heard the king command you and Abishai and Ittai, ‘Protect the young man Absalom for my sake.’" ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *1,000 shekels* is approximately 25.1 pounds or 11.4 kilograms of silver.
- A few Hebrew manuscripts, LXX, Vulgate, and Syriac; most Hebrew manuscripts *Whoever you may be, protect the young man Absalom.* or *Let no one touch the young man Absalom.*
%%FN-END%%
