# 2 Samuel 1:18

> [!quote] BSB
> "and he ordered that the sons of Judah be taught the Song of the Bow. It is written in the Book of Jashar:" ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Or *be taught the use of the bow*; literally *be taught the bow*
- Or *the Book of the Upright One*, commonly cited as *Jasher*; note that *the Song of the Bow* that follows is not found in known manuscripts attributed to Jasher.
%%FN-END%%
