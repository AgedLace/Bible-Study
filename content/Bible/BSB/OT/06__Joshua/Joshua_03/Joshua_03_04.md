# Joshua 3:4

> [!quote] BSB
> "But keep a distance of about two thousand cubits between yourselves and the ark. Do not go near it, so that you can see the way to go, since you have never traveled this way before.”" ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *2,000 cubits* is approximately 3,000 feet or 914.4 meters.
