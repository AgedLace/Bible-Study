# Joshua 10:1

## The Day the Sun Stood Still

> [!quote] BSB
> "Now Adoni-zedek king of Jerusalem heard that Joshua had captured Ai and devoted it to destruction—doing to Ai and its king as he had done to Jericho and its king—and that the people of Gibeon had made peace with Israel and were living near them." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Forms of the Hebrew *cherem* refer to the giving over of things or persons to the LORD, either by destroying them or by giving them as an offering; also in verses 28, 35, 37, 39, and 40.
%%FN-END%%
