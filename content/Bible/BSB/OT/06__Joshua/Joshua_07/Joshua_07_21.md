# Joshua 7:21

> [!quote] BSB
> "When I saw among the spoils a beautiful cloak from Shinar, two hundred shekels of silver, and a bar of gold weighing fifty shekels, I coveted them and took them. They are hidden in the ground inside my tent, with the silver underneath.”" ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- That is, Babylonia
- *200 shekels* is approximately 5 pounds or 2.3 kilograms of silver.
- *50 shekels* is approximately 1.26 pounds or 569.8 grams of gold.
%%FN-END%%
