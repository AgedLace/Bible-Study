# Joshua 11:2

> [!quote] BSB
> "to the kings of the north in the mountains, in the Arabah south of Chinnereth, in the foothills, and in Naphoth-dor to the west;" ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Hebrew *Shephelah* or *lowlands*; that is, the western foothills of Judea; also in verse 16
- Or *in the heights of Dor*; *Naphoth-dor* is a variant of *Naphath-dor*; see Joshua 12:23.
%%FN-END%%
