# Deuteronomy 24:8

> [!quote] BSB
> "In cases of infectious skin diseases, be careful to diligently follow everything the Levitical priests instruct you. Be careful to do as I have commanded them." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Forms of the Hebrew *tzaraath*, traditionally translated as *leprosy*, were used for various skin diseases; see Leviticus 13.
%%FN-END%%
