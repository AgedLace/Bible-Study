# Deuteronomy 7:2

> [!quote] BSB
> "and when the LORD your God has delivered them over to you to defeat them, then you must devote them to complete destruction. Make no treaty with them and show them no mercy." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Forms of the Hebrew *cherem* refer to the giving over of things or persons to the LORD, either by destroying them or by giving them as an offering; also twice in verse 26.
- Forms of the Hebrew *berit* are translated in most passages as *covenant*.
%%FN-END%%
