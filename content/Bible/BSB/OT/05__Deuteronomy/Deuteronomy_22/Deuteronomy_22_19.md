# Deuteronomy 22:19

> [!quote] BSB
> "They are also to fine him a hundred shekels of silver and give them to the young woman’s father, because this man has given a virgin of Israel a bad name. And she shall remain his wife; he must not divorce her as long as he lives." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *100 shekels* is approximately 2.5 pounds or 1.1 kilograms of silver.
%%FN-END%%
