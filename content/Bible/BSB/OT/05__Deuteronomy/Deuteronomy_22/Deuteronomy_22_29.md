# Deuteronomy 22:29

> [!quote] BSB
> "then the man who lay with her must pay the young woman’s father fifty shekels of silver, and she must become his wife because he has violated her. He must not divorce her as long as he lives." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *50 shekels* is approximately 1.26 pounds or 569.8 grams of silver.
%%FN-END%%
