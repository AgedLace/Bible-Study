# Deuteronomy 32:43

> [!quote] BSB
> "Rejoice, O heavens, with Him, and let all God’s angels worship Him. Rejoice, O nations, with His people; for He will avenge the blood of His children. He will take vengeance on His adversaries and repay those who hate Him; He will cleanse His land and His people." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- See DSS and LXX; MT lacks *Rejoice, O heavens, with Him, and let all God’s angels worship Him.* Cited in Hebrews 1:6
- Cited in Romans 15:10
- DSS and LXX; MT *servants*
- LXX and Vulgate; MT lacks *and repay those who hate Him*
%%FN-END%%
