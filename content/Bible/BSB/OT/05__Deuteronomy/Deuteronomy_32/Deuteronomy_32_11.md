# Deuteronomy 32:11

> [!quote] BSB
> "As an eagle stirs up its nest and hovers over its young, He spread His wings to catch them and carried them on His pinions." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *Pinions* are the outer parts of a bird’s wings, including the flight feathers.
