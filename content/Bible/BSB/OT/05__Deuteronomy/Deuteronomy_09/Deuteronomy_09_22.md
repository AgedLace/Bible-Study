# Deuteronomy 9:22

> [!quote] BSB
> "You continued to provoke the LORD at Taberah, at Massah, and at Kibroth-hattaavah." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *Taberah* means *burning*; see Numbers 11:3.
- *Massah* means *testing*; see Exodus 17:7.
- *Kibroth-hattaavah* means *graves of craving*; see Numbers 11:34.
%%FN-END%%
