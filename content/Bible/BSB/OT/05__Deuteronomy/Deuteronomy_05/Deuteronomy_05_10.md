# Deuteronomy 5:10

> [!quote] BSB
> "but showing loving devotion to a thousand generations of those who love Me and keep My commandments." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Hebrew *loving devotion to thousands*; forms of the Hebrew *chesed* are translated here and in most cases throughout the Scriptures as *loving devotion*; the range of meaning includes *love*, *goodness*, *kindness*, *faithfulness*, and *mercy*, as well as *loyalty to a covenant*.
%%FN-END%%
