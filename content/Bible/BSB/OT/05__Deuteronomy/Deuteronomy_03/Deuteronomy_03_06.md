# Deuteronomy 3:6

> [!quote] BSB
> "We devoted them to destruction, as we had done to Sihon king of Heshbon, utterly destroying the men, women, and children of every city." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Forms of the Hebrew *cherem* refer to the giving over of things or persons to the LORD, either by destroying them or by giving them as an offering.
