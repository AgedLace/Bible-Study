# Deuteronomy 33:8

> [!quote] BSB
> "Concerning Levi he said: “Give Your Thummim to Levi and Your Urim to Your godly one, whom You tested at Massah and contested at the waters of Meribah." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- DSS and LXX; MT does not include *to Levi*; literally *Let Your Perfections and Your Lights be to Your godly one*.
- *Massah* means *testing*; see Exodus 17:7.
- *Meribah* means *quarreling*; see Exodus 17:7.
%%FN-END%%
