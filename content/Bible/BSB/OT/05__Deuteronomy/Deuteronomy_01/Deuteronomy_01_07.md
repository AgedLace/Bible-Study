# Deuteronomy 1:7

> [!quote] BSB
> "Resume your journey and go to the hill country of the Amorites; go to all the neighboring peoples in the Arabah, in the hill country, in the foothills, in the Negev, and along the seacoast to the land of the Canaanites and to Lebanon, as far as the great River Euphrates." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Hebrew *Shephelah* or *lowlands*; that is, the western foothills of Judea
%%FN-END%%
