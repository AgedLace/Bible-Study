# Deuteronomy 16:1

## Passover and the Feast of Unleavened Bread

> [!quote] BSB
> "Observe the month of Abib and celebrate the Passover to the LORD your God, because in the month of Abib the LORD your God brought you out of Egypt by night." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *Abib* was the first month of the ancient Hebrew lunar calendar, usually occurring within the months of March and April; twice in this verse.
%%FN-END%%
