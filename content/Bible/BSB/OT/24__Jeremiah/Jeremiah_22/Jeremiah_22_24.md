# Jeremiah 22:24

## A Warning to Coniah

> [!quote] BSB
> "“As surely as I live,” declares the LORD, “even if you, Coniah son of Jehoiakim king of Judah, were a signet ring on My right hand, I would pull you off." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *Coniah* is a variant of *Jehoiachin*; also in verse 28.
