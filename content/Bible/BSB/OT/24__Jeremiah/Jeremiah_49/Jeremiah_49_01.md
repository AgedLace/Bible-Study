# Jeremiah 49:1

## Judgment on the Ammonites

> [!quote] BSB
> "Concerning the Ammonites, this is what the LORD says: “Has Israel no sons? Is he without heir? Why then has Milcom taken possession of Gad? Why have his people settled in their cities?" ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Or *their king*; *Milcom* is a variant of *Molech*; also in verse 3; see Leviticus 18:21 and 1 Kings 11:7.
%%FN-END%%
