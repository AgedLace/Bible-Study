# Jeremiah 39:3

> [!quote] BSB
> "Then all the officials of the king of Babylon entered and sat in the Middle Gate: Nergal-sharezer of Samgar, Nebo-sarsekim the Rabsaris, Nergal-sharezer the Rabmag, and all the rest of the officials of the king of Babylon." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Or *Nergal-sharezer, Samgar-nebo, Sarsekim*
- Hebrew *Rabsaris* is the title of the chief eunuch in the Assyrian military; also in verse 13.
- Hebrew *Rabmag* is the title of the chief soothsayer or chief of princes in the Assyrian military; also in verse 13.
%%FN-END%%
