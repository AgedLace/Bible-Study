# Jeremiah 20:3

> [!quote] BSB
> "The next day, when Pashhur released Jeremiah from the stocks, Jeremiah said to him, “The LORD does not call you Pashhur, but Magor-missabib." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *Magor-missabib* means *terror on every side* or *the man who lives in terror*.
