# Jeremiah 52:21

> [!quote] BSB
> "Each pillar was eighteen cubits tall and twelve cubits in circumference; each was hollow, four fingers thick." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Each pillar was approximately 27 feet high and 18 feet in circumference (8.2 meters high and 5.5 meters in circumference).
- *4 fingers* is approximately 2.9 inches or 7.4 centimeters.
%%FN-END%%
