# Jeremiah 9:24

> [!quote] BSB
> "But let him who boasts boast in this, that he understands and knows Me, that I am the LORD, who exercises loving devotion, justice and righteousness on the earth—for I delight in these things,” declares the LORD." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Cited in 1 Corinthians 1:31 and 2 Corinthians 10:17
- Forms of the Hebrew *chesed* are translated here and in most cases throughout the Scriptures as *loving devotion*; the range of meaning includes *love*, *goodness*, *kindness*, *faithfulness*, and *mercy*, as well as *loyalty to a covenant*.
%%FN-END%%
