# Jeremiah 51:1

## Judgment on Babylon

> [!quote] BSB
> "This is what the LORD says: “Behold, I will stir up against Babylon and against the people of Leb-kamai the spirit of a destroyer." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *Leb-kamai* is a code name for Chaldea, that is, Babylonia.
%%FN-END%%
