# Jeremiah 51:3

> [!quote] BSB
> "Do not let the archer bend his bow or put on his armor. Do not spare her young men; devote all her army to destruction!" ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Forms of the Hebrew *cherem* refer to the giving over of things or persons to the LORD, either by destroying them or by giving them as an offering.
