# Jeremiah 32:44

> [!quote] BSB
> "Fields will be purchased with silver, and deeds will be signed, sealed, and witnessed in the land of Benjamin, in the areas surrounding Jerusalem, and in the cities of Judah—the cities of the hill country, the foothills, and the Negev—because I will restore them from captivity, declares the LORD.”" ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Hebrew *Shephelah* or *lowlands*; that is, the western foothills of Judea
- Or *restore their fortunes*
%%FN-END%%
