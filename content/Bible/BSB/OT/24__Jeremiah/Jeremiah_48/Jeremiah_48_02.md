# Jeremiah 48:2

> [!quote] BSB
> "There is no longer praise for Moab; in Heshbon they devise evil against her: ‘Come, let us cut her off from nationhood.’ You too, O people of Madmen, will be silenced; the sword will pursue you." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- The Hebrew for *Heshbon* sounds like the Hebrew for *plot*.
- The name of the Moabite town *Madmen* sounds like the Hebrew for *silenced*.
%%FN-END%%
