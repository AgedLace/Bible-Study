# Isaiah 15:9

> [!quote] BSB
> "The waters of Dimon are full of blood, but I will bring more upon Dimon—a lion upon the fugitives of Moab and upon the remnant of the land." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- MT, twice in this verse; DSS and Vulgate *Dibon*; *Dimon*, a wordplay on *Dibon* (see verse 2), sounds like the Hebrew for *blood*.
%%FN-END%%
