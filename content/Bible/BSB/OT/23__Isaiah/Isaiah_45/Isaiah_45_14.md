# Isaiah 45:14

> [!quote] BSB
> "This is what the LORD says: “The products of Egypt and the merchandise of Cush, along with the Sabeans, men of stature, will come over to you and will be yours; they will trudge behind you; they will come over in chains and bow down to you. They will confess to you: ‘God is indeed with you, and there is no other; there is no other God.’”" ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- That is, the upper Nile region
