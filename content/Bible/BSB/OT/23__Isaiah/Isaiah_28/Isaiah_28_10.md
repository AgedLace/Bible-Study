# Isaiah 28:10

> [!quote] BSB
> "For they hear: “Order on order, order on order, line on line, line on line; a little here, a little there.”" ^text

> [!note] Footnotes
- Or *For He says* or *For it is*; literally *For*
- Or *Do this, do that, a rule for this, a rule for that*; Hebrew *sav lasav sav lasav kav lakav kav lakav* (possibly meaningless sounds to demonstrate an inability to understand); also in verse 13
