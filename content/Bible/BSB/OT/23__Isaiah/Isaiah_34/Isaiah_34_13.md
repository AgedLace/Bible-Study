# Isaiah 34:13

> [!quote] BSB
> "Her towers will be overgrown with thorns, her fortresses with thistles and briers. She will become a haunt for jackals, an abode for ostriches." ^text

> [!note] Footnotes

- Literally *for daughters of an ostrich* or *for daughters of an owl*
