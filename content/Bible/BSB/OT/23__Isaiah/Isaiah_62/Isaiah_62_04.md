# Isaiah 62:4

> [!quote] BSB
> "No longer will you be called Forsaken, nor your land named Desolate; but you will be called Hephzibah, and your land Beulah; for the LORD will take delight in you, and your land will be His bride." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Hebrew *Azubah*
- Hebrew *Shemamah*
- *Hephzibah* means *My delight is in her*.
- *Beulah* means *married*.
%%FN-END%%
