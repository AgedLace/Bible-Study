# Isaiah 7:23

> [!quote] BSB
> "And on that day, in every place that had a thousand vines worth a thousand shekels of silver, only briers and thorns will be found." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Hebrew *a thousand of silver*; that is, approximately 25.1 pounds or 11.4 kilograms of silver
