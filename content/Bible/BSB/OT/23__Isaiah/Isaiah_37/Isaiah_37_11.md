# Isaiah 37:11

> [!quote] BSB
> "Surely you have heard what the kings of Assyria have done to all the other countries, devoting them to destruction. Will you then be spared?" ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Forms of the Hebrew *cherem* refer to the giving over of things or persons, either by destroying them or by giving them as an offering.
%%FN-END%%
