# Isaiah 44:2

> [!quote] BSB
> "This is the word of the LORD, your Maker, who formed you from the womb and who will help you: “Do not be afraid, O Jacob My servant, Jeshurun, whom I have chosen." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *Jeshurun* means *the upright one*, a term of endearment for Israel.
