# Isaiah 11:11

> [!quote] BSB
> "On that day the Lord will extend His hand a second time to recover the remnant of His people from Assyria, from Egypt, from Pathros, from Cush, from Elam, from Shinar, from Hamath, and from the islands of the sea." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- That is, the upper Nile region
- That is, Babylonia
- Or *coastlands*
