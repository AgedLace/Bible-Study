# Isaiah 29:1

## Woe to David’s City

> [!quote] BSB
> "Woe to you, O Ariel, the city of Ariel where David camped! Year upon year let your festivals recur." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Or *Altar Hearth* or *Lion of God*; probably a nickname for Jerusalem; twice in this verse, twice in verse 2, and once in verse 7
%%FN-END%%
