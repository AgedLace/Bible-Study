# Isaiah 43:20

> [!quote] BSB
> "The beasts of the field will honor Me, the jackals and the ostriches, because I provide water in the wilderness and rivers in the desert, to give drink to My chosen people." ^text

> [!note] Footnotes

- Literally *and daughters of an ostrich* or *and daughters of an owl*
