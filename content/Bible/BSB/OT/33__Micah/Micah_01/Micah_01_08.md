## Weeping and Mourning

> [!quote] BSB
> "Because of this I will lament and wail; I will walk barefoot and naked. I will howl like a jackal and mourn like an ostrich." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Or *a serpent* or *a dragon*
- Literally *like daughters of an ostrich* or *like daughters of an owl*
