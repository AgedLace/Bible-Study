# Micah 1:11

> [!quote] BSB
> "Depart in shameful nakedness, O dwellers of Shaphir. The dwellers of Zaanan will not come out. Beth-ezel is in mourning; its support is taken from you." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *Shaphir* means *pleasant*.
- *Zaanan* sounds like the Hebrew for *come out*.
- *Beth-ezel* means *adjoining house*.
%%FN-END%%
