# Micah 1:14

> [!quote] BSB
> "Therefore, send farewell gifts to Moresheth-gath; the houses of Achzib will prove deceptive to the kings of Israel." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Or *give dowry*
- *Moresheth* sounds like the Hebrew for *gift* or *dowry*.
- *Achzib* means *deception*.
%%FN-END%%
