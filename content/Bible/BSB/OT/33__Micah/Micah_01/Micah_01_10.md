# Micah 1:10

> [!quote] BSB
> "Do not tell it in Gath; do not weep at all. Roll in the dust in Beth-leaphrah." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *Gath* sounds like the Hebrew for *tell*.
- *Beth-leaphrah* means *house of dust*.
%%FN-END%%
