# Ruth 3:9

> [!quote] BSB
> "“Who are you?” he asked. “I am your servant Ruth,” she replied. “Spread the corner of your garment over me, for you are a kinsman-redeemer.”" ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Or *Spread your wing*; the word for *wing* can also mean *corner of a garment*; see Ruth 2:12.
- The Hebrew word for *kinsman-redeemer* or *guardian-redeemer* is a legal term for the kinsman who redeems or vindicates a relative; similarly in verses 12 and 13; see Leviticus 25:25–55.
%%FN-END%%
