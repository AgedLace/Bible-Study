# Ruth 4:1

## Boaz Redeems Ruth

> [!quote] BSB
> "Meanwhile, Boaz went to the gate and sat down there. Soon the kinsman-redeemer of whom he had spoken came along, and Boaz said, “Come over here, my friend, and sit down.” So he went over and sat down." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- The Hebrew word for *kinsman-redeemer* or *guardian-redeemer* is a legal term for the kinsman who redeems or vindicates a relative; also in verses 3, 6, 8, and 14; see Leviticus 25:25–55.
%%FN-END%%
