# 2 Chronicles 26:10

> [!quote] BSB
> "Since he had much livestock in the foothills and in the plain, he built towers in the desert and dug many cisterns. And since he was a lover of the soil, he had farmers and vinedressers in the hill country and in the fertile fields." ^text

> [!note] Footnotes
- Hebrew *Shephelah* or *lowlands*; that is, the western foothills of Judea
