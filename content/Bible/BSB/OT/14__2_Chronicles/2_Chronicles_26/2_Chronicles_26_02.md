# 2 Chronicles 26:2

> [!quote] BSB
> "Uzziah was the one who rebuilt Eloth and restored it to Judah after King Amaziah rested with his fathers." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *Eloth* is a variant of *Elath*; see LXX, 2 Kings 14:22, and 2 Kings 16:6.
- Literally *after the king*
