# 2 Chronicles 5:9

> [!quote] BSB
> "The poles of the ark extended far enough that their ends were visible from in front of the inner sanctuary, but not from outside the Holy Place; and they are there to this day." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Some Hebrew manuscripts and LXX *The poles extended far enough that their ends were visible from the Holy Place in front of the inner sanctuary*; see 1 Kings 8:8.
- Literally *not from outside*
%%FN-END%%
