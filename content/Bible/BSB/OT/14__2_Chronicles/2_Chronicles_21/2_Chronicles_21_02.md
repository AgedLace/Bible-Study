# 2 Chronicles 21:2

> [!quote] BSB
> "Jehoram’s brothers, the sons of Jehoshaphat, were Azariah, Jehiel, Zechariah, Azariah, Michael, and Shephatiah; these were all sons of Jehoshaphat king of Israel." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- That is, Judah; in 2 Chronicles, Judah is occasionally called Israel, as representative of the true Israel.
