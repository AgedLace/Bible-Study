# 2 Chronicles 4:1

## The Bronze Altar and Molten Sea

> [!quote] BSB
> "He made a bronze altar twenty cubits long, twenty cubits wide, and ten cubits high." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- The altar was approximately 30 feet in length and width and 15 feet high (9.1 meters in length and width and 4.6 meters high).
