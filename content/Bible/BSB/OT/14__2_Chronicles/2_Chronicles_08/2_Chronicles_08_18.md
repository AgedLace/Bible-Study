# 2 Chronicles 8:18

> [!quote] BSB
> "So Hiram sent him ships captained by his servants, along with crews of experienced sailors. They went with Solomon’s servants to Ophir and acquired from there 450 talents of gold, which they delivered to King Solomon." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *450 talents* is approximately 17 tons or 15.4 metric tons of gold.
%%FN-END%%
