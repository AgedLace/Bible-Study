# 2 Chronicles 25:17

## Jehoash Defeats Amaziah

> [!quote] BSB
> "Then Amaziah king of Judah took counsel and sent word to the king of Israel Jehoash son of Jehoahaz, the son of Jehu. “Come, let us meet face to face,” he said." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *Jehoash* is a variant of *Joash*; also in verses 18, 21, 23, and 25.
%%FN-END%%
