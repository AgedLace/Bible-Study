# 2 Chronicles 33:18

> [!quote] BSB
> "As for the rest of the acts of Manasseh, along with his prayer to his God and the words of the seers who spoke to him in the name of the LORD, the God of Israel, they are indeed written in the Chronicles of the Kings of Israel." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- That is, Judah; in 2 Chronicles, Judah is occasionally called Israel, as representative of the true Israel.
%%FN-END%%
