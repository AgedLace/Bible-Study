# 2 Chronicles 24:26

> [!quote] BSB
> "Those who conspired against Joash were Zabad son of Shimeath the Ammonitess and Jehozabad son of Shimrith the Moabitess." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *Zabad* is a variant of *Jozabad*; see 2 Kings 12:21.
- *Shimrith* is a variant of *Shomer*; see 2 Kings 12:21.
%%FN-END%%
