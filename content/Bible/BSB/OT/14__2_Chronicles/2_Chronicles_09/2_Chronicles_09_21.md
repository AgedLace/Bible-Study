# 2 Chronicles 9:21

> [!quote] BSB
> "For the king had the ships of Tarshish that went with Hiram’s servants, and once every three years the ships of Tarshish would arrive bearing gold, silver, ivory, apes, and peacocks." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Or *a fleet of trading ships*; twice in this verse
- Hebrew *Huram’s*, a variant of *Hiram’s*
- Or *baboons*
%%FN-END%%
