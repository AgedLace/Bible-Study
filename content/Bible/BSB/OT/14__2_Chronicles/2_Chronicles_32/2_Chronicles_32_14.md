# 2 Chronicles 32:14

> [!quote] BSB
> "Who among all the gods of these nations that my fathers devoted to destruction has been able to deliver his people from my hand? How then can your God deliver you from my hand?" ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
