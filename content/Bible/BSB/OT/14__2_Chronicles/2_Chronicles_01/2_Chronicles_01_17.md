# 2 Chronicles 1:17

> [!quote] BSB
> "A chariot could be imported from Egypt for six hundred shekels of silver, and a horse for a hundred and fifty. Likewise, they exported them to all the kings of the Hittites and to the kings of Aram." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *600 shekels* is approximately 15.1 pounds or 6.8 kilograms of silver.
- *150 shekels* is approximately 3.8 pounds or 1.7 kilograms of silver.
%%FN-END%%
