# 2 Chronicles 6:13

> [!quote] BSB
> "Now Solomon had made a bronze platform five cubits long, five cubits wide, and three cubits high and had placed it in the middle of the courtyard. He stood on it, knelt down before the whole assembly of Israel, spread out his hands toward heaven," ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- The platform was approximately 7.5 feet in length and width, and 4.5 feet high (2.3 meters in length and width, and 1.4 meters high).
%%FN-END%%
