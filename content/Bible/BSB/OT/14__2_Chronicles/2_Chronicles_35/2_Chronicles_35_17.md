# 2 Chronicles 35:17

> [!quote] BSB
> "The Israelites who were present also observed the Passover at that time, as well as the Feast of Unleavened Bread for seven days." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- That is, the seven-day period after the Passover during which no leaven may be eaten; see Exodus 12:14–20.
