> [!quote] BSB
> "The total wingspan of the cherubim was twenty cubits. One wing of the first cherub was five cubits long and touched the wall of the temple, and its other wing was five cubits long and touched the wing of the other cherub." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *5 cubits* is approximately 7.5 feet or 2.3 meters; also in verse 15.
