# 2 Chronicles 3:4

> [!quote] BSB
> "The portico at the front, extending across the width of the temple, was twenty cubits long and twenty cubits high. He overlaid the inside with pure gold." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *20 cubits* is approximately 30 feet or 9.1 meters; also in verses 8, 11, and 13.
- Some LXX and Syriac manuscripts; Hebrew *120 cubits high* (approximately 180 feet or 54.9 meters)
%%FN-END%%
