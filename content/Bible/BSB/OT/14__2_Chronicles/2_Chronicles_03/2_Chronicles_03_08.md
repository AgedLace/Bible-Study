# 2 Chronicles 3:8

> [!quote] BSB
> "Then he made the Most Holy Place; its length corresponded to the width of the temple—twenty cubits long and twenty cubits wide. And he overlaid the inside with six hundred talents of fine gold." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Or *the Holy of Holies*; also in verse 10
- *600 talents* is approximately 22.6 tons or 20.5 metric tons of gold.
%%FN-END%%
