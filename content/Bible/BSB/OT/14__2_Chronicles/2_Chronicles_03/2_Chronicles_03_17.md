# 2 Chronicles 3:17

> [!quote] BSB
> "Then he set up the pillars in front of the temple, one on the south and one on the north. The pillar on the south he named Jachin, and the pillar on the north he named Boaz." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *Jachin* probably means *He establishes*.
- *Boaz* probably means *in Him is strength*.
%%FN-END%%
