# 2 Chronicles 36:3

> [!quote] BSB
> "And the king of Egypt dethroned him in Jerusalem and imposed on Judah a levy of a hundred talents of silver and a talent of gold." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *100 talents* is approximately 3.77 tons or 3.42 metric tons of silver.
- *A talent* is approximately 75.4 pounds or 34.2 kilograms of gold.
%%FN-END%%
