# 2 Chronicles 2:10

> [!quote] BSB
> "I will pay your servants, the woodcutters, 20,000 cors of ground wheat, 20,000 cors of barley, 20,000 baths of wine, and 20,000 baths of olive oil.”" ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *20,000 cors* is approximately 124,800 bushels or 4.4 million liters (probably about 3,800 tons or 3,400 metric tons of wheat).
- *20,000 cors* is approximately 124,800 bushels or 4.4 million liters (probably about 2,910 tons or 2,700 metric tons of barley).
- *20,000 baths* is approximately 116,000 gallons or 440,000 liters of wine.
- Or *20,000 baths of oil*; that is, approximately 116,000 gallons or 440,000 liters
%%FN-END%%
