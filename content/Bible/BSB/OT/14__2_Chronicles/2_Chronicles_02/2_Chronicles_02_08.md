# 2 Chronicles 2:8

> [!quote] BSB
> "Send me also cedar, cypress, and algum logs from Lebanon, for I know that your servants have skill to cut timber there. And indeed, my servants will work with yours" ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Or *pine* or *juniper* or *fir*
- *Algum* is probably a variant of *almug*; see 1 Kings 10:11.
