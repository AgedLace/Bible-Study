# 1 Samuel 10:1

## Samuel Anoints Saul

> [!quote] BSB
> "Then Samuel took a flask of oil, poured it on Saul’s head, kissed him, and said, “Has not the LORD anointed you ruler over His inheritance?" ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Hebrew; LXX *“Has not the LORD anointed you ruler over Israel? And you will rule over the LORD’s people and save them from their enemies around them. This will be the sign to you that the LORD has appointed you to be leader over His inheritance.*
