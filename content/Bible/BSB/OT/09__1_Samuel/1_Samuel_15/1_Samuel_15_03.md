# 1 Samuel 15:3

> [!quote] BSB
> "Now go and attack the Amalekites and devote to destruction all that belongs to them. Do not spare them, but put to death men and women, children and infants, oxen and sheep, camels and donkeys.’”" ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Forms of the Hebrew *cherem* refer to the giving over of things or persons to the LORD, either by destroying them or by giving them as an offering; also in verses 8, 9, 15, 18, 20, and 21.
%%FN-END%%
