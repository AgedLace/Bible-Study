# 1 Samuel 1:24

> [!quote] BSB
> "Once she had weaned him, Hannah took the boy with her, along with a three-year-old bull, an ephah of flour, and a skin of wine. Though the boy was still young, she brought him to the house of the LORD at Shiloh." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- DSS, LXX, and Syriac; MT *three bulls*
- *An ephah* is approximately 20 dry quarts or 22 liters (probably about 25.5 pounds or 11.6 kilograms of flour).
%%FN-END%%
