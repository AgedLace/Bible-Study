# 1 Samuel 1:1

## Elkanah and His Wives

> [!quote] BSB
> "Now there was a man named Elkanah who was from Ramathaim-zophim in the hill country of Ephraim. He was the son of Jeroham, the son of Elihu, the son of Tohu, the son of Zuph, an Ephraimite." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Or *from Ramathaim, a Zuphite*; see LXX and 1 Chronicles 6:26 and 35.
- *Elihu* is also called *Eliab* and *Eliel*; see 1 Chronicles 6:27 and 34.
%%FN-END%%
