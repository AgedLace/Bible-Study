# 1 Samuel 25:18

## Abigail Intercedes for Nabal

> [!quote] BSB
> "Then Abigail hurried and took two hundred loaves of bread, two skins of wine, five butchered sheep, five seahs of roasted grain, a hundred clusters of raisins, and two hundred cakes of figs. She loaded them on donkeys" ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *5 seahs* is approximately 33 dry quarts or 36.5 liters of roasted grain.
%%FN-END%%
