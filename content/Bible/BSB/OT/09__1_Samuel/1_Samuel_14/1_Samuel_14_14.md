# 1 Samuel 14:14

> [!quote] BSB
> "In that first assault, Jonathan and his armor-bearer struck down about twenty men in about half an acre of land." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Hebrew *half a yoke*. A *yoke* was the amount of land plowed by a pair of yoked oxen in one day.
%%FN-END%%
