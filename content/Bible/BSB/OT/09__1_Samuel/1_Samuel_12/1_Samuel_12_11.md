# 1 Samuel 12:11

> [!quote] BSB
> "So the LORD sent Jerubbaal, Barak, Jephthah, and Samuel, and He delivered you from the hands of your enemies on every side, and you dwelt securely." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *Jerubbaal* is another name for *Gideon* and probably means *let Baal contend*; see Judges 6:32.
- LXX and Syriac; Hebrew *Bedan*
- LXX and Syriac *Samson*
