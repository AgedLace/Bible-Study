# Zechariah 7:1

## A Call to Justice and Mercy

> [!quote] BSB
> "In the fourth year of King Darius, the word of the LORD came to Zechariah on the fourth day of the ninth month, the month of Chislev." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *Chislev* is the ninth month of the Hebrew lunar calendar, usually occurring within the months of November and December.
