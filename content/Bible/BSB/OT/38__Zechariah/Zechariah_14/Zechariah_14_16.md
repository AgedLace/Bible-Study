# Zechariah 14:16

## All Nations Will Worship the King

> [!quote] BSB
> "Then all the survivors from the nations that came against Jerusalem will go up year after year to worship the King, the LORD of Hosts, and to celebrate the Feast of Tabernacles." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- That is, Sukkot, the autumn feast of pilgrimage to Jerusalem; also translated as *the Feast of Booths* or *the Feast of Shelters* and originally called *the Feast of Ingathering* (see Exodus 23:16 and Exodus 34:22).
%%FN-END%%
