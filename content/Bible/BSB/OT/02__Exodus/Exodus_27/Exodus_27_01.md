# Exodus 27:1

## The Bronze Altar

> [!quote] BSB
> "“You are to build an altar of acacia wood. The altar must be square, five cubits long, five cubits wide, and three cubits high." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- The altar was approximately 7.5 feet in length and width, and 4.5 feet high (2.3 meters in length and width, and 1.4 meters high).
%%FN-END%%
