# Exodus 27:18

> [!quote] BSB
> "The entire courtyard shall be a hundred cubits long and fifty cubits wide, with curtains of finely spun linen five cubits high, and with bronze bases." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- The courtyard was approximately 150 feet long and 75 feet wide (45.7 meters long and 22.9 meters wide).
- *5 cubits* is approximately 7.5 feet or 2.3 meters.
%%FN-END%%
