# Exodus 19:1

## Israel at Mount Sinai

> [!quote] BSB
> "In the third month, on the same day of the month that the Israelites had left the land of Egypt, they came to the Wilderness of Sinai." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Hebrew *In the third month, on the same day*; that is, two months after leaving Egypt; see Numbers 33:3.
