# Exodus 21:32

> [!quote] BSB
> "If the ox gores a manservant or maidservant, the owner must pay thirty shekels of silver to the master of that servant, and the ox must be stoned." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *30 shekels* is approximately 12 ounces or 342 grams of silver.
