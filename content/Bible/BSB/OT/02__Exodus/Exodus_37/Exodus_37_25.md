# Exodus 37:25

## The Altar of Incense

> [!quote] BSB
> "He made the altar of incense out of acacia wood. It was square, a cubit long, a cubit wide, and two cubits high. Its horns were of one piece." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- The altar was approximately 1.5 feet in length and width, and 3 feet high (45.7 centimeters in length and width, and 91.4 centimeters high).
%%FN-END%%
