# Exodus 37:6

## The Mercy Seat

> [!quote] BSB
> "He constructed a mercy seat of pure gold, two and a half cubits long and a cubit and a half wide." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- The mercy seat was approximately 3.75 feet long and 2.25 feet wide (114.3 centimeters long and 68.6 centimeters wide).
%%FN-END%%
