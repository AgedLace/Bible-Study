# Exodus 25:23

## The Table of Showbread

> [!quote] BSB
> "You are also to make a table of acacia wood two cubits long, a cubit wide, and a cubit and a half high." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- The table was approximately 3 feet long, 1.5 feet wide, and 2.25 feet high (91.4 centimeters long, 45.7 centimeters wide, and 68.6 centimeters high).
%%FN-END%%
