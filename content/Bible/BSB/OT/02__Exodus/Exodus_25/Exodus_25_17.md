# Exodus 25:17

## The Mercy Seat

> [!quote] BSB
> "And you are to construct a mercy seat of pure gold, two and a half cubits long and a cubit and a half wide." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Or *an atonement cover*; here and throughout Exodus
- The mercy seat was approximately 3.75 feet long and 2.25 feet wide (114.3 centimeters long and 68.6 centimeters wide).
%%FN-END%%
