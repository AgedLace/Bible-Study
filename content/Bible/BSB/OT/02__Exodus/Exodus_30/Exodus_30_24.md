# Exodus 30:24

> [!quote] BSB
> "500 shekels of cassia—all according to the sanctuary shekel—and a hin of olive oil." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *500 shekels* is approximately 12.6 pounds or 5.7 kilograms of cassia.
- *A hin* is approximately 0.97 gallons or 3.67 liters of olive oil.
%%FN-END%%
