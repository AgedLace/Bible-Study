# Exodus 30:13

> [!quote] BSB
> "Everyone who crosses over to those counted must pay a half shekel, according to the sanctuary shekel, which weighs twenty gerahs. This half shekel is an offering to the LORD." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *A half shekel* is approximately 0.2 ounces or 5.7 grams; also in verse 15.
- *20 gerahs* is equivalent to one shekel (approximately 0.4 ounces or 11.4 grams).
%%FN-END%%
