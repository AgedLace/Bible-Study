# Exodus 30:23

> [!quote] BSB
> "“Take the finest spices: 500 shekels of liquid myrrh, half that amount (250 shekels) of fragrant cinnamon, 250 shekels of fragrant cane," ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *500 shekels* is approximately 12.6 pounds or 5.7 kilograms of myrrh.
- *250 shekels* is approximately 6.3 pounds or 2.9 kilograms of cinnamon.
- *250 shekels* is approximately 6.3 pounds or 2.9 kilograms of cane.
%%FN-END%%
