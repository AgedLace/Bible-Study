# Exodus 38:26

> [!quote] BSB
> "a beka per person, that is, half a shekel, according to the sanctuary shekel, from everyone twenty years of age or older who had crossed over to be numbered, a total of 603,550 men." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *A beka* is half a shekel, or approximately 0.2 ounces or 5.7 grams.
%%FN-END%%
