# Exodus 38:18

> [!quote] BSB
> "The curtain for the entrance to the courtyard was embroidered with blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, and finely spun linen. It was twenty cubits long and, like the curtains of the courtyard, five cubits high," ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *20 cubits* is approximately 30 feet or 9.1 meters.
- *5 cubits* is approximately 7.5 feet or 2.3 meters.
%%FN-END%%
