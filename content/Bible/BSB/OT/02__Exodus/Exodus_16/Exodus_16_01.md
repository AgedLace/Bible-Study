# Exodus 16:1

## Manna and Quail from Heaven

> [!quote] BSB
> "On the fifteenth day of the second month after they had left the land of Egypt, the whole congregation of Israel set out from Elim and came to the Desert of Sin, which is between Elim and Sinai." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- The geographical name *Sin* is related to *Sinai* and should not be mistaken for the English word *sin*.
%%FN-END%%
