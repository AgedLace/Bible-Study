# Exodus 16:22

## The Sabbath Observed

> [!quote] BSB
> "On the sixth day, they gathered twice as much food—two omers per person—and all the leaders of the congregation came and reported this to Moses." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *2 omers* is approximately 4 dry quarts or 4.4 liters per person.
%%FN-END%%
