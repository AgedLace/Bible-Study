# Exodus 4:6

## Moses’ Hand

> [!quote] BSB
> "Furthermore, the LORD said to Moses, “Put your hand inside your cloak.” So he put his hand inside his cloak, and when he took it out, his hand was leprous, white as snow." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Hebrew *into your bosom*; twice in this verse and twice in verse 7
- The Hebrew word traditionally translated as *leprous* was used for various skin diseases; see Leviticus 13.
%%FN-END%%
