# Hosea 3:2

> [!quote] BSB
> "So I bought her for fifteen shekels of silver and a homer and a lethech of barley." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *15 shekels* is approximately 6 ounces or 171 grams of silver.
- Or *a homer and a half of barley*; that is, a total of approximately 9.36 bushels or 330 liters (probably about 436 pounds or 198 kilograms of barley); LXX *a homer of barley and a wineskin full of wine*
%%FN-END%%
