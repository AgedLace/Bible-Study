# Hosea 3:1

## Hosea Redeems His Wife

> [!quote] BSB
> "Then the LORD said to me, “Go show love to your wife again, though she is loved by another and is an adulteress. Love her as the LORD loves the Israelites, though they turn to other gods and love to offer raisin cakes to idols.”" ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Or *Go show love to a woman who is loved by another*
- Literally *and love raisin cakes*
