# Hosea 5:8

> [!quote] BSB
> "Blow the ram’s horn in Gibeah, the trumpet in Ramah; raise the battle cry in Beth-aven: Lead on, O Benjamin!" ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *Beth-aven* means *house of wickedness*. This is a derogatory term for Bethel, which means *house of God*; see 1 Kings 12:28–29.
%%FN-END%%
