# Hosea 4:15

> [!quote] BSB
> "Though you prostitute yourself, O Israel, may Judah avoid such guilt! Do not journey to Gilgal, do not go up to Beth-aven, and do not swear on oath, ‘As surely as the LORD lives!’" ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *Beth-aven* means *house of wickedness*. This is a derogatory term for Bethel, which means *house of God*; see 1 Kings 12:28–29.
%%FN-END%%
