# Hosea 10:8

> [!quote] BSB
> "The high places of Aven will be destroyed—it is the sin of Israel; thorns and thistles will overgrow their altars. Then they will say to the mountains, “Cover us!” and to the hills, “Fall on us!”" ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Or *of wickedness*; *Aven* is a reference to Beth-aven (a derogatory term for Bethel); see verse 5.
- Cited in Luke 23:30; see also Revelation 6:16.
%%FN-END%%
