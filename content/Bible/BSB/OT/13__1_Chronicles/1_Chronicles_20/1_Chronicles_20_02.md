# 1 Chronicles 20:2

> [!quote] BSB
> "Then David took the crown from the head of their king. It was found to weigh a talent of gold and was set with precious stones, and it was placed on David’s head. And David took a great amount of plunder from the city." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Or *from the head of Milcom*. Milcom, also called Molech, was god of the Ammonites; see Leviticus 18:21 and 1 Kings 11:7.
- *A talent* is approximately 75.4 pounds or 34.2 kilograms of gold.
%%FN-END%%
