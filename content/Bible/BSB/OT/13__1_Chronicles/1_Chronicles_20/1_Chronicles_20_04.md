# 1 Chronicles 20:4

## Battles against the Philistines

> [!quote] BSB
> "Some time later, war broke out with the Philistines at Gezer. At that time Sibbecai the Hushathite killed Sippai, a descendant of the Rephaim, and the Philistines were subdued." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *Sippai* is a variant of *Saph*; see 2 Samuel 21:18.
- Or *the giants*; see  also *descendants of Rapha* in verses 6 and 8.
%%FN-END%%
