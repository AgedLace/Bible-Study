# 1 Chronicles 25:4

> [!quote] BSB
> "From the sons of Heman: Bukkiah, Mattaniah, Uzziel, Shebuel, Jerimoth, Hananiah, Hanani, Eliathah, Giddalti, Romamti-ezer, Joshbekashah, Mallothi, Hothir, and Mahazioth." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *Shebuel* is a variant of *Shubael*; see verse 20.
- *Jerimoth* is a variant of *Jeremoth*; see verse 22.
%%FN-END%%
