# 1 Chronicles 3:5

> [!quote] BSB
> "and these sons were born to him in Jerusalem: Shimea, Shobab, Nathan, and Solomon. These four were born to him by Bathsheba daughter of Ammiel." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *Shimea* is a variant of *Shammua*; see 2 Samuel 5:14 and 1 Chronicles 14:4.
- One Hebrew manuscript and Vulgate (see also LXX and 2 Samuel 11:3); most Hebrew manuscripts *Bath-shua*
- *Ammiel* is a variant of *Eliam*; see 2 Samuel 11:3.
%%FN-END%%
