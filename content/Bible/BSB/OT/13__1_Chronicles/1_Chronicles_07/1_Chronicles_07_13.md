# 1 Chronicles 7:13

## The Descendants of Naphtali

> [!quote] BSB
> "The sons of Naphtali: Jahziel, Guni, Jezer, and Shallum—the descendants of Bilhah." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *Jahziel* is a variant of *Jahzeel*; see Genesis 46:24.
- Most Hebrew manuscripts; some Hebrew and LXX manuscripts *Shillem*; see Genesis 46:24 and Numbers 26:49.
