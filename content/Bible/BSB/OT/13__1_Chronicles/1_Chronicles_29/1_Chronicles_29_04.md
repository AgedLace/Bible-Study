# 1 Chronicles 29:4

> [!quote] BSB
> "three thousand talents of gold (the gold of Ophir) and seven thousand talents of refined silver, to overlay the walls of the buildings," ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *3,000 talents* is approximately 113 tons or 103 metric tons of gold.
- *7,000 talents* is approximately 264 tons or 239.5 metric tons of silver.
