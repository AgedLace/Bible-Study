# 1 Chronicles 26:21

> [!quote] BSB
> "From the descendants of Ladan, who were Gershonites through Ladan and heads of the families of Ladan the Gershonite, were Jehieli," ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *Ladan* is a variant of *Libni*; see 1 Chronicles 6:17.
- *Jehieli* is a variant of *Jehiel*; also in verse 22; see 1 Chronicles 23:8.
