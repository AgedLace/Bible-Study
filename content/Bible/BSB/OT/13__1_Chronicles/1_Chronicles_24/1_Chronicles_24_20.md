# 1 Chronicles 24:20

## The Rest of the Levites

> [!quote] BSB
> "Now these were the rest of the descendants of Levi: From the sons of Amram: Shubael; from the sons of Shubael: Jehdeiah." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *Shubael* (twice in this verse) is a variant of *Shebuel*; see 1 Chronicles 23:16 and 1 Chronicles 26:24.
%%FN-END%%
