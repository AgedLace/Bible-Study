# 1 Chronicles 15:20

> [!quote] BSB
> "Zechariah, Aziel, Shemiramoth, Jehiel, Unni, Eliab, Maaseiah, and Benaiah were to play the harps according to Alamoth." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *Aziel* is a variant of *Jaaziel*; see verse 18.
- *Alamoth* is probably a musical or liturgical term; here and in Psalm 46:1.
%%FN-END%%
