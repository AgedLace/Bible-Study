# 1 Chronicles 6:27

> [!quote] BSB
> "Eliab his son, Jeroham his son, and Elkanah his son." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *Eliab* is also called *Eliel*; see verse 34. Both of these are other names for Elihu; see 1 Samuel 1:1.
- Hebrew; some LXX manuscripts include *and Samuel his son*; see verses 33–34 and 1 Samuel 1:19–20.
%%FN-END%%
