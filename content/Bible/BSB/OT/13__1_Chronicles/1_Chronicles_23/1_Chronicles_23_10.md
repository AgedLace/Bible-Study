# 1 Chronicles 23:10

> [!quote] BSB
> "And the sons of Shimei: Jahath, Zina, Jeush, and Beriah. These were the sons of Shimei—four in all." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *Shimei* was possibly a son or grandson of the *Shimei* listed in verse 9.
- Most Hebrew manuscripts; one Hebrew manuscript, LXX, and Vulgate (see also verse 11) *Zizah*
