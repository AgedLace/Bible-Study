# 1 Chronicles 4:14

> [!quote] BSB
> "Meonothai was the father of Ophrah, and Seraiah was the father of Joab, the father of those living in Ge-harashim, which was given this name because its people were craftsmen." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Literally *Ge-harashim, for they were craftsmen*. The Hebrew *Ge-harashim* means *valley of craftsmen*.
%%FN-END%%
