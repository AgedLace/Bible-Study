# 1 Chronicles 1:17

> [!quote] BSB
> "The sons of Shem: Elam, Asshur, Arphaxad, Lud, and Aram. The sons of Aram: Uz, Hul, Gether, and Meshech." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- One Hebrew manuscript and some LXX manuscripts (see also Genesis 10:23); most Hebrew manuscripts do not include *The sons of Aram*.
- *Meshech* is a variant of *Mash*; see Genesis 10:23.
%%FN-END%%
