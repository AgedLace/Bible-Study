# 1 Chronicles 19:16

> [!quote] BSB
> "When the Arameans saw that they had been defeated by Israel, they sent messengers to bring more Arameans from beyond the Euphrates, with Shophach the commander of Hadadezer’s army leading them." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Hebrew *the River*
- *Shophach* is a variant of *Shobach*; also in verse 18; see 2 Samuel 10:16.
