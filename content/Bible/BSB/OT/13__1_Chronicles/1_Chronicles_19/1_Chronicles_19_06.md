# 1 Chronicles 19:6

> [!quote] BSB
> "When the Ammonites realized that they had become a stench to David, Hanun and the Ammonites sent a thousand talents of silver to hire for themselves chariots and horsemen from Aram-naharaim, Aram-maacah, and Zobah." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *1,000 talents* is approximately 37.7 tons or 34.2 metric tons of silver.
- That is, Mesopotamia; *Aram-naharaim* means *Aram of the two rivers*, likely the region between the Euphrates and Balih Rivers in northwestern Mesopotamia.
%%FN-END%%
