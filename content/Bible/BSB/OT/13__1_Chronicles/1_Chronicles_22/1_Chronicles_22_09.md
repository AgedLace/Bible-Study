# 1 Chronicles 22:9

> [!quote] BSB
> "But a son will be born to you who will be a man of rest. I will give him rest from all his enemies on every side; for his name will be Solomon, and I will grant to Israel peace and quiet during his reign." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *Solomon* sounds like and is probably derived from the Hebrew word for *peace*.
%%FN-END%%
