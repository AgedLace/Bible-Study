# 1 Chronicles 18:16

> [!quote] BSB
> "Zadok son of Ahitub and Ahimelech son of Abiathar were priests; Shavsha was the scribe;" ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Some Hebrew manuscripts, Vulgate, and Syriac (see also 2 Samuel 8:17); most Hebrew manuscripts *Abimelech*
- *Shavsha* is also called *Seraiah*, *Sheva*, and *Shisha*; see 2 Samuel 8:17, 2 Samuel 20:25, and 1 Kings 4:3.
