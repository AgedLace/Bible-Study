# 1 Chronicles 13:6

> [!quote] BSB
> "David and all Israel went up to Baalah of Judah (that is, Kiriath-jearim) to bring up from there the ark of God the LORD, who is enthroned between the cherubim—the ark that is called by the Name." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Or *the ark of God, which is called by the Name of the LORD who is enthroned between the cherubim*; or *the ark of God, the LORD, who is enthroned between the cherubim, where His Name is called*
%%FN-END%%
