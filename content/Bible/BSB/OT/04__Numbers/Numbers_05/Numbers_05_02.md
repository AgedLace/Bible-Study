# Numbers 5:2

> [!quote] BSB
> "“Command the Israelites to send away from the camp anyone with a skin disease, anyone who has a bodily discharge, and anyone who is defiled by a dead body." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Forms of the Hebrew *tzaraath*, traditionally translated as *leprosy*, were used for various skin diseases; see Leviticus 13.
