# Numbers 28:14

> [!quote] BSB
> "Their drink offerings shall be half a hin of wine with each bull, a third of a hin with the ram, and a quarter hin with each lamb. This is the monthly burnt offering to be made at each new moon throughout the year." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *Half a hin* is approximately 1.9 quarts or 1.8 liters of wine.
- *A third of a hin* is approximately 1.3 quarts or 1.2 liters of wine.
%%FN-END%%
