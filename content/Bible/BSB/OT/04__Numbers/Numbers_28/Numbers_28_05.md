# Numbers 28:5

> [!quote] BSB
> "along with a tenth of an ephah of fine flour as a grain offering, mixed with a quarter hin of oil from pressed olives." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *A tenth of an ephah* is approximately 2 dry quarts or 2.2 liters (probably about 2.6 pounds or 1.2 kilograms of flour); also in verses 13, 21, and 29.
- Or *a quarter hin of pure olive oil*; Hebrew *a quarter hin of pressed oil*; that is, approximately 0.97 quarts or 0.92 liters; similarly in verses 7 and 14
%%FN-END%%
