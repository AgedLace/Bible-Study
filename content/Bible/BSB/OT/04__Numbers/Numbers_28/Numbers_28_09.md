# Numbers 28:9

## The Sabbath Offerings

> [!quote] BSB
> "On the Sabbath day, present two unblemished year-old male lambs, accompanied by a grain offering of two-tenths of an ephah of fine flour mixed with oil, as well as a drink offering." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *Two-tenths of an ephah* is approximately 4 dry quarts or 4.4 liters (probably about 5.1 pounds or 2.3 kilograms of flour); also in verses 12, 20, and 28.
%%FN-END%%
