# Numbers 28:26

## The Feast of Weeks

> [!quote] BSB
> "On the day of firstfruits, when you present an offering of new grain to the LORD during the Feast of Weeks, you are to hold a sacred assembly; you must not do any regular work." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- That is, Shavuot, the late spring feast of pilgrimage to Jerusalem; it is also known as *the Feast of Harvest* (see Exodus 23:16) or *the Feast of Pentecost* (see Acts 2:1).
%%FN-END%%
