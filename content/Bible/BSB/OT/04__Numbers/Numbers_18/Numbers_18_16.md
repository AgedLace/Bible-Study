# Numbers 18:16

> [!quote] BSB
> "You are to pay the redemption price for a month-old male according to your valuation: five shekels of silver, according to the sanctuary shekel, which is twenty gerahs." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *5 shekels* is approximately 2 ounces or 57 grams of silver.
- *20 gerahs* is equivalent to one shekel (approximately 0.4 ounces or 11.4 grams).
%%FN-END%%
