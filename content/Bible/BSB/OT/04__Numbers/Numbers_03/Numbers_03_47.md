# Numbers 3:47

> [!quote] BSB
> "you are to collect five shekels for each one, according to the sanctuary shekel of twenty gerahs." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *5 shekels* is approximately 2 ounces or 57 grams.
- *20 gerahs* is equivalent to one shekel (approximately 0.4 ounces or 11.4 grams).
%%FN-END%%
