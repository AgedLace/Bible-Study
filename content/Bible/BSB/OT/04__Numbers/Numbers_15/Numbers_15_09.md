# Numbers 15:9

> [!quote] BSB
> "present with the bull a grain offering of three-tenths of an ephah of fine flour mixed with half a hin of olive oil." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *Three-tenths of an ephah* is approximately 6 dry quarts or 6.6 liters (probably about 7.6 pounds or 3.5 kilograms of flour).
- Or *half a hin of oil*; that is, approximately 1.9 quarts or 1.8 liters; similarly in verse 10
%%FN-END%%
