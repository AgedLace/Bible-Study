# Numbers 21:2

> [!quote] BSB
> "So Israel made a vow to the LORD: “If You will deliver this people into our hands, we will devote their cities to destruction.”" ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Forms of the Hebrew *cherem* refer to the giving over of things or persons to the LORD, either by destroying them or by giving them as an offering; also in verse 3.
