# Numbers 7:85

> [!quote] BSB
> "Each silver platter weighed a hundred and thirty shekels, and each silver bowl seventy shekels. The total weight of the silver articles was two thousand four hundred shekels, according to the sanctuary shekel." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *2,400 shekels* is approximately 60.3 pounds or 27.4 kilograms.
%%FN-END%%
