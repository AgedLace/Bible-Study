# Numbers 7:13

> [!quote] BSB
> "His offering was one silver platter weighing a hundred and thirty shekels, and one silver bowl weighing seventy shekels, both according to the sanctuary shekel and filled with fine flour mixed with oil for a grain offering;" ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *130 shekels* is approximately 3.3 pounds or 1.48 kilograms; here and throughout this chapter.
- *70 shekels* is approximately 1.76 pounds or 797.8 grams; here and throughout this chapter.
%%FN-END%%
