# Numbers 12:10

> [!quote] BSB
> "As the cloud lifted from above the Tent, suddenly Miriam became leprous, white as snow. Aaron turned toward her, saw that she was leprous," ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- The Hebrew word traditionally translated as *leprous* was used for various skin diseases; see Leviticus 13.
