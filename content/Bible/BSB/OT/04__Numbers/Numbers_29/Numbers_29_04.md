# Numbers 29:4

> [!quote] BSB
> "and a tenth of an ephah with each of the seven male lambs." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *A tenth of an ephah* is approximately 2 dry quarts or 2.2 liters (probably about 2.6 pounds or 1.2 kilograms of flour); also in verses 10 and 15.
