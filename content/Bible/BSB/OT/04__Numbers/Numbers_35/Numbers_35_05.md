# Numbers 35:5

> [!quote] BSB
> "You are also to measure two thousand cubits outside the city on the east, two thousand on the south, two thousand on the west, and two thousand on the north, with the city in the center. These areas will serve as larger pasturelands for the cities." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *2,000 cubits* is approximately 3,000 feet or 914.4 meters.
%%FN-END%%
