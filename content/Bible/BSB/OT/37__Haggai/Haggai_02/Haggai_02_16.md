# Haggai 2:16

> [!quote] BSB
> "from that time, when one came expecting a heap of twenty ephahs of grain, there were but ten. When one came to the winepress to draw out fifty baths, there were but twenty." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Literally *a heap of twenty*
- Literally *to draw out fifty from the winepress*
