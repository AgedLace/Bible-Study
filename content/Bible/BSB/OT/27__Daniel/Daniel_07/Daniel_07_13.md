# Daniel 7:13

## Daniel’s Vision of the Son of Man

> [!quote] BSB
> "In my vision in the night I continued to watch, and I saw One like the Son of Man coming with the clouds of heaven. He approached the Ancient of Days and was led into His presence." ^text

> [!note] Footnotes

- See Matthew 24:30, Matthew 26:64, Mark 13:26, Mark 14:62, Luke 21:27, Revelation 1:13, and Revelation 14:14.
