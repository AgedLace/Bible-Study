# Daniel 3:1

## Nebuchadnezzar’s Golden Statue

> [!quote] BSB
> "King Nebuchadnezzar made a golden statue sixty cubits high and six cubits wide, and he set it up on the plain of Dura in the province of Babylon." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Or *image*; here and throughout Daniel 3
- The statue was approximately 90 feet high and 9 feet wide (27.4 meters high and 2.7 meters wide).
%%FN-END%%
