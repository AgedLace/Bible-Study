# Daniel 9:24

> [!quote] BSB
> "Seventy weeks are decreed for your people and your holy city to stop their transgression, to put an end to sin, to make atonement for iniquity, to bring in everlasting righteousness, to seal up vision and prophecy, and to anoint the Most Holy Place." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Or *Seventy sevens*; also twice in verse 25 and once in verse 26
- Or *the Holy of Holies* or *the Most Holy One*; literally *the Most Holy*
%%FN-END%%
