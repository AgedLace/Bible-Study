# Daniel 9:27

> [!quote] BSB
> "And he will confirm a covenant with many for one week, but in the middle of the week he will put an end to sacrifice and offering. And on the wing of the temple will come the abomination that causes desolation, until the decreed destruction is poured out upon him.”" ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Or *for one seven*; similarly again in this verse
- Literally *And on the wing (will come) the abomination that causes desolation,* or *And on the wing of abominations (will come) one who causes desolation,*
- Literally *until the decreed consummation is poured out upon (him who) is desolate.*
%%FN-END%%
