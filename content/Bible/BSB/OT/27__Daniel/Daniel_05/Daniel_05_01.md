# Daniel 5:1

## Belshazzar’s Feast

> [!quote] BSB
> "Later, King Belshazzar held a great feast for a thousand of his nobles, and he drank wine with them." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Aramaic does not include *Later*. Some translators include *Many years later* to account for the time elapsed, probably about 30 years, since the previous chapter.
