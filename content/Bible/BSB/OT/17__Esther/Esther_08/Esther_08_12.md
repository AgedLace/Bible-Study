# Esther 8:12

> [!quote] BSB
> "The single day appointed throughout all the provinces of King Xerxes was the thirteenth day of the twelfth month, the month of Adar." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *Adar* is the twelfth month of the Hebrew lunar calendar, usually occurring within the months of February and March.
