# Esther 3:7

> [!quote] BSB
> "In the twelfth year of King Xerxes, in the first month, the month of Nisan, the Pur (that is, the lot) was cast before Haman to determine a day and month. And the lot fell on the twelfth month, the month of Adar." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *Nisan* is the first month of the Hebrew lunar calendar, usually occurring within the months of March and April.
- LXX *was cast before Haman—a day and month—and the lot fell on the fourteenth of the month of Adar*; Hebrew *was cast before Haman a day and month—the twelfth, Adar*; the month of *Adar* (also in verse 13) is the twelfth month of the Hebrew lunar calendar, usually occurring within the months of February and March.
%%FN-END%%
