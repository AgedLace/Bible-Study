# Esther 5:14

> [!quote] BSB
> "His wife Zeresh and all his friends told him, “Have them build a gallows fifty cubits high, and ask the king in the morning to have Mordecai hanged on it. Then go to the banquet with the king and enjoy yourself.” The advice pleased Haman, and he had the gallows constructed." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *50 cubits* is approximately 75 feet or 22.9 meters high.
