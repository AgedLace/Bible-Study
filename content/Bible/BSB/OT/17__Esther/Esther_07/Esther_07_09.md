# Esther 7:9

> [!quote] BSB
> "Then Harbonah, one of the eunuchs attending the king, said: “There is a gallows fifty cubits high at Haman’s house. He had it built for Mordecai, who gave the report that saved the king.” “Hang him on it!” declared the king." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *50 cubits* is approximately 75 feet or 22.9 meters high.
%%FN-END%%
