# Genesis 17:5

> [!quote] BSB
> "No longer will you be called Abram, but your name will be Abraham, for I have made you a father of many nations." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *Abram* means *exalted father*.
- *Abraham* means *father of many*.
- Cited in Romans 4:17
%%FN-END%%
