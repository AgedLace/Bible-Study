# Genesis 17:15

> [!quote] BSB
> "Then God said to Abraham, “As for Sarai your wife, do not call her Sarai, for her name is to be Sarah." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Both *Sarai* and *Sarah* mean *princess*; the change in spelling may reflect the difference in dialect between Ur and Canaan.
%%FN-END%%
