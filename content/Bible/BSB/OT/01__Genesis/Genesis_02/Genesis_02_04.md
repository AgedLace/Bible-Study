# Genesis 2:4

## Man and Woman in the Garden

> [!quote] BSB
> "This is the account of the heavens and the earth when they were created, in the day that the LORD God made them." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *LORD* or *GOD*, with capital letters, represents the proper name of the God of Israel and the one true God, transliterated from the Hebrew as *YHWH*; here and throughout the Scriptures.
