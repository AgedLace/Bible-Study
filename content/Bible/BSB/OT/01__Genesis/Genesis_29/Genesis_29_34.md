# Genesis 29:34

> [!quote] BSB
> "Once again Leah conceived and gave birth to a son, and she said, “Now at last my husband will become attached to me, because I have borne him three sons.” So he was named Levi." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *Levi* sounds like the Hebrew for *being attached to* or *feeling affection for*.
