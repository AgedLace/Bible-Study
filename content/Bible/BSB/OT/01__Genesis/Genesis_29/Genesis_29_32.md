# Genesis 29:32

> [!quote] BSB
> "And Leah conceived and gave birth to a son, and she named him Reuben, for she said, “The LORD has seen my affliction. Surely my husband will love me now.”" ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *Reuben* means *Look, a son* and also sounds like the Hebrew for *He has seen my misery*.
%%FN-END%%
