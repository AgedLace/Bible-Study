# Genesis 18:6

> [!quote] BSB
> "So Abraham hurried into the tent and said to Sarah, “Quick! Prepare three seahs of fine flour, knead it, and bake some bread.”" ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *3 seahs* is approximately 19.8 dry quarts or 21.9 liters (probably about 24.5 pounds or 11.1 kilograms of flour).
