# Genesis 3:15

> [!quote] BSB
> "And I will put enmity between you and the woman, and between your seed and her seed. He will crush your head, and you will strike his heel.”" ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Or *He will bruise your head, and you will bruise his heel*. The same Hebrew root for *crush*, *bruise*, or *strike* appears twice in this verse.
%%FN-END%%
