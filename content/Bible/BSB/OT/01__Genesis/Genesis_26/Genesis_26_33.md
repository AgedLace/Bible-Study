# Genesis 26:33

> [!quote] BSB
> "So he called it Shibah, and to this day the name of the city is Beersheba." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *Shibah* can mean *oath* or *seven*.
- *Beersheba* means *well of seven* or *well of the oath*.
%%FN-END%%
