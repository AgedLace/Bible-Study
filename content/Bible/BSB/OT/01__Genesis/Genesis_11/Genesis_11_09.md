# Genesis 11:9

> [!quote] BSB
> "That is why it is called Babel, for there the LORD confused the language of the whole world, and from that place the LORD scattered them over the face of all the earth." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Or *Babylon*; the Hebrew word for *Babel* sounds like the Hebrew for *confused*.
