# Genesis 30:11

> [!quote] BSB
> "Then Leah said, “How fortunate!” So she named him Gad." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Alternate MT reading (see also LXX); the other alternate reads *“A troop is coming!”*
- *Gad* sounds like the Hebrew for *good fortune*, or alternately for *band of raiders*.
