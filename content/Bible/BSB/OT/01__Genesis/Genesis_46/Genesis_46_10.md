# Genesis 46:10

> [!quote] BSB
> "The sons of Simeon: Jemuel, Jamin, Ohad, Jachin, Zohar, and Shaul the son of a Canaanite woman." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *Jemuel* is another name for *Nemuel*; see Numbers 26:12.
- *Zohar* is a variant of *Zerah*; see Numbers 26:13 and 1 Chronicles 4:24.
%%FN-END%%
