# Genesis 32:28

> [!quote] BSB
> "Then the man said, “Your name will no longer be Jacob, but Israel, because you have struggled with God and with men, and you have prevailed.”" ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *Jacob* means *he grasps the heel* or *he deceives*.
- *Israel* means *he struggles with God*.
%%FN-END%%
