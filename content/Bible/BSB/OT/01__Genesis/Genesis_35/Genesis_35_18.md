# Genesis 35:18

> [!quote] BSB
> "And with her last breath—for she was dying—she named him Ben-oni. But his father called him Benjamin." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *Ben-oni* could mean *son of my sorrow* or *son of my strength*.
- *Benjamin* means *son of my right hand*.
%%FN-END%%
