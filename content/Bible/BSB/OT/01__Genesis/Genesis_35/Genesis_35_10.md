# Genesis 35:10

> [!quote] BSB
> "And God said to him, “Though your name is Jacob, you will no longer be called Jacob. Instead, your name will be Israel.” So God named him Israel." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *Jacob* means *he grasps the heel* or *he deceives*.
- *Israel* means *he struggles with God*.
%%FN-END%%
