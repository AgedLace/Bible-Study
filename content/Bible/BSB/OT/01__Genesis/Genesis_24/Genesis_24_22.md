# Genesis 24:22

> [!quote] BSB
> "And after the camels had finished drinking, he took out a gold ring weighing a beka, and two gold bracelets for her wrists weighing ten shekels." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *A beka* is half a shekel, or approximately 0.2 ounces or 5.7 grams.
- *10 shekels* is approximately 4 ounces or 114 grams.
%%FN-END%%
