# Genesis 6:16

> [!quote] BSB
> "You are to make a roof for the ark, finish its walls a cubit from the top, place a door in the side of the ark, and build lower, middle, and upper decks." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Or *skylight* or *window*
- *A cubit* is approximately 18 inches or 45.7 centimeters.
%%FN-END%%
