# Genesis 6:15

> [!quote] BSB
> "And this is how you are to build it: The ark is to be 300 cubits long, 50 cubits wide, and 30 cubits high." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- The ark was approximately 450 feet long, 75 feet wide, and 45 feet high (137.2 meters long, 22.9 meters wide, and 13.7 meters high).
%%FN-END%%
