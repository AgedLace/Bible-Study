# Nehemiah 3:15

> [!quote] BSB
> "The Fountain Gate was repaired by Shallun son of Col-hozeh, ruler of the district of Mizpah. He rebuilt it, roofed it, and installed its doors, bolts, and bars. He also repaired the wall of the Pool of Shelah near the king’s garden, as far as the stairs that descend from the City of David." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Hebrew; Syriac *Shallum*
- *Pool of Shelah* is another name for the Pool of Siloam.
%%FN-END%%
