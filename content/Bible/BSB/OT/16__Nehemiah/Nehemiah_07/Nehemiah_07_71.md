# Nehemiah 7:71

> [!quote] BSB
> "And some of the heads of the families gave to the treasury for the project 20,000 darics of gold and 2,200 minas of silver." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Or *20,000 gold drachmas*; that is, approximately 370.4 pounds or 168 kilograms of gold coins; also in verse 72
- Or *2,200 silver minas*; that is, approximately 1.38 tons or 1.25 metric tons of silver
%%FN-END%%
