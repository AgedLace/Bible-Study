# Nehemiah 13:6

> [!quote] BSB
> "While all this was happening, I was not in Jerusalem, because I had returned to Artaxerxes king of Babylon in the thirty-second year of his reign. Some time later I obtained leave from the king" ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Artaxerxes king of Persia is identified here as the king of Babylon because Persia had conquered the Babylonian Empire.
%%FN-END%%
