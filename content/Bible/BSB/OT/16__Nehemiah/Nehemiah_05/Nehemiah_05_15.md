# Nehemiah 5:15

> [!quote] BSB
> "The governors before me had heavily burdened the people, taking from them bread and wine plus forty shekels of silver. Their servants also oppressed the people. But I did not do this, because of my fear of God." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *40 shekels* is approximately 1 pound or 453.6 grams of silver.
%%FN-END%%
