# 1 Kings 7:21

> [!quote] BSB
> "Thus he set up the pillars at the portico of the temple. The pillar to the south he named Jachin, and the pillar to the north he named Boaz." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *Jachin* probably means *He establishes*.
- *Boaz* probably means *in Him is strength*.
%%FN-END%%
