# 1 Kings 7:27

## The Ten Bronze Stands

> [!quote] BSB
> "In addition, he made ten movable stands of bronze, each four cubits long, four cubits wide, and three cubits high." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- The stands were approximately 6 feet in length and width, and 4.5 feet high (1.8 meters in length and width, and 1.4 meters high).
%%FN-END%%
