# 1 Kings 7:10

> [!quote] BSB
> "The foundations were laid with large, costly stones, some ten cubits long and some eight cubits long." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes

- *8 cubits* is approximately 12 feet or 3.7 meters.
