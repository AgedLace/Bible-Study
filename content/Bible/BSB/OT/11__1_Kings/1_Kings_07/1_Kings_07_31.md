# 1 Kings 7:31

> [!quote] BSB
> "The opening to each stand inside the crown at the top was one cubit deep, with a round opening like the design of a pedestal, a cubit and a half wide. And around its opening were engravings, but the panels of the stands were square, not round." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *One cubit* is approximately 18 inches or 45.7 centimeters.
- *A cubit and a half* is approximately 2.25 feet or 68.6 centimeters wide; similarly in verse 32.
%%FN-END%%
