# 1 Kings 7:2

> [!quote] BSB
> "He built the House of the Forest of Lebanon a hundred cubits long, fifty cubits wide, and thirty cubits high, with four rows of cedar pillars supporting the cedar beams." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- The house was approximately 150 feet long, 75 feet wide, and 45 feet high (45.7 meters long, 22.9 meters wide, and 13.7 meters high).
%%FN-END%%
