# 1 Kings 7:26

> [!quote] BSB
> "It was a handbreadth thick, and its rim was fashioned like the brim of a cup, like a lily blossom. It could hold two thousand baths." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *A handbreadth* is approximately 2.9 inches or 7.4 centimeters.
- *2,000 baths* is approximately 11,600 gallons or 44,000 liters; LXX does not include this sentence.
%%FN-END%%
