# 1 Kings 7:23

## The Molten Sea

> [!quote] BSB
> "He also made the Sea of cast metal. It was circular in shape, measuring ten cubits from rim to rim, five cubits in height, and thirty cubits in circumference." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- The Sea was approximately 15 feet from rim to rim, 7.5 feet in height, and 45 feet in circumference (4.6 meters from rim to rim, 2.3 meters in height, and 13.7 meters in circumference).
%%FN-END%%
