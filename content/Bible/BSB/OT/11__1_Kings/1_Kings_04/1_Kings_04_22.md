# 1 Kings 4:22

> [!quote] BSB
> "Solomon’s provisions for a single day were thirty cors of fine flour, sixty cors of meal," ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *30 cors* is approximately 187 bushels or 6,600 liters (probably about 5.5 tons or 5 metric tons of flour).
- *60 cors* is approximately 375 bushels or 13,200 liters (probably about 11 tons or 10 metric tons of meal).
%%FN-END%%
