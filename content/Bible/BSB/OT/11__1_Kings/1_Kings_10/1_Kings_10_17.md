# 1 Kings 10:17

> [!quote] BSB
> "He also made three hundred small shields of hammered gold; three minas of gold went into each shield. And the king put them in the House of the Forest of Lebanon." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *3 minas* is approximately 3.77 pounds or 1.71 kilograms of gold; possibly a reference to double minas, that is, approximately 7.54 pounds or 3.42 kilograms.
%%FN-END%%
