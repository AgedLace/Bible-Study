# 1 Kings 18:32

> [!quote] BSB
> "And with the stones, Elijah built an altar in the name of the LORD. Then he dug a trench around the altar large enough to hold two seahs of seed." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *2 seahs* is approximately 13.2 dry quarts or 14.6 liters (probably about 25.4 pounds or 11.5 kilograms of seed).
%%FN-END%%
