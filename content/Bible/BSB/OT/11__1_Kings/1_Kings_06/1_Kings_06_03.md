# 1 Kings 6:3

> [!quote] BSB
> "The portico at the front of the main hall of the temple was twenty cubits long, extending across the width of the temple and projecting out ten cubits in front of the temple." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *20 cubits* is approximately 30 feet or 9.1 meters; also in verses 16 and 20.
- *10 cubits* is approximately 15 feet or 4.6 meters; also in verses 23–26.
%%FN-END%%
