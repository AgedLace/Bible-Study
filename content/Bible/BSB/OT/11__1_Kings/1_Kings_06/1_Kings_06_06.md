# 1 Kings 6:6

> [!quote] BSB
> "The bottom floor was five cubits wide, the middle floor six cubits, and the third floor seven cubits. He also placed offset ledges around the outside of the temple, so that nothing would be inserted into its walls." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *5 cubits* is approximately 7.5 feet or 2.3 meters; also in verses 10 and 24.
- *6 cubits* is approximately 9 feet or 2.7 meters.
- *7 cubits* is approximately 10.5 feet or 3.2 meters.
%%FN-END%%
