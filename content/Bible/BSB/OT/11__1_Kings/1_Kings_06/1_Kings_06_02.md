# 1 Kings 6:2

> [!quote] BSB
> "The house that King Solomon built for the LORD was sixty cubits long, twenty cubits wide, and thirty cubits high." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- The house was approximately 90 feet long, 30 feet wide, and 45 feet high (27.4 meters long, 9.1 meters wide, and 13.7 meters high).
%%FN-END%%
