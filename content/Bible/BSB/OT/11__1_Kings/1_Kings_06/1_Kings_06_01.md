# 1 Kings 6:1

## Temple Construction Begins

> [!quote] BSB
> "In the four hundred and eightieth year after the Israelites had come out of the land of Egypt, in the fourth year of Solomon’s reign over Israel, in the month of Ziv, the second month, he began to build the house of the LORD." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Hebrew; LXX *four hundred and fortieth*
- *Ziv* was the second month of the ancient Hebrew lunar calendar, usually occurring within the months of April and May; also in verse 37.
%%FN-END%%
