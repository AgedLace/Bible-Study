# 1 Kings 6:38

> [!quote] BSB
> "In the eleventh year, in the month of Bul, the eighth month, the temple was finished in every detail and according to every specification. So he built the temple in seven years." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *Bul* was the eighth month of the ancient Hebrew lunar calendar, usually occurring within the months of October and November.
%%FN-END%%
