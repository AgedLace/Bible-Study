# Joel 2:20

> [!quote] BSB
> "The northern army I will drive away from you, banishing it to a barren and desolate land, its front ranks into the Eastern Sea, and its rear guard into the Western Sea. And its stench will rise; its foul odor will ascend. For He has done great things." ^text

%%FN-START%%

- That is, the Dead Sea
- That is, the Mediterranean Sea, also called the Great Sea
