> [!quote] BSB
> "I will gather all the nations and bring them down to the Valley of Jehoshaphat. There I will enter into judgment against them concerning My people, My inheritance, Israel, whom they have scattered among the nations as they divided up My land." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *Jehoshaphat* means *the LORD judges*; also in verse 12.
