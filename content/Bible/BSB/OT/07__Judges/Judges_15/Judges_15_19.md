# Judges 15:19

> [!quote] BSB
> "So God opened up the hollow place in Lehi, and water came out of it. When Samson drank, his strength returned, and he was revived. That is why he named it En-hakkore, and it remains in Lehi to this day." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *En-hakkore* means *the spring of him who calls*.
