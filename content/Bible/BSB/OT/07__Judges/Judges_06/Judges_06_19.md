# Judges 6:19

> [!quote] BSB
> "So Gideon went in and prepared a young goat and unleavened bread and an ephah of flour. He placed the meat in a basket and the broth in a pot and brought them out to present to Him under the oak." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *An ephah* is approximately 20 dry quarts or 22 liters (probably about 25.5 pounds or 11.6 kilograms of flour).
%%FN-END%%
