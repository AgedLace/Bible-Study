> [!quote] BSB
> "said to his mother, “The eleven hundred shekels of silver that were taken from you and about which I heard you utter a curse—I have the silver here with me; I took it.” Then his mother said, “Blessed be my son by the LORD!”" ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *1,100 shekels* is approximately 27.6 pounds or 12.5 kilograms of silver; also in verse 3.
