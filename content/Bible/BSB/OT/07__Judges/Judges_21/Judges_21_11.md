# Judges 21:11

> [!quote] BSB
> "This is what you are to do: Devote to destruction every male, as well as every female who has had relations with a man.”" ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Forms of the Hebrew *cherem* refer to the giving over of things or persons to the LORD, either by destroying them or by giving them as an offering.
%%FN-END%%
