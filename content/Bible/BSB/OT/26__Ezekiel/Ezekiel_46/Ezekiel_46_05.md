# Ezekiel 46:5

> [!quote] BSB
> "The grain offering with the ram shall be one ephah, and the grain offering with the lambs shall be as much as he is able, along with a hin of oil per ephah." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *An ephah* is approximately 20 dry quarts or 22 liters (probably about 38.3 pounds or 17.4 kilograms of grain); also in verses 7 and 11.
%%FN-END%%
