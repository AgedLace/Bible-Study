# Ezekiel 46:14

> [!quote] BSB
> "You are also to provide with it every morning a grain offering of a sixth of an ephah with a third of a hin of oil to moisten the fine flour—a grain offering to the LORD. This is a permanent statute." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *A sixth of an ephah* is approximately 3.3 dry quarts or 3.7 liters (probably about 4.2 pounds or 1.9 kilograms of flour).
- Or *a third of a hin of olive oil*; that is, approximately 1.3 quarts or 1.2 liters
%%FN-END%%
