# Ezekiel 43:17

> [!quote] BSB
> "The ledge shall also be square, fourteen cubits long and fourteen cubits wide, with a rim of half a cubit and a gutter of a cubit all around it. The steps of the altar shall face east.”" ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *14 (long) cubits* is approximately 24.5 feet or 7.5 meters.
- *A half cubit* is approximately 10.5 inches or 26.7 centimeters.
%%FN-END%%
