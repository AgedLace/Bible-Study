# Ezekiel 43:13

## The Altar of Sacrifice

> [!quote] BSB
> "These are the measurements of the altar in long cubits (a cubit and a handbreadth): Its gutter shall be a cubit deep and a cubit wide, with a rim of one span around its edge. And this is the height of the altar:" ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- The *long cubit*, about 21 inches or 53.3 centimeters, is the basic unit for linear measurement throughout Ezekiel 40–48.
- *One span* is approximately 9 inches or 22.9 centimeters.
%%FN-END%%
