# Ezekiel 48:8

## The Portions for the Priests and Levites

> [!quote] BSB
> "Bordering the territory of Judah, from east to west, will be the portion you are to set apart. It will be 25,000 cubits wide, and the length of a tribal portion from east to west. In the center will be the sanctuary." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *25,000 (long) cubits* is approximately 8.3 miles or 13.3 kilometers; also in verses 9, 10, 13, 15, 20, and 21.
%%FN-END%%
