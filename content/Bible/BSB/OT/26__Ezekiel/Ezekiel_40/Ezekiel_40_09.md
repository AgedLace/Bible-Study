# Ezekiel 40:9

> [!quote] BSB
> "it was eight cubits deep, and its jambs were two cubits thick. And the portico of the gateway faced the temple." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *8 (long) cubits* is approximately 14 feet or 4.3 meters.
- *2 (long) cubits* is approximately 3.5 feet or 1.1 meters.
%%FN-END%%
