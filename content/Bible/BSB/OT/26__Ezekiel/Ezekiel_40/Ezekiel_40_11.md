# Ezekiel 40:11

> [!quote] BSB
> "And he measured the width of the gateway entrance to be ten cubits, and its length was thirteen cubits." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *10 (long) cubits* is approximately 17.5 feet or 5.3 meters.
- *13 (long) cubits* is approximately 22.75 feet or 6.9 meters.
