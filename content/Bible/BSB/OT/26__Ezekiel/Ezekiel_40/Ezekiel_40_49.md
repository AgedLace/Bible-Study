# Ezekiel 40:49

> [!quote] BSB
> "The portico was twenty cubits wide and twelve cubits deep, and ten steps led up to it. There were columns by the side pillars, one on each side." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *20 (long) cubits* is approximately 35 feet or 10.7 meters.
- LXX; *12 (long) cubits* is approximately 21 feet or 6.4 meters; Hebrew *eleven cubits deep*; that is, approximately 19.25 feet or 5.9 meters.
- LXX; Hebrew *and steps led up to it*
%%FN-END%%
