# Ezekiel 40:19

> [!quote] BSB
> "Then he measured the distance from the front of the lower gateway to the outside of the inner court; it was a hundred cubits on the east side as well as on the north." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *100 (long) cubits* is approximately 175 feet or 53.3 meters; also in verses 23, 27, and 47.
%%FN-END%%
