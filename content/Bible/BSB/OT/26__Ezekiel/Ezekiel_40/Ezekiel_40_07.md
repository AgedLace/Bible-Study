# Ezekiel 40:7

> [!quote] BSB
> "Each gate chamber was one rod long and one rod wide, and there were five cubits between the gate chambers. The inner threshold of the gate by the portico facing inward was one rod deep." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *5 (long) cubits* is approximately 8.75 feet or 2.7 meters; also in verses 30 and 48.
%%FN-END%%
