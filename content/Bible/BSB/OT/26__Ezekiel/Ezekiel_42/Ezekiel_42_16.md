# Ezekiel 42:16

> [!quote] BSB
> "With a measuring rod he measured the east side to be five hundred cubits long." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- See LXX; *five hundred cubits* from verse 17 LXX and implied in verses 16, 18, 19, and 20 is approximately 875 feet or 266.7 meters in length. Hebrew *five hundred reeds, with the measuring reed round about*, that is approximately 5,250 feet or 1,600 meters; similarly in verses 17, 18, 19, and 20.
%%FN-END%%
