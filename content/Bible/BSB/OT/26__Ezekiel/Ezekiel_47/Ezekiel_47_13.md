# Ezekiel 47:13

## The Borders of the Land

> [!quote] BSB
> "This is what the Lord GOD says: “These are the boundaries by which you are to divide the land as an inheritance among the twelve tribes of Israel; Joseph shall receive two portions." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Since Levi had no portion, Joseph’s sons Ephraim and Manasseh received land as two tribes.
%%FN-END%%
