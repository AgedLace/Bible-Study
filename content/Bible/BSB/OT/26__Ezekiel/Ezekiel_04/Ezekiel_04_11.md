# Ezekiel 4:11

> [!quote] BSB
> "You are also to measure out a sixth of a hin of water to drink, and you are to drink it at set times." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *A sixth of a hin* is approximately 0.65 quarts or 0.61 liters of water.
