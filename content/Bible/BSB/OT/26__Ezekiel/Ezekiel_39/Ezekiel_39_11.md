# Ezekiel 39:11

> [!quote] BSB
> "And on that day I will give Gog a burial place in Israel, the Valley of the Travelers, east of the Sea. It will block those who travel through, because Gog and all his hordes will be buried there. So it will be called the Valley of Hamon-gog." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- That is, the Dead Sea
- *Hamon-gog* means *multitude of Gog*.
%%FN-END%%
