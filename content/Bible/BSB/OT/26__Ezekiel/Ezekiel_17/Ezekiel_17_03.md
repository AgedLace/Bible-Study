# Ezekiel 17:3

> [!quote] BSB
> "and tell them that this is what the Lord GOD says: ‘A great eagle with great wings and long pinions, full of feathers of many colors, came to Lebanon and took away the top of the cedar." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *Pinions* are the outer parts of a bird’s wings, including the flight feathers.
