# Ezekiel 41:1

## Inside the Temple

> [!quote] BSB
> "Then the man brought me into the outer sanctuary and measured the side pillars to be six cubits wide on each side." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *6 (long) cubits* is approximately 10.5 feet or 3.2 meters; also in verses 3, 5, and 8.
- One Hebrew manuscript and LXX; most Hebrew manuscripts *on each side—the width of the tent*
%%FN-END%%
