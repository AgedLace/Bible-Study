# Ezekiel 41:4

> [!quote] BSB
> "Then he measured the room adjacent to the inner sanctuary to be twenty cubits long and twenty cubits wide. And he said to me, “This is the Most Holy Place.”" ^text

> [!note] Footnotes

- Or *the Holy of Holies*
