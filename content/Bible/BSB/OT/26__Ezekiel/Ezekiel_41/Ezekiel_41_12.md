# Ezekiel 41:12

> [!quote] BSB
> "Now the building that faced the temple courtyard on the west was seventy cubits wide, and the wall of the building was five cubits thick all around, with a length of ninety cubits." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *70 (long) cubits* is approximately 122.5 feet or 37.3 meters.
- *90 (long) cubits* is approximately 157.5 feet or 48 meters.
%%FN-END%%
