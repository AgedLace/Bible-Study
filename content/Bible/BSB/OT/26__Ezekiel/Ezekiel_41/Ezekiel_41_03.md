# Ezekiel 41:3

> [!quote] BSB
> "And he went into the inner sanctuary and measured the side pillars at the entrance to be two cubits wide. The entrance was six cubits wide, and the walls on each side were seven cubits wide." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *2 (long) cubits* is approximately 3.5 feet or 1.1 meters.
- *7 (long) cubits* is approximately 12.25 feet or 3.7 meters.
%%FN-END%%
