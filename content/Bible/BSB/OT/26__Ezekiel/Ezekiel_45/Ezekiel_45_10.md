# Ezekiel 45:10

## Honest Scales

> [!quote] BSB
> "You must use honest scales, a just ephah, and a just bath." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *An ephah* is a dry measure of approximately 20 dry quarts or 22 liters.
- *A bath* was a liquid measure of approximately 5.8 gallons or 22 liters.
%%FN-END%%
