# Ezekiel 45:24

> [!quote] BSB
> "He shall also provide as a grain offering an ephah for each bull and an ephah for each ram, along with a hin of olive oil for each ephah of grain." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Literally *a hin of oil for each ephah*; that is, approximately 0.97 gallons or 3.67 liters of oil for each ephah of grain
%%FN-END%%
