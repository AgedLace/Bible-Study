# Ezekiel 45:12

> [!quote] BSB
> "The shekel will consist of twenty gerahs. Twenty shekels plus twenty-five shekels plus fifteen shekels will equal one mina." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *20 gerahs* is equivalent to one shekel (approximately 0.4 ounces or 11.4 grams).
- That is, 60 shekels total; elsewhere, the common mina was 50 shekels. Sixty shekels weighed approximately 1.5 pounds or 683.8 grams.
%%FN-END%%
