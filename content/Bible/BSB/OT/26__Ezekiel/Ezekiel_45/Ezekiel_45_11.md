# Ezekiel 45:11

> [!quote] BSB
> "The ephah and the bath shall be the same quantity so that the bath will contain a tenth of a homer, and the ephah a tenth of a homer; the homer will be the standard measure for both." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *A homer* was a dry measure of approximately 6.24 bushels or 220 liters; also in verses 13 and 14.
%%FN-END%%
