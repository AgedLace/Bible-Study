# Ezekiel 45:6

> [!quote] BSB
> "As the property of the city, you are to set aside an area 5,000 cubits wide and 25,000 cubits long, adjacent to the holy district. It will belong to the whole house of Israel." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- The property of the city was to be approximately 1.7 miles wide and 8.3 miles long (2.7 kilometers wide and 13.3 kilometers long).
%%FN-END%%
