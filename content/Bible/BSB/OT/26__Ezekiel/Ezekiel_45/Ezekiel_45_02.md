# Ezekiel 45:2

> [!quote] BSB
> "Within this area there is to be a section for the sanctuary 500 cubits square, with 50 cubits around it for open land." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *500 (long) cubits* is approximately 875 feet or 266.7 meters.
- *50 (long) cubits* is approximately 87.5 feet or 26.7 meters.
%%FN-END%%
