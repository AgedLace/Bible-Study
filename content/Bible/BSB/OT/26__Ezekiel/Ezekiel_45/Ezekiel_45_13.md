# Ezekiel 45:13

## Offerings and Feasts

> [!quote] BSB
> "This is the contribution you are to offer: a sixth of an ephah from each homer of wheat, and a sixth of an ephah from each homer of barley." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- That is, a sixtieth of the harvest of wheat and of barley; *a sixth of an ephah* was approximately 3.3 dry quarts or 3.7 liters.
%%FN-END%%
