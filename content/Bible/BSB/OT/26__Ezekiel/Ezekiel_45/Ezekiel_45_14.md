# Ezekiel 45:14

> [!quote] BSB
> "The prescribed portion of oil, measured by the bath, is a tenth of a bath from each cor (a cor consists of ten baths or one homer, since ten baths are equivalent to a homer)." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- That is, one percent of one’s oil; *a tenth of a bath* was approximately 2.3 quarts or 2.2 liters.
%%FN-END%%
