# Leviticus 24:5

## The Showbread

> [!quote] BSB
> "You are also to take fine flour and bake twelve loaves, using two-tenths of an ephah for each loaf," ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *Two-tenths of an ephah* is approximately 4 dry quarts or 4.4 liters (probably about 5.1 pounds or 2.3 kilograms of flour).
%%FN-END%%
