# Leviticus 13:2

> [!quote] BSB
> "“When someone has a swelling or rash or bright spot on his skin that may be an infectious skin disease, he must be brought to Aaron the priest or to one of his sons who is a priest." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Forms of the Hebrew *tzaraath*, traditionally translated as *leprosy*, were used for various skin diseases; here and throughout verses 3–46.
%%FN-END%%
