# Leviticus 23:6

> [!quote] BSB
> "On the fifteenth day of the same month begins the Feast of Unleavened Bread to the LORD. For seven days you must eat unleavened bread." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- That is, the seven-day period after the Passover during which no leaven may be eaten; see Exodus 12:14–20.
%%FN-END%%
