# Leviticus 23:13

> [!quote] BSB
> "along with its grain offering of two-tenths of an ephah of fine flour mixed with oil—a food offering to the LORD, a pleasing aroma—and its drink offering of a quarter hin of wine." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *Two-tenths of an ephah* is approximately 4 dry quarts or 4.4 liters (probably about 5.1 pounds or 2.3 kilograms of flour); also in verse 17.
- *A quarter hin* is approximately 0.97 quarts or 0.92 liters of wine.
%%FN-END%%
