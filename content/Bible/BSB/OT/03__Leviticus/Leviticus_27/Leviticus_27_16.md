# Leviticus 27:16

> [!quote] BSB
> "If a man consecrates to the LORD a parcel of his land, then your valuation shall be proportional to the seed required for it—fifty shekels of silver for every homer of barley seed." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *A homer* is a dry measure of approximately 6.24 bushels or 220 liters (probably about 291 pounds or 132 kilograms of barley seed).
%%FN-END%%
