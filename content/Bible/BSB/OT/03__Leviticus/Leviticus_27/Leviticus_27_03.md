# Leviticus 27:3

> [!quote] BSB
> "if the valuation concerns a male from twenty to sixty years of age, then your valuation shall be fifty shekels of silver, according to the sanctuary shekel." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *50 shekels* is approximately 1.26 pounds or 569.8 grams of silver; also in verse 16.
- *A shekel* weighed approximately 0.4 ounces or 11.4 grams; also in verse 25.
%%FN-END%%
