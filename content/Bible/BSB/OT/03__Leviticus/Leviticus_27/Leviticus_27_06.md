# Leviticus 27:6

> [!quote] BSB
> "Now if the person is from one month to five years of age, then your valuation for the male shall be five shekels of silver, and for the female three shekels of silver." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *5 shekels* is approximately 2 ounces or 57 grams of silver.
- *3 shekels* is approximately 1.2 ounces or 34.2 grams of silver.
%%FN-END%%
