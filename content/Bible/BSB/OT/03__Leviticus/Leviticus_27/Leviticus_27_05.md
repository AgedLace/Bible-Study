# Leviticus 27:5

> [!quote] BSB
> "And if the person is from five to twenty years of age, then your valuation for the male shall be twenty shekels, and for the female ten shekels." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *20 shekels* is approximately 8 ounces or 228 grams of silver.
- *10 shekels* is approximately 4 ounces or 114 grams of silver; also in verse 7.
%%FN-END%%
