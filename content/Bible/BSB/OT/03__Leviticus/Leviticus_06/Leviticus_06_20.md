# Leviticus 6:20

> [!quote] BSB
> "“This is the offering that Aaron and his sons must present to the LORD on the day he is anointed: a tenth of an ephah of fine flour as a regular grain offering, half of it in the morning and half in the evening." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *A tenth of an ephah* is approximately 2 dry quarts or 2.2 liters (probably about 2.6 pounds or 1.2 kilograms of flour).
%%FN-END%%
