# Leviticus 5:15

> [!quote] BSB
> "“If someone acts unfaithfully and sins unintentionally against any of the LORD’s holy things, he must bring his guilt offering to the LORD: an unblemished ram from the flock, of proper value in silver shekels according to the sanctuary shekel; it is a guilt offering." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Or *flock or its equivalence*
- *A shekel* is approximately 0.4 ounces or 11.4 grams of silver.
%%FN-END%%
