# Leviticus 14:2

> [!quote] BSB
> "“This is the law for the one afflicted with a skin disease on the day of his cleansing, when he is brought to the priest." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Forms of the Hebrew *tzaraath*, traditionally translated as *leprosy*, were used for various skin diseases; also in verses 3, 7, 32, 54, and 57.
%%FN-END%%
