# Leviticus 14:34

> [!quote] BSB
> "“When you enter the land of Canaan, which I am giving you as your possession, and I put a contamination of mildew into a house in that land," ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Forms of the Hebrew *tzaraath*, traditionally translated as *leprosy* regarding skin diseases, are translated as *mildew* regarding blemishes on garments, utensils, or buildings; here and throughout the remainder of this chapter.
%%FN-END%%
