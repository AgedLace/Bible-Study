# Leviticus 22:4

> [!quote] BSB
> "If a descendant of Aaron has a skin disease or a discharge, he may not eat the sacred offerings until he is clean. Whoever touches anything defiled by a corpse or by a man who has an emission of semen," ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- Forms of the Hebrew *tzaraath*, traditionally translated as *leprosy*, were used for various skin diseases; see Leviticus 13.
%%FN-END%%
