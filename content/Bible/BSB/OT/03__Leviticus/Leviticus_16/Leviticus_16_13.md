# Leviticus 16:13

> [!quote] BSB
> "He is to put the incense on the fire before the LORD, and the cloud of incense will cover the mercy seat above the Testimony, so that he will not die." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *The Testimony* refers to the stone tablets in the ark of the covenant inscribed with the Ten Commandments.
%%FN-END%%
