# Leviticus 19:36

> [!quote] BSB
> "You shall maintain honest scales and weights, an honest ephah, and an honest hin. I am the LORD your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt." ^text

%%FN-START%%
> [!note] Footnotes
- *An ephah* is a dry measure of approximately 20 dry quarts or 22 liters.
- *A hin* is a liquid measure of approximately 0.97 gallons or 3.67 liters.
%%FN-END%%
