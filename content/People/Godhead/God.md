# God

## For future agent

Used when the biblical text says "God" without specifying which person of the Godhead. In Genesis 1:1-5, "God" appears in every verse — sometimes referring to the Father as Creator, sometimes to the Son as the Word speaking, sometimes to the Spirit hovering. This is a placeholder for unresolved references until further study clarifies the person in view.
