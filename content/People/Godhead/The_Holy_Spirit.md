# The Holy Spirit

## For future agent

The third person of the Godhead. In Genesis 1:2, "the Spirit of God was hovering over the surface of the waters" — the Spirit is active in creation, bringing order to the formless and void.

## Biblical references

- [[Genesis_01_02\|Genesis 1:2]] — "And the Spirit of God was hovering over the face of the waters."
- [[Psalms_104_30\|Psalms 104:30]] — "When you send forth your Spirit, they are created"
- [[Titus_3_5\|Titus 3:5]] — "renewal of the Holy Spirit"
