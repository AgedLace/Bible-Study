# Jesus the Son

## For future agent

The second person of the Godhead. The Word through whom all things were made. In Genesis 1:3, "And God said, 'Let there be light'" — the Son is the creative Word, the agent of the Father's speaking.

## Biblical references

- [[John_01_01\|John 1:1]] — "In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God."
- [[John_01_03\|John 1:3]] — "All things were made through him, and without him was not any thing made that was made."
- [[Colossians_1_16\|Colossians 1:16]] — "For by him all things were created, in heaven and on earth."
