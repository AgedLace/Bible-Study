# The Father

## For future agent

The first person of the Godhead. Creator and sustainer of all things. In Genesis 1:1, "God created the heavens and the earth" — the Father is the primary actor in creation, working through the Son (the Word) and by the Spirit.

## Biblical references

- [[Genesis_01_01\|Genesis 1:1]] — "In the beginning God created the heavens and the earth."
- [[1_Corinthians_8_6\|1 Corinthians 8:6]] — "one God, the Father, from whom are all things"
- [[Ephesians_4_6\|Ephesians 4:6]] — "one God and Father of all"
