### 4d. Genre-specific questions
#### Module: Apocalyptic
**Questions**
- A1. Where is this in the book's structure (letters, seals, trumpets, bowls, visions)? →
- A2. What is the vision's frame (heaven/earth, time/eternity)? →
- A3. Who is the audience and what is their situation (persecution, compromise)? →
- A4. What is the immediate pastoral purpose? →
- A5. Who gives or interprets the vision (angel, Christ, Spirit)? →
- A6. Does the text interpret its own symbols? List them. →
- A7. For each symbol: its OT source, and its meaning in this context. Fill in the table. →
- A8. Which numbers appear, and are they symbolic (3, 4, 7, 10, 12, 42, 144,000, 1000)? →
- A9. Which colors, animals, metals, or celestial bodies appear? →
- A10. Is the symbol literal, figurative, or both? →
- A11. What OT passages are being echoed (Dan, Ezek, Zech, Isa, Exodus, Gen)? →
- A12. Is it recapitulation (parallel cycles) or chronological sequence? Evidence. →
- A13. What do the interpretive frameworks (preterist, historicist, futurist, idealist) say? Fill in the table. →
- A14. Which parts are hardest for each framework? →
- A15. Who are the major actors (God, Lamb, dragon, beasts, witnesses, saints)? →
- A16. What is the role of worship and the throne room? →
- A17. What is the call to the church (endure, repent, come out, conquer)? →
- A18. How does it end (judgment, new creation, new Jerusalem)? →
- A19. How do Jesus' Olivet Discourse and Paul's eschatological texts relate? →
- A20. What certainties can I hold, and which areas need humility? →
- A21. What does this reveal about Christ's victory and sovereignty? →
