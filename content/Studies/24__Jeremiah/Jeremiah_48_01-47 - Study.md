<summary>2. Context</summary>

| Layer | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Textual Note** — Jeremiah 48:2 | The Hebrew for *Heshbon* sounds like the Hebrew for *plot*. The name of the Moabite town *Madmen* sounds like the Hebrew for *silenced*. |
| **Textual Note** — Jeremiah 48:4 | Hebrew; LXX *her cries are heard as far away as Zoar* |
| **Textual Note** — Jeremiah 48:6 | Or *like a wild donkey* or *like Aroer* |
| **Textual Note** — Jeremiah 48:9 | Or *Give wings to Moab, for she would fly away* |
| **Textual Note** — Jeremiah 48:21 | *Jahzah* is a variant of *Jahaz*; see verse 34. |
| **Textual Note** — Jeremiah 48:31 | *Kir-heres* is a variant of *Kir-hareseth*; also in verse 36; see Isaiah 16:7. |
| **Textual Note** — Jeremiah 48:32 | Probably the Dead Sea Two Hebrew manuscripts and LXX; most Hebrew manuscripts *to the Sea of Jazer* |
| **Textual Note** — Jeremiah 48:47 | Or *restore the fortunes of Moab* |
