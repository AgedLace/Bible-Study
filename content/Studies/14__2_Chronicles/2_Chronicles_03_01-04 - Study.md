<summary>2. Context</summary>

| Layer | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Textual Note** — 2 Chronicles 3:1 | See LXX; Hebrew *where He had appeared* *Ornan* is a variant of *Araunah*; see 2 Samuel 24:16. |
| **Textual Note** — 2 Chronicles 3:3 | The foundation was approximately 90 feet long and 30 feet wide (27.4 meters long and 9.1 meters wide). The old standard of measurement was a cubit equal to 18 inches or 45.7 centimeters. The new standard, a cubit of approximately 21 inches or 53.3 centimeters (the long cubit) is the basic unit of length throughout Ezekiel 40 to 48. |
| **Textual Note** — 2 Chronicles 3:4 | *20 cubits* is approximately 30 feet or 9.1 meters; also in verses 8, 11, and 13. Some LXX and Syriac manuscripts; Hebrew *120 cubits high* (approximately 180 feet or 54.9 meters) |

- N5. Which words or phrases repeat (Leitwort)? →

- N7. What is said by characters vs. by the narrator? →
- N8. Where is God acting even when not named? →

- N10. Is the character a model, a warning, or neither? (descriptive ≠ prescriptive) →
- N11. What is not said (gaps), and what is implied? →
