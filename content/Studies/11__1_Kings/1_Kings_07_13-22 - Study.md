<summary>2. Context</summary>

| Layer | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Textual Note** — 1 Kings 7:13 | Hebrew *Hiram*, a variant of *Huram*; also in verses 40 and 45; see 2 Chronicles 4:11. Note that this is not Hiram king of Tyre mentioned in 1 Kings 5:1. |
| **Textual Note** — 1 Kings 7:15 | Each pillar was approximately 27 feet high and 18 feet in circumference (8.2 meters high and 5.5 meters in circumference). |
| **Textual Note** — 1 Kings 7:16 | *5 cubits* is approximately 7.5 feet or 2.3 meters. |
| **Textual Note** — 1 Kings 7:17 | Hebrew; LXX *one for each capital* |
| **Textual Note** — 1 Kings 7:19 | *4 cubits* is approximately 6 feet or 1.8 meters; also in verse 38. |
| **Textual Note** — 1 Kings 7:21 | *Jachin* probably means *He establishes*. *Boaz* probably means *in Him is strength*. |
