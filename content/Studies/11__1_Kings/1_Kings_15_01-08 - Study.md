<summary>2. Context</summary>

| Layer | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Textual Note** — 1 Kings 15:1 | *Abijam* is a variant of *Abijah*; some Hebrew manuscripts and LXX *Abijah*; also in verses 3, 6, 7, and 8; see 2 Chronicles 12:16. |
| **Textual Note** — 1 Kings 15:2 | *Abishalom* is a variant of *Absalom*; also in verse 10; see 2 Chronicles 11:20. |
| **Textual Note** — 1 Kings 15:6 | Literally *war between Rehoboam* (most Hebrew manuscripts); some Hebrew manuscripts and Syriac *war between Abijam* (that is, Abijah); most LXX texts do not contain this verse. Literally *of his life* |
