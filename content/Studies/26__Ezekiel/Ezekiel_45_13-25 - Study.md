<summary>2. Context</summary>

| Layer | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Textual Note** — Ezekiel 45:13 | That is, a sixtieth of the harvest of wheat and of barley; *a sixth of an ephah* was approximately 3.3 dry quarts or 3.7 liters. |
| **Textual Note** — Ezekiel 45:14 | That is, one percent of one’s oil; *a tenth of a bath* was approximately 2.3 quarts or 2.2 liters. |
| **Textual Note** — Ezekiel 45:17 | Or *purification offerings*; also in verses 19, 22, 23, and 25 |
| **Textual Note** — Ezekiel 45:24 | Literally *a hin of oil for each ephah*; that is, approximately 0.97 gallons or 3.67 liters of oil for each ephah of grain |
| **Textual Note** — Ezekiel 45:25 | That is, Sukkot, the autumn feast of pilgrimage to Jerusalem; also translated as *the Feast of Booths* or *the Feast of Shelters* and originally called *the Feast of Ingathering* (see Exodus 23:16 and Exodus 34:22). |
