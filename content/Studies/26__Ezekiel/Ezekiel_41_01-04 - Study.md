<summary>2. Context</summary>

| Layer | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Textual Note** — Ezekiel 41:1 | *6 (long) cubits* is approximately 10.5 feet or 3.2 meters; also in verses 3, 5, and 8. One Hebrew manuscript and LXX; most Hebrew manuscripts *on each side—the width of the tent* |
| **Textual Note** — Ezekiel 41:2 | *10 (long) cubits* is approximately 17.5 feet or 5.3 meters. *5 (long) cubits* is approximately 8.75 feet or 2.7 meters; also in verses 9, 11, and 12. The outer sanctuary was approximately 70 feet long and 35 feet wide (21.3 meters long and 10.7 meters wide). |
| **Textual Note** — Ezekiel 41:3 | *2 (long) cubits* is approximately 3.5 feet or 1.1 meters. *7 (long) cubits* is approximately 12.25 feet or 3.7 meters. |
| **Textual Note** — Ezekiel 41:4 | Or *the length of the inner sanctuary* The room was approximately 35 feet long and 35 feet wide (10.7 meters long and 10.7 meters wide). Or *the Holy of Holies* |
