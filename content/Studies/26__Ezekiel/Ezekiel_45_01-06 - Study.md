<summary>2. Context</summary>

| Layer | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Textual Note** — Ezekiel 45:1 | LXX; the holy portion was to be approximately 8.3 miles long and 6.6 miles wide (13.3 kilometers long and 10.7 kilometers wide). Hebrew *25,000 cubits long and 10,000 wide*. See also verses 3 and 5 and Ezekiel 48:9. |
| **Textual Note** — Ezekiel 45:2 | *500 (long) cubits* is approximately 875 feet or 266.7 meters. *50 (long) cubits* is approximately 87.5 feet or 26.7 meters. |
| **Textual Note** — Ezekiel 45:3 | The portion was to be approximately 8.3 miles long and 3.3 miles wide (13.3 kilometers long and 5.3 kilometers wide); similarly in verse 5. Or *the Holy of Holies* |
| **Textual Note** — Ezekiel 45:5 | LXX; Hebrew *their possession, twenty chambers* |
| **Textual Note** — Ezekiel 45:6 | The property of the city was to be approximately 1.7 miles wide and 8.3 miles long (2.7 kilometers wide and 13.3 kilometers long). |
