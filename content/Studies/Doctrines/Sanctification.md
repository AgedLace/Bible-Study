# Sanctification

God's work of making believers holy, both definitively and progressively, by the Spirit through the Word.

## Summary

Sanctification is God's transforming work in the believer, grounded in union with Christ and empowered by the Spirit.

## Key texts

- John 17:17 (sanctify by truth)
- Rom 6:1-14 (dead to sin, alive to God)
- 1 Thess 4:3-8 (God's will: sanctification)
- 2 Cor 3:18 (transformed into Christ's image)
- Heb 12:14 (pursue holiness)
