- C11. What OT passages are quoted in the speeches, and how are they used? →

- C14. How does the church respond (prayer, unity, giving, organization)? →
- C15. What conflicts arise and how are they resolved (Acts 6, 11, 15)? →
- C16. What signs, miracles, or healings occur, and what do they accomplish? →
- C17. How does the passage relate to epistle teaching (e.g., Romans, Galatians, 1 Corinthians)? →
- C18. What does it show about Gentile inclusion? →
