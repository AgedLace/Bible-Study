- C7. What is the role of the Holy Spirit (filling, guidance, power, gifts)? →
- C8. Is the event descriptive (what happened) or normative (what should always happen)? Fill in the table. →
- C9. Is the pattern repeated elsewhere, or unique to this situation? →
- C10. What is the apostolic role, and is it transferable? →
- C11. What OT passages are quoted in the speeches, and how are they used? →
- C12. How is the gospel message framed to this audience (Jews, God-fearers, Gentiles)? →
- C13. What is the response (belief, rejection, persecution)? →
- C14. How does the church respond (prayer, unity, giving, organization)? →
- C15. What conflicts arise and how are they resolved (Acts 6, 11, 15)? →
- C16. What signs, miracles, or healings occur, and what do they accomplish? →
- C17. How does the passage relate to epistle teaching (e.g., Romans, Galatians, 1 Corinthians)? →
- C18. What does it show about Gentile inclusion? →
- C19. What does it teach about the progress of the word (Acts 6:7; 12:24; 19:20)? →
