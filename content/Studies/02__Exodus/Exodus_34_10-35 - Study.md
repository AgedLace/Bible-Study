| **Textual Note** — Exodus 34:12 | Forms of the Hebrew *berit* are translated in most passages as *covenant*. |
| **Textual Note** — Exodus 34:18 | That is, the seven-day period after the Passover during which no leaven may be eaten; see Exodus 12:14–20. *Abib* was the first month of the ancient Hebrew lunar calendar, usually occurring within the months of March and April; twice in this verse. |
| **Textual Note** — Exodus 34:22 | That is, Shavuot, the late spring feast of pilgrimage to Jerusalem; it is also known as *the Feast of Harvest* (see Exodus 23:16) or *the Feast of Pentecost* (see Acts 2:1). That is, Sukkot, the autumn feast of pilgrimage to Jerusalem; it is later called *the Feast of Tabernacles* (or *Booths* or *Shelters*). |
