- W3. Is it a proverb (general truth) or a promise (guarantee)? →
- W4. Is it descriptive or prescriptive? →
- W5. What is the surrounding unit or collection? →
- W6. Who is speaking (father, Wisdom, Job, friend, Qoheleth, narrator)? →
- W7. Does the book endorse or correct this speaker? (Job 42:7; Eccl 12:13) →
- W8. How does the "fear of the LORD" frame it (Prov 1:7; 9:10)? →
- W9. What contrasts are used (wise/fool, righteous/wicked, diligent/lazy)? →
- W10. What images does it use (path, house, tree, woman Wisdom/Folly)? →
- W11. What are the exceptions or qualifiers elsewhere in Scripture? →
- W12. What is the tension (Prov vs. Job/Eccl)? How does the canon resolve it? →
- W13. What does it assume about creation order? →
- W14. What are its social, economic, or family assumptions? →
- W15. What does it say about speech, work, wealth, or relationships? →
- W16. What Hebrew words matter (hokmah, tov, hebel, sakal)? →
- W17. How is wisdom personified, and how does the NT connect it with Christ (1 Cor 1:24, Col 2:3)? →
- W18. How would a young person or a king have heard it? →
- W19. What skill or habit of life is being formed? →
- W20. What should I do, and where should I avoid over-claiming? →
