### 4d. Genre-specific questions
#### Module: Poetry / Psalms
**Questions**
- Y1. What type of psalm/poem is this (lament · praise · thanksgiving · royal · wisdom · imprecatory · messianic · pilgrimage · penitential)? →
- Y2. Is there a superscription, author, or setting? What does it add? →
- Y3. Who is speaking, and does the speaker change? →
- Y4. Who is addressed (God, self, congregation, enemies)? →
- Y5. What is the emotional movement (e.g., complaint → petition → trust)? →
- Y6. Where is the turning point (the "but", "yet", "nevertheless")? →
- Y7. How is the poem divided (strophes, stanzas, refrains, selah)? →
- Y8. Is it an acrostic or does it use another structure? What effect? →
- Y9. What parallelism types occur (synonymous, antithetic, synthetic, climactic)? Fill in the table. →
- Y10. What images dominate? Fill in the imagery table. →
- Y11. Which images are literal, which figurative? →
- Y12. What does the poem say about God's character and names used? →
- Y13. What does it say about the speaker's state and need? →
- Y14. What covenant or Torah themes stand behind it? →
- Y15. Is the poem intended for corporate worship? How might it have been used? →
- Y16. Is there a messianic or royal reading? What does the NT do with it? →
- Y17. If imprecatory, what is being asked, and on what basis? →
- Y18. What Hebrew word-plays, repeated roots, or sounds matter? →
- Y19. How does it fit its place in the Psalter (book, neighbors, group)? →
- Y20. What is the main emotional and theological point? →
- Y21. What does it teach me to pray or sing? →
