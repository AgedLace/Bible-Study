| **Textual Note** — 1 Chronicles 1:4 | LXX; Hebrew does not include *The sons of Noah:*; see Genesis 5:32. |

| **Textual Note** — 1 Chronicles 1:13 | Or *of the Sidonians, the foremost* Hebrew *and of Heth* |
| **Textual Note** — 1 Chronicles 1:17 | One Hebrew manuscript and some LXX manuscripts (see also Genesis 10:23); most Hebrew manuscripts do not include *The sons of Aram*. *Meshech* is a variant of *Mash*; see Genesis 10:23. |
| **Textual Note** — 1 Chronicles 1:19 | *Peleg* means *division*. |
| **Textual Note** — 1 Chronicles 1:22 | LXX and Syriac (see also Genesis 10:28); Hebrew *Ebal* |
| **Textual Note** — 1 Chronicles 1:24 | Literally *Shem, Arphaxad, Shelah*; some LXX manuscripts *Shem, Arphaxad, Cainan, Shelah*; see also Genesis 10:24 LXX and Luke 3:35–36. |

- N5. Which words or phrases repeat (Leitwort)? →

- N7. What is said by characters vs. by the narrator? →

- N10. Is the character a model, a warning, or neither? (descriptive ≠ prescriptive) →

- N16. What does it reveal about human nature and sin? →
- N17. How does it advance the promise (seed, land, blessing, kingdom)? →
