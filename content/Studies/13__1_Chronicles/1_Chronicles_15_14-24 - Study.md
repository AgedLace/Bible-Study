|---|---|
| **Textual Note** — 1 Chronicles 15:18 | Several Hebrew manuscripts and most LXX manuscripts (see also verse 20 and 1 Chronicles 16:5); most Hebrew manuscripts *Zechariah son of* or *Zechariah, Ben,* Hebrew; some LXX manuscripts *Obed-edom, Jeiel, and Azaziah* or *Obed-edom, Jeiel, and Ozias* |
| **Textual Note** — 1 Chronicles 15:20 | *Aziel* is a variant of *Jaaziel*; see verse 18. *Alamoth* is probably a musical or liturgical term; here and in Psalm 46:1. |
| **Textual Note** — 1 Chronicles 15:21 | *Sheminith* is probably a musical term; here and in Psalm 6:1 and Psalm 12:1. |
| **Textual Note** — 1 Chronicles 15:23 | Literally *gatekeepers*; also in verse 24 |
