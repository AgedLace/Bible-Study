| Layer | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Textual Note** — 1 Chronicles 2:6 | *Zimri* is a variant of *Zabdi*; see Joshua 7:1. Most Hebrew manuscripts; many Hebrew manuscripts, some LXX manuscripts, and Syriac (see also 1 Kings 4:31) *Darda* |
| **Textual Note** — 1 Chronicles 2:7 | Hebrew *sons* *Achar* means *troubler*; also called *Achan* in Joshua 7 and Joshua 22. |
| **Textual Note** — 1 Chronicles 2:9 | Hebrew *Chelubai*, a variant of *Caleb*; see verse 18. |
| **Textual Note** — 1 Chronicles 2:11 | LXX (see also Ruth 4:20–21); Hebrew *Salma*; twice in this verse |
| **Textual Note** — 1 Chronicles 2:13 | *Shimea* is a variant of *Shammah*, *Shimeah*, and *Shimei*; see 1 Samuel 16:9, 2 Samuel 13:3, and 2 Samuel 21:21. |
| **Textual Note** — 1 Chronicles 2:17 | *Jether* is a variant of *Ithra*; see 2 Samuel 17:25. |
| **Textual Note** — 1 Chronicles 2:19 | *Ephrath* is a variant of *Ephrathah*; see verse 50. |
| **Textual Note** — 1 Chronicles 2:23 | Or *the villages of Jair* |
| **Textual Note** — 1 Chronicles 2:24 | Or *After Hezron died, Caleb had relations with Ephrathah, the wife of Hezron his father, and she bore*; see also LXX. Or *the founder*; also in verses 42, 45, 49, and possibly elsewhere |
| **Textual Note** — 1 Chronicles 2:31 | Hebrew *sons*; three times in this verse |
| **Textual Note** — 1 Chronicles 2:42 | Hebrew; LXX *Mareshah* |
| **Textual Note** — 1 Chronicles 2:50 | LXX and Vulgate; Hebrew *son* *Ephrathah* is a variant of *Ephrath*; see verse 19. |
| **Textual Note** — 1 Chronicles 2:55 | Or *of the Sopherites* Or *the father of Beth-rekab* or *the founder of the house of Rechab* |
