# Fall

## For future agent

Doctrine note for the Fall — humanity's fall into sin through Adam and Eve. Links to all studies where Fall was touched, plus key scripture references.

## Definition

Adam and Eve's disobedience in Genesis 3 introduced sin, death, and separation from God into the world, affecting all creation and all humanity.
