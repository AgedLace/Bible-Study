# Holy Spirit

## For future agent

Doctrine note for the Holy Spirit — the third person of the Godhead. Links to all studies where the Holy Spirit was touched, plus key scripture references.

## Definition

The Holy Spirit is the third person of the Godhead, active in creation (hovering over the waters), regeneration, sanctification, and empowerment of believers.

## Key Scripture

- [[Genesis_01_02\|Genesis 1:2]] — "The Spirit of God was hovering over the face of the waters."
- [[Acts_02_01\|Acts 2:1]] — "When the day of Pentecost arrived..."
- [[John_14_16\|John 14:16]] — "I will ask the Father, and he will give you another Helper..."
