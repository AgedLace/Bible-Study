# Christology

## For future agent

Doctrine note for Christology — the person and work of Jesus Christ. Links to all studies where Christology was touched, plus key scripture references.

## Definition

Jesus Christ is the eternal Son of God, fully divine and fully human, the Creator and Redeemer, the Word through whom all things were made.

## Key Scripture

- [[Genesis_01_01\|Genesis 1:1]] — "In the beginning God created the heavens and the earth."
- [[John_01_01\|John 1:1]] — "In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God."
- [[John_01_03\|John 1:3]] — "All things were made through him."
- [[1_John_01_01\|1 John 1:1]] — "That which was from the beginning..."
- [[Revelation_01_01\|Revelation 1:1]] — "The revelation of Jesus Christ..."
