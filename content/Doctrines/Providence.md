# Providence

## For future agent

Doctrine note for Providence — God's sovereign governance over all events and circumstances. Links to all studies where Providence was touched, plus key scripture references.

## Definition

God's providence is His continuous, sovereign upholding and governing of all creation and all events to accomplish His purposes.

## Key Scripture

- [[Esther_04_14\|Esther 4:14]] — "For such a time as this."
- [[Proverbs_16_9\|Proverbs 16:9]] — "The heart of man plans his way, but the LORD establishes his steps."
- [[Romans_08_28\|Romans 8:28]] — "For those who love God all things work together for good..."
