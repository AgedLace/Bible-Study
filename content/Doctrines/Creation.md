# Creation

## For future agent

Doctrine note for Creation — God's act of bringing the universe into existence. Links to all studies where Creation was touched, plus key scripture references.

## Definition

God created the heavens and the earth and all that is in them, ex nihilo, by His spoken word, and declared it good.

## Key Scripture

- [[Genesis_01_01\|Genesis 1:1]] — "In the beginning God created the heavens and the earth."
- [[Genesis_01_31\|Genesis 1:31]] — "God saw everything that he had made, and behold, it was very good."
- [[Psalms_033_006\|Psalms 33:6]] — "By the word of the LORD the heavens were made."
