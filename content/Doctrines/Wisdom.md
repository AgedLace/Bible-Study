# Wisdom

## For future agent

Doctrine note for Wisdom — God's wisdom and its application in human life. Links to all studies where Wisdom was touched, plus key scripture references.

## Definition

Wisdom is the skill of living skillfully in God's world, rooted in the fear of the LORD and revealed in Christ.

## Key Scripture

- [[Proverbs_01_07\|Proverbs 1:7]] — "The fear of the LORD is the beginning of knowledge."
- [[James_01_05\|James 1:5]] — "If any of you lacks wisdom, let him ask of God..."
- [[Colossians_02_03\|Colossians 2:3]] — "In whom are hidden all the treasures of wisdom and knowledge."
